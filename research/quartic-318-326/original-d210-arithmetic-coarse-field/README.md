# Arithmetic field of the original D=210 coarse model

The four radicals are identified over the rational parameter field, not merely after extending scalars. For a finite nonbranch selected point their residue field already splits the quaternion algebra at every ramified prime. The second note identifies that algebra with the actual non-CM moduli descent obstruction and removes the extra quadratic extension on that route.

The statements here concern the original coarse curve and the same selected non-CM abelian surface. They do not construct the fine-level marking, a model at the bad primes, or the arithmetic Kodaira--Spencer comparison. Neither paper is Lean-verified. Draft Lean files are deliberately excluded from this paper bundle; the unchanged full source manifest and paper-only replay manifest are both included.
