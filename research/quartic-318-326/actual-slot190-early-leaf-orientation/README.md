# Actual detector contact forces one split orientation

[The paper proof](linked-detector-eight-leaf-orientation-r1/ACTUAL-EARLY-LEAVES-FORCE-SPLIT-ORIENTATION.md) applies to the three unchanged original detector slots with one actual h and literal labels. At a good odd contact prime with positive odd contact valuation and unit scale and Delta, a nonzero pole admitting a PointPair forces p to split in Q(sqrt(2)). Exactly one orientation is possible: [2h]R reduces to O on one arm, and [h-1]R lies in {O,(0,0)} on the opposite arm. This follows from the eight literal early leaves and the last six squarings, not any extra hypothesis on the other fold coefficients.

Except at p=41 the opposite contribution to the indexed norm has depth zero. The positive arm alone must meet the earlier 3e+1 bound; at a prime not dividing h this means the first formal return already has depth at least four. Split and inert signs, the finite quotient, and the scope were [checked independently](joined-early-eight-orientation-cold-r1/INDEPENDENT-EARLY-EIGHT-ORIENTATION-REVIEW.md). Also included are the exact earlier paper receipts for the denominator guard, signed depth, and fixed-index application; the latter is not needed for the general theorem.

It does not produce a full support pattern, certify a rational PointPair, or supply a Lean theorem. The separate singleton retune canary is not included.
