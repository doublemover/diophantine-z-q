# Original a=617: marked first Cassels row and oriented-prime tests

This paper gives an exact first Cassels row for the original (a=617) degree-two graph and a finite local source design. It does not evaluate a full pairing on a fixed scalar, prove a new global Selmer admission, prove rank, or give a graph point.

## Exact conics and row

Use the fixed graph Jacobian J_delta: y²=x(x−δe)(x−δf), with e,f the exact ordered negative roots; z=u/w=368900221027188880/362538667625927811, M=z²+2, λ=M/18=βt₀², t₀=1/(3w), β=199477972060937362209233596181078921, βraw=(11·359)²β. Let r²=λ, K=Q(r), µ=βc², δ=1−µ, j=c/t₀, so δ=N(1+jr). Define

X₀=−4z(z−2)(z²+2)/3,
B_e=12z(z²+2z+4),
B_f=6z(z²−4z−8),
α_e=X₀+rB_e, α_f=X₀+rB_f.

Multiplication gives Nα_e=e and Nα_f=f. Equivalently these are the two conic coordinates from the known δ=1 point v=1. The full [2]-class of the graph is η=(1,λ,λ): its cocycle is χβ T₀ with T₀=(0,0), and the Weil pairing of T₀ with itself is +, with the other two roots −. The corresponding cover in (X,Y,Z,W) has X²−λY²=δeW², X²−λZ²=δfW². Tangents on its three conics use L₀=Y−Z (tangent at Y=Z=1,W=0) and Le=(X₀+λjB_e)X−λ(B_e+jX₀)Y−δeW, Lf similarly with f,Z. This is an explicit rational tangent triple: the conic points at W=1 are the coordinates of α_e(1+jr), α_f(1+jr). The standard full-2 Cassels formula pairs these in order with the three squareclasses b=(b₀,b_e,b_f) of an actually admitted Sel₂ vector, multiplying local Hilbert symbols. Its radical is the image from Sel₄; the local formula is not itself an admission test. Cassels 1998, Lemma 7.4 (DOI 10.1515/CRLL.1998.001); see also Fisher arXiv:1509.03234.

Let Σ include 2,3 and every p supporting numerator or denominator of z,z±4,M,Q₂=z²−40z−32,C₃=7z³+16z²+32z+32. This contains the fixed sign support and denom(z); it ensures r,e,f,e−f and α_e,α_f are units outside, and no ramification of K. At p∉Σ with vδ odd, δ has positive depth (poles are even), K splits and j is a unit. Orient the arm by jr≡1. Take P=(1,r⁻¹,−r⁻¹,0) exactly over Q_p. Tangents evaluate to 2/r, bar(α_e) δ/(1+jr), α_f(1+jr). The local E/2 is generated by T_e=(δe,0), T_f=(δf,0): finite quotient after a pro-p subgroup has quotient-by-2 size |E(Q_p)[2]|=4, and their valuation parities (1,0,1),(1,1,0) are independent. Kummer triples Te=(δe,e(e−f),δ(e−f)); Tf=(δf,δ(f−e),f(f−e)). The local Tate signs are respectively the Legendre symbols of

κ_e=α_f e(e−f)/r,  κ_f=bar(α_e)(e−f)/r,

at that exact oriented prime of K. The unit ~2 in 1+jr and (δ,δ)_p=(δ,−1)_p yield these signs; in Tf the extra minus from the two odd valuations reverses f−e. For general admitted b the exponents are v_p(b_f), v_p(b_e) modulo 2; T0 uses both. Thus no first unit of δ is needed outside fixed support (but admission itself can depend on it).

At p∉Σ and δ even depth the local Kummer classes are units, because scaling off the even valuation makes J good. In split arms the same W=0 evaluation gives nothing unless j has a pole of depth k, in which case Le and Lf both have depth −k and the sign is (b₀/p)^k. In an inert arm with j integral, δ is a unit and primitive conic tangents have even depth: the tangent divisor on the smooth conic is twice its marked section and projected tuples stay primitive (otherwise the remaining root equation forces the whole tuple nonprimitive). At an inert pole rescale X,Y,Z by p^−k and the conic seeds by p^−k; normalize δ by p^−2k. Norm is now a unit and all tangent points primitive, L0 has scale p^−k, Le/Lf scale p^−2k, so the same denominator sign. This leaves a rational Jacobi factor of b₀ against the outside-fixed part of the reduced denominator of c, plus the two oriented ideal Artin factors, plus all fixed Σ local pairings. None of the fixed rows or actual global b has been supplied.

## What reciprocity determines; explicit evaluation for supplied subsets

Write j=C/D lowest with D>0 and principal A=D−Cr. Outside Σ, at p|D both numerator arms are units. If δ is a nonunit at an integral p, the oriented arm of A has depth v_pδ and its conjugate arm is a unit. Thus the full oriented θ Artin product for either κ can be computed by Hilbert reciprocity as ∏_{w|Σ or real}(κ,A)_w; A>0 in each real embedding since µ<1. The proper subsets for b_f,b_e are instead specified by the ideal gcd (A,b_i) away Σ, modulo even exponents. Rational b_i alone involves both arms. Rational torsion Te,Tf also gives the full products from its pairing being 1, but only complements the relevant subsets and supplies no independent mixed bits.

For the normal computational path, the relative conic is not needed. Reduce the oriented root (r=D/Cmod b') with unit (C,D) modulo (b') (or compute it from the supplied (jmod b')). Evaluate (kappa_e) or (kappa_f) at that root modulo (b'), using the fixed rational coefficient inverses. The whole proper-ideal product is then the ordinary Jacobi symbol of that residue modulo (b'). The norm-conic construction below gives a second, principal-up-to-squares certificate that uses fixed-place Hilbert symbols. The two routines use the same supplied oriented root and do not discover a Selmer vector.

If a full b is supplied in explicit squarefree integers, the outside part b'=|b_i|_outside has support entirely on odd θ, so no additional θ extraction is required. Given the oriented root ρ=√β mod b', which equals D/(Ct₀) if j mod b' is available, one can evaluate the proper Artin product without factoring b' or computing a class group. Enlarge Σ ahead of time by a finite roster of correction primes for the even parity patterns at p|β. All p|b' split β, so ∏_{p|β}(b'/p)=+ by reciprocity and β≡1 mod4. Choose a fixed positive prime ℓ≡1 mod8 in the row whose (ℓ/p)=(b'/p) for each p|β. Such primes exist by Dirichlet and also split β. Put B=b'ℓ (or ℓ=1 for the all-square row), adjoining an explicit chosen root at ℓ to ρ by CRT. Now ρ²=β mod B and a root σ²=B mod β is obtained by its fixed factorization. For x²−βy²=B z₁², the CRT constraints x≡ρy mod B and x≡σz₁ mod β give an index βB lattice. On it the bilinear form diag(1,−β,−B)/(βB) is integral, indefinite signature (1,2), determinant 1. Feed this directly to Simon's unimodular indefinite conic algorithm, bypassing his factor-dependent minimization (Math. Comp. DOI 10.1090/S0025-5718-05-01729-1), or use Cremona–Rusin with the explicit solubility certificate (https://johncremona.github.io/papers/conics.pdf). Primitive (x,y,z₁) has at each p|B exactly one arm of γ=x+y√β with positive odd valuation: both would force p|x,y,z₁; by the norm identity their sum is odd. The orientation-mismatch factor is g=B/gcd(B,x+ρy). Multiply γ by rational g to switch the odd arm at every mismatch. At other nonexceptional p primitivity rules out both arms positive or any inert division, and the norm gives even valuation. Thus (gγ) has the exact prescribed odd oriented ideal support modulo squares outside fixed places, with ℓ a known correction. Reciprocity plus the explicitly known ℓ-Artin factor reduces the supplied subset sign to finite local Hilbert evaluations. This is polynomial in explicit input length using the solubility certificate; does not bound length of actual Selmer integers or construct/compute j modulo them. For a pure-second b=(1,s,s), on p|s∩θ the type is T₀, hence ef is square locally. Then κ_eκ_f≡α_eα_f modulo squares and both oriented signs agree (norm ef); the outside row is an unoriented central D₄ character of K(√ef,√(α_eα_f)) on the proper subset. The conic norms give (s,ef)_v=1 everywhere. Fixed rows remain necessary.

## Genuine additional source primes, not the literal numeric h,J

At split p13 we have v(f)=1 and e,e−f,r units; the valuations on the two primes over 13 of κ_e and its conjugate are complementary bits, and κ_f and its conjugate are units. At split p37649 we have v(e)=1 and f,e−f,r units; the κ_f pair is complementary, and the κ_e pair is odd on both. Splitting follows already from e/f norm with odd valuation at an unramified prime. Thus the four classes κ_e,barκ_e,κ_f,barκ_f are independent and every nontrivial quadratic combination ramifies at some place over 13 or 37649. Full, proper, and conjugate products cannot be identified merely by un-oriented norm information.
