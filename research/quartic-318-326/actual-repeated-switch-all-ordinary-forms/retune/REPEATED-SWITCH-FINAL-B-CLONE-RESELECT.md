# Final marked Sel₄ tuning after all finite pair switches

This extends the checked single-pair replacement argument
(4c8a7d1a…) to the finitely many explicitly constructed pairs
of 6bce1645…. Fix the same literal XVI, a, the provisional
b=p_b q_b*, and every new pair and its exact w² contact balls.
The conclusion is the ordinary full Sel₄ lift of the same
normalized mark after replacing the provisional b-clone,
with every normalized same-line still zero. No pointed third
factor, maximal arithmetic radical, parity, or rational
point is asserted.

1. During each pair's positive-density prime selection discard
   the finite set of divisors of N((π_b*)⁴−1), where π_b* is
   the fixed actual generator of the provisional clone. At
   each newly selected contact both group counts are chosen
   prime to q_b* by the stripped q_b* ray. Neither count at
   q_b* itself is divisible by q_b*: it is a good large prime,
   each count is divisible by four and lies between zero and
   twice q_b* by Hasse. When all pairs are chosen, remove
   q_b*'s contact ball and its prime-power part from the
   final index modulus, retaining all orders and all other
   local balls. The pair selections and the original clone
   guarantee no other h-modulus part at q_b*. The actual
   q_b* fulfills all inherited point-order caps after this
   deletion, by construction of the preceding common h
   congruences.

2. Use q_b* itself as an actual order/Frobenius pilot for the
   final b-clone. Copy its Gaussian generator at the fixed
   modulus with all still-retained contact primes stripped
   from the order layers. At every retained contact,
   including each prime of every pair, copy the norm sign
   of q_b*. It is compatible with the group-count units:
   q_b* avoided μ₄ there in its first selection or by the
   finite exclusions in step 1. At every fixed place the
   ratio of the two b-clones is a square. Both a and each
   pair product are squares at the new b-clone by
   reciprocity from those retained signs, just as at
   q_b*. Its ordinary t-root ball can be chosen with
   the correct stripped unit in the new b-factor. The
   original CRT, formal and real checks are unchanged.

3. The starting unswitched normalized V is fixed under the
   clone comparison. Inductively in the exact repeated
   switches, the normalized V⁰ kernel at each pair and the
   actual old torsion evaluation C there are unchanged:
   the same global cocycles evaluate at those same primes,
   and the ratio of the literal twists is a square there.
   Hence every subsequent normalized V and each
   H_new(T)=χ_d T+H(L_H⁻¹ C(T)) is literally fixed under the
   comparison. This does not renormalize at any pair prime.
   Each global cocycle is trivial at either b-clone by the
   starting normalization and the local squareness of
   a and all pair products. In particular all same-line
   spaces remain zero and the normalized mark F_* is
   exactly (t,aδ,atδ).

4. The two exact inertias at p_a and 2399 used in the prior
   marked commutator proof remain central in the order
   governor over Q(i). The former is a stripped contact.
   For the latter, initial L is prime to 2399, the
   preliminary q_a counts are 1645 and 2144 modulo 2399,
   and q_b* and every added pair were selected using the
   unchanged p_b Gaussian phase there, hence counts 1854
   and 1621. None is zero, and all new contact primes
   lie off 2399. So no additional layer from a new pair
   introduces 2399 ramification except the abelian ray.
   The proof of 4c8a7d1a with the fixed final V and the
   expanded fixed finite support now gives the same exact
   result: the two independent evaluations of F_* reduce
   an annihilated row to JF_*+χ_{−1}v, and 2,3,∞ and the
   fixed odd collision exclude everything but multiples
   of F_*. These local conditions do not see the added
   pair products, all locally square at those places.
   [Smith, Theorem 3.2 and its proof (3.1)–(3.3)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.07860)
   compares the two normalized twists of the same fixed
   E_{a p_b∏d_j}. The central shifts kill the whole
   reference marked Cassels row while remaining trivial
   on the exact order/ray data and normalized cocycle
   fields. All extra governor ramification lies in the
   finitely enlarged old bad set, so the same positive
   density and Gaussian tail sieve applies. The full
   Cassels criterion then puts the literal marked class
   in the image of Sel₄.

This is a re-tuning of one chosen q_b after the finite induction,
not an assertion that an arbitrary previously selected q_b
can be kept through the Cassels lift. Its earlier provisional
w-ball is replaced, while all original XVI and newly added
pair contact balls are retained.
