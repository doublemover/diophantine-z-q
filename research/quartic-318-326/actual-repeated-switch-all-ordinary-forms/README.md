# Complete control of the remaining ordinary pairing

A finite sequence of actual prime switches preserves the original sixteen
contact conditions, and the final prime reselection restores the full
marked 4-Selmer lift. In the resulting ordinary Selmer family, the record
of the switches supplies an actual hyperbolic block and allows every
alternating pairing on the remaining quotient after removing that block
and the marked line.

This determines the attainable rank in terms of parity. If the remaining
dimension is even, precisely the marked line is the normalized radical;
otherwise the smallest possible radical has dimension two. The proof does
not compute the actual parity, assert a pointed genus-17 lift on the new
family, supply a Mordell–Weil point, or certify a Lean theorem.
