# The original rational compact differential in the original Abel period frame

This is the exact original paper proof and its independent mathematical
review. The author and reviewer files are unmodified.

On the actual ordinary rational chart the selected analytic evaluation is
fixed by the literal J and primitive root coordinates. Its derivative is
a derivation over that very evaluation map. Thus the checked contraction
coefficient in the normalized dJ frame is evaluated at the same period
and multiplied by the literal analytic derivative j′(τ). Both charts of
the original compact normalization give the same coefficient. Passing to
the chosen complex embedding and the actual pointed Abel map into J_X
gives the same ordered evaluation matrix, with j′(τ_j) in its j-th column
and no additional scale. No equality with the other abelian variety in
the arithmetic comparison is asserted.

The chart and dJ inputs of the argument are from the strict Compact70
Lean run identified in EXACT-PROVENANCE.tsv. The paper's analytic
uniformization, flat scalar passage, and pointed Abel comparison are
paper arguments. This package does not assert native acceptance for
analytic readout/height proofs, regularity of a chosen center, or the
closed original height theorem. The independently reviewed same-J
derivative/Haar comparison is the companion cited in EXACT-PROVENANCE.tsv.
