# Two Mestre directions at the original scalar

The [genus-nine cover](ct-original-scalar-mestre-genus-nine-local-r1/LITERAL-MESTRE-GENUS-NINE-MULTISECTION.md) has points over every completion at the unchanged original scalar. The [exact Mordell-Weil obstruction](ct-original-scalar-mestre-genus-nine-mw-sieve-r1/ACTUAL-GENUS-NINE-MORDELL-WEIL-OBSTRUCTION.md) proves it has no rational point: reductions at 17 and 131 force opposite parity in the same rank-one elliptic quotient.

The [different genus-one cover](ct-original-scalar-mestre-marked-genus-one-r1/LITERAL-MESTRE-MARKED-MULTISECTION.md) remains open. Its local condition is a norm from Q(sqrt(5)) at the same original coefficient. Neither result decides rationality of the original Kummer surface or of the separate fixed quartic fibre.

Independent reviews and exact arithmetic replays are included. No Lean proof is claimed.
