# A prime-pair switch preserving the marked 4-Selmer lift

An actual two-prime change keeps the old sixteen contact conditions and the
normalized total 2-Selmer dimension, but lowers two specified single-line
dimensions by one. Reselecting one of the auxiliary primes restores the full
4-Selmer lift of the marked class on the switched curve. These are
existence results; no numerical primes, rational point, or reduction in the
remaining obstruction after projection through a replacement pairing block
are proved. This packet does not retain the older pointed genus-17 lift,
nor does it assert that the previous pairing block survives the switch.
