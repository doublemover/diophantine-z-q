**Scope.** Fix the original literal XVI scalar, scales, sixteen
contact balls and even index progression. The old two-class support
is a=p_a q_a, b=p_b q_b, with the already verified pilots
p_a=415721, p_b=9807649. The old unseen primes may be selected anew.
For this same source there exist q_a,q_b and an added pair r_1,r_2

and the s-line dimension stays unchanged. There is no numerical
claim for the new primes, no retained Sel₄ lift claimed after the
switch, and no global rational point or PointPair.
