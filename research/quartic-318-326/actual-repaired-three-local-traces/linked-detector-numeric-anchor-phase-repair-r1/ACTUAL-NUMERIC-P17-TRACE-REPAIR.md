## Why a further numerical witness need not clone every power of B

For the original **saturation** method, the direct conditions
are precisely unit local squareness of \(a,b\) at every \(p\mid B\);
odd local square checks use only the residue modulo \(p\) and
the dyadic square check only modulo \(8\). The actual saturation

ramification prime and the avoidance of shared odd group divisors

counts modulo \(p\)); the finite order-cap and unramified-detachment
proofs otherwise use the unchanged \(L_0\). The primorial has

factorial; it is only a bound for these exact-coordinate rays,

avoid these coordinate congruences altogether if all requisite
quadratic signs, exact point orders, and actual ordinary signs
are verified. The first four extra anchor primes here are not
the full thousands needed by that saturation approach.
