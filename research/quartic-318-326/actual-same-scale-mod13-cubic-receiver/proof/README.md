## 1. The actual order-three detector

the fixed support conditions. Put \(d'=rD\) and
\(\Delta=h'd'\). At 13 one has \(r\equiv2\) nonsquare
and \(h'\equiv3\) square. Thus \(\Delta\) is nonsquare,
the 13 prime is inert in \(K'=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{\Delta})\),
and over the quadratic residue field the \(d'\)-twist
eigenspace is the **negative** Frobenius space.
As in the 61 argument the rational 13 ideal in \(K'\)
is principal, so no higher Hilbert-class residue field
is needed for the reduced marked orbit.

The exact counts on \(E^\flat\) are 16 over \(\mathbb F_{13}\)
and 192 over \(\mathbb F_{13^2}\), since
\(a_{13}=-2\) and \(192=(14+2)(14-2)\). The anti-invariant

\([4]\bar Q'\ne O\) would be exactly a nonzero order-3
projection and prove a rational nontorsion point on the

a local square), excluding rational 3-torsion.
The same fixed even donor and Gross–Zagier comparison
would then give an odd derivative, with no converse.
This is a sufficient finite test, not a nonzero assertion.

The full order-3 subgroup over \(k=\mathbb F_{13^2}\)
is cyclic of order 3, **not** full geometric 3-torsion.

## 2. Exact tiny cubic on the elliptic target only

In short coordinate \(X=x+4\) over \(\mathbb F_{13}\), the
target is \(y^2=X^3+5X+7\), so its nonsquare-2 twist is
\[
 E^-:y^2=X^3+7X+4.
\]
It has a rational order-three generator \(g=(12,3)\).
The isomorphism over \(k\) from \(E^-\) to the short
\(E^\flat\) divides \(X,y\) by \(2,2\sqrt2\),
respectively, and identifies \(g\) up to the chosen
sign of \(\sqrt2\) with the order-three coordinate.

Take the normalized Vélu quotient by \(\langle g\rangle\).
It is \(E^\star:y^2=X^3+11X+9\). Its dual isogeny
into \(E^-\) has kernel generated by \(K=(3,2)\);
one can verify this using the normalized quotient
by \(K\): its two coefficients are \(3^4\cdot7\) and
\(3^6\cdot4\), and division of the coordinates by
\(3^2,3^3\) gives the map with differential 3. To
verify duality without inferring it only from the target,
the kernel of the dual is the image of \(E^-[3]\)
under the original quotient, is rational by Cartier
duality since \(\mu_3\) is rational over \(\mathbb F_{13}\),
and \(E^\star(\mathbb F_{13})\) has precisely one
rational line (its count is also 12). The sign of
the isogeny is determined by its differential 3.

For the fibre over the actual \(g\), the \(X\)-coordinate
is a root of
\[
                    Z^3+3Z^2+5Z+12.
\]
It follows directly from the dual x-map
\[
 x\longmapsto \frac{x+2(3K_x^2+A^\star)/(x-K_x)
                         +4K_y^2/(x-K_x)^2}{9}.
\]
Its y-map multiplies \(y\) by the derivative of the
numerator in this display, divided by 27. The polynomial
is irreducible (no prime-field roots), and the actual
division point in this model is \((Z,7+6Z)\).
The exact field calculation gives arithmetic
\(\operatorname{Frob}_{13}(P)-P=-K\) and
\(\operatorname{Frob}_{169}(P)-P=+K\).
So the one-fibre readout in the fixed deck-generator

It costs arithmetic over degree at most three once an

This supplies **only** the elliptic receiver and the

## 3. No cusp-unit model, and why ordinary congruence level still misses this line

As before take the primitive rational isogeny from the
optimal elliptic quotient into the exact \(E^\flat\).
It may be chosen of degree prime to 3: the residual
representation is irreducible over \(\mathbb F_3\)
at the good Frobenius 61, whose polynomial is
\(X^2+1\bmod3\) because \(a_{61}=-6\).
If the degree of a rational isogeny were divisible by
3, the dual would kill all of \(E^\flat[3]\) and factor
out \([3]\); remove such a scalar factor. A fixed map
having that factor would have zero order-three
projection, not a usable normalization. For the
primitive one the pullback on elliptic Picard 3-torsion
is injective, also after good specialization at 13.
Its pulled-back geometric Kummer line is the image of
\(G\) and has prime-field Frobenius scalar \(-1\).
Although it becomes rational over \(k\), it is **not**
a cuspidal divisor class: every cusp is already rational
over \(\mathbb F_{13}\) at squarefree level (one for every
divisor, all obtained from infinity by Atkin–Lehner).
A Kummer modular unit for this étale cover over any
constant extension would have \(\operatorname{div}(U)=3B\)
with \(B\) cusp-supported. That forces the nonzero
geometric class of \(B\) to be 13-Frobenius-fixed,
regardless of coefficient field, a contradiction.

Here a blanket claim that there are no congruence
cyclic order-three quotients would be false: cyclic
diamond characters can occur. There is a sharper
criterion for a **compact unramified** connected
congruence cyclic cover. Over the complex fibre,
a cyclic character of \(\Gamma_0(N)\) factoring
through a finite congruence modulus and unramified
at the cusps must vanish on both \(U(1)\) (the
upper unipotent at infinity) and \(L(N)\) (the
lower unipotent at zero, sign immaterial).
Enlarge the modulus to a multiple of \(N\).
CRT powers of those two generators give the
upper local unipotents \(U(t)\) and the lower
\(L(s)\) for \(s\in N\mathbb Z/\ell^a\), one
prime at a time. At a level prime \(\ell\) they
also kill the principal diagonal by
\[
 U(t)L(s)U\!\left(-t/(1+ts)\right)
               L\!\left(-s(1+ts)\right)
             =\operatorname{diag}(1+ts,(1+ts)^{-1})
 \quad(s\equiv0\bmod\ell).
\]
The remaining quotient is diagonal modulo \(\ell\)
by elementary elimination in the Iwahori subgroup.
At nonlevel primes the full local \(\mathrm{SL}_2\)
is generated by elementary unipotents (already
killed; both generators are present there).
Thus any such character factors through the
diamond quotient of \(X_1(N)\to X_0(N)\),
including for modulus divisible by 3 or 13.
No assumption about the list of allowable tame
ramification beyond these two necessary cusp
conditions is used.

Those geometric diamond lines are Frobenius-fixed
at 13: the diamond action is constant over
\(\mathbb Q\), and the corresponding Picard line
is defined over \(\mathbb Q(\mu_3)\); its
specialization at 13 has scalar \(+1\) because
\(13\equiv1\bmod3\). This can be checked before
reduction via the Kummer line of the constant
cyclic quotient and then by finite étale
specialization of Picard 3-torsion. The \(-1\) line
of the actual pullback is different. This
excludes realization via an ordinary congruence
cover in the geometric characteristic-zero model,
using the lift of the actual finite étale
elliptic 3-subgroup along the good 13-model
and specializing it back. It does not identify
the arithmetic Frobenius with a complex loop,
and does not forbid an unrelated sparse
noncuspidal model over the special field.
