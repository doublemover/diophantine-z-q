# Universal nonsquare scalar coefficient; n=323 cannot be linked

This is an elementary obstruction about the literal scalar
`PreparedIntegerSource318.scalarB`, not the detector, anchor, or
an elliptic rank claim.

Write t=a(a+1) for a∈Z and

    g₁=7446t+661,     g₂=7446t+1861,
    g₃=29784t+7445,   g₄=476544t+119479.

All gᵢ are positive. Exact expansion of the literal
`ScalarPolePreparation318.integerR` gives A=114444 R=(204)²·11·g₁g₂g₃g₄.
The actual scalar coefficient is A(W/(1+A))². Its positive
real margin implies W≠0, so its rational squareclass is precisely
the squarefree part K of this **integer** A, independent of h and
the fixed labels. A square in Q, if integral, is an integer square.

In any squarefree part d₃ of g₃, a prime outside K and {11}
could occur only if it also divides another gᵢ: otherwise its
odd g₃ exponent remains odd in 11g₁g₂g₃g₄. The exact identities are
4g₁−g₃=−4801, 4g₂−g₃=−1, and 16g₃−g₄=−359.
Thus d₃ divides the squarefree radical of 11·359·4801·K; 359
and 4801 are prime (trial division through 18 and 69).
In fact 11 never divides g₃: modulo 11 its root t would be 5,
but t=a(a+1) lies in {0,1,2,6,8,9}, or equivalently
(g₃=7446(2a+1)^2-1) and (1/7446) is a nonsquare mod 11.
Hence

         d₃ | rad(359·4801·K).

Since g₃≡5 mod8 and g₃≡2 mod3, its squarefree part satisfies
d₃≡5 mod24. If K=1 the only possible d₃ have residue
1 or 23 mod24, contradiction. This uses only two exact
factor differences, one coprime difference, and local
congruences. Alternatively allow 11 in the support: modulo 8
would force both 11 and 359, contradicting d₃≡2 modulo 3.

For general K the same argument requires a divisor of K
congruent to 5 or 19 modulo 24, because 359≡23, 4801≡1.
All four gᵢ are prime to 2,3,17,73. Their reductions, together
with 11, give K≡1 mod24 (mod8 the first two odd factors coincide,
third/fourth are 5,7; mod3 entries are 1,1,2,1).
Therefore K has a divisor in {5,19} mod24 and complementary
divisor of that same residue, so in particular K≥145.
Here β=17K, since A has exact 17-adic valuation 2.
For the two literal minimum inputs a=0,−1 this β is
204618888056724185 with factors 17,5,11,163,661,733,1489,1861.

For selected n=323 the only divisor β of n congruent to 17
mod24 is β=17. It would force K=1, which is impossible.
This exhausts the scalar option even with another b of the
same β. Detector option is separately excluded by its five
prime factors in the selected product, not by this argument.
No chosen chart h, selected-prime threshold, or rank theorem
can change these facts.
