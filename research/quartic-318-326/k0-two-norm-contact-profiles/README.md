# Norm constructions for the original zero-direction descent

The [two-norm family](ct-original-k0-two-norm-contact-profile-r1/SIMULTANEOUS-PRIMITIVE-AND-GAUSSIAN-NORMS-ON-CONTACT-PROFILES.md) realizes both explicit norm conditions and a negative marked Cassels sign for every allowed contact-prime profile. This is a numerical Ready construction with varying complementary squareclass, not a literal output of the folded prime-selection construction.

The separate [one-norm result](ct-original-k0-gaussian-one-norm-wa-r1/SAME-SQUAREPAIR-GAUSSIAN-NORM-VIA-CHATELET.md) preserves both global squareclasses and constructs the Gaussian norm by weak approximation at compatible local conditions. It does not also impose the primitive norm.

Both papers and their independent reviews are included. Neither asserts a rational witness, a universal obstruction to all auxiliary directions, or a Lean proof.
