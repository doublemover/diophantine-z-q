## Uniformity of the positive reference coefficient

Choose fixed base B_ray=16 times a squarefree odd modulus containing
the original local requirements and all coefficient collisions and
small-prime exceptions; use the exact admissible reference Gaussian
norm. The base physical CRT mass is strictly positive: v=0 with
u the same reference norm is an admissible residue at every base
prime simultaneously. The base conditional density is a single
positive constant, independent of later s. At new p beyond the
base, on units the full Gaussian norm fibre contains at least
p−chi_4(p)−4 admitted points out of p−chi_4(p). The actual
ordinary local factors on the original polynomial forms are
positive and 1+O_k(p^-2) outside the fixed exceptional set.
Thus the new normalized factor at p is at least its ordinary
factor times (1−4/(p−chi_4(p)))^k. This lower applies to the
full Gaussian ray choice; do not use it pointwise for a stricter
inert cyclotomic selector which can delete a whole norm fibre.

For all sufficiently large p the logarithm of this loss is
>=−16k/p. The elementary inequality for s≥3

    sum_(p<=s) 1/p <= e log zeta(1+1/log s)
                   <= e log(1+log s)

follows by comparing p^(1/log s)≤e and using the integral test
for zeta and its absolutely convergent Euler product. Hence the
finite wheel loss is at worst a fixed negative power of (1+log s),
and it is bounded below by c/sqrt s for all large s, with c>0
depending only on fixed original coefficient/base data. The
hyperbolic smoothing tau does not enter this fixed arithmetic
constant. No effective PNT or field-degree normalizer is used here.
