# All six genuine key operators can be matched to just two fixed primes

In the exact helper-673 first-arm receiver, one may make each of the five original contact primes on slot 190 have the same finite Hecke operator as 337 on the augmentation character space modulo two. This is a source-selection assertion assuming the fixed Jacobian Frobenius realization in its exact framed form. It uses the genuine row-specific source cell, not arbitrary substitution of a reference prime into the discriminant. It does not evaluate $T_{337}$ or the complete product. The order-only denominator prime is a separate nonsquare row and uses the second exact reference 12049.

## 1. Fixed field, ramification, and a suitable reference

Keep $I=673$, the verified $a=0$ slot-190 local cell $t=61$, and the original Gaussian linear forms at the common old anchor and refined source period. Let $P=\mathbf Q(\operatorname{Jac}(X)[2],\zeta_{96})$ be the **fixed** field of the relevant rigid level curve $X=\Gamma_1(96)\cap\Gamma(2)\cap\Gamma_0(I)$. It is unramified away from $2,3,I$. At $I$, its inertia has order at most two: semistable monodromy has $(g-1)^2=0$ on the 2-adic Tate module, hence every inertia element on the 2-torsion is unipotent of exponent two. Wild inertia is pro-$I$ and acts trivially, and tame inertia has cyclic finite quotient. The cyclotomic factor $\zeta_{96}$ is unramified there. This is the standard semistable monodromy theorem on the exact $[2]$ module, not an approximation to its character group.

Write $G=\mathbf Q(i)\subset P$. Let $R_c$ be the Gaussian ray field of the small core modulus $16\cdot3\cdot I$. The rational prime
$$
    337=9^2+16^2
$$
is unramified in $P R_c$, split in $G$, and its Gaussian representative $9+16i$ has exact residues $(9,0)\bmod16$ and $(0,1)\bmod3$, as prescribed in the original linear key. Its norm is $49\bmod96$, the same rational 2/3 class as the genuine original primes. At 673 the two coordinates in the split Gaussian algebra are $264,427$ (take $i=58,-58$), both nonsquares and both different from $0,\pm1$. Their product is a square, namely $337\bmod673$.

## 2. Exact compatibility with the actual contact forms

Start with a fixed Frobenius element for $337$ of $P R_c/G$, taking the prime specified by $9+16i$. The five required nonzero contact norms $m_k\bmod I$ come from the actual $t=61$ row and the actual $u,q$ relationship: $q\bmod I=5$ is nonsquare, the five normalized contact factors are nonsquares, and each $m_k$ is their product times the common square $(D_{\rm clr}Q)^2$. Thus every required $m_k$ is a square. Neither the equality of the five norms nor equality of them with $337\bmod I$ is assumed.

For each such $m_k$ choose two local factors $x,y$ in $\mathbf F_I^\times$ with $xy=m_k$, having the same respective squareclasses as $264,427$. This is possible with $x,y\ne\pm1$: there are $(I-1)/2$ choices in the desired norm fibre before at most four exclusions. Change the two Gaussian residue components of the reference by $x/264,y/427$, both squares, using the two local $I$-inertias of $R_c/G$. Their square changes lift to inertia in $P R_c/G$ with **trivial action on $P$**, since the corresponding inertia in $P$ has order at most two. The abelian projection over $\mathbf Q$ of this change is exactly cyclotomic norm multiplication by $m_k/337$ at $I$: all ramification there is tame, so the rational abelian local conductor has exponent at most one, and $R_c$ already contains $\mathbf Q(\zeta_I)$. A local $I$-inertia acts trivially on the other cyclotomic prime-power components. Thus there is no hidden higher abelian compatibility involving the moving $m_k$.

For the other fixed wheel components, and for the later finite cutoffs, take the enlarged Gaussian ray field $R$ with all their usual residue conditions. Its quotient over $R_c$ is generated by the independent new local inertia groups (the fixed mod-16 component already removes any diagonal-unit ambiguity). The intersection $P R_c\cap R$ is exactly $R_c$, because $P R_c$ is unramified at each of these new rational primes and their inertia groups generate the relative extension. Thus one can impose their Gaussian residue states without changing the specified $P$-element, and their abelian projections are the required rational norms. If 337 itself lies in the later wheel, only its Frobenius in $P R_c$ was used; no Frobenius at a ramified prime of the larger ray field is invoked.

Choose $Q$ to include all needed prime-to-673 rational abelian conductor exponents at the fixed base, including those from $P R_c$ at 2 and 3. The five forms then have the same residue as $u$ there; take the reference residue $337$ at the complete 2/3 abelian conductor. Other rational base states may be the old ones by CRT, since the changes use only their own local inertias. At 673 excise the 673 factor of $Q$ and use the separate row-specific norm table above; choose $u\equiv D_{\rm clr}^2Q^2 q\cdot61\bmod673$. For the denominator prime use the independently chosen unramified core-reference Frobenius at **12049**. Bounded trial division through $109$ verifies it is prime, and
$$
       12049=105^2+32^2=N(105-32i).
$$
This generator again has the exact Gaussian residues $(9,0)\bmod16$ and $(0,1)\bmod3$, with rational norm $49\bmod96$. Its two split Gaussian coordinates at 673 are $268,615$ of respective nonsquare/square classes; the norm is $608\bmod673$, a nonsquare. The intended denominator residue $5\bmod673$ has the same quadratic character. Thus the same two square inertia changes of §2 realize **this** denominator residue 5 while retaining the reference 12049 Frobenius in $P$. Its $2,3$ rational-abelian conductor residues can be imposed as those of 12049; $u,q$ are separate variables and $Q$ is already deep at those primes, so the contact forms use the residues of $u$ instead. These two Frobenius choices are therefore compatible even with the exact linear equation of the five contacts. Other merged forms not in slot 190 keep their previous Gaussian ray states with their literal norm fibres, without fixing a $P$-element. When a contact form is merged across two slots, it is assigned once to its same literal residue row.

Normality gives rational conjugacy classes in the enlarged compositum for each selected prime form. Their rational abelian restrictions are compatible by the explicit two-variable congruences just given. The same [Frei–Tinková v3 Theorem 1.4 and its local factor (1.15)](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.17441v3) used in the linear-q source therefore applies, with the same finite-complexity nonparallel forms and positive ordered real region. The new fixed compositum has ramification only at rationally fixed primes, so the new-cutoff norm-one fibre counts and the large/medium obstruction bounds in the original Gaussian selection proof remain unchanged outside that fixed support. All fixed new factors only change the positive base constant, before the cutoff and large box are taken. This selects genuinely original contact primes with the imposed Frobenius class while retaining the single-source preparation, not a mere Chebotarev selection of independent primes.

It follows from the framed Eichler–Shimura formula that every one of the five contact operators equals $T_{337}\bmod2$ on the required augmentation module: the $P$-Frobenius and the diamond ($\ell\bmod96$) are matched. The initial $T_{17}$ is still the exact separate operator on full frame deltas. Thus the five moving contact factors can be collapsed to $T_{337}^{5}$ **after** entering the augmentation module. The same applies to the denominator operator with the reference $T_{12049}$, so the six genuine moving factors collapse to $T_{337}^{5}T_{12049}$ on the augmentation module. The proof does not calculate either of these fixed reference operators or assume that their product is nonzero on a relevant vector. The old final/auxiliary helper rows still need their compatible classes and arithmetic operators, and the weighted result need not be nonzero. No original point or rank follows here.
