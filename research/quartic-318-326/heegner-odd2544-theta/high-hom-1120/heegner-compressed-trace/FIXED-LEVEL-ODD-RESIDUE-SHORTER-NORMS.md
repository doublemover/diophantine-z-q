## 2. The very same Gauss sum is a genus-character period

## 3. Ratio of two shorter Fourier-index values

Use [Cai–Shu–Tian Theorem 1.10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.1733#page=12)
for this same fixed quaternionic test vector and its dual.
Here the discriminant of K and the character conductor d are
coprime to the full level, K is split at 2,3 and inert at 53,
and the Eichler order and Gross embedding are the compatible
ones just exhibited. The division-prime restriction of the
toric character is trivial (norm valuations even). At q|d
the character has conductor q and the genuine conductor-q
embedding makes the spherical level vector its test vector;
at 2 and 3 the split optimal embeddings are the prescribed
marked Eichler ones. All orders have units ±1 and the Q
Picard normalization is trivial. There is no common
level/discriminant Euler factor to remove. Every same fixed
norm cancels in the ratio, and the genus extension factors
the Rankin value as twists by \(-d\) and \(md\). The theorem gives
\[
 \frac{|P_d(F)|^2}{|P_{d_0}(F)|^2}
 = \frac{d}{d_0}
 \frac{L(g\otimes\chi_{-d},1)L(g\otimes\chi_{md},1)}
      {L(g\otimes\chi_{-d_0},1)L(g\otimes\chi_{md_0},1)} .
\]
The displayed \(s=1\) is the classical normalization.
The reference product is nonzero because the authentic
reference period is nonzero, so its two fixed types have
root sign +1. Use these same types when varying d.

[Sirolli–Tornaría Theorem 9.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2107.04483)
applies to a weight-two newform over Q of nonsquare level
2544 and discriminants coprime to level, even with dyadic
exponent 4 (no ramified dyadic discriminant is requested).
It supplies two effective fixed ternary-theta sources, with
coefficients \(c_-(d)\) and \(c_+(md)\). They may use separate
fixed auxiliary data for the two signs. In their ratios the
only varying nonconstant normalizing factors are \(1/\sqrt d\)
and \(1/\sqrt{md}\), which cancel \(d/d_0\) exactly here:
\[
 \frac{|P_d(F)|^2}{|P_{d_0}(F)|^2}
 =
 \frac{c_-(d)\overline{c_-(d)}\,c_+(md)\overline{c_+(md)}}
      {c_-(d_0)\overline{c_-(d_0)}\,c_+(md_0)\overline{c_+(md_0)}}.
\]
The eigenfunction F can be taken real algebraic (self-adjoint
Hecke matrices and real eigenvalues, then algebraic unit scaling).
The theta coefficients are algebraic by the finite weighted
ternary formulas. Read each coefficient **and** its complex
conjugate in a common algebraic field before evaluating the
retained prime. There is no hidden assumption that conjugation
fixes that prime or that an absolute square is the square of
an integer. Since the reference period is a unit, the ratio
is a local unit iff the actual detector is nonzero, using a
fixed precision cutoff after fixed-normalizer denominators.
A zero result here does not imply trace vanishing over Q.

## 4. Actual asymptotic bound, not a practical huge-prime run

The two norms to evaluate grow as d and 455d, not 455d².
For a fixed ternary lattice with finite residue weights,
diagonalize over Q once, clear fixed denominators, and
enumerate the first coordinate (\(O(\sqrt d)\) choices).
For each residual binary norm, factor the integer, enumerate
allocations among prime ideals of its fixed imaginary quadratic
field, find principal generators by exact positive binary
form reduction, and enforce the literal fixed congruences.
Remnants of a fixed nonmaximal order are filtered inside the
actual maximal-order lattice; no growing order conductor is
introduced. The number of ideal allocations is \(d^{o(1)}\).
For fixed discriminant, deterministic splitting square roots
are polynomial in log of the prime by
[Schoof 1985](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~cs812-1/Schoof85.pdf).
Conservative deterministic factorization costs \(d^{1/4+o(1)}\)
by [Costa–Harvey](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.2116).
Hence the two single coefficients can be evaluated in
\(d^{3/4+o(1)}\) bit operations after fixed-form precomputation.
No \(d^{1/2}\) guarantee or practicality for \(d>10^{25}\)
is claimed. This is an asymptotic arithmetic improvement
over materializing every one of the \(\varphi(d)\) branches,
conditional only on implementing the fixed effective objects
and satisfying the explicitly restricted local family.

The actual finite eigenline is computed here; the exact
characteristic-zero eigenfunction field and the two effective
ternary functions are not numerically instantiated. The
recoverable route proves which exact classical objects would
suffice and the cost of coefficient evaluation once fixed.
It does not supply a concrete missing prepared (a,labels,h,t),
extend the detector to all incompatible squareclasses, or
solve the general analytic supplier.
