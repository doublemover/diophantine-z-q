## Common coordinates, quadratic degree and target

Use the actual scaled meromorphic primary and alternate poles U=π^d w p₁^+, V=π^d w a₁^−, w the fixed unit square root of the stripped common weight. Their reciprocals r=1/U,s=1/V are relative étale coordinates at the raw pole corner. The one literal square-root function field in all these charts is defined by

    δ = 1−β (κ f_+ f_-/(f_++f_-))²,

with β a 19-unit and v₁₉(κ)=e>0. This is not a comparison of several choices of quadratic fields. The horizontal refinements are birational; stripping a square removes only a denominator, and etale localization or splitting of the residue never replaces the generic equation. After a finite constant extension one can take each actual generic branch separately if necessary.

All four local pieces compare with the same actual good elliptic target. Their rational-fibre arguments require good reduction only, not the target's ordinary Frobenius or the parity of e. They do not lean on the independent genus-15 quotient result. In particular no change of target or 19-depth hypothesis occurs between the toric and coordinate-end calculations.

## Four exhaustive strata on the first blowup

Blow up the horizontal ideal (r,s), a proper modification of the fixed relative chart. Its fibre over the raw point is the actual projective λ-line, λ=s/r on the r-chart; this follows by étale pullback of the coordinate blowup and then taking the fibre at the fixed point. The following four exact frozen proofs cover it:

1. The **off-diagonal open** λ not in {0,1,∞}: here Q=1−1/λ is a unit and Δ=U−V=Q/r. The regular strip D=r^32 gives F=D f_+ with F and F+D f_- units. The normalization is étale and has two rational sheets over that geometric open of the λ-line.
2. The **diagonal direction** λ=1: use Q=1−1/λ as a coordinate and the explicit regular horizontal fan containing ray (16,15), from the frozen finite replay. On one side the multiplier is D_1=(r/Q)^32, on the other D_2=r². The corresponding strip is a unit on every strict orbit and at both ends of the wall ray. The wall's open orbit has K=Q^16/r^15 invertible; only its two genuine infinity directions K=±sqrt(−α) can fail the unit test. The exact normal equation A B=π^(2e)R^64, with R=r/Q, resolves them by regular peeling, with only rational curves over each of those points. Thus the whole proper fibre over λ=1 is controlled; it is not just a leading residue test at its generic point.
3. The **alternate-dominant coordinate end** λ=0: in the regular r-chart s=rλ and Δ=(λ−1)/s. The s^32 strip makes the *separate primary* correction r^30λ^32(Hr−M)^2. It is integral and vanishes at the direction, so the trace is a unit and the normalized geometric fibre there is two points.
4. The **primary-dominant coordinate end** λ=∞: in the regular s-chart η=r/s and Δ=(1−η)/r. Now it is the r^32 strip that makes the primary correction r^30(Hr−M)^2. Again the trace is a unit and the normalized geometric fibre is two points. This case is not obtained by asserting a symmetry of the two leaves.

Here H,M are the actual regular coefficients of the literal final primary square (H−M/r)^2 in the already bound shared-leaf expansion, M a unit. In each unit region the single exact contact is κ F G/(F+D G), G=f_-, so the reduction δ=1 and the sign label is the actual value of sqrt(δ).

For clarity, the exact overlap rescalings of F and D give the same contact function. Between the two ordinary strips, s^32=λ^32 r^32 where λ is a unit, and in the overlap with the first toric side D_1=Q^(−32)r^32 where Q is a unit. Between toric sides on the wall, D_2=K² D_1. Since F=D f_+, each is the same rescaling of numerator and denominator of the trace quotient. The branch normalization at the two K-roots strips the same rational δ, not a different square-root label. This checks overlap without requiring that auxiliary local regular models be globally glued.

## Proper graph coverage

Normalize the proper first blowup in the prescribed generic square-root field or its chosen branches, and take the main graph closure there. It maps properly onto the original main graph: the image is closed and contains the generic graph; the normalization maps into the original normalization because original integral elements pull back integrally. Its image lies in the original graph by density of the generic source, hence is precisely the graph. When localizing to a stratum, the proper regular models cited above map onto that localized graph by the same closed-image argument. Finite unramified base change, or subsequent étale localization at a named branch, preserves this geometric graph test; the generic defining ideal is contracted and schematic closure commutes with this flat change of base.

An irreducible graph component over the fixed raw point with positive-dimensional elliptic image would have a lift through this proper comparison. Its centre above the raw point on the first blowup lies in one of the four strata. On the regular split open in case 1 the map extends by BLR and is constant on the two rational curve sheets, including all their subvarieties. Case 2 uses the same morphism property and rationality for each relevant strict/toroid fibre on its own proper refinement. The two coordinate ends have a regular étale model with a finite geometric fibre, so cases 3 and 4 cannot carry such an image either. This proves the exact local exclusion over the selected **raw pole corner**, not the whole mixed trace model or the coherent cover.
