## 4. All-place simultaneous solubility on the actual degree-eight cover

The smooth projective model is the intersection
\[
 W^2-s Z^2=a U^2,\qquad W^2-t Z^2=b V^2,\qquad
 W^2-u Z^2=c Q^2
\]
in \(\mathbf P^4\). The three root values are distinct and
nonzero, since they are distinct modulo p_c. Over an algebraic
closure it is the connected eightfold cover of the w-line
obtained by adjoining the three independent square roots:
each \(w^2-r\) has its own two simple zeros, so no nonempty
product is square. There are six branch values, each with
inertia order two, and none at infinity (all degrees two).
Riemann–Hurwitz gives \(2g-2=8(-2)+6\cdot4=8\), so g=5.

The model also has smooth geometrically connected reduction
outside B and the six support primes, where characteristic is
odd and all roots and differences are units. For smoothness,
if a nonzero linear relation among the three gradient rows
has only one nonzero coefficient, the corresponding equation
forces W,Z and its matching ordinate to be zero, after which
the remaining two equations also force their ordinates zero.
If there are at least two coefficients, their matching
ordinates must both vanish; the difference of their equations
and the distinct root values force W=Z=0, again impossible.
The independent branch argument for connectedness works in the
residue field over its algebraic closure. For these primes
\(p>76176\), the [Hasse–Weil bound for the curve](https://www.numdam.org/item/SMF_AST_1974__20__273_0/)
gives at least \(p+1-10\sqrt p>32\) points, so one avoids the four
hyperplanes \(Z=0,U=0,V=0,Q=0\) (each fiber set has size at most 8).
Smooth Hensel lifts that point. At the six support primes and
all earlier prescribed balls use §§2–3. At each l|B choose
one w of sufficiently negative valuation in the exact
router saturation, because all three classes are local
squares there. Over R take positive w sufficiently large.

Consequently the same h (chosen by accepted real-progression
density) and one *local* w at every place satisfy the triple
cover and the prescribed balls. Forgetting its third equation
recovers the exact previously fixed a,b curve and its full
marked Sel₄ lift. This argument does not assert that the
separate 4-cover of that pair has a simultaneous local
point over all these triple balls; the pullback is a further
descent problem. It does not turn the three local equations
into a single global rational w.
