# Three completed contact factors on one common index

The two-contact construction can be enlarged to a genus-five curve with
points over the reals and every p-adic field. All three factors have complete
two-prime odd support, and projection to the two-factor curve retains the
same full 4-Selmer lift of its marked class. This does not assert local
solubility after pulling back that lift to the new curve, nor a rational point
on either curve. The choices are existential.
