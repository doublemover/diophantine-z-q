# A specified integral Hodge row and a specified trace entry

Use the fixed level \(M=2^{28}3^6S^2\), the original compact \(X=X_1(M)_L\), its literal \(J\)-map, and \(F=L(X)=L(J)[x]/(p)\) for the same full monic signed equation as in the original normalization argument. Keep the very same selected number field \(L\), rational point \(P_0\) over \(J=1\), Abel–Jacobi map \(j_{P_0}:X\to J_X\), and integral Néron Hodge lattice \(H=e^*\Omega^1_{\mathcal J/\mathcal O_L}\) of the semistable modular Jacobian \(J_X\). Write \(B\) for the integral closure of \(\mathcal O_L[J]\) in \(F\). The arithmetic test surface \(A\) is not \(J_X\) or the elliptic curve below.

**Claim.** There are specified \(h\in H\setminus\{0\}\) and \(b\in B\) such that the original global trace matrix, when its generators include these elements, has entry exactly \(2^{481}\deg(p)\in\mathbb Z_{>0}\), with no unspecified denominator. The whole \(b=1\) column remains identically zero.

## Same weight-two form, exact algebraic normalization

The actual quotient \(\pi:X_1(M)\to X_0(32)\) sends \(P\) to the cyclic subgroup generated by \((M/32)P\). On the target use the coordinates \(x_0^2+y_0^4=1\) and normalized \(f_{32}\,dq/q=\omega_{32}=dx_0/(4y_0^3)\), with cusp branch \(x_0=4q+\cdots,\ y_0=1+\cdots\), of [Li–Long–Tu, §3 and Eq. (3.1)](https://sigma-journal.com/2018/090/sigma18-090.pdf#page=15). [Tian, Proposition 2.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1210.8231#page=6) identifies the actual target over \(\mathbb Q\) with the smooth complete curve of the short Weierstrass equation \(V^2=U^3+4U\). To fix the differential without any Manin-constant or isomorphism-normalization ambiguity, the following explicit birational coordinates suffice:
\[
 U=\frac{2(1+x_0)}{y_0^2},\qquad V=\frac{4(1+x_0)}{y_0^3},\qquad
 y_0=\frac{2U}{V},\qquad x_0=\frac{U y_0^2}{2}-1.
\]
In the common function field, \(V^2=U^3+4U\), and \(4y_0^3\,dy_0=-2x_0\,dx_0\) gives
\[
 dU=\frac{2(1+x_0)}{y_0^6}\,dx_0,\qquad \frac{dU}{2V}=\omega_{32}.
\]
This birational map extends uniquely to an isomorphism of the smooth projective curves. It takes the chosen \(q\)-cusp to \((2,4)\), not to \(O\). Any translation used to fix a base point preserves the invariant differential \(dU/(2V)\). Denote this elliptic curve with origin at infinity by \(E_{32}\).

## The multiplier on the differential is literally 2 over the fixed field

For each finite place \(v\) of \(L\), take a minimal integral Weierstrass model over the actual DVR \(\mathcal O_{L,v}\) of this very \(E_{32,L}\). The original global equation \(V^2=U^3+4U\) has discriminant \(-2^{12}\). For an admissible coordinate change from a minimal equation to the displayed equation with scale \(a_v\in L^\times\),
\[
 \Delta=a_v^{12}\Delta_{\min,v},\qquad \omega_{32}=a_v^{-1}\omega_{\min,v}.
\]
These are the usual admissible change formulas for the invariant differential and discriminant. See [Silverman, The Arithmetic of Elliptic Curves, III.1, Table 3.1; VII.1.3; VIII.8](https://www.math.ens.psl.eu/~obenoist/refs/Silverman.pdf). The differential from the minimal Weierstrass equation generates the Néron cotangent line at the identity: the minimal proper regular resolution is an isomorphism near the already smooth origin of the Weierstrass model, and its smooth locus gives the Néron model. See [Bosch–Lütkebohmert–Raynaud, Néron Models, §1.5, Prop. 1](https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/~kamenova/homepage_files/Bosch_Raynaud_Neron_Model_tc.pdf); the strictly henselian description suffices by étale localization, or use Liu–Tong’s [Néron Models of Algebraic Curves, Thm. 1.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.4822) for the Dedekind version. At the identity one only needs that the Weierstrass differential generates the relative cotangent of the smooth Weierstrass open.

Because \(\Delta_{\min,v}\) is integral,
\[
 v(a_v)=v(2)-\tfrac1{12}v(\Delta_{\min,v})\le v(2).
\]
Consequently \(2\omega_{32}\) is in the Néron lattice at every \(v\), even when the fixed \(L\) is ramified at 2 and without any good-reduction claim about \(E_{32,L}\). No minimal-model parameters or compatible choices of local generators are needed. Since the Néron cotangent line is finite projective over \(\mathcal O_L\), it is the intersection of those local lattices inside its one generic fiber. Thus \(2\omega_{32}\) is an actual global invariant integral differential for \(E_{32,L}\).

Translate \(\pi\), after the displayed compact isomorphism, by the image of the **same** \(P_0\). The pointed map is \(\bar\pi\circ j_{P_0}\) for the induced homomorphism \(\bar\pi:J_X\to E_{32,L}\). The Néron mapping property extends \(\bar\pi\) to the two true Néron models over \(\mathcal O_L\), since its source model is smooth; uniqueness also preserves the identity and group law. Pullback on identity cotangents gives the specified \(h=\bar\pi^*(2\omega_{32})\in H\). Its pullback to \(X\) is \(2\pi^*\omega_{32}\), nonzero by separability of the actual nonconstant curve map in characteristic zero. In particular it is a genuine row of this lattice, not an independent choice of rational frame.

## One matrix entry, now with an explicit integer

In the exact previous level-32 calculation, \(u=4\pi^*x_0,\ Y=2\pi^*y_0\) and
\[
 P(T)=(T^4+240T^3+2144T^2+3840T+256)^3-JT(4-T)^8(4+T)^2.
\]
Let \(Q_a(T)=(P(T)-P(a))/(T-a)\), a polynomial over \(\mathbb Z[J]\), and set
\[
 b=2Y^3P'(u)(-Q_0(u))\,Q_4(u)^8\,(-Q_{-4}(u))^2.
\]
The earlier replay independently verifies \(P(u)=0,\ Y^4=16-u^2\), integrality of these coordinates, and the differential identity \(b=2^{480}dJ/\pi^*\omega_{32}\). In particular \(b\in B\). On the actual canonical coefficient of the row just constructed we obtain
\[
 (h/dJ)b=2^{481},\qquad
 \operatorname{Tr}_{F/L(J)}((h/dJ)b)
   =2^{481}[F:L(J)]=2^{481}\deg p > 0.
\]
This is the exact entry of the literal original monic remainder evaluator. The prior \(b=1\) zero-column argument applies unchanged to every row. Both columns and the specified row can be adjoined to any finite generating families: this does not change either span. The displayed entry avoids requiring the computation of the full generally enormous \(p\) or a numerical choice of \(L\).

**Boundary.** This proves one row/column instance with a prescribed entry on the same stable \(H\), not a construction of all basis vectors, a calculation of the complete finite trace matrix, a Néron implementation in Lean, or a result about \(A\). The small native multiplier and original evaluator consumer still take the literal modular-coordinate and same-canonical-coefficient relations as input. There is no new stable extension, change of selected \(J=1\) center, or different Jacobian.
