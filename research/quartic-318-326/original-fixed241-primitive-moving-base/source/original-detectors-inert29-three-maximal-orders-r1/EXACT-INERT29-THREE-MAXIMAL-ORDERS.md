# The three actual inert-29 maximal orders and a complete small norm shell

This computes the three unmarked source lattices in the
[two-16-arms receiver](../original-detectors-inert29-union16-r1/INERT29-UNION-TWO-16-ARMS.md).
It supplies no level-48 phase evaluation by these lattices and no
production discriminant or global point.

## 1. A maximal order with actual endomorphisms at \(j=0\)

Over \(\mathbf F_{29^2}\) let \(s^2=2\) and
\(A_0:y^2=x^3+1\). Write \(\zeta=(-1+10s)/2\),
\(\omega(x,y)=(\zeta x,y)\), and \(\Pi\) for 29-Frobenius.
The exact point trace is zero (cube is a bijection on
\(\mathbf F_{29}\)), hence
\(\omega^2+\omega+1=0\), \(\Pi^2=-29\) and
\(\Pi\omega=\omega^2\Pi\). The algebra has the rational
basis \(1,\omega,\Pi,\omega\Pi\), with norm
\[
\operatorname{nrd}(x+y\omega+(z+t\omega)\Pi)
=x^2-xy+y^2+29(z^2-zt+t^2).
\]
Let \(\tau=1+2\omega\). It is a separable norm-3 endomorphism
with kernel \(\{O,(0,1),(0,-1)\}\), all fixed by \(\Pi\).
Thus \(\gamma=(\Pi-1)\tau^{-1}=\tau(1+\Pi)/3\) is an
actual integral endomorphism, by the quotient universal
property. Define \(\nu=2\gamma-\omega\Pi\), i.e.
\((2+4\omega+2\Pi+\omega\Pi)/3\). The basis
\((1,\omega,\Pi,\nu)\) consists of actual endomorphisms.
Its integral norm polar determinant is \(29^2\), as the
replay checks. The supersingular geometric end ring is
maximal in the algebra ramified at \(29,\infty\), with
reduced discriminant 29; hence this sublattice is the full
ring. The usual maximal-order identification also underlies
[Cornut–Jetchev §4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1012.0725v1).
No integral-closure assumption on a power basis is used.

## 2. Exact identification of the other two maximal orders

Take \(T\in A_0[16]\) with
\([8]T=(-1,0)\) and \([4]T\) having \(x=-1+4s\).
The replay obtains it by two exact halvings of the latter
order-four point, checking all group relations. It works
in \(\mathbf F_{29}[u]/(u^8-2)\), \(s=u^4\): each
successive quadratic extension is a field, since 2, \(s\),
and then \(u^2\) are nonsquares in the preceding fields
(by exponentiation; the character of 2 in \(\mathbf F_{29}\)
is \(-1\)). Relative to \((T,\omega T)\), whose 256 sums
are distinct, \(\omega,\Pi,\nu\) have matrices
\[
\begin{pmatrix}0&15\\1&15\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\begin{pmatrix}11&3\\14&5\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\begin{pmatrix}14&15\\15&2\end{pmatrix}
\pmod {16}.
\]
These are actions on points, not just one abstract
representation of the quaternion algebra.

The \(x=-1\) 2-kernel quotients \(A_0\) to
\(A_2:y^2=x^3+6x^2-3x\), of \(j=2\), by the standard
normalized rational 2-isogeny after shifting \(x\) by 1.
The image of \([4]T\) on \(A_2\) has \(x=-3+8s\);
this gives the second cyclic 2-kernel and the quotient
\(A_{25}\) of \(j=25\). The displayed \(j\)'s are computed
from coefficients in the replay. The composite has cyclic
kernel generated by \([4]T\) (not the full 2-torsion).

For these degree \(d=2,4\) isogenies \(\lambda:A_0\to A_j\),
identify endomorphism algebras by conjugation by \(\lambda\).
If \(\alpha\) is integral on \(A_j\), its image is \(r/d\)
with \(r=\widehat\lambda\alpha\lambda\in \operatorname{End}(A_0)\).
Conversely \(r/d\) comes from an integral endomorphism on
\(A_j\) iff \(\lambda r\widehat\lambda\) kills
\(A_j[d^2]\). By separability, this is equivalent to
\[
r([d]^{-1}\ker\lambda)\subseteq\ker\lambda.
\]
Indeed \(\widehat\lambda(A_j[d^2])\subseteq
[d]^{-1}\ker\lambda\) by \(\lambda\widehat\lambda=[d]\),
and the groups have the same size \(d^3\). In the basis
\((T',\omega T')\) of the \(d^2\)-layer, the cyclic kernel
is generated by \([d]T'\). Therefore for the matrix \(M_r\)
modulo \(d^2\) the exact conditions are
\[
(M_r)_{21}=0\bmod d^2,\qquad
(M_r)_{11}=(M_r)_{22}=0\bmod d.
\]
The replay enumerates the at most \(16^4\) residue vectors
of \(r\) in the original integral basis and extracts
the basis of the preimage lattice by HNF. The index is
\(d^4\) in each case, before dividing the new lattice by
\(d\). The new norm polar determinant is again \(29^2\).
The JSON gives all three exact bases; no order from an
unlabelled genus is substituted.

## 3. Small unmarked shell control

For any positive \(|D|\) odd fundamental, an embedding of
the maximal order is equivalent to \(v^2=D\) with
trace zero and \((1+v)/2\) integral. Trace zero gives
\(y=2x\), so the exact shell reduces to
\(3x^2+29(z^2-zt+t^2)=|D|\) under the lattice conditions.
The replay scales the fixed coefficient denominators out
and enumerates the two constrained loop variables plus a
single square test for \((2z-t)^2\).
The geometric tangent action is \(x+y\zeta=x\cdot10s\)
because \(d\Pi=0\); this scalar is unchanged by the
separable conjugating isogenies. Thus the p-local Lie test
is one coefficient residue, without a CM lifting computation.

At \(D=-119\) the full three raw shell counts are \(12,8,8\).
The choice \(\sqrt{-119}\mapsto10s\) halves each, and
quotienting by the actual units (orbit sizes \(3,1,1\))
gives **\(2,4,4\)** unmarked class counts. This independently
matches the \(j\)-profile of the ten-label exact \(D=-119\)
ideal-action pilot. The three counts do not recover its
phase: checking the actual level-48 cyclic subgroups for
each embedding remains essential. Nor is there a feasible
runtime assertion for shell computation at an actual large
selected \(D\).
