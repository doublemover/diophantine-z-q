    c_1^2 w^2 + 32/407 = 415721 * q * Y^2,

for a new rational prime q and nonzero rational Y. These are exactly that factor's two odd-support primes. Both contact depths are one and both give valuation +1 in the unchanged stored coefficient b_241 on the same h and t.

The proof uses the exact pilot finite groups, a finite division-field and independent Gaussian ray choice for all inherited p² constraints, and a short coprimality sieve. The [real-index progression proof](linked-detector-real-progression-density-r1/ACTUAL-REAL-MARGIN-IN-EVERY-EVEN-PROGRESSION.md) gives an accepted h after the CRT. All denominator saturation depths at B are retained, not the exact symbolic denominator B^N; any extra denominator primes contribute even depths.
