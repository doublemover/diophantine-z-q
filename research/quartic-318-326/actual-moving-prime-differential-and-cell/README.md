# From a marked modular differential to an exact contact cell

For the same-scale twist, an explicitly normalized modular differential supplies the coefficients in the moving-prime jet. On the same rational twist, the local additive coordinate determines the precise squared-parameter class modulo the square of a moving prime. The exact finite examples replay the formulas and their signs, including a separate ramified CM example at 7.

The argument does not evaluate the selected modular form, certify any required moving residue, or give a simultaneous rational witness. The original full prime-grid and Lean obligations are separate.
