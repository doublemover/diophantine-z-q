# Oriented good-prime valuations for the fixed-conductor CM test

This exact arithmetic supplement describes a coefficient of the oriented fractional ideal from the fixed real CM calibration. Its scope is a good prime outside the moving discriminant and the fixed factors 2, 3, 5, 7 and 241. It keeps both the full cyclic-48 flag and the CM orientation at the named prime. The local intersection length is determined by the depth of a positive simultaneous-embedding norm defect; the determinant on the actual 241 line gives its signed genus weight.

The result does not give exceptional-prime valuations or evaluate any moving exponent, residue, rank or marked point. The fixed calibration and the moving-base/fixed-conductor construction are separate prerequisites.
