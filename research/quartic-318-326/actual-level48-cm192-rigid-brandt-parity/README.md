# An unsigned finite Hecke product for the original first-arm mark

In the existing single-helper residue test, the full unweighted CM product reduces to mod-two multiplicities at finitely many marked supersingular flags. Each necessary count is a coefficient of a product of rigid framed Hecke matrices at the distinct primes of the original first-arm discriminant. The involution \(u\mapsto-\widehat u\) pairs the non-trace-zero maps, retaining the literal 16- and 3-level roots; the ramified helper itself acts by the unique relative Frobenius.

Neither the necessary Hecke matrices at the moving large primes nor the weighted parity over all flags have been evaluated for an original detector. No modular Jacobian operator, output sign, trace, rank or rational point is claimed here.
