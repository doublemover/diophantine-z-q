# Arithmetic and geometric tests on the original scalar fibre

The [marked pairing paper](actual-scalar-kummer-isogeny-and-marked-pairing-r1/ACTUAL-KUMMER-FIBRE-ISOGENY-AND-MARKED-PAIRING.md) uses the actual isogeny orientation and reduces the required pairing to signs at the odd support of the literal coefficient. It proves that tests supported only on the old primes cannot obstruct the mark and that a low-dimensional full-Selmer shortcut does not apply. It does not compute the actual marked outcome.

The separate [full-chart theorem](ct-original-scalar-full-chart-no-section-r1/ORIGINAL-SCALAR-FULL-CHART-NO-SECTION.md) rules out a universal rational-function section over the shifted chart with the first index fixed, even after extending the constant field to the algebraic closure of Q. It does not rule out a point at the original numerical shift or curves in other directions.

Independent reviews and exact arithmetic replays are included; neither paper claims a rational point or Lean verification.
