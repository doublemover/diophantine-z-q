# A fixed residual Selmer calculation for the actual two-contact cover

This paper-level reduction identifies the quotient of the full 2-Selmer group
by the supported test space W with one explicitly specified group on fixed
support, independent of the two new auxiliary primes. It gives exact local
tables and a known-support relaxation of dimension four, not a computation
of the dimension of the full residual group. The remaining unknown support
and the global rational point are not resolved. The signed review notes the
numerator/denominator wording reversal at the dyadic U calculation; the
displayed formula and its valuations are correct.
