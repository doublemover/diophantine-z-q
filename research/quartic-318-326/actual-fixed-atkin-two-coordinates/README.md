# Two coordinates for the fixed Atkin–Lehner quotient

Two explicit modular units supply generators for the quotient function field. Their pole orders at the unique cusp, and the first surviving term after cancellation, prove birationality in characteristic zero and at the relevant good primes. This does not give a low-degree plane equation or a differential value.

An exact separate computation at 13 evaluates marked discriminant products on 2,400 distinct Atkin–Lehner orbits. The discriminant tuples do not distinguish six pairs, even when all 127 characters are retained. This finite test does not evaluate the eta roots in the smaller coordinate pair. Birational coordinates can identify special points in their plane image.
