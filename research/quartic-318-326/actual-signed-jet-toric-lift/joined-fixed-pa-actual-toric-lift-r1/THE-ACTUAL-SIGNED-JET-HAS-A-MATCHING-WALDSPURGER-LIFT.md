## 1. The actual framed Hecke function

Take the definite quaternion algebra \(B/\mathbb Q\) ramified at \(p,\infty\), with maximal order at \(p\), radical \(\mathfrak P\), and tame Eichler units of level \(N\). Use the framed supersingular description of [Serre, letter to Tate, (9)–(11)](https://aghitza.github.io/publications/translation-serre-letter-tate.pdf) with inverted double-coset convention \(B^\times\backslash B^\times(\mathbb A_f)/U\). The \(p\)-part \(1+\mathfrak P\) records a first differential frame. In the integral homomorphism lattices labelling a target, a local generator identifies the reference and target formal groups and transports the reference cotangent covector inversely. Precomposing this generator by a unit of tangent \(d\in\mathbb F_{p^2}^\times\) multiplies the target frame by \(d^{-1}\). This fixes the phase convention.

Let \(\bar\Phi(x)=F_s\), the coefficient of the previously normalized newform differential divided by the first derivative of the locally framed Hasse scalar at the actual label \(s\) of \(x\). Replacing \(\eta\) by \(t\eta\) changes the Hasse scalar and its derivative at its zero by \(t^{1-p}\), so \(F_s\) changes by \(t^{p-1}\). Thus \(\bar\Phi(xu)=\tau(d)\bar\Phi(x)\) for \(u\in\mathcal O_{B,p}^\times\), \(\tau(d)=d^{1-p}\); no such scalar assertion is made for a uniformizer. The tame units and adelic center act trivially (over \(\mathbb Q\) every finite central idele is a rational scalar times an integral unit, and \(\tau|_{\mathbb F_p^\times}=1\)). The **unweighted** Hecke sum over the \(\ell+1\) actual tame arrows eigenacts by \(a_\ell(E^\flat)\), \(\ell\nmid Np\). An arrow of derivative \(d_\ell\), written in separately chosen frames, contributes \(d_\ell^{1-p}F_{s'}\). Indeed pullback of the target Hasse scalar is \(d_\ell^{1-p}\) times the source scalar; on the other hand the sum of the pullbacks of the newform differential is its Hecke eigenvalue times that differential. The same comparison for a single Atkin arrow gives the literal signs
\[
    (w_2,w_3,w_5,w_7,w_{11},w_{37},w_{2399})=(-,+,+,-,+,-,+).
\]
The function is nonzero: if the normalized weight-two cusp form vanished at all supersingular labels, division by Hasse would give a form of weight \(3-p<0\).

## 2. Exact residual vector multiplicity one

Put \(k=\overline{\mathbb F}_p\), \(\bar\rho=E^\flat[p]\otimes k\). The [exact lower-base arithmetic](../../../joined-work-20261120/linked-detector-production-no-level-lowering-r1/NO-RESIDUAL-LEVEL-LOWERING-FOR-THE-ACTUAL-JET.md) gives minimal multiplicative depths \((2,4,4,6,2,2,2)\) at the seven ordered primes of \(N\). They are all prime to \(p\). By the Tate extension there is a nontrivial inertia transvection at each, hence residual conductor exponent one, and away from \(Np\) the representation is unramified. Frobenius at 23 has trace \(-8\) and polynomial discriminant \(-28\), nonsquare modulo \(p\), so excludes an invariant \(\mathbb F_p\) line. The unique invariant line of any one transvection is rational, so this likewise excludes a line after scalar extension. The prime-to-\(p\) residual conductor is exactly \(N\). The [actual ordinary cycle](../joined-actual-pa-ordinary-nonsplit-r2/ACTUAL-PA-ORDINARY-NONSPLIT.md) proves nonsplit ordinarity at \(p\), so \(\bar\rho\otimes\bar\chi_p^{-1}\) has a unique **ramified quotient**, while its subline is unramified.

Let \(X=X_0(N)_k\), \(C\) its cusps, \(A\) the Hasse section, \(\Sigma\) its reduced zero divisor. There are no extra elliptic stabilizers (2 excludes order 3, 3 excludes order 2), so the even Hodge powers descend and \(\Omega_X^1=\omega^2(-C)\); also \(p\nmid\varphi(N)\). Use the Hasse sequence
\[
   0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_X\xrightarrow{A}\omega^{p-1}
    \longrightarrow\omega^{p-1}|_\Sigma\longrightarrow0.
\]
A section of \(\omega^{p-1}|_\Sigma\), read in frame \(\eta^{p-1}\), has frame-change exponent \(1-p\). Pointwise \(p\)-th powers identify the framed functions of exponent \(p-1\) semilinearly with these sections, since all frame scalars lie in \(\mathbb F_{p^2}^\times\). Frobenius on values commutes with the actual good arrow sum on framed labels; the \(a_\ell\) are rational mod \(p\). The normalization across this sequence is \(1/\ell\) times the geometric correspondence, in particular the usual weight-\(p-1\) \(T_\ell\) on the middle term. Prime-to-\(p\) pullback respects Hasse. On \(H^1(\mathcal O_X)\), the adjoint of this normalized action under Serre duality is \(T_\ell^{(2)}/\ell\) on \(H^0(\Omega_X^1)\): trace and pullback are exchanged, dualizing the cyclic isogeny interchanges the projections, and unnormalized geometric trace-pullback of differential 1-forms is the usual weight-two \(T_\ell^{(2)}\). At the width-one cusp, the branch \(z\mapsto\ell z\) gives \(\ell f(\ell z)dz\); the \(\ell\) branches \(z\mapsto(z+j)/\ell\) give \(\sum_j f((z+j)/\ell)dz/\ell\). This verifies the exact normalization here, without assuming a general-weight adjoint formula.

The image of \(H^0(\omega^{p-1})\) in \(H^0(\omega^{p-1}|_\Sigma)\) has no good eigensystem \(T_\ell\mapsto a_\ell/\ell\). At every cusp the constant term has eigenvalue \(1+\ell^{p-2}\), by the \(q\)-formula at infinity and squarefree-level Atkin transitivity. Irreducibility excludes this system by good traces and Chebotarev; localization is therefore cuspidal and removes the Hasse line as well. The weight-\(p-1\) cusp space lifts integrally at this tame level: obstruction \(H^1(\omega^{p-1}(-C))\) vanishes by duality and negative degree of \(\omega^{3-p}\). A nonzero localized cusp space would contain an eigenpacket also for the commuting \(T_p\), which lifts by [Deligne–Serre, Lemma 6.11](https://numdam.org/item/10.24033/asens.1277.pdf). Its residual representation has good traces \(a_\ell/\ell\) and determinant \(\ell^{p-2}=\ell^{-1}\); Brauer–Nesbitt identifies the absolutely irreducible global representation, not just the semisimplification of its local restriction, with \(\bar\rho\otimes\bar\chi_p^{-1}\). By [Deligne ordinary and Fontaine nonordinary, as in Edixhoven, Thms 2.5–2.6](https://geodesic.mathdoc.fr/item/IM_1992__109_2_144035/), in this weight range and tame level a residual representation is either ordinary with an unramified quotient or nonordinary with irreducible restriction to inertia. The actual nonsplit twist has neither. Thus the framed good eigenspace injects into \(H^1(\mathcal O_X)\) with good eigenvalues \(a_\ell/\ell\) under its normalized action.

**From anemic to full.** Let \(T^{\mathrm{good}}_{\mathfrak m}\) be the completed anemic weight-two algebra at eigenvalues \(a_\ell\) under the unnormalized action. It sits in the simultaneous product of the \(p\)-adic coefficient integer rings of its characteristic-zero weight-two eigensystems. Exact prime-to-\(p\) residual conductor excludes all old factors of level properly dividing \(N\) in this product. Trace vectors of *every* global Galois element belong to the completed anemic ring: approximate using good Frobenius and Chebotarev in the common finite quotient of the simultaneous reductions modulo arbitrarily high powers of \(p\), then use closedness of a finite \(\mathbb Z_p\)-module. Each \(1+\ell\) is a \(p\)-unit. At \(p\) every component is ordinary, since the common residual restriction is reducible, unlike the nonordinary Fontaine case. For a lift \(g_0\) of arithmetic local Frobenius and inertia element \(s\) with \(1-\chi_p(s)\) a unit, the unramified unit-root vector is
\[

In the homomorphism-lattice description the \(p\)-local generator is transported by left composition with \(\iota(a_p)\), which commutes with \(\iota(\sqrt\Delta)\); the endomorphism in that transported frame, and hence its coefficient, is unchanged. This applies also to a ramified uniformizer acting on the Dieudonné lattice, not to an arbitrary reselection of the frame. \[
   \mathcal J_p=c_0\sum_{[a]\in\mathrm{Cl}(K)}

\]
No averaging by \(h_K\) and no forgotten genus or Frobenius phase occurs. In any independently rescaled frame the two factors transform reciprocally.

Use their completed \(L\)-factors and quotient measures consistently. The right side is algebraic with place fixed by the left side. If it is a \(p\)-unit, then \(\mathcal J_p\ne0\).
