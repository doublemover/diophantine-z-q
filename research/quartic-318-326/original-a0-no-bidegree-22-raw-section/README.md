# No degree-(2,2) rational parametrization on the fixed raw companion

Neither of the two raw first-coordinate parameters is required to satisfy a stronger ordered mark. Nevertheless, a rational family on the fixed raw companion cannot project with degree two to both conic parameter lines. The proof reduces the shared branch divisor to four exact Möbius maps and rules out all four for positive nonsquare complement. This applies to the archived original input-0 scalar.
