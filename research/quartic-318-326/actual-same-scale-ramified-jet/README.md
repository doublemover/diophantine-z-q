# A finite test for the moving-prime residue

The proof gives a first-order criterion at a moving prime of the actual same-scale twist. A nonzero signed sum certifies a point of infinite order by the stated classical inputs and gives a nonzero additive residue at that prime. It is a reduction to an exact finite evaluation, not an evaluation of the sum, a simultaneous witness at the moving primes, or a Lean result.

See the [argument](proof/ACTUAL-RAMIFIED-JET-FOR-MOVING-CONTACT.md) and the [independent review](review/INDEPENDENT-ACTUAL-RAMIFIED-JET-REVIEW.md).
