# The two actual pair-supported Cassels tests can be made positive via fixed Rédei fields

This is a paper strengthening of the actual two-class local
construction (7a597052), the verified Selmer subspace W
(653cd000), and the affine-denominator Cassels reduction
(7ba028fc). First retain a completed first class with
a=sigma_a=415721 q_a from 336708e6. We will choose the second
prime q_b (for b=sigma_b=9807649 q_b) more restrictively so both
known tests of the actual marked class on W are positive.
All old exact local cells, the common h and the second class's
actual order compatibility are retained.

This does not assert orthogonality against the full Selmer
group, a 4-Selmer lift, vanishing of Sha, or a rational point
on the shared genus-one curve. No numerical new prime is given.

## 1. Normalized two signs and admissible triples

Use the exact notation

    s=−32/(407 c_1²),  t=1/(2399 c_2²),  delta=t−s,  T=2399,
    D=ab.

Represent delta by a positive squarefree delta_0, so
delta=d_0² delta_0 with d_0 rational, supported on B. All
three rational numbers s,t,delta have support in B. The
targeted moving support primes p|ab are all 1 mod8.
At p|a the values b,s,t,delta are square units; at p|b
the values a,t,delta are square units. The fixed B
saturation makes a and b squares at each l|B. It follows
that all the local Hilbert symbols between a,b and
either T or delta_0 are trivial; also (a,b)_v=1 at
every place. For each assertion inspect the primes of
the two respective supports, and elsewhere use two unit
arguments at odd primes and positivity at infinity.
At 2 use a,b≡1 mod8. Since the moving primes are 1 mod4,
also (a,a)_v=(b,b)_v=1 everywhere. No claim or assumption
about (T,delta_0)_v is needed.

Use multiplicative, sign-valued Rédei symbols, namely
(−1)^{[.,.,.]_F2} from
[Stevenhagen, Definition 7.8 and Theorem 7.9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.06250).
All triples used below satisfy his pairwise Hilbert
conditions and gcd of the three discriminants equal to
one. In particular (D,a delta_0,b) is admissible:
(D,a delta_0) follows from the literal H_0 norm
equation; (D,b) and (a delta_0,b) follow from the
preceding facts. The three discriminants have no common
prime because b is supported outside a and B.
The triples after expansion with repetitions a,a or b,b
are admissible for the same reasons.

For Q_0,Q_1 chosen as in 7ba028fc, let g_0,g_1 be their
least affine coordinate denominators. That proof gives
the tested Cassels signs as P_b=kappa and P_a=kappa lambda,
where kappa=(g_0/b) and lambda=(g_1/a). We claim the exact
formulas

    kappa = (b/a)_4 [a,delta_0,b],
    lambda = gamma_a [a,T,b],                               (R)

where the rational quartic symbols are products of the
Legendre signs of square roots at the odd support primes
of their denominators, and

    gamma_a = product_{p|a} chi_p(sqrt(s)+sqrt(s−t)).

In gamma_a the roots are taken in F_p. Both radicands
are squares there. The product inside each p of the two
conjugate sign choices is t, a square unit, and −1 is
a square. Thus this bit is independent of root signs.

## 2. Proof of (R) with primitive and minimal twists kept separate

First use H_1 in affine form W_1²−b V_1²=t. Multiplying
W_1 and V_1 by the rational B-supported number 2399 c_2
changes the norm to T. Clear denominators minimally to
get the primitive integers X,Y,Z with

    X²−bY²=T Z²,    alpha_1=X+Y sqrt(b).

Here Z>0 differs from g_1 only by factors on B; it is
coprime to a and b by the affine choices. Over
Q(sqrt(b),sqrt(T)) take the dihedral norm extension
generated by sqrt(alpha_1), as in Stevenhagen (31)–(33).
At p|a, both quadratic generators split, and the actual
Artin sign in this quadratic extension is the local
unit sign of alpha_1. Reducing the norm generator at
either b-root gives

    product_{p|a} chi_p(alpha_1) = (g_1/a) gamma_a.

Indeed the rational multiplier 2399 c_2 contributes
Jacobi sign + by B-saturation. The remaining affine
unit W_1+sqrt(b)V_1 has, by the tangent normalization,
the character of sqrt(s)+sqrt(s−t) at p. Norm T is a
square unit there, so conjugating the b-root gives
the same sign.

This Artin product equals [b,T,a]. To check the
minimal ramification normalization, a primitive norm
triple X²−A Y²=B' Z² for squarefree A,B' makes
X+Y sqrt(A) even-valued at all places over odd
l not dividing A B'. For a split l both branches
cannot be divisible, since that would force l to
divide X,Y,Z; the possible single branch then has
the even norm valuation. For inert l the same
primitivity precludes divisibility. Over the
biquadratic field the resulting quadratic extension
is consequently unramified at those odd places.
All norm solutions for the fixed A,B' give rational
twists of the same type of dihedral extension:
the ratio of their generators has norm a square,
and Hilbert 90 writes it as a rational factor
times eta/bar(eta), which is a rational factor
up to a square in Q(sqrt(A)). By Stevenhagen
Definition 7.6 and Lemma 7.7 one can choose a
minimally ramified norm solution. Comparing two
extensions unramified at every odd l outside A B'
shows that their rational twist cannot have odd
valuation outside 2 A B' (the biquadratic field
is then unramified too). Thus the twist here is
supported on 2,b,T, with a possible sign. Its
product at p|a is +: the B-supported factors and
sign follow from saturation and a≡1 mod8, and
each r|b has (r/a)=(a/r)=+ by the actual other
support condition and quadratic reciprocity.
This justifies the minimal-symbol comparison,
including any dyadic minimal twist. Reciprocity
[b,T,a]=[a,T,b] proves the lambda formula.

For kappa use H_0 in affine form
a U_0²−b V_0²=delta. The norm of aU_0+V_0 sqrt(D)
is a delta. Dividing both components by d_0 and
clearing denominators minimally gives the primitive
norm triple

    X²−D Y²=a delta_0 Z²,    alpha_0=X+Y sqrt(D).

Here the denominator Z differs from g_0 only on B:
the factor a is a unit outside a's support, and
both old and new coordinates are integral on that
support. At p|b the intermediate quadratic field
Q(sqrt(D)) is ramified, and a delta_0 is a square
unit, so adjoining its square root makes no further
local change. The chosen U_0 is a unit and the
sqrt(D) part reduces to zero. Hence alpha_0 is a
unit, the quadratic extension is unramified there,
and its local Artin sign is

    chi_p(Z) · (a delta_0/p)_4.

Indeed aU_0/d_0 is a square root of a delta_0
in the residue, and its sign choice has character
one. The product over p|b is
kappa·(a delta_0/b)_4.

Compare again to minimal ramification. The same
primitive norm argument and Hilbert 90 restrict the
rational twist to 2,a,b,delta_0 and a sign. Twists
on B and on r|a have product sign + at p|b just as
above. For a twist by a single r|b, at p≠r use
(r/p). At r itself the rational r is the unit
class of D/r in Q_r(sqrt(D)), since D is a square
there. Its contribution is (D/r / r), which equals
(b/r / r) because a is a square unit at r. This
cancels the other b-factor symbols by reciprocity
(all p|b are 1 mod4). Thus the minimally ramified
comparison does not change the total product.
Consequently

    kappa = [D,a delta_0,b] (a delta_0/b)_4.

Expand the Rédei symbol by Theorem 7.9:

    [D,a delta_0,b]
      = [a,a,b] [a,delta_0,b] [b,a,b] [b,delta_0,b].

For the repetitions, use reciprocity and Stevenhagen
Definition 7.1, yielding (b/a)_4, (a/b)_4, and
(delta_0/b)_4 in the first, third and fourth factors.
The latter two cancel against (a delta_0/b)_4.
This proves (R) without dropping the primitive
tangent denominators.

## 3. The two controlling central signs are independently selectable

After fixing a, take minimally ramified dihedral fields
F_T and F_delta for the pairs (a,T) and (a,delta_0).
These are now fixed fields before selecting q_b.
The two B-supported positive squareclasses T and
delta_0 are independent: T is nonsquare, and at
the exact retained prime 4818481, delta has
residue 2532881 and character −, while T has
character +. The companion replay checks these
literal residues with the archived scale data.
The support of a outside B makes it independent
of both. This independence persists over G=Q(i):
a positive rational squareclass cannot become a
square there unless it was a square in Q.

Each F_i is D4 over Q, with maximal abelian
subfield Q(sqrt(a),sqrt(T)) or Q(sqrt(a),sqrt(delta_0)).
It does not contain i since its quadratic subfields
are those real fields. Base-change to G retains D4.
Their intersection over G is exactly G(sqrt(a)):
their distinct abelian subfields meet there, and
any proper normal quotient of D4 is abelian (every
nontrivial normal subgroup contains its commutator).
Thus the composite H over G has the fiber-product
Galois group of two D4 groups over the common
a character. In particular its two central
commutators z_T,z_delta are independent, and the
joint abelian quotient cuts out
G(sqrt(a),sqrt(T),sqrt(delta_0)).

Let F/G be the old finite order-clone and reciprocal
ray compositum for selecting q_b as in 7a597052.
We may refine the norm residue at each p|a so
p_b q_b is a fourth power modulo p, choosing
a nonzero residue for the norm q_b in
p_b^(-1) F_p^{×4}. This refines but preserves the
required quadratic bit, since the relevant root
difference has character +. Either split coordinate
can be chosen outside mu_4: with a fixed allowed
product at most eight first coordinates are forbidden.
So F retains the same relative ramification and
order-selection proof. We now have (b/a)_4=+.

We claim the intersection H∩F lies in the joint
abelian subfield of H. At either p|a the inertia of
F/G is central: the Kummer-clone part is unramified
there (it stripped the updated S' including q_a and
p_a), and the part ramified there is the abelian S'
ray field. In H an inertia element at p maps in
each D4 component to the a flip, fixing the other
quadratic coordinate T or delta_0; a is ramified
while those other coordinates are unramified there.
Taking its commutator with an element that flips
T while fixing a in the first component, and is
identity in the second, gives (z_T,1).
Indeed the two abelianization images in D4 are
independent, so their commutator is nontrivial.
Taking the analogous element in the second
component gives (1,z_delta). Inertia maps onto
inertia under the restriction to a Galois
subextension; both commutators must vanish in
H∩F because the base inertia image is central.
This kills the two-dimensional joint commutator
and proves the asserted intersection bound.

The base q_b conditions already force q_b to split
in the joint abelian subfield: a has unit square
by the exact reciprocal second-place argument of
7a597052, and T and delta_0 are square by the p_b
norm clone on B. These quadratic projections are
indeed among the base data: T and delta_0 are
B-supported quadratic characters of the rational
norm and a is supported at p|a in the S'-ray.
Hence the two central Frobenius signs on H can be
chosen arbitrarily with the base class retained.
No Frobenius on either variable-dependent tangent
conic is being assumed.

Now [a,T,b] and [a,delta_0,b] are products of the
central signs at p_b and at q_b in the two fixed
fields. The p_b signs are fixed (it is unramified
and splits in both biquadratic subfields). Impose
the two q_b central signs to give [a,T,b]=gamma_a
and [a,delta_0,b]=+1. Together with the refined
quartic condition, (R) gives kappa=lambda=+1.
Adding H to the fixed field does not affect the
odd-order gcd sieve: H is ramified only above
B and the support of a, already in the fixed
exception set. Thus the same Gaussian Brun–
Titchmarsh bound and late ray independence
close the proof of existence of q_b. The
updated CRT and real accepted h remain as before.

## 4. Scope of the result

The actual paired curve is everywhere locally soluble
with the prescribed finite cells, and its desired
marked class pairs trivially with all of the explicitly
verified subspace W. This eliminates both concrete
second-descent tests from 653cd000 for an existential
retune of the second prime. It neither computes the
full 2-Selmer group nor asserts orthogonality to its
remaining, possibly B-supported directions. Thus it
does not yet establish liftability to Sel_4, let alone
the vanishing of the actual Sha class or a rational
point satisfying the opens. No numerical choice of
q_a,q_b,h,w is exhibited.
