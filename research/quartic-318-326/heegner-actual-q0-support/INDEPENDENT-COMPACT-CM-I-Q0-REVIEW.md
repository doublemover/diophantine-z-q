## What was checked

1. **Compact common imaginary field.** At the actual fixed scalar `c=C/Z`, `δ=1−βc²` and `t=21060`, the real margin makes `M=t²δ−1>0`. It is a unit with residue −1 modulo 8 and modulo 3; division by rational unit squares preserves both residues, so its squarefree part `m` is 23 modulo 24. Thus `−m` is odd fundamental and 2 and 3 split. The actual scalar has `δ≡17 mod 53`, giving `M≡41 mod 53` nonsquare, while `δ` itself is square there. The eight β signs test the correct `1−t²`, not its negative. At any prime of the actual `θ=sf(δ)`, a negative `v(δ)` could only arise from `2v(c)` at a β-unit prime and would be even; at β primes, `c` is integral and `δ≡1`. Therefore every θ prime has positive `v(δ)` and splits by `1−t²δ≡1`. This argument covers unknown support without replacing the original scalar by a residue surrogate. The literal norm identity and coprimality of the compact numerator with the retained old moduli check out. Replayed the exact Python diagnostic to byte-identical JSON.

2. **Seven I incidences.** The actual compact numerator constant has 2-adic and 3-adic valuations 4 and 2 respectively, and the actual local units give `θ≡1 mod 24`. Consequently the narrow Hilbert class field of the real `F=Q(√δ)` is Galois over Q by invariance of both the real modulus and the narrow principal ideal subgroup; at finite primes its ramification is supported on θ only. The retained congruences form a unit CRT ray with modulus prime to θ, including the compact numerator and earlier chosen I primes. The intersection with the cyclotomic ray field has no finite ramification and is trivial by Minkowski. Chebotarev allows the identity in the narrow class field and the prescribed cyclotomic Frobenius simultaneously. A totally positive degree-one ideal generator `a=x+y√δ` has norm `p`, so `δ=(x/y)²−p(1/y)²` with `y≠0`. At each actual θ prime, this exact rational norm certificate contradicts inertness in `Q(√p)` because the local norm valuation would be even. The certificate is sufficient for the entire I row; it does not characterize narrow principality in reverse or extract generators efficiently. 3. **Exact imaginary q₀ incidence gate.** At a trial `q≡7 mod 12` prime to θ and satisfying the old rows, `D=−qθ` is odd fundamental. The actual unsigned off-diagonal `[r/p]` is precisely Stevenhagen's genus-character-row entry `[p*/r]`: writing `s_p=[−1/p]`, signed reciprocity adds `s_ps_r` twice. His column-sum diagonal agrees with our row-sum diagonal because `Σs_p=1` and the difference at p is `s_p(1+s_p)=0`. Both sums are zero. Summing all rows into the q row and all columns into the q column preserves rank and leaves the exact old companion block H; hence `nullity H = |supp D|−1−rank R₄ = r₄ Cl(Q(√(−qδ)))`. There is no dyadic row or real narrow-class correction for this field. Equivalently the ordinary class number has `v₂h=ω(θ)`; for `θ>1`, the latter constant is obtainable once as `1+r₂ Cl⁺(Q(√δ))`. The companion norm cross-check follows from the Hilbert-symbol identity `(d,qθ/d)=(d,−qθ)`; ramified ideal parity then identifies `d` norms with square classes of those prescribed ambiguous ideals. Primary orientation: [Stevenhagen, Theorem 3.1 and equation (16)](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004121000335).
