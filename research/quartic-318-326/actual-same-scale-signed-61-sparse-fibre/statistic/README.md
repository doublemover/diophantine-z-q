## 2. Actual free ramified action and Frobenius quotient

Let \(H\subset C\) be generated by the three ramified ideal classes
at 11, 37 and 2399. Each squares to one. The only relation among
**all** ramified ideal classes is the product of all of them:
if a subset product is principal, its generator \(\alpha\) has
\(\alpha/\bar\alpha=\pm1\) because the ideal is conjugation-invariant
and the unit group is \(\{\pm1\}\). If \(\alpha\) is rational, its
norm has only even valuations. If purely imaginary, then
\(\alpha=t\sqrt{\Delta}\), \(t\in\mathbb Q\); the squarefree support
of its norm is all of \(|\Delta|\). The full product is indeed
\((\sqrt{\Delta})\). Here \(H\simeq C_2^3\). The actual \(D\) has
four distinct movers, so the eight total ramified primes give
\(|C/H|\ge2^7/8=16\).

At a ramified slot \(\ell\) the CM \(\mathfrak l\)-isogeny sends
\(x_a\) to \(W_\ell x_a=x_{\mathfrak l a}\): quotient by the
unique \(\mathfrak l\)-mark, transport every other mark, and
note that the dual mark is also the unique \(\mathfrak l\)-mark
since \(\mathfrak l^2=(\ell)\). Inversion of the class-action
convention is immaterial. The prior unramified-auxiliary calculation
gives \(\psi(\mathfrak l)=w_\ell\). Consequently
\(v_{ha}=\psi(h)v_a\) for \(h\in H\).

There is no geometric collapse *inside* a ramified eight-orbit.
None of the five supersingular \(j\)'s at 61 is \(0,1728\), so
the only automorphisms of the underlying curves are \(\pm1\).
Fixing a marked supersingular label by a nonidentity \(W_m\)
with \(m\mid r\) gives an endomorphism \(u\) of degree \(m\):
quotient by the \(m\)-mark then apply the isomorphism back.
It kills the \(m\)-mark and maps \(E[m]\) to that mark because
the isomorphism sends the dual mark back to it. Thus
\(u^2=\pm[m]\) by factoring through \([m]\) and comparing degrees.
The plus case contradicts squarefree \(m>1\):
\(u^2-\operatorname{tr}(u)u+[m]=0\) would give \(u\in\mathbb Q\)
with square degree (the trace cannot be zero in the plus case).
The minus case preserves each residual marked line \(C_\ell\),
\(\ell\mid N/m\), and hence requires \((-m/\ell)=+1\) at each
odd such \(\ell\). All seven are excluded by the following
actual pairs \((m,\ell)\) with \((-m/\ell)=-1\):
\[
(11,7),(37,3),(2399,11),(407,5),(26389,3),(88763,5),(976393,3).
\]
This does not give injectivity of CM reduction on the quotient.

Frobenius at the fixed 61 place acts on the Hilbert field as
\(c\sigma_\tau\) with complex conjugation \(c\), \(\tau\in C\)
(61 is inert and the principal ideal gives trivial square
Frobenius). Since \(d'>0\) and \(d'\) is locally square at 61,
\(\psi(\tau)=+1\). CM conjugation is class inversion with the
known level shift \(\mathfrak N^{\pm1}\) and \(W_N\). Thus
\[
 \operatorname{Frob}_{61}(\bar x_a)=W_N\bar x_{\nu a^{-1}},
 \qquad \psi(\nu)=\psi(\tau)\psi(\mathfrak N)=-1.
\]
We do not assume \(\tau=1\); other genus bits can survive.
The 17-line is Frobenius-fixed and \(w_N=-1\), so
\(v_{\nu a^{-1}}=-v_a\).

## 3. The single statistic and its sharp scalar-interface limit

Set \(w_{aH}=\psi(a)v_a\) and \(\iota(aH)=\nu a^{-1}H\).
The first is well-defined and constant on \(\iota\)-orbits
\(O\subset C/H\); the second is an involution. The decisive
statistic is
\[
 T=8\sum_O |O|w_O\in\mathbb F_{17},\qquad |O|=1,2.
\]
There is no averaging, deduplication, or dropped sign of a
coset representative. A producer of the sixteen signed counts
\(n_t=\#\{aH:w_{aH}=t\}\bmod17\), \(t\ne0\), suffices:
the nonzero test depends on \(\sum_{t=1}^8t(n_t-n_{-t})\).

Even the entire **unsigned histogram** of the readouts \(v_a\),
together with \(C\), \(\psi\) and both equivariances, cannot
determine this scalar in the stated interface. Any values of
\(w_O\) give scalar solutions \(v_a=\psi(a)w_O\). Pick two
different \(\iota\)-orbits of equal size \(s\) (there are at
least eight orbits of sizes one or two). Compare \(w_1,w_2=1,1\)
with \(w_1,w_2=1,-1\) and all other entries zero. They have
identical unsigned histograms because every \(H\)-coset has
four of each genus sign. Their values of \(T\) are \(16s\ne0\)
and zero. This is a limitation of the reported data, **not**
a claim that both arrays arise from CM geometry. An exact
geometric theorem about the signed count would address the
missing information; ordinary unsigned equidistribution does not.
