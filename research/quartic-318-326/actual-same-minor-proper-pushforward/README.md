# A shorter finite-place determinant bound

Push the actual regular canonical forms from the semistable resolution to the normalized model; then evaluate the cleared forms at the original sections. The proper comparison is the identity on the same generic field, and the separate finite trace is the trace in the fixed coordinate. This keeps the original determinant and the same final bound without an exceptional correction or a regularity calculation for the sections on the resolution.

This is the independently checked paper route. The native proof still needs the actual semistable, duality and Hodge-lattice attachments.
