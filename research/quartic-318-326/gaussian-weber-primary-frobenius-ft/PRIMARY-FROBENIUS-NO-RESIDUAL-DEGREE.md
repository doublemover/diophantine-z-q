## Exact paper theorem

On the literal curve E:y²=x³−x over Fp, take J(x,y)=(−x,i₀ y). With the same chosen prime and the **positive** arithmetic Frobenius, the reduction of the Gaussian endomorphism of the primary generator π₀ of (π) is exactly (x,y)↦(x^p,y^p) and O↦O. Here “primary” means π₀≡1 modulo (1+i)^3. On the Weber value x² no primary normalization is required at all: conjugation of the ideal is not allowed, but every global Gaussian unit leaves x² unchanged.

This replaces the residual degree-[p], geometric automorphism, and purely inseparable function-field factorization route by exact rational degree, actual same-place point reduction, and the formal mixed translation already proved on the literal model. It does not prove the Frei–Tinková distribution theorem, the original rational ranks, or the whole quartic solution. No Lean build claimed.

## 3. Literal derivative via the same small chart and all relevant poles
Over k̄((t)) form P(t) with w+t w²=t³ and w=t³+O(t⁷), from monic Hensel or direct recursion. Then x=t/w has pole, y=−1/w, and the origin parameter is t. Take any finite Q over k̄ with y_Q≠0 and r regular at x_Q; these omit finitely many x. ## 4. Unit, prime selection, and equality on every actual point
