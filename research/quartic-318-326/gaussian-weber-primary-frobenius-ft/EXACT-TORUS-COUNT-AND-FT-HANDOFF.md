## Initial classical contract

The lift h through 0 of a holomorphic group homomorphism of these tori is additive because h(z+w)−h(z)−h(w) lies in the discrete lattice and vanishes at (0,0); holomorphic additive h is h′(0)z. The literal parameter t=−x/y gives t(JP)=it(P), so the chosen derivative and lift of J are i, not −i. For π=a+bi of norm p, the lift of [π]=[a]+[b]J is multiplication by this same π and preserves Λ. The induced map is surjective; every fibre on C/Λ has [Λ:πΛ]=p points by the real determinant a²+b².

The actual generic coordinate x([π]P) is r_π(X)∈K(X), and under K→C the same stock formulas give its coefficient embedding. For a reduced nonconstant ratio A/B in characteristic zero, a generic target x=c has D=max(deg A,deg B) distinct finite inverse x-values (exclude critical/branch and leading-coefficient exceptions). Each admissible inverse x-value contributes two points to the two disjoint target point fibres, both of size p. Thus D=p. This is the entire initial proof input needed for the companion: no degree-[p] theorem in the residue field, generic derivation API, or automorphism classification. Primarity is not needed for this size.
