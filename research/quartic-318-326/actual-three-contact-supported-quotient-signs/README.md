# Absolute positive-pair sign and jointly avoidable supported tests

The absolute sign calculation distinguishes the passing rigid branch from
the obstructed norm-one modification. A separate existence argument shows
that one prime choice passes all known supported pairing tests on the five
elliptic quotients while keeping the pointed three-contact construction.
These are not the entire pairing radicals of the remaining elliptic factors
and do not supply a rational point or numerical prime choices.
