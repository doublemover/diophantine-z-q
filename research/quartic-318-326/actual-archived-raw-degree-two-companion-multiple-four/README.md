# Degree-two projection on the archived raw companion

On the unchanged archived input-zero scalar, any nonconstant rational raw pair with one projection of degree two must have the other degree divisible by four. This excludes (2,6) and (6,2) without a coefficient search, in addition to the separately excluded low bidegrees. It does not exclude (4,4), certify a surviving higher degree, or decide any individual rational fibre.
