# An actual nonzero paired term at the fixed moving prime

On the selected same-scale twist family with the original moving prime fixed, the exact local differential sum has a nonzero contribution after pairing by ramified Frobenius and quotienting by the three ramified level classes. This holds for all sufficiently large members of the stated family; the threshold is qualitative.

Other terms may cancel it. No full signed sum, qualifying rational point, numeric trace value, or Lean theorem is claimed.
