# Original modular height: geometric witness map and review

[Witness map](WHAT-INSTANTIATES-THE-ONE-LATTICE-HEIGHT.md) and
[independent signed review](INDEPENDENT-ONE-LATTICE-GEOMETRIC-WITNESS-REVIEW.md)
are the exact paper-reviewed versions. The conclusion here is a bound
for the **modular Jacobian J_X** of the original compact curve with its
literal J-coordinate, not for the separate quaternionic surface A.
Transport to A belongs to the same-object quotient/isogeny argument.

The witness map explains how a single invariant Néron lattice, its
actual localizations, the canonical whole-field trace, and the same
ordered period integrals supply the inputs of the source draft
SelectedOriginalOneLatticePeriodHeight318.original_height_from_one_lattice.
That source snapshot (426 selected modules, 473 project-closure
entries) was not compiled by this review. Neither publication nor the
review activates a proof target or claims a Lean theorem for the
modular or quaternionic height.

## References for the witness map

- Ariyan Javanpeykar, *Polynomial bounds for Arakelov invariants of
  Belyi curves*, Algebra & Number Theory 8 (2014), Theorem 4.3.2.
  [Published theorem](https://msp.org/ant/2014/8-1/ant-v8-n1-p04-s.pdf#page=31).
  It applies to the actual finite J-map, without dividing J by 1728.
- The [Néron mapping property](https://msp.org/ant/2010/4-2/ant-v4-n2-p01-s.pdf#page=24)
  extends the genuine generic Abel–Jacobi map on the smooth locus.
- Stacks Project: [0AU3](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0AU3)
  for duality on finite-type separated schemes;
  [0FKW](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0FKW) for the finite Hom;
  [0BW0](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BW0) for the generic trace
  pairing in that same finite Hom.
