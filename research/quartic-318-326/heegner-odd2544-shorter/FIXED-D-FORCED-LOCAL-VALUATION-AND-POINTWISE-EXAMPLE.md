# A fixed-d compatible-field infinitude, and a new pointwise positive control

Use the exact shorter fixed mod-13 coefficient sources. Two complementary consequences are proved here; neither selects a production prime graph from the fixed scalar.

## 1. True valuation-one positive sieve and infinitely many m after one compatible unit

Fix one already known positive squarefree d≡11 mod24 with (d/53)=−1, 13∤d and A₋^37(d) a unit at the retained residue prime. Suppose there is a single compatible squarefree m_* for which A₊^(−35)(m_*d) is a unit. Then there are infinitely many compatible squarefree m for this SAME d with A₊^(−35)(md) a unit. Consequently the actual toric residue s(m,d) is nonzero throughout that infinite subfamily.

For D_* = m_* d, keep the positive Fourier filter D≡13 mod24 and (D/53)=+1. For each odd q|d impose v_q(D)=1, and impose the unit symbol of D/q that its true seed D_*/q has at q:
```
(D/q over q) = (D_*/q over q).
```
This extends exact valuation control without allowing a bad prime in the squarepart. It is a genuine half-integral weight modular sieve. With U_q extracting the q multiples and V_q restoring the indexing, apply U_q first, then one half of the sum of its principal twist and its quadratic twist with the specified sign, and finally V_q. At the level of coefficients only terms D=qn with q∤n and the specified symbol of n survive. By [James–Ono, Proposition 3](https://uva.theopenscholar.com/files/ken-ono/files/043.pdf), each U_q or V_q multiplies the level by at most q and changes the character by (4q/·). Their composite here recovers the original character inflated at the common higher level (the intermediary quadratic or principal twist has square principal). The successive filters commute on coefficients; their levels introduce only powers of the named q. At the auxiliary Type-I primes 5,7, also filter the valuation to be 0 or 1 according to the seed D_* (with a matching unit symbol if valuation one), so no aux prime can occur in the squarepart. All filter primes avoid 13.

The surviving sieve is invariant under multiplying or dividing the index by squares of units at its bad primes. Hence T(p²) away from the named modulus and source exclusions still has the same eigenvalue, by inspection of the three terms in its coefficient formula. Every prime in a retained squarepart u has its T(p²) recurrence available; at forced primes this is precisely why the valuation-one, not mere divisibility, condition is needed. Apply the finite-squareclass contradiction from the companion infinitude note to obtain infinitely many squarefree D in this sieve with unit coefficient. Each equals md with (m,d)=1. Its given mod24 and mod53 conditions force m≡23 mod24 and (m/53)=−1. At q|d the retained quotient symbol is that of m_*·(d/q); removing (d/q) gives (m/q)=(m_*/q), so each q splits in Q(√−m), as it did in Q(√−m_*). This proves actual compatibility and the toric conclusion for the same d, without presupposing a factorization of any eventual m.

One exact seed where the statement applies is d=323, m_*=1079=13·83. Both 17 and 19 split; the two quotient symbols are (+,−), the positive Fourier index is 348517, and the five class contributions in the new positive theta are (−172,−190,−684,160,−306), hence 4 mod13. Neither 5 nor 7 divides this seed. The fixed negative d323 already has signed phase 12. The auxiliary m is allowed to contain 13 here: we impose no sieve or bad-prime condition at that coefficient prime and use the available T(13²). This proves an infinite family of compatible fields for one fixed known twist. For a real selected d the required first negative unit and first compatible positive unit are still missing; independently proving this for d and dθ does not assert one shared field m.

## 2. A new bounded rational nontorsion point by the coefficient route alone

For the bounded control d=2579 and m=455 from b77a6965…, the exact coefficient formula gives s²=1 in F₁₃. This concerns the actual class trace on the true level-48d² curve after its explicit Gauss map, not a count of reduced classes. There is no need to evaluate each individual Heegner summand. The raw unaveraged D=1 trace is rational by the general Fricke calculation from the fixed-scalar guards, which uses d≡11 mod24 and the exact oriented split orbit, not the shape of the scalar itself. At 37 the curve E₍₂₅₇₉₎ has good reduction. At every residue degree ≤2 its group order is 32 or 44 (degree one) or 1408 (degree two), by the true good-twist trace count. Thus its quadratic field cannot have any 13-primary torsion; the trace detected at 53 is nontorsion. This is an existence proof for a rational nontorsion point on that specific curve, not yet an explicit coordinate and not the actual production marked β class. The matched d1931 has no such mod-13 detection, which is not a bound on its global rank.
