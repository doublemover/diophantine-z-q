# A fixed-place mod-13 nonvanishing theorem for the relaxed local family

This is a precise paper theorem for the actual reduced weighted sources, not a production prime-selection theorem. It separates finite one-index local sieves from the exact many-prime incidence selector of the original scalar.

## Statement

Use the unchanged definite level-2544 packet F, fixed residue λ above 13, and exact shorter theta sources A₋^37, A₊^(−35) from b77a6965…. In the negative direction there are infinitely many positive squarefree d with
```
d≡11 (mod 24), (d/53)=−1, gcd(d,37)=1, A₋^37(d) not 0 at λ.
```
In the positive direction there are infinitely many positive squarefree D with
```
D≡13 (mod 24), (D/53)=+1, gcd(D,35)=1, A₊^(−35)(D) not 0 at λ.
```
Here the negative coefficient may be read either before or after removal of the same quartic unit on each fundamental row. Any further finite exclusion-of-primes or unit quadratic-symbol (±1, not 0) sieve matching a specified surviving mod-13 unit seed is allowed as long as its moduli are prime to 13 and the retained indices are coprime to every extra sieve prime. For forced valuation-one primes instead use the separate U/V construction below. The respective unit seeds can be d=323 (signed phase 12 mod13) and D=37 (10 mod13); the corresponding raw five-class sum for the new positive seed is (−16,120,16,56,−36). This does not impose an index that is multiple of the full β or θ, or any primality/cofactor-incidence requirement.

## The actual residual image is GL₂(F₁₃)

By the old full Hecke lift, the residual representation of the same g is that of E₋₁, since the good-prime Hecke characters agree and it is absolutely irreducible. At 3 the actual E₋₁ has split multiplicative reduction, with minimal discriminant valuation 2 (c₄ unit, split tangents in the original cubic). The Tate curve therefore gives a nontrivial order-13 unipotent in residual inertia: the cyclotomic order-13 roots are unramified at 3, and a Tate parameter of valuation 2 is not a thirteenth power even over the maximal unramified extension. Irreducibility over F₁₃ gives a conjugate fixing a different line. In a basis of these two lines the unipotents are upper and lower elementary matrices with nonzero parameters. Their powers run through the whole prime field and generate SL₂(F₁₃). The determinant is the surjective cyclotomic character, hence the image is exactly GL₂(F₁₃). This uses neither a speculative mod-13 level raise at a new p nor a Serre-exception heuristic.

## Proof for a sieved source

The two sources are actual half-integral weight cusp forms: [Sirolli–Tornaría Prop. 5.1 and 5.11](https://arxiv.org/html/2107.04483) give level 4·lcm(2544,f_χ²), and only 3,53 occur in the Type-II conductor f_χ. Their Type-I primes 37 or 5,7 do not enter this level. The skew source becomes classical holomorphic by u=−1 in their Prop. 2.3 and Eq. (2.7), after the usual z↦2z normalization; this retains the literal Fourier coefficients at their absolute indices. The classical Hecke compatibility away from 2·2544·|l| gives the g eigenvalues up to the fixed character factor; even if a normalization is used, zero traces are preserved by its nonzero unit factors. In particular both new auxiliaries leave prime 13 in the available Hecke relation. Coefficients are λ-integral in the fixed F normalization (finite integral weight sums with only fixed unit stabilizer denominators); algebraic denominators at other primes can be cleared by one scalar that is a λ-unit.

Filter the q-expansion onto the stated mod-24 unit class, the sign at 53, and the avoidance of the relevant auxiliary primes, adding further square-invariant unit character filters only if they preserve a unit seed and require the index to be prime to the extra filter moduli. These are linear combinations of quadratic and principal Dirichlet twists (all units mod24 have order at most 2), with the power-of-two denominators invertible at 13. Twisting and promotion to a common level of the same nebentype only introduce primes of the filter modulus. Thus 13 remains prime to the level. The filtered form G is nonzero modulo λ by the stated seed. Filtering commutes with T(p²) at primes away from that level and the source exclusions, including at 13, because the retained index tests are invariant under multiplication by a unit square and division by one when defined. We work with the actual raw Fourier coefficients, not a new index-wise division that might destroy modularity.

Suppose there are only finitely many squarefree indices s with a_G(s) nonzero mod λ. If a_G(n) is nonzero and n=s u² then every prime of u avoids the level and the auxiliary exclusions by the filter (p=13 also permissible). At these primes the standard T(p²) coefficient recurrence expresses a_G(su²) as a polynomial multiple of a_G(s); this follows inductively on the p-exponents, since a_G(n/p²)=0 when p²∤n. Therefore modulo λ the whole nonzero support lies in the finitely many square classes s_i just named.

Apply the eigen-congruence conclusion of [Bruinier–Ono, *Coefficients of half-integral weight modular forms*, Theorem 1(2), Remark (1), and proof of Corollary 2](https://www.mathematik.tu-darmstadt.de/media/algebra/homepages/bruinier/publikationen/halfint.pdf). More precisely, their proof of the eigen-congruence is conditional on the expression with finite squareclass support, and is valid for algebraic integer coefficients as the remark states. For a prime p outside the level and 13 with every (s_i/p)=+1, it makes (p−1)G an eigenvector with eigenvalue χ(p)((−1)/p)(p+1) modulo λ, since the weight is 3/2. Any character changes from the fixed source are units; the conclusion at any p with p−1,p+1 both nonzero mod13 is a nonzero eigenvalue.

There exist primes p meeting this split condition with p≡3 mod13 and a_p(g)≡0 mod λ. Let L be the multiquadratic compositum of Q(√s_i). The image of G_L contains SL₂(F₁₃), since L/Q is abelian and the commutator of GL₂(F₁₃) is SL₂(F₁₃). Its determinants still contain the order-three subgroup of F₁₃× because L has degree a power of two. Thus an element of determinant 3 and trace zero occurs in the image of G_L. Apply Chebotarev in the compositum with the residual representation field, excluding the finitely many bad and filter primes. The resulting prime splits in L, and both p−1,p+1 are units at 13; the actual Hecke eigenvalue on G at this p is zero modulo λ, contradiction. The claimed infinitude follows.

## Exact obstruction to using this as production selection

This allows any finite one-index sieve preserving a preexisting unit, not the original selector. The required missing assertion for even one actual selected twist is a mod-13 unit at n=β·∏I·q₀ **with those exact prime factors, count, incidence matrix and partner-support restrictions**. The above cannot force this squarefree shape, and a sieve enforcing even β|n has no known surviving fixed-scalar seed here. A separate need for the odd trace detector is a unit at A₊(mn) with this SAME n and a compatible m; separate infinitude of the two directions gives no such joint assertion. The negative coefficient is independent of m, and the genuine matched local control d1931 has zero at the negative coefficient even when the positive coefficient is a unit. No standard quadratic-twist infinitude theorem is being promoted to a selection theorem for the actual scalar.
