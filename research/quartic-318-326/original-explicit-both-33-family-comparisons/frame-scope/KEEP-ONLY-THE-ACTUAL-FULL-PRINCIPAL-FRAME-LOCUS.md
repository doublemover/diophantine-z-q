# Full principal frames: precise locus and minimal published inputs

The two explicit frame-complement constructions are statements about full principal markings. Yuan v2 §2.1 first describes the local representative as an O-linear map. One should not infer from that displayed formula alone that every such map, or every point of a broader non-full level problem, is invertible. The following argument needs neither a corrected statement for the entire model nor a bad-prime representability theorem from Buzzard.

For N=3,11,33 use [Yuan v2 §2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2205.11334v2#S2.SS1) for the actual fine principal-level integral model X_N, its universal object, and the fixed μ-compatible principal polarization. The principal congruence subgroup U(N) acts trivially modulo N. Therefore the universal level datum at this principal level is a descended O-linear morphism η_N from the constant finite group scheme O/NO to A_N[N] (the local maps agree on every prescribed étale overlap). The chosen original marks in this argument are actual full frames; no nonsurjective map is substituted for them.

Both source and target of η_N are finite étale group schemes over X_N, since N is invertible on this base. The locus where η_N is an isomorphism is open and closed. To see this, simultaneously trivialize the two finite étale schemes on an étale cover. The actual map is then a locally constant map of finite sets, and the union of parts on which the map is bijective is open and closed. The condition and its complement descend, or equivalently can be checked on geometric fibres. Thus there is a canonically determined clopen subscheme X_N^full, with its same universal family, representing the full-frame subfunctor. This does not assert that X_N^full exhausts the entire named X_N. If an independently checked canonical generic principal-frame identification shows that the entire characteristic-zero fibre lies there, flatness would additionally imply exhaustion; that extra assertion is not used.

The explicit twelve-kernel and one-kernel constructions are naturally identified, by their full-frame representing property, with that very X_33^full over X_3^full and X_11^full respectively. Their relative family squares preserve the full selected generic marks and compatible principal polarization. They are not independently chosen branches of a coarse family and they do not require identifying every component of a broad shorthand moduli condition.

Every designated generic point in the construction belongs to X_N^full, by the original choices of full level frames. The restriction from X_N to this clopen locus is harmless for the downstream proof:

* It is proper on the same base because it is closed in the actual proper model, and it remains regular and semistable because it is open.
* The relative universal family, actual zero, cotangent and dualizing line on it are the restrictions of the ones on X_N. The arithmetic image equality and complex metric statement of [Yuan Theorem 1.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2205.11334v2#Thmtheorem1) restrict unchanged; neither 210 nor the Hodge metric normalization changes.
* A chosen proper extension into X_N automatically factors through X_N^full: the complement's inverse image is clopen and avoids the dense generic point of the integral base. Equivalently, one can apply properness directly to the clopen model. At places over 3 we still use only level 11, and vice versa.

This is a classical clarification of the actual input to both comparison squares. It does not assert native PEL representability, unnoticed whole-model isomorphisms, or an analytic period conclusion beyond the previous exact papers.
