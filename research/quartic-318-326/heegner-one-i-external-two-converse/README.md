Let S = Sel₂∞(J). The finite and infinite Kummer sequences give the exact comparison
```text
0 → J(ℚ)[2∞]/2J(ℚ)[2∞] → Sel₂(J) → S[2] → 0.
```
The map on the two Tate–Shafarevich right terms is the identity on Sha(J)[2]. The finite Mordell–Weil Kummer term maps onto the 2-torsion in the tensor product of J(ℚ) with ℚ₂/ℤ₂, with exactly the displayed torsion kernel. Thus S[2] consists of zero and the nonzero marked image s. For each m ≥ 1, the image xₘ in S of a finite lift has 2ᵐ⁻¹xₘ = s by compatibility of multiplication and coefficient inclusions. It has order exactly 2ᵐ. Each doubling fiber in S has at most two elements, so S[2ᵐ] has at most 2ᵐ elements by induction and is cyclic of order exactly 2ᵐ. Their ascending union is the Prüfer group ℚ₂/ℤ₂. This argument does not assume Sha is finite or require that compatible lifts were chosen in advance.
