# One fewer independent local image is sufficient

Keep the same actual E_d, sign −1, and the exact 14 local rows of the
faithful λ point-field computation. If r0 is any proved upper bound
on dim Cl(F)[13]_λ, it suffices to exhibit **5+r0** actual λ S-powerclasses
with independent images in those local rows. There is no need to
compute a full global basis or to force finite Sel13 dimension at most one.

Indeed the global ambient space has dimension at most 7+r0.
Independence of the displayed images gives kernel dimension at most
2, hence dim Sel13(E_d)≤2 using the exact injection. The Kummer exact
sequence implies that the full 13-primary corank s13 is at most
dim Sel13: each Mordell–Weil rank and each divisible-primary Sha
summand already contributes one dimension at level 13.
By [Dokchitser–Dokchitser, Theorem 1.4 (=4.19)](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2010/172-1/p11)
the full corank has the parity of the actual sign, so it is odd.
Thus it is exactly 1. This does not assume finite Sha or identify
finite-level Sel13 with the full corank.

This good ordinary non-CM E_d at p=13 meets the hypotheses of
[Yan–Zhu, Theorem 5.11](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.20078v4#S5.SS3).
If the foregoing certificate were obtained, it would give analytic
rank one, Mordell–Weil rank one, and finite Sha. In particular the
same original marked split cover would have a rational point:
rank one passes to its isogenous J, and Sel₂(J) dimension 3 with
two rational torsion classes forces Sha(J)[2]=0, so the exact
original class is rational. Existence is not coordinates. The
14-row width permits this sufficient test for r0≤9, rather than
r0≤8; no such r0 or independent classes have yet been certified.
