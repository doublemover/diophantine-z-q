# Full 4-Selmer lift for the marked two-contact cover

For some admissible choices of the two auxiliary primes, the marked class of
the actual two-contact cover lifts from 2-Selmer to 4-Selmer on its own
Jacobian, while the underlying construction retains the earlier local
contact conditions at one common index. This controls the entire marked
pairing row, without factoring the fixed coefficients. The proof is
existential and paper-level: no numerical primes or explicit 4-cover, no
rational point, and no conclusion on rank or vanishing of Sha. The lifted
cover is not claimed to meet the original local incidence neighborhoods.
