# The two actual Ext values are the two polarized Yuan columns

Fix the actual full-frame level-3 model \(Y/S\), \(S=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z[1/3]\), for the originally selected \(D_B=210\) surface. Its actual universal family is \(\pi:P\to Y\), with its original order structure and compatible principal \(\lambda:P\to P^\vee\). Take the chosen height-one point \(y\) above 13 or 5, and \(k=\kappa(y)\) or its already chosen residue extension. At 5 use the smooth generic point of the chosen reduced component, not the node of a potentially ramified trait. Set \(A=P_y\), \(W=(\Omega_{Y/S})_y\otimes k\), and \(D=k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2)\). The nonzero relative tangent \(v:W\to k\) gives the original \(\xi:\operatorname{Spec}D\to Y\); \(X=\xi^*P\) has the actual fibre \(A\) and its original zero mark. No lifting of \(k\) to the mixed-characteristic stalk or differentiation of the arithmetic trait is involved.

## The actual pair of classes

On \(A\) restrict the original cotangent sequence of \(P/Y/S\), denoting it by \(S_0\). Its left term is \(\mathcal O_A\otimes_k W\), its right term is \(\Omega_{A/k}\), and it remains exact because the original smooth row is locally split. The two rows may be constructed using the actual \(k\)-base changes \(Y_k,P_k\), retaining relative differentials, not substituting absolute differentials over the possibly imperfect residue field. The directional row \(S_\xi\) has actual middle \(\Omega_{X/k}|_A\), left \(1\mapsto d\epsilon\), and that same right. The genuine map \(S_0\to S_\xi\) has left \(1\otimes v\) and right identity. In the actual affine pushout \(C=T\otimes_R D,\,B=T\otimes_R k\), derivations of \(C\) into a \(B\)-module are pairs of derivations from \(T,D\) agreeing on \(R\), with \(d_D(r)=v(dr)d_D(\epsilon)\). Thus this is the pushout of the **restricted** locally split row by \(1\otimes v\); the actual middle module is \(\Omega_{C/k}/\epsilon\Omega_{C/k}\), not the quotient killing \(d\epsilon\). No claim of flatness is made for \(C\to B\).

Let \(u_1,u_2\) be the actual two invariant forms obtained by translating the original ordered zero-cotangent basis. These are the two inputs of the native boundary owner with its actual ordered frame I. Its two classes are
\[
 b_i=H^1(1\otimes v)(\delta_0(u_i))=\delta_\xi(u_i)\quad (i=1,2).
\]
Here \(\delta_0,\delta_\xi\) are the genuine connecting maps of these exact additive sheaves. The following explicit identification is a classical identity of the full two columns, not just a test for their simultaneous vanishing.

## One cocycle and the actual represented dual

Take a finite affine cover of the actual family over a small affine neighborhood of \(y\) on the smooth locus. All intersections are affine by separatedness and remain so on the actual base-changed fibre. Shrink the base so the two Hodge modules and the base cotangent line have frames. Over the local stalk the chosen \(u_i\) lift to invariant forms by local freeness and translation; for an extension \(k/\kappa(y)\) first do the comparison over \(\kappa(y)\), then use \(k\)-linear extension of the pair. Extend the stalk lifts to a smaller affine base. Lift each form locally to the original smooth total cotangent. With Čech differential \(db_{ab}=b_b-b_a\) the unique left coefficient of the difference gives its connecting cocycle. The affine Čech complexes compute cohomology of these quasi-coherent sheaves and their connecting maps on underlying additive sheaves. Restrict those same local lifts to \(A\), and apply \(1\otimes v\) to their same left coefficients. If \(f_{i,ab}\) denotes the resulting scalar, the actual directional middle has differences \(f_{i,ab}\,d\epsilon\), with \([f_i]=b_i\). This is both the restriction and evaluation on \(v\) of Yuan's \(\phi_0\), defined from that original sequence, and the signed boundary of the actual complex used by the Ext owner. Equivalently the additive torsor of local lifts has differences \(b_b-b_a\); the comparison does not require a statement about the derived direct image of the middle term.

For the cohomology-to-tangent identification use the **constant** scheme \(A_D=A\times_k\operatorname{Spec}D\), not the directional deformation \(X\). Glue trivial lines on these actual affines of \(A_D\) by \(g_{ab}=1+\epsilon f_{ab}\), with local generators \(s_b=g_{ab}s_a\). The equation on triples and the change of generators are the exact signed cocycle and coboundary equations, since \((1+\epsilon f)(1+\epsilon g)=1+\epsilon(f+g)\). Conversely a line with its reduction trivialized is locally of this form: on each affine lift the generator of its reduced module; since the ideal is nilpotent this generates the rank-one projective module (and is a basis, as seen at each localization). The ratios of lifted generators are unique units of the stated form. At the original zero, rigidifying absorbs a scalar from \(D^\times\); rescaling globally by this same constant shows this does not add or lose a class. Likewise fixing the trivialization of the reduction causes no extra classes. These lines lie in the relative \(\operatorname{Pic}^0\): their sole geometric reduced fibre is trivial. By the represented rigidified Picard functor this is exactly the kernel of \(A^\vee(D)\to A^\vee(k)\), hence the true \(\Lambda:H^1(A,\mathcal O_A)\simeq\operatorname{Lie}(A^\vee)\) without an additional sign. Dual-number dilations give \(k\)-linearity. The construction is natural for the actual original family and its base changes (its abelian dual also commutes with base change). In particular it identifies the fibre of \(R^1\pi_*\mathcal O_P\) used in Yuan §1 with this Lie space. Only the projection formula for the locally free base line is used. This comparison does not assert base change for the derived middle term.

## Ordered entries at 13 and 5

Let \(w_1,w_2\) be the true dual-family Hodge basis at this point; these are cotangent forms of \(A^\vee\), not the dual vector space to the chosen \(u\). Let \(P_\lambda\) be the matrix with \(j\)-th column the coefficients of \(\lambda^*w_j\) in \(u\). Define unpolarized \(C_i=(w_j(\Lambda b_i))_j\). If \(C\) has these columns, the ordered polarized direction on the actual pair is
\[
 C_{\rm pol}=P_\lambda^{-T}C .
\]
Indeed \(w_j(d\lambda z)=(\lambda^*w_j)(z)\), so \(P_\lambda^T (C_{\rm pol})_i=C_i\) when \(z=(d\lambda)^{-1}\Lambda b_i\). Thus this is the original connecting tensor evaluated on \(v\), **before** taking any determinant. A common change of the actual \(u\)-basis by \(U\) changes the ordered matrix by congruence \(U^T C_{\rm pol}U\); an auxiliary change of \(w\) by \(V\) cancels. The proof does not replace the original ordered determinant frame by an independent one.

For the following readout one may use Yuan §3's independently proved pre-determinant theorem; it is not used to prove the equality above. In the adapted frames of §3.2 on the same smooth height-one stalk (at 5, after its chosen unramified quadratic branch if necessary), the unpolarized matrix is the transpose of the generator of \(\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal O_B}(T',T)\), not an independently chosen element of the full residual commutant. Let \(r\) be the value of the chosen base cotangent generator under \(v\) times the original unit coefficient, reduced in the residue field. Then:

| place | adapted \(C\) | adapted \(C_{\rm pol}\) |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | \(r I_2\) | \(r P_\lambda^{-T}\) |
| 5, first orientation | \(\operatorname{diag}(r,0)\) | \(r P_\lambda^{-T}E_{11}\) |
| 5, reversed orientation | \(\operatorname{diag}(0,r)\) | \(r P_\lambda^{-T}E_{22}\) |

For \(v\ne0\), \(r\ne0\): the original model sends a primitive tangent generator to a primitive intertwiner. At 13 matching split action bases give the identity; at 5 the opposite integral order actions give \(\operatorname{diag}(1,5)\) or \(\operatorname{diag}(5,1)\) before reduction. Principal \(\lambda\) implies its basis matrix stays invertible in the residue field. Consequently the genuine pair of directional classes spans dimension 2 at 13 and 1 at 5. At 5 exactly one adapted column survives; in the fixed original basis at least one survives, but need not be the same individual column. We do not assume that reducing the Hom kernel computes its full residual commutant. The ramified determinant is zero: a determinant-only test would miss the surviving class. There is no division by 5 in \(k\).

Alternatively, *without* Yuan 3.1 the comparison of columns can be used in the already audited noncircular proof of the pre-determinant quotient. If \(b_1=b_2=0\), the genuine global-split theorem constructs the one right inverse. LocalAgreement then constructs the original pointed trivialization; PEL rigidity restores the original marks. Full-frame faithfulness identifies two distinct classifying maps, a contradiction. The preceding rank readout is not used in that argument. It only uses the exact comparison before invoking the ordered action equations.
