# The full Weber conductor without a Weil-pairing formalization

## Inputs and quantifiers

Fix one actual Gaussian modulus M divisible by 4 and the actual Weber
field W generated by the squared abscissae of E[M], over K=Q(i) with
E: y²=x³−x. In the original application the modulus is divisible by 16.
The following are the previously proved paper inputs, on the same
chosen embeddings and arithmetic Frobenius convention:

1. W/Q is finite Galois, contains K, and H=Gal(W/K) identifies with
   (Z[i]/M)×/μ₄. Absolute complex conjugation acts on H by Gaussian
   conjugation. The maximal abelian subfield W^ab has degree φ(2M).
2. Outside a fixed finite set S, for a rational split prime p and a
   prime of W over either chosen Gaussian factor π of norm p,
   the relative arithmetic Frobenius has exactly the class of π.
   For absolute Frobenius above this same prime the restriction to K
   is trivial, and its action equals the relative element (residue
   degree one). Thus this assertion makes no claim at inert primes.
3. Ordinary arithmetic Frobenius occurs in every specified conjugacy
   class of every fixed finite Galois number field over Q, avoiding any
   given finite set of primes. This is exactly qualitative Chebotarev,
   and is also stated in the already-needed analytic source:
   [Frei–Tinková v3, (1.10)–(1.12)](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.17441v3#S1.SS2),
   at q=1. There the weighted mean is |C|/[F:Q]>0, so the
   O(√X log²X) weight of proper prime powers and the O(1) weight of
   a fixed finite set of prime inputs cannot account for it. This
   application has no modulus or conductor to compute.

No quantitative uniformity over growing M is used in proving the field
identity. All fields, classes and exception sets in each class-occurrence
use are fixed before its unbounded scale parameter.

## Full norm mod 2M, including the sign

Put O=Z[i]. For any α, β in O, writing a=Re α, b=Im α,
x=Re β and y=Im β gives

    N(α+Mβ)−N(α)
      = 2M (a x+b y+(M/2)(x²+y²)).

Since M is even, this is an integer multiple of 2M. Norm is unchanged
by conjugation and by multiplying by a global unit. Thus the norm
residue modulo 2M makes sense on every relative class, and on
its full conjugation orbit. If the coordinate class is a unit mod M,
the norm is coprime to M (its product with the norm of an inverse
representative is 1 modulo M); because M is even, it is coprime to
2M as well. In particular if π is a norm-p generator whose relative
class is the identity, π≡ε mod M for one genuine ε∈μ₄, so
p≡1 mod 2M. This includes both signs of the selected Gaussian factor
and does not choose a new field or degree convention.

## Field inclusion and exact minimal conductor

Set C=Q(ζ_(2M)), inside the same algebraic closure, and F=WC. If C⊄W,
there is g∈Gal(F/W) nontrivial on C: a nonidentity automorphism
of F fixing W and C would fix their compositum, and if all
automorphisms fixing W fixed C then Galois fixed fields give C⊂W.
Choose an unramified rational prime p outside S∪{p:p|2M} with
absolute arithmetic Frobenius conjugate to g in Gal(F/Q), using (3).
Restriction to W is trivial for every conjugate, since W/Q is normal;
in particular p splits in K. Its relative class in W/K is the identity,
so input (2) and the norm identity give p≡1 mod 2M. On C, however,
restriction of every conjugate is g|C because C/Q is abelian.
Cyclotomic arithmetic Frobenius sends ζ_(2M) to ζ_(2M)^p, so this
nonidentity restriction implies p≠1 mod 2M, a contradiction.

Consequently C⊂W^ab, and equality follows from degree φ(2M) on both
sides. The minimal rational cyclotomic conductor of this field is
indeed 2M, not merely a divisor. If Q(ζ_(2M))⊂Q(ζ_f), take
L=lcm(2M,f); the compositum is Q(ζ_L), so φ(L)=φ(f). On a multiple of
f, equal totients allow only L=f or L=2f with f odd (Euler's local
formula: adding any odd prime, or increasing an existing prime's
exponent, strictly increases φ). Here 4|L, so the latter is impossible.
Thus 2M|f.

## The exact norm character for the FT coefficient

For any relative g∈H, use (3) in **W** with the absolute conjugacy
class of g, outside the same S and primes dividing 2M. All its
elements restrict trivially to K, so this p is split in K. By (2)
its chosen relative class is g or its Gaussian conjugate. The cyclotomic
restriction of these two elements is the same since C/Q is abelian.
It is arithmetic Frobenius of p on C, hence exponent p mod 2M.
If γ represents the actual class g, the previous norm congruence
shows p≡N(γ) mod 2M, also when the chosen prime represents its
conjugate. This proves the exact exponent required for the local
factor η in (1.10), for all relative tags, without a Weil pairing.

The remaining simultaneous lower is the genuinely stronger
Frei–Tinková theorem, with its full fixed conductor now computed.
No assumption about actual rational points or the independent
2-converse/rank input is made here. This note and the complete
field-identification argument are paper results, not Lean results.
The two attached source lemmas implement only the even-norm step.
