# Fixed 177 total variables, N=15: a two-weight quartic
Under the **same explicit arithmetic inputs** as the included fixed-178 predecessor, the rational projection of this specific integer quartic onto x1 is Z. It quantifies **176 rational witnesses** in addition to x1. This is not an unconditional theorem or a claim that 177 is minimal.

Read PROOF.md for both directions and WEIGHTS.md for the new effective height proof. INDEPENDENT-177-REVIEW.md treats the forward supplier and arithmetic startup independently. The change removes the stored scalar square, without removing either component of an actual elliptic abscissa. It replaces the scalar map by a six-factor positive rational map; its multiplier is **557·149^37 C²**, C=149^15. The two effective H4.1 uses are on the original five-contact family. The cost of clearing both moving positive weights is included. At a generic odd scalar prime we get only a square divisor of the index, not a cube; the two height applications together pay for this loss. Three genuine detector comparisons and their signed inputs are kept.
