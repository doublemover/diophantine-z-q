# Version 11 retained-class binding argument

Authored by /root/restart_geometric_model. This is the contribution's proposed connecting argument, subject to separate review. It uses the existing Version 11 construction, not a new Euler system or a proof of the whole restricted converse.

Fix the actual curve E_l required by the restricted converse, with positive squarefree l congruent to11 modulo12. Fix its conductor N and the original modular form, lattice and symbol combination. For one constituent character chi of primitive conductor D=2^a d, retain the same smoothing integers c,d_s. By K5 and d dividing F, c*d_s is prime to2Nd. Put N_K=2Nd and N'_K=c*d_s.

The original z_(Dm) construction is permitted for every positive m prime to c*d_s: its construction support is prime(2NDm), disjoint from the smoother primes. Tensoring with e_chi is defined over Q(zeta_Dm), where chi is trivial. Integral corestriction gives y_m over Q(zeta_m), and K7 supplies its integral j_* condition even if the extension is ramified. Restriction to the fixed open with N_K inverted yields the full cohomology used by Kato1999. No instruction to invert every prime of the varying construction support is introduced.

Every required 1999 index m satisfies N_K dividing m and gcd(m,N'_K)=1, so the extended definition supplies it. For ell not dividing N'_K there are exactly two cases. If ell divides m, adding ell changes no support prime, so Proposition8.12 has empty new-support product and the norm equals y_m. This includes bad, twisting and dyadic primes. If ell does not divide m, then ell does not divide2Nd; the only new support prime is ell. Tensor/corestriction equivariance converts its coefficient to a_ell chi(ell), and the exact norm factor is1−a_ell chi(ell)ell^−1 sigma_ell^−1+ell^−1 sigma_ell^−2, namely P_ell(ell^−1 sigma_ell^−1) for arithmetic Frobenius on T tensor chi. These are the two required cases without extra bad-prime factors.

For the limiting class, choose the divisor parameter1 from §0.4 in the §0.7 norm construction. At every n≥1, the original construction supports for z_(DN_K2^n) and z_(D2^n) are both prime(2Nd). The original norm relation therefore gives norm(z_(DN_K2^n))=z_(D2^n). In the common upper field Q(zeta_DN_K2^n), compose corestrictions through Q(zeta_N_K2^n) or through Q(zeta_D2^n), then down to Q(zeta_2^n). Transitivity makes both composites the same. Tensoring by e_chi commutes with the norm between the two D-containing fields. Consequently norm(y_(N_K2^n))=y_(2^n).

No linear-disjointness assertion is needed. No field-degree multiplier appears, because the upper class is being corestricted, rather than first restricting a lower class and then corestricting it. No Euler factor appears, because the original construction supports are equal. These identities commute with tower transition maps, and n≥1 is cofinal.

If the original image lattice needs the fixed map2^c_E into T, apply that same map at every level. Thus the §0.7 class xi_K is the full-cohomology image of the actual retained dyadic class after this fixed map. At every height-one localization used by K15,2 is invertible; multiplication by2^c_E changes neither its coordinate ideal nor the index length. The supplied ideal is therefore exactly J of the retained class, not the ideal of an arbitrary rank-one generator.

K15 still uses the separately retained nonvanishing, rational representation, finite-generation and local-complex hypotheses. The changed class identity is established before using the index bound, so it is not inferred circularly from that bound. The unchanged full-open/j_* comparison transfers the H2_0 bound in the convention required downstream. This does not produce the separate integral positive-complex assertion at primes containing2 or discharge the remaining central-calibration, coefficient/limit, restricted-converse and actual witness-assembly obligations.

The downstream source statements and finite polynomial equivalences must be bound separately to the exact Version11 consumers. Their verified unchanged passage identities permit this repaired input to be adopted without changing the polynomial definitions; they do not turn this local repair into full-proof acceptance.
