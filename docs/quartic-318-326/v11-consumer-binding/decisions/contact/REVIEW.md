1. The contract does not infer their full revalidation or the stronger integral result from the index repair. Its primary-source scope agrees with the separate source review.

The object is not replaced by an abstract generator of an isomorphic rank-one module. The full-cohomology image and fixed lattice map are named.

3. Domain coverage: K8 is defined for all positive m coprime to c*d_s. With N_K=2Nd and N'_K=c*d_s, every 1999-required index is supplied. The smaller 2004 Xi-family is explicitly a different subfamily. The final RC quantifier is pointwise for every admissible l; support/G uniformity is for each fixed curve, not uniformly over l.

4. Maps and factors: the repeated-prime and new-prime cases exhaust the required transitions. A repeated bad/twisting/dyadic prime adds no support and hence no factor. A genuinely new prime is good and unramified and contributes the stated twisted polynomial with arithmetic Frobenius and inverse deck action. The binding argument retains the directions and the full primitive conductor.

5. Consumed-object identity: the common-upper-field corestriction comparison gives norm(y_(N_K2^n))=y_(2^n), not norm of a restriction. Equal supports remove extra Euler factors, and transitivity introduces no field-degree multiplier. The chosen source divisor parameter is 1. The full-cohomology image after the fixed lattice map is identified before invoking K15, so the equality is not inferred circularly from the index bound.

6. Coefficients and normalization: the fixed 2^c_E map is uniform in the levels and is treated as a unit only at the away-from-two localizations in question. Only the localized coordinate ideal and index are identified. The text does not silently drop smoothers, divide by a growing group order, infer integral equality at 2, or substitute the CM integer-label curve for E_l.

The j_* condition under ramified corestriction and later full-cohomology restriction remain the stated route. H2_0 is not identified with all H2 without the retained cone/local-factor argument. 8. Exceptional cases: repeated bad/conductor/dyadic primes, cofinal n>=1, full dyadic conductor and reused d/xi symbols have explicit treatment. No linear-disjointness assumption or implicit proportionality normalization is added. At the final source interface the zero-conic branch and actual finite normalization are identified through existing results, while simultaneous denominators, unit conditions, guards and point choices are not assumed solved by the Kato repair.

9. Downstream consumption: the two proposed documents now agree on the active V11 baseline while preserving V10 as historical evidence.
