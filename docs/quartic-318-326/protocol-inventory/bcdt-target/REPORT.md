## Primary quotient construction, with the actual target

G. Shimura, *On the factors of the jacobian variety of a modular function field*, JMSJ25(3) (1973),523–544, Theorem1 pp526–527, DOI https://doi.org/10.2969/jmsj/02530523, was read from the original published scan. The group convention on p523 allows Gamma=Gamma0(N). For a normalized rational weight-two eigenform f, Theorem1 gives a Q-defined quotient q_f:J0(N)->A_f, dimension [K_f:Q]=1, and q_f T_n=theta(a_n)q_f. Its Euler-factor assertion explicitly permits Gamma0(N), so no unverified general-nebentypus Fricke-stability argument is needed here.

For the form attached to E, the conductor-level modular realization is the one whose L-series equals L(E,s). Its normalized coefficients are the integral elliptic L-series coefficients. Its character is trivial: det(V_l E) is cyclotomic, whereas the weight-two form has determinant epsilon times cyclotomic. The conductor-level form is new, since an old constituent of smaller level would have smaller representation conductor. This is the ordinary rational elliptic realization, not the arbitrary-coefficient surface realization used elsewhere. Published Agashe–Ribet–Stein, *The Manin Constant*, PAMQ2(2) (2006),617–636, pp617–618, explicitly records both the actual-E quotient and this normalized Gamma0(N) newform/quotient construction; its final published text was read from the author-hosted indexed PDF. Its direct download returned403; no byte pin is claimed for it.

The good Euler factors of A_f and E coincide by Shimura's assertion and the same L-series. Faltings, *Endlichkeitssatze fur abelsche Varietaten uber Zahlkorpern*, Invent.Math.73 (1983),349–366, Section5, Corollary2 p361, then gives a Q-isogeny alpha:A_f->E, using equality at almost every prime. The original page and its Corollary1 Hom comparison were read visually. The published1984 erratum, Invent.Math.75,p381, was read in full: it replaces the height-sequence assertion by eventual stationarity, explicitly sufficient for the subsequent proof. It does not withdraw the Hom/isogeny corollaries. Katz's observation and Gabber's counterexample concern that source correction, not this project.

Define phi=alpha q_f and pi(x)=phi([x]-[infinity]). Both maps are overQ and pi(infinity)=0. The Abel–Jacobi image generates J0(N), so pi cannot be constant while phi is a nonzero quotient. Hence pi is the required parametrization to the **same E**, not just an unspecified member of its isogeny class. For q not dividing N, phi T_q=[a_q(f)]phi. The sign convention in Agashe–Ribet–Stein uses [infinity]-[x]; replacing it by the manuscript's opposite sign merely negates the target map and preserves these properties.

Only an isogeny is asserted between A_f and E. Integral Tate lattices need not coincide. In V13 the actual T2(E), pi, and the resulting fixed finite lattice indices are chosen before ring-class conductors and character families vary. This review supplies no uniformity as E varies, and no assertion that a Manin constant equals1, no modular degree bound, and no extra prime-to2 property of alpha. The actual point-class and pole maps must still be performed on E as the downstream record does.
