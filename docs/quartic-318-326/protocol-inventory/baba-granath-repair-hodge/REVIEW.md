The rational homology is four-dimensional over Q2 and a unital module over the four-dimensional division algebra B6,2, so it is free of rank one over that division algebra. A full O6,2-stable lattice L is principal: choose x of least division valuation, giving yx⁻¹ in O6,2 for every y in L, hence L=O6,2x. Its right multiplier ring is a maximal order. The valuation maximal order is unique and invariant under inner conjugation.
