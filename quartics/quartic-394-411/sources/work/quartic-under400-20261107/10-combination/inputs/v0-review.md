## Source and consumer contract

The following nine obligations are accepted for this change. The exact source pins and label-presence checks are in `SOURCE-PINS.json`. No new external theorem is applied; the inherited arithmetic qualifications remain in force.

1. **Carrier and conventions.** All equations are over K=Q(rho), rho^2=2, represented by both rational components. Put D=(3-rho)^2, a=-1+7rho/4, b=39/8-9rho/2, c=a^2. The fixed point is P=(c,ab), with b^2=c^2-D. These are exactly the conventions of the frozen v12 addition, subsection `subsec:v12-inputs`. The semantic coordinate dictionary comes from pinned `depth519.json`, SHA256 `4466bd973383ba404b1cc50deaf2aa4f77361850c090dc8af80b7ba77249d57c`.

2. **Incoming domain.** The retained H chart has x=h^2, j^2=h^4-D, ordinate hj. Proposition `v9chart:prop:chart` and its supporting lemma `v9chart:lem:squares` in v11 identify this with the old double chart. Their actual proof was read, including the valuation-one exception at (3-rho) and the nonsquare -4 modulo 7. Thus H is in 2E(K), not merely an arbitrary point with square abscissa. Its h,j,x,ordinate are nonzero. The retained UA and A charts likewise supply doubled points. The retained V1 difference guard and Lemma `lem:v5-point-domains` make V1 a finite nonidentity doubled point. These rows are unchanged in the submitted candidate.

3. **Denominators.** Set d=cx+D and N=aj-bh. In either real embedding d=(ah)^2+(3-rho)^2>0, so d is nonzero. Also a is nonzero. The new equation dg=N uniquely defines g=N/d. Set T=-2Dh-abj+2Db*g and k=T/(ad). These divisions are valid without new inverse witnesses.

4. **Algebraic transition.** In K[h,j]/(j^2-h^4+D), expansion gives k=M/d^2 for M=-2aDh(h^2+c)-bj(ch^2-D), and k^2+Dg^4=1. The checked identities identify x(V0)=1/g^2 and y(V0)=k/g^3 with the ordinary subtraction law for H-P when h^2 differs from c. In particular z(V0)=-g/k. These identities keep the ordinate sign, not just the abscissa. The new f equation gives exactly f(V0)=g^2.

5. **The g=0 exception.** If g=0, then N=0 and k^2=1. Hence T=adk is nonzero. The new equation T*ell0+a*N*G0=0 forces ell0=0. The unchanged V1 ratio is ell0*LP1=z(V1)*G2(w), with w=D*f(V1)^2 and G2(w)=1-2w/5-2w^2/15. A nonidentity doubled point has square positive abscissa and nonzero ordinate by `lem:v5-point-domains`; its curve equation yields x(V1)>sqrt(D) in both real embeddings. Therefore 0<w<1 and G2(w)>7/15>0. Since z(V1) is nonzero, the retained ratio contradicts ell0=0. No division by LP1, or nonzero premise for LP1, is used.

6. **The k=0 and equal-abscissa exceptions.** Independently, k cannot vanish: the quartic identity would give D*g^4=1. With delta=3-rho, both real embeddings of delta are positive, so delta*g^2=1. Norms would give 7*Norm(g)^2=1, impossible for a rational Norm(g). Thus T is nonzero. If h^2=c, the field property gives h=+a or -a and j=+b or -b. The two sign choices yielding H=P have g=0 and have just been excluded. The other two yield H=-P. Direct exact doubling at -P gives x=1/g^2 and y=k/g^3 for both choices; `branch_check.py` checks all four cases independently of the generic identities. Hence the extension at H=-P is the actual point -2P, not a spurious branch. Since P is a nonzero element of the inherited infinite cyclic group 2E(K), -2P is nonidentity.

7. **Output consumed downstream.** As g,k are nonzero, the new logarithm row yields ell0=-(a*N/T)*G0=-(g/k)*G0=z(H-P)*G0. The retained polynomial G0 is evaluated at the retained f(V0), so its argument and value are unchanged. The only old private-coordinate consumers are the seven deleted rows and the four replaced f/ell component rows. This was checked from every source residual. Thus all downstream uses see the actual f and truncated logarithm of H-P. The integer index of this point is h_index-1, including the new case h_index=-1. The local formal-group and height arguments depend on this actual point identity and nonzero domain, not on unequal abscissae of H and P.

The old difference guard guarantees the generic subtraction branch, so f and ell0 retain exactly their old values and satisfy the replacement equations. Both g components are rational. All other residuals are unchanged. This is an extension of the entire simultaneous assignment; no prime, root, pole witness, or anchor needs to be reselected. The reverse direction is an arithmetic interface argument, since H=-P can now occur; there is no claim of a bijection with old private slope witnesses.
