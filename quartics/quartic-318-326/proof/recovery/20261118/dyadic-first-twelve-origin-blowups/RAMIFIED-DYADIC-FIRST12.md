# The actual first singular-point blowup for twelve all-odd mixed signs

Fix q=(d₀,d₁,d₂,a)∈{±1}⁴ with the first three not all equal. In each of these twelve dyadic starting models there is just one nonregular point P, the F₂-point over T=0. Its **ordinary reduced-point blowup is itself normal**. For a=+1 its exceptional fibre has exactly one remaining nonregular F₂-point, embedding dimension 4. For a=−1 it has exactly two remaining nonregular geometric points, each of embedding dimension 3 when the equal-sign pair involves root 0 and 4 when the equal-sign pair is roots 1 and 2. In the sign representatives the latter two points are F₂-rational. This is one *actual* normalized singular-point iteration, not the blowup of the parameter ideal (2,T). The successor points are not yet resolved in this note.

Pick indices j,k with the repeated sign d (j<k), and m the remaining index among 0,1,2, whose sign is −d. Write b=(0,32,76832,−343), u=r_j, h=(r_k−u)/2,
    c=343+b_j,   g=(1−d)/2,   H=d(b_j−b_k)/4,   M=d(b_m−b_j)/2 .
Then T=b_j+d u²; h²+uh=H, and (r_m−u)²+2u(r_m−u)=2(M−u²). The dyadic orders of H and M are respectively ≥3 and ≥4. All chosen coordinates are in the literal branch-independent field. Below, localizing at units at P is sufficient; the blowup is the identity away from P. The exact normalized charts away from P have separately been identified.

## a=+1: the minority half-root must use a different uniformizing ratio

Set w=1+r₃, τ=w−u, C=(c−1)/2, D=w+C−uτ−g u². From r₃²=d u²+c obtain
    2D=τ² .
D is a unit at the original point (C odd). Invert it on a neighbourhood and put l=(r_m−u)/τ. The true local normalized algebra is
    τ²=2D,   h²+uh=H,   D l²+τu l=M−u²,
where w=τ+u. It recovers r_k=u+2h, r_m=u+τl and r₃=w−1. The base of the last two monics is the true first-root pair ring in u,τ, localized at D, with the first relation monic in τ before localization. Generic rank matches by the branch valuations; the successive monics, localized at D, yield an embedded complete intersection algebra of dimension two. Characteristic zero is smooth by the original simple branches: the scaling by τ above is invertible as soon as 2D is a unit. At the generic vertical prime τ=0, the base residue is F₂(u), D=u+1+g u². The h residues 0,u are distinct. The l-equation reduces to l²=u²/(u+1+g u²), not a square in F₂(u), since the denominator has derivative 1. Thus (τ) localized on each vertical component is the maximal ideal of the corresponding one-dimensional local ring: the quotient is a field, including the purely inseparable l residue, not a doubled root. These generic vertical primes exhaust the possible vertical height-one points by finite monicity. R₁/S₂ gives the actual normalization around P; equivalently this is finite normal over the localized raw order in the same field (the recovered raw generators are in it). The unique residue point at u=0 has h=l=τ=0 and its maximal ideal is (u,τ,h,l); 2=τ²/D gives no additional generator.

Blow up that exact reduced ideal. The homogeneous u and τ directions cover: with them zero the h and l degree-one generators must vanish by the monic equations (H,M multiples of τ⁶ and τ⁸ respectively here). More algebraically J=(u,τ) gives m³=Jm² since the monic relations put each of h²,l² in Jm; every degree-three monomial involving only h,l contains one of these squares. This directly justifies the available two directions without substituting a different base blowup.

On the τ chart u=τS, h=τA, l=τB use D=C+τ+τS−τ²S−gτ²S². The equations are
    τ²=2D,   A²+SA=H/τ²,   D B²+τ S B=M/τ²−S² .
Writing H=2^h₀H₀, M=2^m₀M₀ with odd H₀,M₀, replace H/τ² by H₀ τ^(2h₀−2)/D^h₀ and M/τ² similarly. These are genuine ordinary blowup ratios. Both A,B adjunctions are monic over the τ-chart of the regular two-dimensional local first-root pair: there u,τ are regular parameters (τ-nonzerodivisor and base modulo τ a smooth u-line). Its chart has regular exceptional line and nonzerodivisor τ; the base equations after substitution are exact by the same scale cancellation or direct first-equation regular sequence. Hence the displayed monic ring has no scale torsion and inverting τ restores the old localized ring. On the exceptional divisor B=S mod its points. Put V=B−S; the third equation becomes
    D V²+(2D+τ)S V+(D+τ+1)S²−M/τ²=0 .
At τ=0 and S≠0, (D+τ+1)/τ reduces to S: C+1 is even hence belongs to (τ²), and 2τ belongs to (τ³). The M term is divisible by τ⁶. Thus the V-equation in the regular base chart with V adjoined has a nonzero linear part equal to τ times the nonzero residue S³. The A extension is étale because S≠0. Hence every such direction is regular, including non-rational points. At S=0 only A=V=0 occurs. In the regular ambient with parameters τ,S,A,V both equations have no linear term, whereas the dimension of the quotient is two, so this actual residual point has embedding dimension four.

On the u chart τ=uR,h=uA,l=uB, D=C+uR+u−u²R−g u². Here 2=u²R²/D and
    A²+A=H/u²,   D B²+uR B=M/u²−1.
The right constants are H₀u^(2h₀−2)R^(2h₀)/D^h₀ and similarly for M, proving the same monic saturation. Modulo u the A extension is étale and B=1 at every exceptional prime. Substituting B=1+V gives constant term D+uR+1−M/u² whose u-linear coefficient is 1 mod the divisor: C+1∈(u²) and 2uR∈(u³). Hence this entire side is regular. The two charts already equal the ordinary blowup, are S₂ hypersurface complete intersections over the regular base charts, normal away from the exceptional divisor where the old ring was normal, and regular in codimension one on it. By Serre's criterion the blown-up surface is normal; no further normalization changes the count of successors.

## a=−1: the extra integral product supplies the missing scale chart

Adjoin α²=−c through (1+α)/2, a quadratic étale extension near 2, with two residue roots over F₂ because −c≡1 modulo 8. If the constant is already a rational square use the split étale cover or choose its square root. At either dyadic localization the coefficient ring has uniformizer 2 and residue F₂. Put t=(1+d)/2, v=r_m−u, z=r₃−u−α, and
    A₀=M−u²−uv,      B₀=−t u²−αu−(u+α)z,     l=vz/2.
Then v²=2A₀, z²=2B₀, vz=2l, vl=A₀z, zl=B₀v and l²=A₀B₀. The algebra generated by these elements over the coefficient polynomial line with h²+uh=H is free on 1,v,z,l. Indeed their products reduce by these displayed identities (replace every vz in the RHS by 2l), and generic independence follows from the four independent root classes after any constant extension, now isolating u and h first. Thus it is finite free over the u-line and Cohen–Macaulay of dimension two (finite flat over a regular equidimensional base; local systems of base parameters are regular sequences). It contains all raw roots, and is integral with the same field. Away from 2 its coordinates give the smooth original model. At the generic vertical point u a residue unit, h splits étale, A₀ a unit (v is nil in the fibre), and v²=2A₀, z=vl/A₀. The quotient at v=0 has l²=u²(t u²+u), nonsquare in F₂(u) since its derivative is u². This gives a field at each generic h label, and the local vertical maximal ideal is generated by v. This proves R₁ and identifies the actual normalization, not the raw algebra missing l. At P the maximal ideal is (2,u,h,v,z,l).

The ordinary blowup is covered by the 2,u Rees directions. If their homogeneous generators vanish, h², v², z² force the corresponding h,v,z directions to vanish: H belongs to (2)³, M to (2)⁴, and all other terms have the 2/u factors displayed. For l²=A₀B₀ use A₀∈m² and B₀∈m, so l²∈m³. Hence l's leading generator vanishes as well (at primes above the closed center, m vanishes in the homogeneous residue). Equivalently away from the center the blowup is the identity, and the relevant exceptional fibre is Proj of the associated graded at m, where degree two of this product vanishes. For explicit chart coverage away from that fibre it suffices to shrink locally at P as usual; outside P the underlying modification is already known.

On the 2 chart write u=2S,h=2A,v=2V,z=2Z,l=2L. The product relation gives L=VZ, and the exact presentation is
    A²+SA=H/4,  V²+2SV=M/2−2S²,  Z²+(2S+α)Z=−2t S²−αS .
All three are independent monics over D'[S], hence 2 a nonzerodivisor; after inverting 2 they are precisely the old localization and generate the genuine ratio chart. The Z extension is étale over the whole exceptional fibre. At S≠0 the V-relation has linear term 2 times a unit where V=0 (M/2∈(2)³), and the A-extension is étale; all such directions regular. At S=0 we have A=V=0 and Z=0 or 1. The V-equation has no linear part in the regular ambient on 2,S,A,V. The A-equation has linear part a nonzero multiple of 2 if v₂(H)=3, and none if v₂(H)>3. Therefore the two residual points have embedding dimension 3 in the former case and 4 in the latter. For pairs (0,1) or (0,2), v₂(H)=3; for (1,2), v₂(H)=8. These are nonregular local rings of dimension two, not inferred merely from the special fibre.

On the u chart 2=uQ, h=uA, v=uV, z=uZ, l=uL. Only Q=0 directions are new; the others already occur on the 2 chart. The genuine Z-base equation is
    Z²+Q D₁=0,   D₁=(u+α)Z+t u+α .
At u=Q=0 there is just Z=0 so D₁ is a unit. This Z-base ring is monic over D'[u,Q]/(uQ−2) and regular at the relevant origin with parameters u,Z: modulo u it is the smooth curve Q=−Z²/D₁, hence regular, with u a nonzerodivisor. Now l=vz/2 implies QL=VZ in the fraction field. Using Q=−Z²/D₁, define B=−L/D₁; then V=ZB (a fraction-field identity also valid integrally). The remaining equations are
    A²+A=H/u²,     D₁ B²−uZB=u−M/u .
H/u² and M/u are regular on the chart: use 2=uQ, v₂(H)≥3, v₂(M)≥4. These equations follow directly by dividing v²+2uv=2(M−u²) by u², then V=ZB and Z²=−Q D₁. Conversely they recover all actual ratio generators V,Z,L,A,Q. They are monic over the indicated localized Z-base up to the unit D₁, so the scale u remains a nonzerodivisor; its inversion restores the original ring localized at u and D₁ (then Q=2/u, the formula B=−L/D₁ is defined even when Z is not inverted, and the old product relations follow by substitution and domain/saturation). For the last identification one may first invert 2uZ, where all substitutions are literal inverses: 2uZ is a nonzerodivisor on the new presentation because the Z-base is a domain in its generic first-root/r₃ field, and the two monics are free over it. It follows that the new presentation injects into the intended common field and equals its generated ratio subring. This removes the risk of dividing by a vanishing direction. At the corner B=0, and the B-equation has nonzero linear part −u in the regular ambient on u,Z,B; the A extension is étale. Thus all new directions are regular. On the two charts the ordinary blowup is normal by S₂ (complete intersections) and R₁: any possibly bad exceptional prime is one of the two closed 2-chart origins, and off the center the starting model was normal.

These two residual labels over the sign representative are rational: the auxiliary coefficient extension at 2 is split on the special fibre (each selected coefficient point has residue F₂), and the original model's fibre base-changed to that coefficient point is the original F₂-fibre. The coordinate labels need not be individually fixed by the automorphism swapping the auxiliary coefficient sheets. The actual blowup of the reduced origin commutes with this étale comparison. For arbitrary rational twists whose four ratios to the representatives are dyadically unramified, the point P and this ordinary blowup descend, and the stated residual *geometric* points, local regularities and embedding dimensions are unchanged. In the two-point case rationality can change under the unramified twist. Away from P previous regularity is untouched. There is no assertion of a complete resolution at the one or two successor origins, no minimality or main Diophantine result, and no Lean acceptance.
