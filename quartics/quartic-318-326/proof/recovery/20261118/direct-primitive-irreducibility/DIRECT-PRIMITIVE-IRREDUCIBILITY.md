# A direct irreducibility proof for the genuine primitive x-polynomial

**Statement.** For N>1 let k=Q(J), where J is transcendental, and let E/k be the actual short cubic y²=x³−3J₀x−2J₀ with J₀=J/(J−1728). Take the unique monic polynomial Q_N∈k[X] obtained by taking the product once over each distinct x-coordinate of an exact-order-N point in a geometric generic fibre. Then Q_N is irreducible over k. This argument is a direct proof of the arithmetic primitive-equation input, not an invocation of the irreducibility statement of van Hoeij and Smith. It does not dispense with the separate modular interpretation when speaking of a named modular curve rather than this polynomial.

1. **Independent algebraic and analytic inputs.** The simultaneous marking-field construction and the uniform transcendental specialization proof give the following precise fact: at every period τ in the upper half-plane with j(τ) transcendental over Q, evaluate Q_N via J↦j(τ). Its simple complex roots are exactly the primitive x-values on the displayed curve over C. Here a fixed finite Galois field realizing the marks at a reference transcendental period embeds afresh into C for the new period; injectivity and the finite cardinality of the primitive period labels give all marks. No argument propagates a polynomial relation from a single numerical zero. The analytic inputs are the true modular j and Eisenstein forms, the ordinary degree-N period lattice marking z=1/N, the Weierstrass uniformization and its SL₂(Z) transformation rule. These are about the same arithmetic curve by the short scaling below.

2. **The one meromorphic branch actually needed.** Put Λτ=Zτ+Z and
   A_N(τ)=12(E₄(τ)/E₆(τ))·℘(1/N;Λτ)/(2π)².
   The function ℘(1/N;Λτ) is holomorphic on the whole upper half-plane: 1/N is never a lattice point there, and the differences (1/N−λ)⁻²−λ⁻² are normally summable locally, using a constant times ∑_{λ≠0}|λ|⁻³ for the tail. Thus A_N is a meromorphic function on the *connected* upper half-plane. This also follows from the general-level standard-period sources. There is no claim that E₆ is invertible everywhere. At j not in {0,1728} both forms and J₀ are nonzero. The normalized period model has A′=−E₄/48, B′=−E₆/864 and point x′=℘/(2π)². If x′=u²x, y′=u³y takes the arithmetic model to that normalized model, then u⁴=E₄/(144J₀) and u⁶=E₆/(1728J₀), so u⁻²=12E₄/E₆. The two admissible scalings differing by sign have exactly the same x. A_N is therefore the true arithmetic x-coordinate of the primitive standard marking, not a numerical surrogate.

3. **The uniform identity on the half-plane.** View k inside M(H), the field of meromorphic functions on the connected upper half-plane, by J↦j; this is an embedding since j is nonconstant. The upper half-plane points with transcendental j form a dense set: j is nonconstant holomorphic and hence takes any nonempty open set to a nonempty open set, which cannot be contained in the countable set of numbers algebraic over Q. At each such point all denominators of the rational coefficients of Q_N are nonzero, and A_N is one of its primitive roots by step 1. Hence Q_N(j,A_N)=0 in M(H), by the identity theorem. Neither E₆-zeros nor rational coefficient poles on the removed locus obstruct the meromorphic identity.

4. **Transitivity and comparison at an actual point.** Fix the reference transcendental τ₀ and λ=j(τ₀). The normalized x-values A_N(γτ₀), as γ ranges over SL₂(Z), run through ALL primitive x-values on Eλ. Indeed, if γ=(a b;c d), then Λ_{γτ₀}=(cτ₀+d)⁻¹Λ_{τ₀} and
   ℘(1/N;Λ_{γτ₀})=(cτ₀+d)²℘((cτ₀+d)/N;Λ_{τ₀}).
   E₄/E₆ has weight −2, so no multiplier remains. The rows (c,d) modulo N cover all primitive residues: any primitive residue pair has a coprime integral lift, extendible by Bezout to the bottom row of a determinant-one integral matrix. Equivalently use the proved inverse-transpose transport of the standard label with γ inverted. Taking x identifies ± and does not lose any geometric x-values, including N=2. j(γτ₀)=λ, so all evaluations in the following step use precisely the same field embedding k→C.

5. **Conclude irreducibility.** If Q_N=f g in k[X], the identity in the meromorphic field implies either f(j,A_N) or g(j,A_N) vanishes identically. Suppose it is f. At every γτ₀ the rational coefficients of f are finite and A_N has no pole. Thus f(λ,X) vanishes at *all* roots of Q_N(λ,X). That polynomial is separable, so it divides f(λ,X). The injective field embedding J↦λ preserves the nonzero leading coefficient and the degrees of f, g and Q_N. It follows that deg f≥deg Q_N, so g has degree zero and is a unit. The other case is symmetric. A primitive mark exists, so the monic Q_N is nonconstant. This proves the assertion even without knowing a closed formula for its degree.

**Scope.** This closes the primitive-polynomial irreducibility at the mathematical level using direct stated objects; its analytic identity/SL₂ action has not yet been entirely replayed or packaged in Lean. The known external source using the identical coefficient normalization remains a separate check and supplies the named X₁(N) interpretation: [van Hoeij–Smith, §3 eq. (22)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.13644). N=1 remains the j-line. This proves no vertical integral moduli identification or full Arakelov estimate.
