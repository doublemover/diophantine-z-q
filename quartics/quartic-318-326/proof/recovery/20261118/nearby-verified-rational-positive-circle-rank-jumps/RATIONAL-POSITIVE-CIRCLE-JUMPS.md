# Marked positive-circle rank jumps, dense when the prepared scale is allowed to vary; literal ranks four and two at one small fixed pole

Fix the same ray-compatible first class β (in particular β is a sum of two squares). At any eligible prepared pole b* of this numerator class, and any finite collection of local neighborhoods, there are infinitely many OTHER prepared poles b in those neighborhoods, with distinct complementary squareclasses, at which a positive-circle matching Jacobian has rational rank at least TWO and a rational point on the original matched cover. In particular the marked δ AND ε both occur in the rational Kummer image, and infinitely many distinct original marked twist pairs occur at each constructed b. This is a genuine jump from generic geometric rank one on its positive circle. The previously derived original quartic bit for each newly constructed b is necessarily +. No density statement within a globally fixed θ is being made, and this does not solve the matching problem at the original specified b*.

## Rationality of the total constant-coefficient torsor in two variables

Let K=Q(√β), q₁(t)=1−9βt², q₂(t,k)=(1+βkt)²−9β(t+k)². On the positive circle C=θ(1−βk²) is β times a square; thus the full marked cover, with its two variables, has model
    S: Y²=β q₁(t)q₂(t,k).
Despite the generic lack of a section over k, this surface is rational over Q. In fact
    q₂/q₁=Norm(1+kA(t)),    A(t)=(βt+3√β)/(1+3t√β).
On the dense open with q₁Y≠0, set V=Y/q₁, ξ=(β/V)(1+kA); then Norm ξ=β. The fixed conic Norm ξ=β has a rational point since β is the norm of √β times an element of norm −1, the latter existing precisely by the sum-of-two-squares condition. It is rational. Conversely, with independent t and ξ on this conic, solve
    1+kA(t)=(V/β)ξ
by the two rational scalar equations in the basis (1,√β). They determine k,V uniquely on the open where A,ξ are linearly independent. A has nonzero √β coefficient at general t (it is 3(1−βt²)/(1−9βt²)), so this open is dense and the rational inverse agrees with the original. Thus the surface is birational to P¹ times a rational conic; no generic section over k follows from this rationality. Rational weak approximation applies on smooth local points off these finite birational exceptions by choosing local representatives in the common dense open; a dense Zariski open is locally dense at smooth local points of an integral variety in characteristic zero.

## Simultaneous local matching and infinitely many new complements

At b*, write D*=1−βr*²=θ*z*². Choose a positive-circle slope k* such that H_(k*,θ*) has points at all specified places including 2,3,p|β and infinity. If its quartic bit is + choose an everywhere-soluble slope by the prime-numerator theorem. If the bit is −, choose the single obstruction prime outside the finite set by that same theorem; this is the precise reason no a priori sign restriction at b* is necessary for this approximation. Then C*=θ*(1−βk*²)=βq*². Scale the local H points by q* to local S points over k*, taking nonbranch, nonboundary t and smoothly varying within the open. Apply rational weak approximation on S to obtain a rational (k,t,Y), preserving positivity of 1−βk² and at all specified places the squareclass of (1−βk²)/β, originally θ*. The abscissas t,φ_k(t) avoid the exceptional marking list. Put θ equal to the positive squarefree representative of (1−βk²)/β. Then C=θ(1−βk²) has squareclass β; rescale Y back to an actual H-point.

To force θ new, take any odd P outside the fixed list, all previous complements and 6β, with β square mod P. Existence needs no prime-values conjecture (a new prime of 1−βT² with T divisible by forbidden primes supplies it). In Q_P choose k near a simple root of 1−βk² with v_P(1−βk²)=1. An S-point exists above that k: take t=1/(3√β) with Y=0, and perturb on the smooth local curve off its branch. The branch is simple provided m≠1,9,9/25 and k≠0, all ensured by avoiding finitely many rational/local values in this neighborhood; the branch has a whole smooth local neighborhood of nonbranch points. Add this local S open in rational approximation. It forces P|θ, preserving the other conditions. Therefore infinitely many distinct θ arise.

Finally θ is a norm squareclass from K: 1−βk² is a norm and β itself a norm. Thus L²−βA²=θZ² is a rational conic with rational point. As in the earlier fixed-numerator theorem use its weak approximation to choose r=A/L with 1−βr²=θz² and b=17βr² near b* at the prescribed places, preserving the required depths, square reduced denominator, and real bounds. In the default unconstrained case choose v₂, v₃, v₁₇ of r =2,1,0. The local class matching just proved makes r* a genuine local input in constrained neighborhoods. The positive-circle section gives rational nontorsion ε, the constructed H-point gives rational δ, and their torsion-translated Kummer images are independent for θ>1 as already proved. Hence rank≥2. The elliptic torsor now has infinitely many rational t; each original twist squareclass contains only finitely many such t by the six-branch genus-three/Faltings argument (φ still fixes the two spare branches). Therefore each of the produced poles gives infinitely many distinct original twist classes, with nonzero full marked points. None of the abstract specializations is asserted to have exact rank two.
