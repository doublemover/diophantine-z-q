# The fourth-negative, close-pair minority cases: resolving the remaining four all-odd sign types

Assume q_i∈{±1}, q₃=−1, with the first three mixed and equal-sign pair jk=01 or 02, repeated sign d. The actual four-root normalization is explicitly resolved at 2 by the following normalized/ordinary point blowups; they extend to a proper flat regular model over Z, also for independent dyadically unramified scalar variations. Together with the two jk=12 fourth-negative cases and six fourth-positive cases, these finish **all twelve genuinely mixed all-odd sign representatives**. This says nothing new about completing the other mixed types with some even squarefree coefficient.

## I. One normalized blowup, with only one residual direction

In RAMIFIED-DYADIC-FIRST12 the first ordinary mixed-origin blowup leaves two étale labels (one may forget the étale Z equation near each). At each the equations are A²+SA=2c and V²+2SV+2S²=8m, with
    jk=01:  c=−d, m=2401d;       jk=02: c=−2401d, m=d .
Put X=A, Y=A+S, so XY=2c and S=Y−X. The regular two-dimensional base at the origin has parameters X,Y; the surface maximal ideal is (X,Y,V). Define positive rational dyadic units g,h by g²=−m/c³, h²=−m/c=c²g². Thus h=49 or 1/49. Set κ=1/c. The exact equation (V+S)²=8m−S² will be useful.

Blow up the reduced origin. The X,Y directions cover since the quadratic initial form forces V to vanish if both initial X,Y vanish. On the X chart let x=X, Z=Y/X, t=Z−1, K=Z/c; here 2=x²K and S=x t. Adjoin b=V/x² to the ordinary chart (whose ratio V/x is x b). Its equation is precisely
    b²+xK t b+K t²+ x²g²Z³=0 .
It is monic over the true regular base blowup chart X²Z=2c, so finite over the unnormalized ratio chart, scale a nonzerodivisor, and inversion restores the old field algebra. Modulo x its divisor is b²+κ Z(Z−1)²; in characteristic two the Z-derivative is κ(Z−1)², nonzero away from Z=1. At Z=1 there is exactly one bad candidate b=0; it is singular since its defining equation has no linear part relative to the ambient regular parameters x,t,b. On the Y chart an identical computation with x'=Y,Z'=X/Y and S=−x'(Z'−1) changes only the sign of the mixed b term. At its extra corner Z'=0 the same derivative is nonzero, and nonzero Z' overlaps the X chart. Both charts are normal by the complete intersection property and R₁ (they are normal after scale inversion, and only the closed point just seen can be bad on scale). Thus they are the literal finite normalization of the ordinary blowup. Only one nonregular point remains per original étale label.

## II. Two more ordinary blowups near Z=1; keep track of the side charts

At this origin blow up (x,t,b), with x,t directions covering by the b equation. In the t direction x=tR,b=tB the equation is B²+xK B+K+R²g²Z³=0. Modulo t, Z=1, so at R=0 B=1. Shifting B by 1+R, the defining equation at this corner has a nonzero linear t term from K=κ(1+t): 1+κ is even, hence belongs to (x²), and every other term contributes no linear part since x=tR. Thus the total space is regular there. R≠0 is already on the x chart. The independent monic and the regular base blowup prove saturation at scale; this and succeeding ordinary monic charts use inversion to identify the actual ratio algebra.

On the x chart t=xz, b=xB, so Z=1+xz, K=κZ, and
    B²+xKz B+K z²+g² Z³=0 .
Write y=B−P with P=g+z+xg. The equation is
    y²+(2P+xKz)y+N=0,       N=P²+xKz P+Kz²+g²Z³ .
The coefficient of y and N are divisible by x, and modulo x the quotient N/x is z²(1+z): use 2=x²K, κ,g odd, Z=1+xz. Hence all exceptional points other than z=0,1 are regular by the nonzero linear x term. At z=1 the equation is y²+xF=0, and at the point its F residue has nonzero z-derivative (derivative of z²+z³ at 1 is 1, and y=0). Consequently x,y,F are true regular ambient parameters and one ordinary blowup resolves the smooth projective tangent conic y²+xF. At z=0 retain the following more precise calculation in the regular base at x=z=0:
    2P+xKz ∈ x(x,z),       N ≡ xz²+x³ mod x(x,z)³ .
Indeed expand P², substitute 2=x²K, and K=κ(1+xz). The apparent quadratic terms 2g²+x²g²=x²g²(K+1) and xz(κg+3g²) have order at least four; (K+1)z² also does, since κ+1 even. The two contributions 2gz and x²Kgz cancel to this precision. The remaining cubic terms are xKz² and 2xg²=x³Kg². Every higher term has an x factor, giving the indicated stronger congruence. The residual origin is singular.

Blow up (x,z,y) here. The x,z directions cover since the only quadratic initial term is y². On the z chart x=zR,y=zW, the only direction not on the x chart is z=R=W=0. By the preceding congruence the divided constant N/z² is z(R+R³) plus terms in z²R; the divided y coefficient belongs to zR times a regular function. In regular ambient parameters z,R,W the quadratic tangent form is W²+zR, so one more ordinary point blowup resolves this extra corner. These divided equations have nonzero reduction modulo scale (the W-square is present) and actual saturation by monicity. Both side nodes mentioned in this paragraph and the preceding one are disjoint from the continuing direction and may be resolved as soon as they appear.

On the x direction put z=xq and keep y/x. Then Z=1+x²q, R₀=t/2=q/K, so Z=1+2R₀ and conversely q=KR₀ (K=Z/c unit near this origin). This is an exact reversible base change; denominator 2 has not been inverted. In particular x²(1+2R₀)=2c on this chart. In the fraction field set U=(V+S)/(2x). The exact equation (V+S)²=8m−S² with S=x t gives
    U²=−R₀²−h²(1+2R₀)=C_*−s²,     s=R₀+h²,   C_*=h²(h²−1)=2⁵m_* .
Here m_* is a dyadic unit: 49²−1=2400=75·2⁵, and 1/49²−1 has the same valuation. Also K=(b₀+2s)/c with b₀=1−2h². The next integral coordinate v=(U−s)/x is truly in the **ordinary** ratio chart, not an illicit second scale division. Indeed V=x²b=x³B at this step's x charts and (V+S)/x=t+x²B, hence
    y/x=K v+(Kh²−g)/x−(q+g).
The quotient (Kh²−g)/x is regular: κh²−g is an even dyadic rational, and K−κ=κ x² q. Conversely this identity expresses v using y/x since K is a unit. This proves exact equality of the native generators without asserting a rational parameter scaling extends over the entire projective model.

Set w=v−s, L=K(1+x), J=K+xK((c+b₀)/2+s)/c, H=m_* K⁵. Then L,J,H are units near the exceptional locus in this direction, and the exact resulting surface equation is
    w²+xL s w+xJ s²=H x⁸,          2=x²K .
In fact v²+xK s v+K s²=C_*/x² and 1+K+xK=xJ since 1+K=2((c+b₀)/2+s)/c. K,L,J,H all reduce to 1 modulo x. At x=0 we have w=0. At s≠0 the independent linear x term shows regularity at all such points; only the F₂ origin s=0 can remain singular. The base is regular at this whole exceptional line (native blowup of regular x,z chart, also described by x²(b₀+2s)=2c). All first/second ordinary ratio presentations above are monic after units are inverted; they are S₂ and have at worst isolated singular points on their exceptionals, hence are normal.

## III. Four transverse rescalings and ordinary side conics

Starting at that origin, after n=0,1,2,3,4 continuing x charts write s=xⁿP,w=xⁿW. Use the actual pulled-back units L,J,H,K, with K=(b₀+2xⁿP)/c. The current equation is
    W²+xL P W+xJ P²=H x^(8−2n) .
For n=0,1,2,3 blow up each origin (x,P,W). Initial vanishing of x,P forces initial W to vanish, so the x,P directions cover. The x direction gives the next displayed equation. For n+1<4 the exceptional points have W=0. At P≠0 they are regular because the coefficient of the independent linear x term is nonzero. When P=0 the surface has no linear term, hence is actually singular. On the P chart (incoming n), x=PR,W=P Y:
    Y²+PR L Y+PR J=H P^(6−2n)R^(8−2n).
Only R=0 is new relative to the x direction. At P=R=Y=0 the tangent conic is Y²+PR, or Y²+PR−Hbar R² for n=3. Both are projectively smooth in characteristic two (the P derivative forces R=0, then Y=0, then P=0). One ordinary blowup resolves each such side node, independent of all subsequent events on the continuing chart. The scale charts are native: over each regular base chart they are monic and preserve the generic localization, and at each final conic the nonzero divided reduction modulo scale ensures cancellation in the regular ambient chart. Including these corner resolutions is necessary; checking only the x chart would be insufficient.

After n=4, W=1 on the exceptional fibre. Shift W=1+D. The equation has the form D²+xF=0 with integral F, and F at x=D=0 is P²+P. To see the constant contributes no term, H−1∈(2)⊂(x²), since m_* and b₀/c are odd rationals and K=(b₀+2s)/c. The term with coefficient 2 after the shift likewise contributes nothing modulo x. The derivative in P of P²+P is 1, so both remaining geometric points P=0,1 have true regular ambient parameters x,D,F; each ordinary smooth-conic blowup resolves. Off their loci the linear x test gives regularity. No non-rational exceptional direction has been discarded.

## Global joins and limits

The computations concern the two genuine residue labels inherited from the first mixed ordinary blowup for the sign representatives. Work étale-locally at each (the auxiliary α was unramified), and use whole reduced singular unions to realize these operations on the global model. There is one extra normalized blowup in §I; after it all the operations are ordinary, computed at closed points. The shorter side-node branches can be blown up simultaneously with the longer one at each step. Regularity of each side follows from the full charts/tangent-conic calculation, not a statement limited to rational points. As every center is the intrinsic singular union over the older center, and normalization commutes with étale base change, the construction transfers to independent dyadically unramified coefficient-ratio twists as well. Over an actual neighbourhood of 2 omit all other contact/coefficient/modification primes; this family of finite closed dyadic centers changes no other fibre there. Glue to the prior arbitrary-twist D(2) arithmetic model on the overlap identified with the original X_q. The resulting integral model is proper and flat over Z, with the original parameter and generic curve and regular total space. Together with prior sign packets this gives fully evaluated all-Z models for every tuple of odd signed squarefree representatives; arbitrary even/mixed patterns still have the general terminating algorithm but not thereby evaluated exceptional chains. No local Lean computation, minimality, semistability, KS/rank or 318/326 conclusion.
