# The twelve remaining opposing-sign, even-majority cases on the unramified quarter chart

Continue UROPP12 with ε=1. The marked t-line comparison, two blowups resolving its nonintegral parameter map, and agreement on t≠0 are those of its common first boundary. On the remaining u=2S chart put Y=y/2,Z=w/2. We have
    Y²+S²=2^h C,   Z²=2ad S²+8D
where h=2 when j=0, h=7 for pair12. C=dN,D=−aN' with {N,N'}={1,2401} when j=0; C=75d,D=a when j=1. There are twelve original ordered representatives, two majority signs and two minority signs for each of the three index pairs. On S≠0 the corner-free unit τ=Z as a radical uniformizing variable has τ²=2f with f=ad S²+4D a unit. B=(Y−S)/τ is integral and fB²+τ S B+S²=2^(h−1)C. Its special value is B=1 and shifting by 1 gives unit linear τS everywhere S≠0 (f+S² even). This yields an actual regular normalization there by monic freeness and inverse substitution. Below we use proper blowups of the parameter followed by finite normalization at the remaining origins. Such blowups are not asserted to be canonical blowups of the surface maximal ideal.

## The second parameter chart and its resolved nonoverlap

Blow up (2,S) on the parameter, normalize. On 2 side S=2s, set y'=Y/2,z'=Z/2:
    y'²+s²=2^(h−2)C,   z'²=2ad s²+2D .
On the S side 2=SR we need only R=0, with integral Y₁=Y/S, W=Z/S,
    Y₁²=−1+2^(h−2)C R²,   W²=2(ad+D R²).
For h=7, the first equation splits a Gaussian coefficient by a genuine parameter-étale extraction of the square root of 1−2⁵C R² (use (1+root)/2) and its inverse, not by a ramified scalar extension of the original model. In that Gaussian factor π=1+i,η=−i,2=ηπ². The integral B=W/π has B²=η(ad+D R²). Shift p=B−1−R. Its monic gives p²=π times a unit near R=p=0: (1+R)²−η(ad+D R²) is divisible by π and its quotient has nonzero residue at R=0 since ηad≈1+π modulo π²; the term 2(1+R)p is divisible by π². Before the parameter S is reintroduced this local ring is regular in R,p (π and 2 eliminated from its maximal ideal). Thus S R=2 is exactly S R=unit·p⁴, normal with a sole corner, resolved by two ordinary blowups. The finite generators and inverse equations show this is the true normalized chart. 

For h=2 at the S corner use τ=W, f=ad+D R², τ²=2f. Adjoin B=(Y₁−1−R)/τ via
    f B²+τ(1+R)B+1+R+(1−C)R²/2=0 .
Its base before adjoining S is regular in τ,p=B−1: τ is a nonzerodivisor, and modulo τ the R derivative at B=1,R=0 is 1. At R=0 its exact quotient is τ²=2ad, ad(1+p)²+τ(1+p)+1=0. This has unit τ-linear term, hence R is a parameter together with p, and τ is of exact order two on R=0 (2∈(p⁴) after observing τ∈(p²)). Thus in that regular base write τ=αR+βp², β unit. The remaining relation S R=2=τ²/f becomes X R=unit·p⁴ after shifting S by the R-divisible terms in τ²/f. Again the finite monic enlargement is regular except for this normal product, resolved by two ordinary blowups. Each side description covers every point over its affected corner: its variables have the indicated unique residue values, and away from R=0 the charts overlap the 2 side.

## h=7 pair12 on the 2 side

Here y'²+s²=32 C, z'²=2a(1+d s²). Near s=0 put τ=z', f=a(1+d s²) unit, τ²=2f. With B=(y'−s)/τ one has fB²+τsB+s²=16C. Shift v=B−s:
    f v²+(2f+τ)s v+(f+τ+1)s²=Cτ⁸/f⁴ .
At generic s, the residue B=s/(1+s) has a nonzero τ-linear residual term from τsB (f does not depend on τ); all remaining constant-in-B terms are even after this shift. Thus this monic is genuinely normal near the origin by R₁/S₂; it is free over the regular s,τ base. Make four ordinary reduced-origin blowups, at n=1,…,4 putting s=τⁿP,v=τⁿV on the τ side. The RHS becomes Cτ^(8−2n)/f⁴ and the same quadratic with P for s. At n<4 all P≠0 are regular since f+τ+1≡τ mod τ² and V=0 on the divisor. At n=4 V=1; after shifting by 1 the τ-linear coefficient at that line is P²+P+constant, whose derivative is 1, because (2f+τ)PV contributes P and f−f₀ vanishes to high order. Each of its simple critical roots has a smooth projective tangent conic (the τ·(P−p₀) term), resolved once. At each other chart s_old, R=τ/s_old, the R=0 equations give a node with tangent V'²+s_old R, with an extra multiple of s_old² at n=1 from f−a and of R² at n=4 from the right side. Neither addition destroys smoothness. All four main-step ratio charts are free monics over the true base blowups with unchanged field on scale inversion, covering by their leading square.

For s≠0 extract the square root of 1−32C/s² by its parameter-étale half-sum, so y'/s gives an actual Gaussian coefficient factor. Then A=z'/π satisfies A²=η a(d s²+1). On the special fibre its regularity off s=1 follows, for instance, by shifting A−(s+1): the π-linear coefficient reduces to s²+1 since both ηa and ηad are nonsquares modulo π², whereas the 2s cross-term is order π². At s=1 pick β²=η a(d+1), available as β=0 for d=−1 or a square root of −a times π when d=1. With x=s−1,y=A−β−x the equation is
    y²+2(β+x)y+π J x²+2δ x=0,    J=(1−ηad)/π,  δ=β−ηad ,
both units. The Gaussian ordinary two-stage node calculation from MIXEDPOINTS or GAUSSIAN54 applies: the π chart of the reduced origin has two simple Artin–Schreier nodes, the x chart one smooth-conic corner; all resolve in one further ordinary blowup. The chart before those blowups is normal by isolated nonregularity and its finite monic embedding.

## h=2: all regular away from s=1

We have y'²+s²=C, z'²=2(ad s²+D). Where s≠1 put τ=z', f=ad s²+D a unit. Adjoin B=(y'−s−1)/τ, satisfying
    f B²+τ(s+1)B+s²+s+(1−C)/2=0 .
Modulo τ its s derivative is 1 everywhere, so this actual finite monic normalization is regular on that entire open. Near s=1 set x=s−1,y=y',w=z',
    y²=−x²−2x+2C₁,   w²=2ad x²+4ad x+4D₁,
    C₁=(C−1)/2,    D₁=(ad+D)/2 .
The next local procedure depends on d.

### d=+1: another proper parameter blowup

Here C=N≡1 mod32, C₂=(C−1)/4 integral and D₁ even. On the 2 chart of the parameter (2,x) blowup set X=x/2,U=y/2,V=w/2. Then U²=C₂−X(X+1), V²=2ad X(X+1)+D₁. Eliminate X(X+1): this is an étale X-extension near 2 of
    V²=−2ad U²+16D',    D'=(ad C+D)/32 odd,
since ad C+D=a(N−N') has valuation five. This single monic's only bad residue U is zero (on U≠0 the 2-linear coefficient is a unit). Two successive 2,U_old ordinary blowups give on the main direction W²=−2ad P²+2^(4−2n) D', n=1,2, with smooth-conic side nodes W'²+ad sR and extra R² at n=2. At the last main chart shift W by 1 and P by its sole critical root p₀∈{0,1} with −ad p₀²+(D'−1)/2≡0 mod2. In the 2 chart of its point blowup the divided equation is étale in the shifted W/2 via its coefficient 1; in the P−p₀ side the remaining tangent is Z²+RZ+ad(P−p₀)R+κR². This is exactly the explicit critical-root formula of URMATCH and resolves by a last conic blowup.

On the x side 2=xR put Y=y/x, L=w/x, both integral:
    Y²=−1−R+C₂R²,   L²=ad xR(1+R)+D₁R² .
At R=0 write z=Y−1. Then z²=R A, A=−1−x(1+z)+C₂R a unit, and L²=xR A', A'=ad(1+R)+(D₁/2)R² a unit. Adjoin B=L/z via B²=xA'/A. At the unique corner the two equations z²=RA and B² A=xA' have independent linear parts R,x in the regular ambient over D[x,R]/(xR−2) with z,B adjoined. Their local presentation is therefore a regular two-dimensional domain. It is finite over the old ratios (one extra integral root) and its localization away from the scales gives exactly the old field by the inverse relations, so it is the true normalization. This side needs no more blowups.

### d=−1: the remaining four points, resolved in a different regular base

Here C₁=(−N−1)/2 and D₁=−a(1+N')/2 are odd, and K=ad C₁+D₁=a(N−N')/2=±1200a is of precise dyadic order four. Take the *whole* point x=y=w=0, not the parameter blowup just used for the positive sign. The y-base is regular with parameters x,y since 2U=x²+y², U=C₁−x a unit; its rank-two finite extension by w has the given square relation, with w² of order at least four. Blow up the reduced point and normalize, using x,y directions (the initial w-square vanishes). On x chart t=y/x, e=U w/x² is integral and
    2=x²(1+t²)/U,  e²=(1+t²)[K+(D₁+ad x)t²] .
The identity (t−1)²=(1+t²)(U−x²t)/U allows a second integral expression E=e/(t−1), with
    E²=L[K+(D₁+ad x)t²],    L=U/(U−x²t) .
The displayed denominator is a unit around the full exceptional line x=0, including t=1. E is monic integral over the ordinary w/x ratios, which it recovers via w/x=x(t−1)E/U. This gives the actual finite closure once normality is checked; it is free over the genuine regular base blowup and embeds in the generic field (after inverting 2 and x, t−1 is also invertible on this localization by the identity). At t≠0 put E/t=1+v. Modulo x², its square equals D₁+ad x, since L−1∈(x²t), K∈(2⁴), D₁ is an odd constant, and 2=x²(1+t²)/U. Therefore its hypersurface has unit linear term ad x, giving regularity at every such direction.

On the y chart s'=x/y write U=C₁−ys', 2=y²(1+s'²)/U. Near s'=0 take E'=(U w/y²)/(s'−1); its square is
    E'²=L'[D₁+ad y s'+K s'²],    L'=U/(U−y²s') .
This follows by swapping x,y in the previous computation while retaining the linear ad x term, or by direct substitution; the finite extra generator again recovers the old w/y. At s'=0, p=E'−1, the tangent is p²+ad y s'+κy² (D₁−1 multiple of 2 and L'−1∈(y²s')). This smooth conic resolves in one ordinary point blowup; nonzero s' overlaps the x chart. The two finite monics have only isolated bad points, hence exactly the integral closures by the scale and R₁/S₂ tests. Consequently this procedure yields a proper modification of the actual starting normalization, even if one regards its finite raw w-order before the blowup rather than presuming it normal: normalizing the blowup factors through the true normalization and agrees with it outside the center.

At t=0 on the x chart put v=E−t, M=(1+t²)/U unit, 2=x²M, J=(1−L(D₁+ad x))/x unit (1−D₁∈(2), L−1∈(x²t), linear coefficient −ad). With D₀=K/16 odd the exact equation is
    v²+x² M t v+x J t²=D₀ L M⁴ x⁸ .
Four ordinary reduced-origin blowups on x,t_old directions, t=xⁿP,v=xⁿW, reduce exponent 8→6→4→2→0. For n<4 the nonzero x-linear J P² proves regularity at P≠0 and only the main origin remains. Each side t_old=s, x=sR has a smooth tangent W'²+sR J̄ at its nonoverlap corner, plus R² term at n=4. At n=4 divide W by the unit M². The resulting equation is
    W²+x²M' P W+x J'P²=D₀ L
with units M',J'. Along x=0, W=1; the only critical P is 0, since L−1∈(x²t) and D₀≡1 modulo x². Shift b=W−1 and blow up x,P,b. On the x chart P=xQ,b=xZ the equation is
    Z²+xM Z+x² M'QZ+xJ'Q²+x M'Q+(1−D₀L)/x²=0.
Here the final constant is (1−D₀)M/2+D₀(1−L)/x²; on this chart t=x⁵Q, so its residue and its x-linear coefficient are independent of Q, since M=(1+t²)/(C₁−x), (1−L)/x²∈(t). After shifting Z by a residue lift 0 or 1, the x-linear bracket has polynomial J̄'Q²+M̄'Q+constant, with simple roots. Each gives a smooth conic (nonzero x·(Q−q₀) term, with possible xZ and x² terms), resolved by one ordinary point blowup. On P side x=PR,b=PZ the divided equation is integral since 2=x²M; its nonoverlap tangent at P=R=Z=0 is Z²+PR J̄'+κR², likewise smooth. This settles the last deep point using its exact order-four constant, not only its leading fibre.

All normalized calculations above take place on proper finite normalizations of actual blowups. Monic scale freeness and field reconstruction show saturation; ordinary hypersurface conic charts instead use nonzero residual equation on the regular ambient exceptional plane. The basepoint of s=2t was already resolved on the common first boundary, with agreement on finite t≠0; subsequent operations involving the central S=s, x=s−1 charts take place entirely over finite dyadic t=0. They glue to ODDBRIDGE and to the unchanged odd-prime model. Reduced singular unions and normalized integral closures commute with the parameter and coefficient étale comparisons. Where parameter point ideals on the auxiliary base were used, they are fixed by the original sign choices (u and its dyadic scales change only by sign; s=1 and s=−1 have the same dyadic closed center, and the subsequent blowup is of that actual reduced parameter point). In the d=−1 chart one starts from the whole singular point, and normalizes so that any choice of the explicit finite order must give an equivariant modification: x=s−1 changes to −x−2, y changes by sign and w by sign, so the local reduced ideal (x,y,w) is fixed (2=(x²+y²)/(C₁−x) lies in it); the raw finite order is itself stable under those sign changes. The finite order here is generated by the original scaled y',z' over s and translations, hence preserved on overlapping neighborhoods. Thus the construction descends also for independent unramified scalar twists. This is a proper birational flat regular arithmetic model over Z for all twelve stated types, no claim of minimality or Lean proof.
