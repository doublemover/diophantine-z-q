# Lifting all sixteen deck signs and descending the unramified twist models

**Mathematical result, not a Lean-accepted construction.** Fix the ordered branch constants b=(0,32,76832,−343). Let X₁ be the *actual* two-chart normalization of the projective parameter line over Z in Q(T)(u_i), u_i²=T−b_i, and let Y₁ be the twenty-morphism regular modification in the frozen untwisted dossier (eight disjoint centers in one step). The full constant group E={±1}⁴ acts over the parameter line on X₁ and on every stage of Y₁→X₁. For arbitrary q_i∈Q×, put K=Q(√q₀,…,√q₃), Δ=|disc(K)| (1 for K=Q). Over U=Spec Z[1/Δ], the **actual** normalized projective model X_q for r_i²=q_i(T−b_i) has a specific proper birational modification Y_q with regular total space, proper and flat over U. It is étale-locally on U the previously constructed model; no new good-reduction assumption at 2,3,5,7 is necessary. This does not extend the prescribed construction across any prime ramified in K, nor assert that a regular model there cannot exist. The result is about the total space, not smooth, minimal or semistable reduction.

## 1. Lift the honest group action to all twenty blowups

The four signs act on the raw order generators, fixing the entire polynomial base; FourRootOrderSigns318.signEquiv_generator gives the actual four-generator action. The group laws may also be checked on those generators. They extend to automorphisms of the same fraction field, preserve each integral closure by integrality over the *fixed* parameter algebra, and commute with its Laurent inclusions. This proves equivariance of the original two-chart X₁, not merely its generic fibre. At any step a group preserving a center's ideal sheaf acts functorially on its Rees algebra (each actual power is preserved), hence its relative Proj, compatibly with projection. Reduced closed ideals are intrinsic to their supports, so setwise invariance suffices for these centers; it does not require pointwise invariance of each residue-field element.

* At 3 the selected F₉ point is the unique underlying point over T=2. The first U chart with nonzero homogeneous direction of u=u₁ is stable: u is multiplied by a sign. There the next reduced center has the actual ideal (u,u₂/u,u₃/u) by the source-bound first-U residual-center description; these three generators are multiplied by units, so its closed point is stable. Its retained u₀ may undergo a nontrivial residue-field automorphism. The argument does not choose a branch of that square root.
* At 5 there is one F₂₅ point over the triple collision T=2, hence stable. At 7 the point above the triple collision T=0 is similarly stable. In the first 7-chart π=7, set U=u₀/π, V=u₂/π, W=u₃/π. Its next center is (U,V,W), with π=W²−U². Its W chart is stable and has next center (W,U/W,V/W). All displayed generators transform by unit signs. These support arguments are for the full models, since the other-fibre blowups are isomorphisms near each center.
* At 2 the union of eight simple nodes is stable: four are exactly the underlying points above the *fixed* finite parameter T=1 in characteristic 2, and four are exactly those above s=0. P* is the unique underlying point above T=0. This uses the **normalized** finite/reciprocal chart computations, not naïve signs in the raw nonreduced dyadic fibre. For clarity, write ε_i=±1, δ_i=ε_i ε₀. In the finite chart u'=ε₀u, h'=ε₁h+((ε₁−ε₀)/2)u, k' similarly, w'=ε₃w+(ε₃−ε₀)u+ε₃−1. In the reciprocal chart x'=ε₀x, H'=δ₁H+(δ₁−1)/2, K' similarly, W'=δ₃W+(δ₃−ε₀)x+δ₃−1. Half-gaps are integers, not an inversion of 2 on the chart. At the finite and reciprocal nodes the residues of h,k or H,K can be interchanged. Only the reduced *union* is required to be invariant.
* After blowing up P*, and after each subsequent blowup except the final one, choose the unique nonregular point on the fibre over the preceding center. To justify this intrinsic description rather than assume sign-stable tangent coordinates: the two intermediate w-chart origins n=1,2 have embedding dimension four. Indeed in the regular ambient ring on 2,w,S,A,B the equations w²+2Lₙ, A²+SA−w^(6−2n)/Lₙ³, and B²+SB−2401w^(6−2n)/Lₙ³ have just one nonzero independent linear part, a unit times 2. They cut out dimension two (the two monics and the first hypersurface are a regular sequence). After the third preparatory blowup the candidate origin has equation ev−Hw¹⁰ in the square of the maximal ideal of an actual 3-dimensional regular local ambient; it is a nonzero hypersurface of embedding dimension three and dimension two. The next crossing origin and all subsequent XY=pᵏ·unit origins for k≥2 satisfy the same test (the first crossing p-chart has E₁V₁−H p⁸Z₁¹⁰ in the square). No other point of any such exceptional fibre is nonregular, by the exact-chart coverage in TERMINAL-RESOLUTION. Thus each of the successive reduced centers is fixed as a point by every already lifted automorphism. The high-order split variables need not themselves be eigenvectors for the signs.

This proves that every ideal, including the one simultaneous union, is preserved on the actual global scheme at the step where it is used. Empty centers after restriction to an open mean identity blowups.

## 2. Identify the original twist BEFORE descending any modification

The project's q-twist clears denominators in each equation by multiplying q_i by den(q_i)², as in OriginalIntegerAffineModel318.coefficient_comparison. This gives the *same generic field* L_q after scaling each root back by its rational denominator. On either parameter chart take the actual integral closure in L_q, with its fixed action of Q[T] or Q[s] (s=T⁻¹); denominators need not be units on U. Write V=Spec O_K[1/Δ]. Since K/Q is finite Galois, V→U is finite étale, surjective, and a G=Gal(K/Q) torsor. Indeed unramified primes have trivial inertia (decomposition acts faithfully on the finite residue extension), and G acts freely transitively on each *geometric* fibre of this degree-|G| finite étale cover. Equivalently V×_U V≅∐_G V. This is the maximal-order cover, not Z[√q_i] in which 2 could be unnecessarily ramified.

For any constant extension F/Q in characteristic zero, the four squareclasses q_i(T−b_i) are independent in F(T)×/F(T)×²: their valuations modulo 2 at T=b_j give the four coordinate vectors. The four quadratic adjunctions therefore give a field of degree 16 by elementary Kummer theory. In particular K⊗_Q L_q is the expected degree-sixteen function field over K(T), not a product from accidental shared constants: K⊗_Q Q(T)=K(T) for finite K, and the exact rank-16 presentation retains that rank and is a field after base change. Choose α_i=√q_i in K. The K(T)-identification L_q K ≃ L₁ K takes r_i to α_i u_i and its inverse takes u_i to r_i/α_i. It fixes the parameter actions on BOTH projective-line charts. The generic G descent becomes g on constants and u_i↦χ_i(g)u_i, χ_i(g)=g(α_i)/α_i=±1 (inverse signs are identical). The cocycle is a homomorphism because the two signs are rational.

This identification extends to an isomorphism (X_q)_V ≅ (X₁)_V over the base-changed parameter line. On either chart this is compatibility of integral closure in the same identified field with étale base change. One can check it without a field-isomorphism assumption about normalized special fibres: the tensor of the finite normal chart with O_K[1/Δ] is étale over the original normal chart, hence normal; it embeds in its scalar-extended generic field by flatness/localization (the finite étale scalar ring is locally free and torsion-free, and the generic tensor is the field just checked). It is finite integral over the corresponding V-parameter ring with the same fraction field, hence exactly that parameter ring's integral closure. The two identifications coincide on the Laurent overlap. The semilinear descent on this isomorphism is the above sign character composed with the action on constants; equality on the common fraction field determines equality of the normal-chart maps. In particular this compares the actual X_q, even if a denominator-clearing root change would not yield an integral raw-order isomorphism over V.

## 3. Descend centers on a base scheme already in hand

Restrict Y₁→X₁ to U and pull its sequence to V. Start on X_q|U with the comparison above. Suppose its stage over V is identified equivariantly with the corresponding untwisted stage over V. The next untwisted center ideal after base change is invariant under the combined action: it is defined over Z before base change and is sign-invariant by §1. Its submodule inclusion into the structure sheaf thus has compatible descent data on V×_U V, because this is the disjoint union indexed by G with its genuine group-law cocycle. Effective fpqc descent of quasi-coherent modules and their maps gives a unique ideal inclusion downstairs (injectivity, equality with its image and the ideal property are faithfully-flat local). Blow up this actual descended ideal on the existing scheme. Blowups commute with flat base change; functoriality identifies its comparison and its descent data with those upstairs. Induction gives the entire scheme Y_q, without assuming effectiveness of a general scheme descent datum or taking a potentially singular coarse quotient by a group on a ramified base. The descended centers are closed, finite over the relevant primes and reduced, by finite étale base change and faithful flatness; their residue degrees/splittings need not be those of Y₁.

The normalization X_q is finite over the projective parameter line; all blowups are projective over their predecessor. Thus the construction is proper, is an isomorphism generically and off the descended centers, and has the original twisted generic fibre. The V-pullback of Y₁ is regular, since V→U is étale and Y₁ regular, and Y_q is regular by étale descent (each local ring has a faithfully flat étale local extension upstairs). Its flatness over U descends as well, or follows from the integral birational model dominating a localized PID. This proves the announced regular model for every twist on the explicit discriminant open, not a single uniform finite exceptional set independent of q. For instance q=(5,1,1,1) has K=Q(√5) with discriminant 5; this actually includes 2, 3 and 7. For q_i rational squares the cover is trivial and the same argument applies over all Z. No height lattice, rank, point count, tame/wild ramified resolution, or Lean acceptance follows.
