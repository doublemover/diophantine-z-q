# The actual [2],[2] cochains give the classical Cassels–Tate pairing on Sel₂

**Precise claim, mathematical not kernel-certified.** Over Q let n≠0 and E: y²=x(x−n)(x+3n). Let χ and ψ be the project's continuous E[2]-valued 1-cocycles and require **both** full elliptic classes to be everywhere locally trivial (equivalently these are 2-Selmer representatives). For each place use the explicitly described *Kummer* local adjustment below, not an arbitrary primitive of the unit or binary defect. Choose the global sign-valued primitive of the project's actual unitDefect as in the real-detection argument. The sum of the resulting local Brauer invariants, in the order local correction divided by the restriction of that global primitive, is the classical [2],[2] Cassels–Tate pairing of the two Sha[2] classes. This is an identification in finite level using exact published definitions, not an assertion that the current Lean repository has defined the local-invariant sum or solved the full rank problem.

1. **Coefficient comparison on the literal points.** The original cubic has nonzero discriminant 2304 n⁶. Its four geometric 2-torsion points are O, (0,0), (n,0), (−3n,0); all are rational. On this group the two-torsion Weil pairing e₂ is a nondegenerate alternating bilinear form to μ₂, since char Q≠2. See [Sutherland, Elliptic Curves, 2023 notes, Theorem 23.29](https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.783/2023/LectureNotes23.pdf). The only such form on the two-dimensional binary plane is the determinant in sign coordinates: pick a basis P,Q; its diagonal entries are 1, the off-diagonal entries are equal by evaluating on P+Q, and that common entry is −1 by nondegeneracy. Bilinearity then determines the entire table. The repository supplies a concrete equivariant coordinate equivalence and the same table via originalTorsionPairing/originalRootPairing; the coefficient equivalence sends 1 to 0 and −1 to 1. Thus its root pairing equals geometric e₂ on *every* ordered pair, including O, without assuming a theta-line-bundle realization of the particular function-field presentation. Inverting a sign or transposing the Weil pairing cannot change e₂ in degree two. This uniqueness is not a higher-level Weil pairing theorem.

2. **The exact finite Selmer sequence.** Put M₁=E[2], M=E[4], M₂=E[2] with inclusion and [2]. Use standard local Kummer conditions for [2], [4], [2]. They form an exact sequence of decorated finite modules and the dual Selmer condition is again the elliptic Kummer condition under the Weil pairing and the principal polarization. This is exactly the elliptic specialization of [Morgan–Smith, The Cassels–Tate pairing for finite Galois modules, §6.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.08530), taking both isogenies to be [2]. Our two inputs qualify: in the local Kummer exact sequence membership in the image of E(Q_v)/2 is equivalent to zero after passage to H¹(Q_v,E); this is what the two Sha conditions assert. The named representatives really are cocycles, since E[2] has trivial Galois action here. Locally and globally identify ψ with the character-valued cocycle ψ^∨(T)=e₂(T,ψ); its Galois action on the values is trivial, not the action on the ambient closure units.

3. **Use the project's literal half-lift, not a replacement.** Let f=halfCochain(χ). It is locally constant, 2f=χ and 4f=0; it is a 1-*cochain* in E[4]. Its twisted differential df is exactly the E[2]-valued torsionDefect. By Step 1, the sign-valued unitDefect is df∪ψ^∨. In the usual inhomogeneous cup formula an action of the product of the first two group factors on the last character is present, but it is the identity on E[2]^∨ here. The earlier Milne I.4.10(c) application supplies a continuous sign-valued global ε with dε=unitDefect (the first Sha assumption is enough). This is one admissible choice in Morgan–Smith Definition 3.2. It is not presumed equal on restriction to any local choice.

4. **Why the local lift satisfies the missing stronger condition.** At v choose Q_v∈E(overline Q_v) such that χ_v(σ)=σQ_v−Q_v as literal E-valued functions. This exists from the project's actual_full_class_zero criterion, not just from a claim about cohomology sizes. The point P_v=2Q_v is fixed by all σ because χ_v takes values in E[2]; hence P_v∈E(Q_v). Take R_v=actualHalf(Q_v). Then 4R_v=P_v and ℓ_v(σ)=σR_v−R_v is a continuous E[4]-valued 1-cocycle projecting pointwise to χ_v. Its class is the local **[4]-Kummer** class of P_v, so it belongs to the required Selmer condition. Consequently b_v=f_v−ℓ_v agrees with restrictedAdjustment. Here f_v is the specified global f transported with the chosen closure embedding (restrictedHalf), **not** a fresh local half choice on χ_v. As b_v takes values in E[2] we have db_v=(df)_v, and correctionCup followed by binaryUnit is the same pointwise cochain as b_v∪ψ_v^∨. Picking an arbitrary primitive with the same differential would not prove that the corrected lift has the [4]-Kummer property.

5. **Formula, support, independence.** Set γ_v=(b_v∪ψ_v^∨)/ε_v in the local closure-unit cochains. It is closed because both differentials are (df∪ψ^∨)_v. The oriented difference matches Definition 3.2 exactly: it is restricted global lift *minus* local Kummer lift in the cup, then *minus* restricted global ε (written here as division). This does not select a local class solely by choosing a solution of a coboundary equation.

For clarity, the tuple really gives a finite sum in this setup. Remove 2, primes of bad reduction and finitely many primes ramifying in a common finite Galois quotient through which the locally constant f,χ,ψ,ε factor, with the coordinates of E[4] fixed by its kernel. Such a quotient exists by compactness; ε has sign values. At a remaining prime, E extends to an elliptic scheme over the local integers and P_v extends to a section by properness. The [4]-division fibre over that section is finite étale since 4 is a residue unit; thus every geometric R_v above P_v is defined over an unramified extension. It follows that the local cochains used above factor in their arguments through a finite cyclic quotient of the unramified Galois group and γ_v is sign-valued, with trivial induced action on those signs. Its class inflates to zero: a central extension of a cyclic group C_r by μ₂ splits after pullback along C_(2r)→C_r, since any lift of its generator has order dividing 2r. Equivalently one may use cohomological dimension one of a finite residue field. This proves zero local invariant there for all of these actual Kummer choices; at the exceptional finite and real places the local invariant is the ordinary Brauer invariant.

By Definition 3.2 and Proposition 3.3 in Morgan–Smith, the sum Σ_v inv_v[γ_v] is independent of the admissible choices and bilinear: changing ε changes the tuple by a global Brauer restriction, whose invariants sum to zero, and altering a local Kummer lift pairs trivially with the dual condition. Section 6.1 identifies this finite-module Selmer pairing with the pullback of the classical pairing on Sha(E)[2]×Sha(E)[2]. Propositions 3.5 and 3.6 identify its radicals on Sel₂ as the images of [2]:Sel₄→Sel₂ on the two sides (self-dual here). No claim about a separately defined numerical local-symbol implementation is made without comparing it to these γ_v.

**Boundary and consequence.** This identifies the cochain recipe at the [2],[2] level on *admissible* data, not any old function taking arbitrary local or global primitives. Under the earlier three bounds giving a trivial reduced primary Sha, this finite pairing is identically zero and every 2-Selmer class lifts to Sel₄. In particular zero of this pairing cannot distinguish the divisible Prüfer case from trivial primary Sha. The pre-existing concern about eliminating the divisible alternative by a pointwise 2-converse is not resolved here; the October 2026 manuscript making such a claim is not used as a premise of this identification. No inference that χ has a rational-point lift; no higher [2^k] identification; no Lean acceptance.
