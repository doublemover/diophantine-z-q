On the specific elliptic curve E=E_(−3N): y²=x(x−9N)(x+3N), the rational rank is **0** and **Sha(E/Q)[2^∞] ≅ (Z/2)²**. This result concerns the negative companion curve of the five-prime test, not its positive companion, any required indexed twist, the earlier order-eight Selmer dichotomy, or odd-primary Sha. **Exact 2-Selmer bound.** Write δ(P)=([x(P)],[x(P)−9N]); the third coordinate is their product. At root points T_i use the product of the other two root differences for the missing self-coordinate. This is the usual split 2-descent Kummer map in the stated ordering. The global 2-Selmer group consists of those pairs of squareclasses lying in each local Kummer image. At odd good p∤6N the image equals the unramified classes, as in the companion certificate. Thus it suffices to take 26 global bits: sign and valuations of 2,3,17,409,2857,3673,6121,6529,131,577,1367,9133 in each coordinate. At infinity keep just the sign bit. At a bad odd p encode its local squareclass by valuation parity and quadratic unit character. At 2 use valuation parity and two unit characters with values on the generators −1 and 5; the formula in the replay is the standard sign-of-unit-mod4 bit and the bit for a unit congruent to 3 or 5 mod8.

At infinity T1 generates the local Kummer image of order two. At every odd prime of 3N the two points T1,T2 suffice; at 2 add a local point of abscissa −12. Its product of root differences is a nonzero dyadic square, checked by its valuation and unit, so the point really exists over Q₂. The first two root-pair coordinates of T1,T2 are (−27N²,−9N) and (9N,108N²). Computing the spans gives the full possible local cardinalities 4 at every listed odd prime and 8 at 2. To see these are exact bounds also at bad primes, for G=E(Q_p) take a sufficiently small torsion-free formal open subgroup H≅Z_p. Then [2G:2H]=[G:H]/|G[2]| and [G:2G]=|G[2]|·[H:2H]; the geometric 2-torsion is all rational. At infinity the identity component is a circle and E(R) has two components.

**From the explicit pairing to exact arithmetic.** The independently reviewed five-prime negative-anchor companion proves that α and ε have nonzero 2-Selmer Cassels–Tate pairing by evaluating the exact local tangent symbols, not by taking the output of a rank search. See NEGATIVE-ANCHOR-CERTIFICATE.md (SHA256 fade873467bb63393777cc9d3a578be8d5262103fb3cb9c2abe66d5bbf491b4b), and [Shukla §3.1, Theorem 3.3.7](https://epub.uni-bayreuth.de/id/eprint/8348/1/thesis.pdf). Alternation of the elliptic pairing and this pair of elements give a rank-two submatrix, so its radical in the 4-dimensional Selmer space has dimension at most 2. The image of E(Q)/2 lies in this radical because the pairing is pulled back from Sha. The image of rational torsion already has dimension 2: for a finite abelian torsion group T, T/2T and T[2] have the same order, and T[2] is the full rational E[2]. The Mordell–Weil splitting embeds T/2T into E(Q)/2. Consequently the radical equals the full rational Kummer image, has dimension exactly two, and the free rank is zero. The 2-Kummer exact sequence now gives Sha[2]≅(Z/2)².

Moreover the radical of the finite Selmer pairing is [2]Sel₄ by Cassels' theorem (equivalently [Morgan–Smith, §§3, 6.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.08530)). Any t∈Sha[4] lifts to Sel₄ by the level-four Kummer sequence; the image of this lift in Sel₂ is in the just-identified rational Kummer subgroup, so 2t=0 by naturality of the Kummer sequences. Hence no element of Sha has order 4; every element of higher 2-power order would give one by multiplying by a suitable power of two. Thus the whole 2-primary subgroup equals Sha[2], of order four. This does not assert finiteness of full Sha.
