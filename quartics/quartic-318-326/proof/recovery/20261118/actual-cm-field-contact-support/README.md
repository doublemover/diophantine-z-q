# Controlled field discriminants and the literal five-contact support

For actual number fields K⊂F, the field discriminant tower gives the prime support of disc K in disc F. Unramifiedness of the actual ring of integers of F away from the union of the fifteen bad primes and the five-form positive-odd-contact support bounds the radical of disc F by precisely their product. Thus three CMInputs fields (nonzero absolute base discriminant, prime support and literal radical bound) no longer need separate assumptions when these geometric hypotheses are available. The last quadratic bound additionally takes the fundamental-discriminant property of disc K. No number field of a PEL point or unramifiedness theorem for that field is constructed here.
