# The dyadic normalization, eight ordinary nodes and one residual point

**Scope.** For the constant-one twist of the original proper two-chart model, the prime 2 is not covered by the odd-prime calculation. Here we identify the literal normalized dyadic affine chart, and the normalized reciprocal chart in a neighbourhood of infinity, and perform a simultaneous blowup at eight reduced F₂-points. Together with the six earlier odd-prime blowups the resulting proper flat scheme over Z has regular total-space stalks at every point **except one explicitly singular F₂-point on the finite chart**. Thus it is NOT yet a regular model over Z. This is a mathematical argument with an uncompiled component, not a verified Lean theorem. Do not infer equivalence of the special fibres of the raw order and its normalization.

## 1. The finite chart must change before reducing modulo 2

Let D=Z_(2), write u=r₀ (so T=u²), and take the following elements in the original field L:
  h=(r₁−u)/2,  k=(r₂−u)/2,  w=r₃−u−1.
Let E over D[u] have the independent monic relations
  h²+uh+8=0,   k²+uk+19208=0,   w²+2L=0,   L=(u+1)w+u−171.
They follow exactly from rᵢ²=T−cᵢ, c=(0,32,76832,−343). Conversely r₁=u+2h, r₂=u+2k and r₃=u+1+w. The abstract E is free of rank 8 over D[u], hence has no 2-torsion and is finite free of rank 16 over D[T]. After inverting 2 the explicit substitutions identify it with the original raw ring after the same coefficient change. It therefore embeds in the same field, with fraction field L. In particular 2 is not being inverted in the integral chart; the half-differences are integral by their monic equations.

This complete intersection is Cohen–Macaulay, hence S₂. To check R₁, in characteristic zero use the original distinct-contact simple-branch étale charts. Above 2 one has w=0 in the residue field, and h(h+u)=k(k+u)=0. Where u is nonzero in the residue field, the first two quadratic adjunctions are standard étale locally (derivatives 2h+u and 2k+u). If u is also not 1, then L is a unit at that point. In the regular ambient ring D[u,w] localized at the contraction, 2 is not in the square of its maximal ideal: its quotient modulo 2 is a regular localization of F₂[u,w], and 2 is a nonzerodivisor, hence part of a regular system of parameters. Therefore w²+2L is not in the square, and its hypersurface is regular there. This argument includes nonclosed vertical primes. The only possible bad points lie over (2,u=0) or (2,u=1). In E the former gives a single point P* with h=k=w=u=0; the latter gives four points with h,k independently 0 or 1. All five are F₂-rational and have height ≥2 by flat going-down over D[u]. Thus R₁ holds. Serre's criterion proves E normal. Being finite over D[T] and integrally closed in its exact fraction field L, it IS the coefficient-localized integral closure of the finite parameter chart, not merely an auxiliary cover.

## 2. An exact dyadic normalization on the missing reciprocal boundary

Use s=1/T as in the actual reciprocal order, zᵢ=s rᵢ, x=z₀ with x²=s. Put d(s)=∏_{i=1}³(1−cᵢs), and restrict to D(d) of the reciprocal chart. In L put yᵢ=zᵢ/z₀, H=(y₁−1)/2, K=(y₂−1)/2, W=y₃−1−x. Form C over D[x,1/d(x²)] with
  H²+H+8x²=0,   K²+K+19208x²=0,
  W²+2J=0,   J=(1+x)W+x−171x².
These identities follow from yᵢ²=1−cᵢx². Conversely z₀=x, z₁=x(1+2H), z₂=x(1+2K), z₃=x(1+x+W). All adjunctions are monic and C is free of rank 8 over D[x,1/d(x²)], rank 16 over D[s,1/d(s)]. Invert both 2 and x for the comparison with the original raw reciprocal order: the two displayed sets of substitutions are mutual inverses there. Multiplication by 2x on C was already injective by freeness over the polynomial localization. Thus C embeds into the same L with the intended reciprocal coefficient action and fraction field L; x need not be invertible on the arithmetic chart.

In characteristic zero this is the regular reciprocal chart of PROJECTIVE-OPEN: after inverting 2 we recover the same unshifted unit-ratio chart. In characteristic two W=0 at every prime over 2 and H,K have derivative 1 mod 2. On this restricted chart x cannot equal 1 in any dyadic residue field since d(x²) mod 2 is 1+x². If x is not 0 then J mod the maximal ideal is x(1−x)≠0; as in §1 the total-space hypersurface W²+2J is regular. Thus the only possible singular points are over x=0, W=0, where H,K can be independently 0 or 1. These are four F₂ points in codimension ≥2 (flat finite free over the polynomial base D[x,1/d(x²)]). C is a Cohen–Macaulay domain, regular in codimension one, hence normal. Its finiteness and fixed fraction field identify it with the **actual integral closure** on this restricted reciprocal chart. This is not the entire reciprocal affine chart; it suffices since all points outside the finite chart have s=0 and d(0)=1. On overlap the two normal rings use the original actual Laurent field embeddings. The finite u=1 points are not counted twice on the restricted reciprocal chart: there x=1 over 2, hence d=0. The finite u=0 point lies over T=0 and is in no reciprocal parameter chart.

## 3. The eight simple points and the actual blowup

At u=1 above 2 in §1, 1+w is a unit and t=L=(u+1)w+u−171 is an invertible change of polynomial coordinate after inverting 1+w: u=(t+171−w)/(1+w). The base equation is precisely w²+2t=0. The h,k extensions are étale there. At x=W=0 in §2 put t=J. The map from the node ring D[t,W]/(W²+2t) to D[x,W,1/d(x²)]/(W²+2J) is étale near these points: adjoin x subject to −171x²+(1+W)x+W−t=0, made monic by a unit of D. Its x-derivative 1+W−342x is a unit there. The H,K extensions are again étale around the four points. The standard node has a nonregular two-dimensional local ring at (2,t,w): in the three-dimensional ambient regular local ring its equation is in (2,t,w)², leaving embedding dimension three. These are actual singular total-space points, not a consequence of a nonreduced special fibre alone (regularity is reflected by a local étale map).

Blow up the union of the eight reduced points. Locally at each point its ideal equals the extension of (2,t,w) of the node: the chart is étale there, and the fibre over that closed point is zero-dimensional and reduced, with local ring the residue field. After restriction to a neighbourhood this is equality of the center ideal sheaves (coherence). For example the residual values of H,K or h,k distinguish the four points, not four thickened structures. Flat base change transports the blowup of the node to these neighbourhoods.

For the node B=D[t,w]/(w²+2t), the ordinary blowup at (2,t,w) is regular. Its Rees directions satisfy W₀²+P₀T₀=0; vanishing of P₀,T₀ in a homogeneous prime forces W₀ to vanish. Hence only the 2 and t charts are needed. On the former w=2Y, t=−2Y² gives D[Y]; on the latter w=tY, 2=−tY² gives D[t,Y]/(tY²+2). In both cases the generator is a nonzerodivisor and inversion restores the original localized ring: D[Y] is free over D, while tY²+2 is primitive irreducible as a linear polynomial in t over D[Y] with coprime coefficients Y²,2. Thus these are the genuine ratio subalgebras, not unsaturated chart equations. On the second chart the quotient by t is F₂[Y] regular, so all original total-space local rings along t=0 are regular. The two charts form the canonical Rees transition and their étale pullbacks are regular. Away from the center the blowup is the identity.

## 4. Exactly what remains at 2

At P* (u=h=k=w=0 mod 2), L≡1 mod the maximal ideal, so 2=−w²/L and the maximal ideal is generated by u,h,k,w. In the five-dimensional regular ambient local ring D[u,h,k,w]_(2,u,h,k,w), the linear parts of the three defining equations span precisely the one direction 2: the first two have no linear terms and the third is 2 times a unit plus w². The local ring of E at P* thus has embedding dimension four while its dimension is two (finite flat over the two-dimensional base, or the three monic regular-sequence equations). It is **not regular**. None of the eight disjoint blown-up points is P*, and no finite residue extension has silently split it.

All dyadic points used as centers are closed in the actual global proper model: they are closed in its special fibre of finite type over F₂, which itself is closed. Coefficient localization preserves that scheme-theoretic fibre and its reduced centers and the relevant total-space stalks. Construct the six odd-prime blowups over Z by the unchanged closed-fibre centers, then this disjoint dyadic center. Outside 2 the latter contributes nothing, and above 2 the odd modifications were identities. The resulting integral scheme is proper over Z and flat by torsion-freeness over Z; no vertical modification altered its generic fibre. Its regular locus now misses just P*. Explicitly resolving that remaining mixed-characteristic singularity, other twists, and formal global Lean acceptance are open in this packet.
