# Three matching odd valuations, fourth unit: resolve the parameter map as well

Take the original contacts (0,32,76832,−343). We construct a proper birational modification of the **actual** original projective normalization X_q, flat proper regular over Z, for q⁰=(2d,2d,2d,a), d,a=±1, times arbitrary independently dyadically unramified factors. Combining the reviewed cases yields the following sufficient condition with **no restriction on q₃**: for i=0,1,2 the parities v₂(q_i) agree and the signed unit parts 2^(−v₂(q_i))q_i agree modulo 4. Equivalently, the first three q_i represent the same class modulo the subgroup of Q₂-squareclasses giving unramified quadratic or trivial extensions. Unequal first-three classes remain outside this construction. The crucial scaling t=2T is NOT an integral automorphism of the dyadic line; an actual basepoint resolution is required and supplied below.

## Auxiliary normalization and genuine morphism back to T

Work near 2 with t=2T as an auxiliary generic parameter. In the same field set u=r₀, h=(r₁−u)/2, k=(r₂−u)/2, w=2r₃. Then the true normalization of the finite *t*-chart over D=Z_(2) has
    t=d u²,   h²+uh=−16d,   k²+uk=−16·2401d,   w²=2a(d u²+686).
These independent monics are finite free of rank 16 over D[t], in the correct field by branch valuations and rank, and hence give an S₂ complete intersection domain. At dyadic u≠0, the h,k quadratics are étale, d u²+686 is a unit, and the w-equation has nonzero linear part 2 in the regular ambient. The only other dyadic point is the codimension-two origin u=h=k=w=0. In char zero all contacts are distinct. Hence R₁ proves normality. Near infinity use x=1/u,s=1/t=d x², H=h/u,K=k/u,W=w/u: the first two monics have right sides −16d x² and −16·2401d x², and W²=2a(d+686x²). On the reciprocal open inverting the nonzero branch factors at s=0, this gives the correct normal ring by finite freeness, exact agreement on x≠0, and regularity at x=0 (in char 2 the last coefficient is a unit; in char 0 the ratio roots are nonzero). In either chart the displayed normalization also describes the invariants of the true normalization after splitting the original coefficients: the semilinear scalar/sign action has precisely the twist field as fixed field, and integrality in it is intersection with the larger integral closure. We do not use a wild scalar quotient to descend regularity.

We allow the étale coefficient β²−β+86=0 with α=2β−1, α²=−343 whenever a=−1, as justified in RAMIFIED-DYADIC-DDMINUS. Calculations on either dyadic coefficient DVR still have uniformizer 2 and residue F₂. Let c=α for a=−1, c=1 for a=1, and j=(343a−c²)/2 (0 or 171). Put L=w−2c. Its origin together with 2,u,h,k is the same closed reduced point as before. The exact transformed equation is
    L²+4cL=2ad u²+8j.
Blow up that reduced point. The ordinary 2,u directions cover: h,k initial squares vanish if initial 2,u vanish, and so does the square of the initial L by this relation. The native u chart 2=uQ,h=uA,k=uB,L=uY is
    uQ=2,  A²+A=−d u²Q⁴, B²+B=−2401d u²Q⁴,
    Y²=uQ(ad+jQ²−cQY).
It is saturated: base D'[u,Q]/(uQ−2) a domain over the chosen coefficient DVR, then separate monics, scale u a nonzerodivisor; inversion gives the old localization. Above u=0 the locus Q≠0 is already in the 2 chart. At Q=0 there are four degree-one labels A,B=0 or 1, étale locally in these two variables. The remaining three-dimensional regular ambient with parameters u,Q,Y has surface tangent conic Y²=ad uQ, smooth projectively also in characteristic 2. Blow up the four reduced origins. Their native u and Q directions cover the conic and their divided equations are smooth modulo scale. Nonzero reduction in the ambient polynomial exceptional plane gives scale torsion-freeness and the true ratio charts; outside the origins there is no change.

This last operation resolves the rational parameter map: generically on the u chart T=d u²/2=d u/Q. On the new u direction Q/u is regular and 1/T=d Q/u; on the Q direction u/Q is regular and T=d u/Q. The two directions cover over the base locus because both homogeneous u,Q zero force the homogeneous Y zero by its tangent conic. Off u=Q=0 one of the two old ratios is already regular. Their pullbacks to the h,k étale charts give the same morphism. On the 2 chart below T=2d S² is regular. Away from the original center, if 2 is invertible the parameter change is a projective automorphism; for dyadic t≠0 one has regular 1/T=2/t, also at t=∞ using 1/t. These maps agree on the dense generic locus so glue by separatedness on the integral scheme. All subsequent modifications retain this global morphism to the original T-line by composition. Thus once regularity has been established, normality of the resulting integral model gives a map to the actual normalization X_q: each original integral function above a T-patch is integral over every local ring above that patch, hence belongs to it in the common field. The finite normalization and these embeddings ensure the stalkwise maps glue. The map is proper (source proper over D and target separated over D, use graph), birational and is the identity on the originally identified generic function field. This step would not follow merely by asserting t=2T is a dyadic coordinate change.

## First-case analysis on the 2 chart

Write u=2S,h=2A,k=2B,L=2y. This chart is monic free over D'[S], with equations
    A²+SA=−4d, B²+SB=−4·2401d, y²+2c y=2ad S²+2j.
Thus saturation at 2 follows directly. When a=−1 we have y=r₃−α, and the third equation becomes y²+2αy=−2d S². If S≠0 on the dyadic fibre, the last equation has independent parameter 2 and the other quadratics are étale, hence regular. At S=0 only A=B=y=0 occurs. Blow up the reduced ideal (2,S,A,B,y) there, covered by 2,S directions as forced by the three quadratics. On the S chart 2=SR,A=S A',B=S B',y=S Y':
    A'²+A'=−d R²,  B'²+B'=−2401d R²,  Y'²+α RY'=−d SR.
Both distinguished scales are nonzerodivisors (all three independent monics over D'[S,R]/(SR−2) here, similarly over D'[s] on the 2 chart); after inversion the original equations return. At S=R=0, the first two extensions are étale with four labels; the tangent conic Y'²+α RY'+d SR=0 in regular ambient parameters S,R,Y' is smooth: a vanishing S-derivative forces R=0, then the equation forces Y'=0 and the R-derivative forces S=0. One ordinary point blowup resolves the four label preimages. R≠0 overlaps the 2 chart. There S=2s,A=2U,B=2V,y=2Z:
    U²+sU=−d,  V²+sV=−2401d,  Z²+αZ=−2d s² .
The third extension is étale, s≠0 gives two further étale adjunctions on the exceptional divisor, and s=0 leaves U=V=1 mod 2 with Z=0 or 1. Work near these two labels, forgetting the étale Z. U is a unit and s=(−d−U²)/U, so the first-root base is the localized regular D'[U,U⁻¹] with parameters 2,p=U−1. Put e=V/U−1−2400, t'=4801−d/U². The actual second-root equation, with its inverse V=U(1+e+2400), is
    e(e+t')=−2400·2401=H·2⁵,   H=−75·2401.
For d=−1, numerator U²t'=4801−d+4801(2p+p²) has a nonzero 2-linear term. Thus e,v=e+t',p are regular ambient parameters; in the DVR modulo e,v the length of further quotient by 2 is 2 (equation p²=0). Hence 2=α₁e+β₁v+γ₁p² with γ₁ unit. Two crossing-direction blowups give successively E₁V₁=H p³(α₁E₁+β₁V₁+γ₁p)⁵ and E₂V₂=H p⁶(α₁E₂+β₁V₂+γ₁)⁵. At the sole remaining crossing the last factor is a unit; three ordinary blowups take exponent 6 to 4,2 and resolve the conic. As in TERMINAL §III all side-chart reductions are linear, chart saturation follows by cancelling scale against nonzero divided initial forms.

For d=+1, t'∈(2,p)² since 4800 is divisible by 4. Blow up the nonreduced ideal (2,p) (support the e=0 origin in each étale sheet) and normalize. The surface is quadratic monic over the regular 2,p base, hence flat; its blowup is the base-blowup pullback. On either regular base chart of scale b=2 or p adjoin E=e/b², monic E(E+t'/b²)=H·2⁵/b⁴. The algebra is finite over the old blowup chart, same field by scale inversion and freeness. At p direction 2/p=0 the coefficient t'/p² reduces to 1, so étale. On the 2 chart the RHS is 2H, a uniformizer times a unit. If the fibre derivative in E is a unit the cover is étale, and at vanishing derivative its residue E=0. At such points the defining equation has independent linear term −2H (the product E(E+t'/4) lies in the square). The base 2-chart is regular with 2 a regular parameter even at nonrational directions. Thus the normalized chart is regular everywhere, hence indeed the integral closure. The stable ideal check is given below.

## Second-case analysis on the same 2 chart

If a=+1, c=1, y=r₃−1 and the last equation reads y²=2f, f=dS²−y+171. For S≠0,1 in the residue field the coefficient is a unit and the other two extensions étale, giving regularity. At S=1 the h/k descendants A,B are étale with four labels. Put z=S−1; blow up the reduced ideals (2,z,y) under these étale maps. 2,z cover by the equation. On the 2 chart z=2V, y=2Y:
    Y²+Y=2d(V²+V)+(d+171)/2,
étale at every dyadic prime. On the z chart 2=zR, y=zY:
    Y²+RY−((d+171)/2)R²=d zR(1+R).
Scale nonzerodivisors and exact inversions follow by monicity over the same regular base charts as before. The remaining z=R=0 origin has tangent conic Y²+RY−((d+171)/2)R²−d zR, smooth by the same derivative argument. Its four residue label origins resolve in one ordinary blowup. All R≠0 directions already occur on the 2 chart.

At S=0, f a unit and 2=y²/f. The remaining local equations A²+SA=−4d and B²+SB=−4·2401d therefore have RHS −d y⁴/f² and 2401 times this. Two (not three) preparatory ordinary origin blowups give y,S charts as in TERMINAL §I: after n=0,1,2, S=yⁿs,A=yⁿU,B=yⁿV, f=171−y+d y^(2n)s², and
    U²+sU=−d y^(4−2n)/f²,  V²+sV=−2401d y^(4−2n)/f² .
For n=0,1 the two directions cover by initial quadratics. In the s direction y=sR one obtains right side −d s^(2−2n) R^(4−2n)/f² in the monic U/s quadratic with derivative one, likewise V/s. The scale test in each chart (scale, 2·unit, two independent monics form a regular sequence, permute scale last locally) gives true ratio algebras. The s direction has regular exceptional divisor, and y direction s≠0 gives étale monics modulo scale. The sole intermediate origin has all residues zero; at n=2 the only remaining point is y=s=0,U=V=1.

Omitting V gives the regular two-dimensional local base C: y is a nonzerodivisor and C/(y)=F₂[U,U⁻¹] localized at U=1, since s=U⁻¹−U modulo y. Set **p=fU−1**, so y,p form regular parameters, and
    e=V/U−1−2400,   t'=4801−d/(1+p)²,   e(e+t')=H y¹⁰,   H=−75·2401/f⁵ .
This change of p removes f² from t' exactly. Define c₀=(4801−d)/2 and m its parity (0 or 1; m=1 for d=−1). Then
    (1+p)² t'=2c₀+4801(2p+p²).
As t'∈(y,p)², blow up the supported nonreduced ideal (y,p), normalize with E=e/b² in each scale b as in the preceding case. On p-direction y/p=0 the coefficient reduces to 1. On y-direction p=yP:
    T₁=t'/y² = [c₀+4801(yP+fP²)]/[f(1+yP)²],    E(E+T₁)=H y⁶.
Modulo y, T₁=P²+m, with sole remaining P=m and E=0. Write z=P−m, N=c₀+4801(ym+fm²). Then T₁ has numerator
    N+4801(y+2fm)z+4801 f z².
Crucially N∈(y²) even for m=1: using m²=m, f=171−y+d y⁴s², this is c₀+4801·171m+4801md y⁴s², with the rational constant even. Thus T₁∈(y,z)². In particular the possible linear y term **cancels**; one cannot reuse the transverse first-normalized-chart argument of the old constant-f proof. The first normalized chart is an honest normal hypersurface: regular except possibly this isolated closed point, finite monic over a regular base, hence R₁/S₂. At the point the equation is indeed singular by the embedding dimension test.

Blow up (y,z) and normalize a second time by E₂=E/b². On the z chart y/z=0 the coefficient T₁/z² reduces to 1 (the other terms contain y/z, and N divisible by y²). On the y chart z=yQ:

Both normalized chart families glue by their ratio-square transitions, equal the original surface off the given center, and are proper modifications (finite over proper blowups). The reduced later labels are taken in one union.

## Stable nonreduced ideals and final arithmetic model

All first-chart centers used above may be chosen as entire finite reduced sets: the first point above t=0, the four u=Q=0 points (the u and 2 directions are individually sign-stable), the subsequent origins on the 2 chart over S=0 or S=1, and the indicated singular fibres or unique remaining origins above each. Explicitly in the first ordinary blowup u'=ε₀u, S'=ε₀S, Q'=ε₀Q. At later ordinary S-,s- or 2-directions sign changes preserve 2 and the scale from u; at the two preparatory a=+ charts near S=0 one has y'=ε₃ y+ε₃−1 equal to y times a unit since 2=y²/f. The successor W-chart and side-chart tests show no unseen singular directions. For a=−1 the two Y/Z-type roots exchanged by sign or conjugation of α need not be individually fixed; singular descendants over them can be taken simultaneously. The ordinary initial ideals are actual reduced-support ideals, not just closed geometric labels.

For the a=−1,d=+1 nonreduced ideal (2,U−1) the scale 2 is fixed, and U'=ε₁U+g s, where g=(ε₁−ε₀)/2 is an integer (from the two 2-chart scalings of the half-gap for h). Modulo 2, s=U⁻¹−U. Hence this ideal is preserved; use inverse signs for equality. It is independent of α and Z. For a=+1 put k=y'/y=ε₃+(ε₃−1)/y a unit near the preparatory origins with residue 1 modulo y. The relation y²=2f gives f'=k²f. At n=2 the scales give U'=k⁻²(ε₁U+g s), hence
    p'=ε₁p+(ε₁−1)+g f s .
As s lies in (y,p) (reduce s=U⁻¹−U mod y), this preserves the first ideal (y,p). On its y chart the exact identity s=U(t'−4802) gives s∈(y²), since 2∈(y²) and t'=y²T₁. Thus P'=p'/y' reduces modulo y to P itself, and (y,P−m) is preserved as an ideal as well. Normalization of these blowups is functorial. Every local nonreduced ideal is supported at the stated closed finite points (mod split-product parameters the remaining generator has square zero), hence extends coherently by the unit ideal on the complement, after ordinary shrinking at the points. Residue-label étale pullbacks preserve the ideals.

Choose an actual neighbourhood of 2 with other coefficient/contact/ramification/modification primes removed, including an étale maximal-order scalar cover splitting the q/q⁰ ratios and the optional −7 constant. The auxiliary normalizations match there after scalar base change in their exact generic fields, so their sign/constant comparison is effective for the stable ideal inclusions. Blowups commute with flat base change; finite normalization commutes with étale base change. The constructed regular model and its T-morphism therefore descend (the morphism to the T-line is fixed on the generic field and agrees on the entire integral charts). Regularity follows by étale descent. Away from 2 here the change t=2T *is* an integral projective automorphism and all the modifications are identities on the true normalization. Glue on the common original X_q to the earlier model over D(2). The resulting arithmetic surface is proper and regular; it is integral, hence flat over Z. For the full sufficient condition in the introduction: the odd-first-three case with arbitrary fourth is the earlier RAMIFIED-DYADIC-DDMINUS corollary, the all-four-odd-valuations case is RAMIFIED-DYADIC-2222, and the present note supplies fourth even valuation with first three odd valuations. For example (2,10,−6,−1) and (2,10,−6,1) both qualify, by different charts. There is no conclusion for general mixed first-three coefficient classes, no minimality, semistability, KS or height consequence, and no Lean acceptance.
