# Unramified quarter chart: the 24 mixed-parity matching-majority types

On ODDBRIDGE's q₃=−1 chart z_i²=q_i(t−c_i), c=(0,8,19208), consider the two first-three radicals of majority valuation parity ε=0 or 1, at indices j<k, with equal sign d=±1. The minority at m has sign a. Work on the auxiliary s=2^ε t line with contacts e_i=2^ε c_i. If ε=1 replace the minority root by twice itself. Then the working coefficients are d,d,2a. This gives 2·3·2·2=24 types. We treat the s→t parameter morphism explicitly below; only the finite t-chart needs a resolution here.

Let u=z_j (working roots), h=(z_k−u)/2, w=z_m, H=d(e_j−e_k)/4=2^h₀H₀, D=e_j−e_m=2^m₀D₀. We have h₀=1+ε for j=0 or 6+ε for pair12, m₀=3+ε, and the true s-finite normalization is
    h²+uh=H,   w²=2ad u²+2aD,  s=e_j+d u² .
It is finite free of the proper degree in the common generic field, regular at every nonzero dyadic u (h étale, independent linear 2 in w relation); only isolated origin, hence R₁/S₂ normal. In the charts below all ordinary independent unit-leading monics over genuine regular base blowups are saturated via scale inversion and scale nonzerodivisors. Normalized extra scales are integral, generate finite rings containing the old ratios, and equal the normalizations by single-monicality or complete intersection plus isolated nonregularity. The chart-cover tests for homogeneous directions follow from the displayed quadratics: setting the two chosen base directions zero forces each other leading square zero. Smooth projective tangent conic nodes resolve by one ordinary blowup, with nonzero divided equation modulo scale proving saturation.

## Common preparatory powers, including the marked parameter

While h₀≥2n use n successive ordinary reduced-origin blowups along 2,u_old directions as long as the preceding h and w values vanish and both of their constants have scale valuation at least two. On the main 2 side
    u=2ⁿS,h=2ⁿA,w=2ⁿB,
    A(A+S)=2^(h₀−2n)H₀,    B²=2ad S²+2^(m₀+1−2n)aD₀ .
This is used for n=1,2 when j=1, and only n=1 when j=0,ε=1. On the side s'=u_old, 2=s'R, the A' monic has derivative 1, and B'²=ad s'R+aD₀ s'^(m₀+1−2n) R^(m₀+3−2n). At the nonoverlap corner the tangent is B'²+ad s'R with an additional multiple of R² if m₀+1=2n. If m₀+1−2n=1 instead, that second term has order ≥4. Both labels resolve by one conic blowup. On all nonterminal iterations S≠0 is regular by the 2-linear coefficient while A is étale, except at the final specific constant collisions checked below. The cases where h₀=1 start by a different chart calculation in the last section.

For ε=1 the initial n=1 nodes of that side resolve the marked map t=e_j/2+d u/R exactly as in UNRAM6: the two conic directions u,R cover, giving u/R or R/u. The main side has t=e_j/2+2dS²; elsewhere use 1/t=2/s, including s=∞. As in that note, the proper projective s normalization gives a proper model over the finite t-chart after restriction; any dyadic s=∞ maps to t=∞. On t≠0 in the dyadic overlap, u²=2d(t−e_j/2), R=2/u=u/[d(t−e_j/2)], u/R a unit. The side-u cone ratios h/u,w/u are integral by the side monics; the last needed ratio (w/u)/u is integral from its square =2ad/u²+2aD/u⁴ on this open (use u²=2·unit and m₀=4). Thus the resulting finite regular chart agrees with the original normalization there; all subsequent main centers map to t=0. ε=0 requires no such comparison.

## Pair12, both ε

At n=2, D₀=1 and A(A+S)=2^(2+ε)H₀. If ε=0 then B²=2ad S²+a. If a=−1, around S=0 write p=B−1, so 2=−p²/(1+p−ad S²) exactly. More generally for B²=2ad S²+a, a=±1, its base bad value is the unique F₂ root s₀ of ad s₀²+(a−1)/2=0, read modulo 2. At that root write q=S−s₀, b=B−1, k=(2ad s₀²+a−1)/4 integral. Then b²+2b=2ad q²+4ad s₀q+4k. On the 2,q blowup its 2 chart is Z²+Z=2ad P²+2ad s₀P+k, étale; the q-side corner has tangent Z²+RZ+ad qR+k̄R², a smooth conic. On 2 side S=2P,b=2Z,A=2Q one has Z²+Z=2adP², Q(Q+P)=H₀; the latter is étale for P≠0 and regular for P=0,Q=1 by its P derivative. On S side 2=SR,b=SZ,A=SY the Y monic is étale and Z²+RZ=ad SR has an ordinary smooth tangent conic at R=0. No other bad points.

If ε=1 at n=2, B²=2(ad S²+a). At S=0 use 2=B²/(a+ad S²), the base regular in B,S; A(A+S)=unit·B⁶ resolves by three ordinary blowups. On 2 side q=2P,B=2Z the equation Z²=2ad(P²+P)+k has only simple bad roots after shifting Z by its residue 0 or 1: the 2-linear bracket has P derivative ad unit. ## Close pairs j=0, ε=1

After n=1 we have A²+SA=G=−dN with N=1 or 2401, B²=2ad S²+8aD₀. Let K=(e₀−e_m)/(e₀−e_k)=2401 or its reciprocal, Λ=2K−1, X=B/A, Y=S/A+2K=Λ(1−Γ/A²), Γ=dN/Λ. Then
    X²=2ad Y²+2⁸ D',    D'=−ad K(K−1)/32 odd .
Indeed D₀/(dG)=K. The base at S=0 identifies S=(G−A²)/A; X is a unit-slope variable change. All other directions preceding here are regular (the original 2 chart at S≠0 has a nonzero 2-linear B constant).

If d=1 take an étale γ²=Γ and p=A−γ. Thus Y=E p(p+2γ), E=Λ/A². Normalize the ordinary 2,p point blowup: on p=2P adjoin X/4=V, y=Y/4=E P(P+γ), V²=2ad y²+16D'. The p-side 2=pR,X=p²Z has Z²=pR ad y_s²+D' p⁴R⁸, y_s=E(1+Rγ), regular except its R=0 smooth conic node. On the main side y=0 has two simple roots and each yields genuine regular parameters 2,y. Two ordinary 2,y_old blowups give, on the main side at step n=1,2, W²=2ad P²+2^(4−2n)D'; at n=1 only P=0 remains. Each other side has a smooth tangent W'²+ad sR (extra R² term at n=2). At n=2 the terminal equation W²=2ad P²+D' is resolved by the single critical-root calculation b²+2b=2ad q²+4ad s₀ q+4k in the preceding section, now s₀ determined by (D'−1)/2. This computation used only oddness of the constant.

If d=−1 take p=A−1, C=(1−Γ)/2 odd, H'=C+p and Y=E(p²+2H'). Blow up 2,p,X. On the 2 side p=2P,X=2V, V²=2ad (Y/2)²+2⁶D' is regular (Y/2 a unit). On p side 2=pR,X=pV, y'=Y/p=E(p+RH'), V²=pR ad y'²+D' p⁶R⁸. Normalize the next p,R point blowup by R=pT,V=p²Z, t=y'/p=E(1+TH'):
    Z²=ad T t²+D' p¹⁰T⁸ .
At p=0, derivative of T(1+T)² is nonzero except T=1. In the R direction p=Rb, V=R²Z', the analogous equation is Z'²=ad b E²(b+H')²+D'R¹⁰b⁶; at b=0 it is regular. The integral extra scales prove a true finite normalization by the isolated tests above. At T=1 use regular base p,t and shift Z=z+t. Coefficient 2 of the cross term is p²T. N=1−ad T satisfies
    N=B₀ t+p J₀,   B₀,J₀ units .
In fact on t=0, N=(C+ad+p)/(C+p); C+ad is an even constant and 2=p²T, hence the p-linear coefficient is a unit. Modulo p the residue of N is precisely t=1+T. Thus the equation is
    z²+p² M t z+(B₀ t+p J₀)t²=U p¹⁰
with units M=T,U=D'T⁸. Blow up with p,t directions. On t side p=tR,z=tV only R=0 outside the overlap, regular by the unit linear t B₀. On p side t=pS,z=pV the equation becomes V²+p² M S V+p(B₀ S+J₀)S²=U p⁸. The exceptional simple root S≠0 of B̄₀ S+J̄₀ gives a smooth-conic node (conic V²+p·(S−s₀)+terms in p²), resolved once. In the remaining origin J'=B₀S+J₀ is a unit. Perform four ordinary main p,S_old blowups: on p chart S=pⁿP,V=pⁿW,
    W²+p² M P W+p J' P²=D'T⁸ p^(8−2n) .
At n=1,2,3 only P=0 continues by the p-linear coefficient; on each side the nonoverlap has smooth tangent W'²+sR J̄' (extra R² at n=4). At n=4 divide W by the unit T⁴ and rename it W.     Z²+pT Z+p²M'Q Z+p J'' Q²+p M'Q+(1−D')T/2=0 .
Over p=0 the last term reduces to a constant, hence Z has a fixed residue z₀. After shifting by a lift 0 or 1, the p-linear coefficient has polynomial J̄''Q²+M̄'Q+constant with simple roots. ## Close pairs j=0, ε=0: base regular but 2 is already a product

Here H=2H₀ with H₀=−dN odd, D=8D₀. Put x=h, y=h+u: xy=2H₀, u=y−x. This base is regular with parameters x,y. The marked t already equals s. Blow up the reduced origin on x,y directions (w² vanishes to order four), and **normalize** by adjoing w/x² or w/y². On x chart y=xz, L=w/x²:
    2=x² z/H₀,   L²=G z(z−1)²+L₀ x⁴ z⁴,
    G=ad/H₀,  L₀=a D₀/H₀⁴ .
The y chart is symmetric (interchange x,y, note u² unchanged); at its nonoverlap x/y=0 the L'-square polynomial has derivative G in x/y, hence regular. The x-divisor likewise is regular except z=1. The monic finite extra generators contain the ordinary ratios, and isolated R₁ plus S₂ prove normality. Write S=z−1, v=L−S near that sole singularity:
    v²+x² M S v+(B₀ S+x²N₀)S²=x⁴ U
where M=z/H₀, B₀=−G, N₀=(1−G)z/(2H₀), U=L₀ z⁴; the latter is a unit. The first ordinary x,S blowup gives regular S-side nonoverlap by the S-linear B₀. On x side S=xP,v=xA:
    A²+x² M P A+x B₀ P³+x² N₀P²=x²U ;
only P=0 remains. Blow up the reduced origin x,P,A and normalize as follows.

On x side P=xQ,A=xV, S=x²Q, write e=w/4, P₀=S/2=H₀ Q/z. The base change between Q and P₀ is exact, with z=1+2P₀, Q=zP₀/H₀. The ordinary variable e=H₀²(Q+V)/z² has unit slope in V (w=x²L=x⁴(Q+V)). Its exact equation is
    z e²=D_* z+K_* P₀²,   D_*=aD₀,    K_*=ad H₀ ,
by w²=2ad u²+16aD₀, u=xS. Adjoin b=(e−1−P₀)/x; it is integral by the exact monic equation below obtained using 2=x² z/H₀. Set q=P₀+1, so z=2q−1. Expansion gives exactly
    H₀ b²+x z q b+[q−(1+K_*)/2]q²=(K_*−D_*)(1−2q)/2 .
Here K_*=−aN and D_*=−aN' with {N,N'}={1,2401}; thus v₂(K_*−D_*)=5 and the right is x⁸ times a unit. On x=0 the q derivative of the cubic is q² and the mixed term vanishes, hence all directions q≠0 are regular and q=0 leaves b=0.
On the P direction write x=PR,A=P A'. Then z=1+P²R, 2=P²R² z/H₀, and the native equation from L=S+v is (1+A')²=G z+L₀ R² z⁴. Adjoin J=(A'−R)/P. Its monic is
    J²+[(2/P)(1+R)]J+[(1+R)²−Gz−L₀ R²z⁴]/P²=0.
All terms are integral since 1−G and 1−L₀ are even, z−1=P²R and 2=P²R² z/H₀. At P=R=0 the constant term after division is −G R plus terms in (R²), so the R derivative is a unit and this corner is regular. Other directions overlap. Both extra-scale rings are finite and monic over the genuine regular base charts, with unit-slope recovery of preceding A ratios and isolated exceptional bad locus; hence actual normalizations.

Finally let C=(1+K_*)/2. If C even, write C=x² C' using the coefficient relation. The q=0 equation above has precisely cusp shape (a,b,c)=(1,2,8) with units M,B₀,U and B₁ allowed nonunit, as in GAUSSIAN54. One ordinary then one normalized cycle gives (0,0,2): explicitly first x-side q=xS,b=xV gives unit-linear xB₀S³ at S≠0 and regular q-side nonoverlap; next S=xP,V=x²Z gives Z²+M P Z+(B₀P+B₁)P²=U x². Its S-side R=0 is regular by unit linear R B₀ (the R² B₁ term is harmless even if B₁ unit). At last x,P blowup the x line has Z'²+M̄ QZ'+B̄₁ Q²=Ū, smooth incl Q=0; the P side is étale in Z'. If C odd shift b=b'+q so the q² coefficient becomes q+H₀−C+xz. Since H₀−C even this rewrites as q+xJ for a unit J. After dividing by H₀, the b' q coefficient is x times a unit (2H₀+xz), and the right remains Ux⁸. Blow up x,q once: q side at R=0 regular by unit q-linear term; x side q=xP,b'=xV gives
    V²+x M P V+x(B₀P+J')P²=U x⁶
with B₀,J',M units. At P≠0 only the simple zero of B̄₀P+J̄' is bad and is a smooth-conic node, resolved once. Near P=0 put J''=B₀P+J', a unit, and make three main ordinary x,P_old blowups, obtaining W²+x M QW+x J''Q²=U x^(6−2n), n=1,2,3. The mixed coefficient has unit residue. The simple roots yield the usual smooth conic with x·(Q−q₀) term even over residue extensions. All are resolved by one ordinary blowup. This completes the h₀=1 case.
