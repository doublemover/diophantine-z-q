# Ramified twists at 7: normalize the double components before taking invariants

**Mathematical result, not Lean-accepted.** Following the ramified-5 packet, every rational four-root twist has a compatible proper flat regular modification of its actual normalized projective model over **Z[1/gcd(6,Δ)]**, Δ=|disc Q(√qᵢ)|. Thus Z[1/6] works uniformly. The new case is 7 ramified. The untwisted 7-model has multiplicity-two components: we do NOT apply the semistable-at-5 argument to it. Over the quadratic ramified coefficient cover the correct *normalization* is regular except at eight geometric nodes of one chart; one upstairs blowup resolves those. The tame quotient then has eight isolated geometric singularities for the masks {1}, {0,1,2,3}, {0,2}, {0,3}, {2,3}, and none for the ten other nonempty masks. The indices are original root indices, with retained root r₁. The counts concern the stated intermediate models, not a minimal resolution or a smooth twisted special fibre.

## 1. Actual ramified normalization in the three-blowup atlas

Work first at D=Z_(7). Put e_i=v₇(q_i) mod 2, ε_i=(−1)^{e_i}, I={i:e_i=1}. As in RAMIFIED-FIVE, after generic rescaling by rational squares take coefficient p^{e_i}a_i with p=7, a_i=q_i·7^(−v₇(q_i)) units. D₁=D[α_i:α_i²=a_i] is the full finite étale unit-root torsor. D₂=D₁[ρ]/(ρ²−7) is a DVR at each point above 7, with uniformizer ρ. The sign action on the untwisted field is σ(ρ)=−ρ and σ(u_i)=ε_i u_i, with the same separate compensating G₀={±1}⁴ action on α_i and u_i. All four independent branch valuations still give degree 16 after extending constants. Form the finite normalization of the actual three-blowup untwisted model after scalar change to D₂ in these unchanged extended generic fields; finiteness follows from excellence. The sign actions extend canonically by integrality. A priori the ramified scalar change itself need NOT be normal or regular. We identify that normalization chart by chart. Uppercase/lowercase labels in this paragraph follow SEVEN-OPEN.md exactly: u=u₀, v=u₂, w=u₃, b=u₁.

* Away from the triple collision at T=0, the one-remaining-root affine charts and reciprocal unit-ratio chart are smooth over D, hence stay regular/normal over D₂. On the first p-chart away from its next blowup center, U=u/p is nonzero on the exceptional direction, V=v/p, W=w/p, b are nonzero and the chart is smooth étale-locally over D[U]. The first u-chart adds, outside that p-chart, eight geometric nodes u=P=0 (P=p/u), labeled b²=3 and (v/u,w/u)=(±1,±1). It is étale over uP=p there. After ramified base change the equation becomes uP=ρ². It is already normal: the hypersurface is a domain, Cohen–Macaulay and regular off its codimension-two closed node (étale localization preserves normality). Its one true closed-point blowup is regular, by the explicit charts of RAMIFIED-FIVE §2. The 8-point union is the sole remaining singular locus of the normalized scalar change, as the following charts show.
* In the second U-chart put h=W/U,j=V/U. The ORIGINAL total space has
  p=U²(h²−1),  j²=1−32U²(h²−1)²,  b²=p²U²−32.
  Multiplicity two along U=0 cannot be discarded. In the extended generic field set t=ρ/U. Adjoin t with t²=h²−1 and Ut=ρ, retaining both monics for j,b. This presentation is precisely the finite subalgebra obtained by adjoining this actual t, not a discarded vertical component. After inverting U, t=ρ/U restores the preceding base-changed chart. U is a nonzerodivisor before inversion: in the polynomial ambient over D₂ put U first; the successive equations modulo U are ρ, t²−h²+1, j²−1 and b²+32 (up to sign). They form a regular sequence, by nonzero polynomial and separate monics. At primes containing all equations and U, local permutation puts U last; elsewhere the nonzerodivisor test is automatic. This proves the native presentation embeds into the same generic algebra. Earlier scalar changes here are torsion-free in the generic field because the untwisted chart is torsion-free over D and D₂ finite flat, with the constant-extended generic field fixed above. Finiteness follows from the monic square relation. Near U=0, j,b are units, hence their adjunctions étale. If t≠0 in the residue field, eliminate U=ρ/t, and t²=h²−1 has unit t-derivative: the chart is smooth over D₂ in coordinate h. At t=0, h=±1, so one instead solves for h étale-locally, leaving Ut=ρ; this has regular total space. These checks above the exceptional divisor, together with the unchanged regular complement, identify the stated finite chart locally as the actual normalization. The special divisor over D₂ is now Ut=0, not the old doubled component.
* The second W-chart outside the second U-chart over its center has just Q′: W=a=c=0, a=U/W,c=V/W, b²=3. After the third original blowup write W=z w₀, a=z a₀, c=z c₀, using one of the three directions as scale z. In a neighbourhood of the fibre over Q′, 1−a² is a unit. The exact original equations include
  p=W²(1−a²),   c₀²−a₀²+32 w₀²(1−z²a₀²)²=0.
  Put τ=ρ/W in the extended generic field. Here τ²=1−a² is a **unit**. The finite algebra over the *original regular untwisted* chart obtained by adjoining τ is étale there. Indeed the monic quadratic has unit derivative; before adjoining ρ it has generic field equal to the wanted constant extension since ρ=Wτ (and conversely). At each component after D₁ the generic quadratic is a field: otherwise 7 would be a square in the untwisted geometric function field over Frac(D₁), contradicting independence after adjoining constants and 7's odd valuation in Frac(D₁). The monic algebra embeds in that field. Thus it is normal and finite over the pulled-back ring, and hence the actual normalization of the ramified base change near the whole third conic. The new uniformizer is ρ=z w₀ τ. At w₀≠0 the exceptional z=0 is a smooth special component (the conic is smooth, as below); at w₀=0 one has a₀,c₀≠0, c₀/a₀=±1. Use the a-direction chart a₀=1. Since c₀,b,τ are units, their equations have invertible derivatives, with c₀²=1−32w₀²(1−z²)², τ²=1−z². The two parameters z,w₀ meet transversely and locally the new special fibre is the crossing z w₀=0. For clarity the completed chart is a node over D₂, not a singular total space: substitute X=zτ, Y=w₀; z↦zτ has nonzero derivative at z=0 (τ unit, equation τ²=1−z²), hence is an étale local coordinate there, and XY=ρ. At w₀≠0 use the W-direction w₀=1, z=W=ρ/τ; at a geometric point of the conic either c₀ or a₀ is nonzero (both zero impossible since 32≠0). The derivative in that variable is a unit, and τ,b are units, so the remaining conic parameter is a smooth fibre coordinate. More explicitly, the equations for (W,τ) are Wτ=ρ, τ²=1−W²a₀²; when W=0 their Jacobian determinant 2τ² is a unit. Together these assertions establish the stated D₂-local smooth or nodal structure without suppressing old multiplicities.

The overlapping finite algebras agree by their universal identification as integral closure in the same fraction field, not by unrelated formal coordinate choices. These charts and the complements accounted for in SEVEN-OPEN cover every point. After the one simultaneous blowup of the eight first-chart nodes, denote the regular normalized upstairs model by W*. Its exceptional chart equations are uP=ρ² resolved into the torus and two end charts; all group actions lift because the node center is the intrinsic reduced nonregular locus. W* is projective over the affine base: the three original blowups are projective, finite normalization is projective, and so is the new blowup.

## 2. All sign masks and actual fixed loci on the normalized upstairs model

At a geometric point over 7 use a marking of the unit scalars. G₀ will subsequently descend this calculation; only σ is inertia. Outside T=0 the branch T=b₁=32 and infinity give precisely eight isolated fixed points for I={1} and I=all respectively, with anti-invariant smooth coordinate u₁ or x=s u₀ as before. On the first p-chart with U nonzero, U,V,W,b are all nonzero, so no nontrivial mask fixes a point. The eight first u-chart nodes can be preserved only if ε₁=+, ε₂=ε₃=ε₀; the nontrivial case is I={0,2,3}. Then (u,P,ρ) all change sign. Their resolved conics are fixed pointwise with anti-invariant normal parameter and invariant tangent coordinate, as in RAMIFIED-FIVE. Other masks exchange the conics, hence yield no fixed point on them.

On the second normalized U-chart over U=0 one has b²=3, j²=1 and
  U↦ε₀U, h↦ε₃ε₀ h, j↦ε₂ε₀ j, t↦−ε₀t, b↦ε₁b.
For t≠0 preservation forces ε₀=ε₂=−, ε₁=+. If ε₃=+, mask {0,2}, then h=0; there are two t with t²=−1, two j and two b, giving eight isolated points. Their smooth coordinate h and ρ are anti-invariant. If ε₃=−, mask {0,2,3}, the whole locus here is fixed; h is invariant and ρ anti-invariant, giving a regular quotient. At t=0 and U=0, h=±1; preservation of h,j,b forces ε₀=ε₂=ε₃, ε₁=+. In the nontrivial case {0,2,3} the parameters of the crossing Ut=ρ have signs (−,+). Otherwise all such points are exchanged, not invisible fixed endpoints.

On the third normalized exceptional divisor there are **four** geometric conics over the centre: b²=3, τ²=1 at z=0; on each the direction equation is c₀²−a₀²+32w₀²=0. The marks transform by ε₁ on b and −ε₃ on τ. Unless ε₁=+ and ε₃=−, the conics are exchanged. For those permitted signs the projective direction action, scaling all three entries by a common sign, is
  [w₀:a₀:c₀] ↦ [w₀:ε₀a₀:ε₂c₀].
* For I={3} the conic is fixed pointwise. Ratios are invariant, ρ changes sign. At its two ends w₀=0 use z=a, signs z↦−z, w₀↦w₀. Because τ is fixed, (zτ,w₀) has signs (−,+).
* For I={0,3} the a-direction is reflected. The fixed directions of this projective involution are the line a₀=0 and the isolated coordinate axis point [0:1:0]; the latter is not on the conic. The line meets it in two distinct points, both with w₀≠0. In w₀=1 the smooth coordinate is a₀ at those points (c₀ is nonzero), and is anti-invariant. Total: eight isolated fixed points across the four labels.
* For I={2,3} the same argument reflects c₀: c₀=0 meets the conic in two distinct points and its coordinate axis point is absent. In w₀=1 the coordinate c₀ is smooth and anti-invariant. Again eight.
* For I={0,2,3} the action is projectively reflection of w₀. The w-axis point [1:0:0] is absent; the two fixed directions w₀=0 are precisely its ends with c₀/a₀=±1. Here z=a is invariant, w₀ is anti-invariant and τ is fixed. The node coordinates (zτ,w₀) have signs (+,−). This is not an isolated (−,−) quotient. No other points of these conics are fixed.

The listed cases are exhaustive; in particular the isolated-centre masks differ genuinely from those at 5. Each of the five masks listed in the header has eight isolated points. The calculations are geometric labels and do not presume rationality over F₇.

## 3. Exact quotient singularities and arithmetic gluing

Use the same finite scheme quotient W*/(H×G₀), H=〈σ〉, as in RAMIFIED-FIVE §4: projectivity ensures invariant affine neighbourhoods, finite invariant quotients commute with flat changes, G₀ is free on the coefficient torsor, and after finite étale residue splitting the completed local ring is computed from the actual fixed or exchanged completed local factors. On exchanged pairs projection gives a regular factor. At the isolated points the stated smooth coordinate v (u₁, x, h, a₀ or c₀) and ρ are anti-invariant, the completion is D̂₂[[v]], and the invariant ring is D̂₁[[A,z]]/(z²−7A) with A=v², z=ρv. On a fixed smooth curve use its invariant smooth coordinate to obtain a regular series ring over D̂₁. At a fixed crossing with ρ=XY and tangent signs (−,+) or (+,−), eliminating ρ and taking even powers of the anti-invariant coordinate yields D̂₁[[A,Y]]/(A Y²−7) (interchange labels if needed); the linear term −7 ensures regularity. The ends of the first resolved pointwise-fixed conics have precisely the same normal form. These formulas explicitly check the whole fixed curves, not just the finite point list. All morphisms here are evaluated at closed points after an unramified extension making the label rational; checking all such closed points handles all vertical stalks by localization.

Thus blow up the intrinsic reduced union of the eight isolated quotient singular points, or do nothing if none. The center descends as in the preceding packets: finite perfect residue fields ensure the reduced finite union survives étale splitting, and blowups commute with flat base change. The completed-node comparison and excellent local regularity argument of RAMIFIED-FIVE §4 apply verbatim with 7, resolving every actual stalk, not only a completed exceptional fibre. The resulting scheme over D is a proper flat regular modification of the exact X_q: the four independent fixed twisted roots identify the generic field, normality of the upstairs local rings extends the maps to the original parameter normalization, and the finite orbit quotient descends those maps.

As at 5, globalize on an actual open neighbourhood of 7, inverting 2,3,5, every prime ≠7 in the numerators/denominators of the q_i, and every other discriminant prime. The three original centres above 7 are the ones of SEVEN-OPEN on the moved-base scheme by the identical closed fibres; all other singular fibres of the untwisted model are inverted. The finite normalization is defined globally by excellence, and away from 7 the scalar changes are étale, every displayed blowup is the identity, and the quotient equals the actual X_q. Over intersections with the preceding Z[1/gcd(42,Δ)] model when 7 ramifies, both sides equal X_q because all other modification primes are inverted. Glue by that identification, obtaining Z[1/gcd(6,Δ)]. Flatness and properness are local on the base, integrality/torsion-freeness and the generic fibre persist. If 7 is unramified the preceding model suffices. This result concerns the total spaces. Ramified 2 and 3 are not covered in general; no twist-minimality, semistability of the final twist, height/rank consequence or Lean acceptance.
