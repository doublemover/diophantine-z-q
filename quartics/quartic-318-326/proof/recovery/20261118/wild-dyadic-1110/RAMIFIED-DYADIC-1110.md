# A ramified dyadic sign family: the first three roots negative

Fix b=(0,32,76832,−343) and L_q=Q(T)(r_i), r_i²=q_i(T−b_i). The *actual* normalization X_q of the parameter P¹_Z in L_q admits an explicit proper birational modification, proper flat regular over **all Z**, when q is (−1,−1,−1,1) times four independently dyadically unramified rational squareclasses. Equivalently, the signed squarefree representatives are odd and congruent to (3,3,3,1) modulo 4. This is a new class, not the previous (1,1,1,1) congruence class, and no base change by the ramified Gaussian integers is used to descend regularity.

## Exact fixed algebras on both parameter charts

Start with q⁰=(−1,−1,−1,1), D=Z_(2). In the split field of untwisted roots a_i²=T−b_i with constants Q(i), the involution acts by conjugation on constants, negates a_0,a_1,a_2 and fixes a_3. The fixed field is exactly L_q⁰: r_j=i a_j for j<3 and r_3=a_3 are invariant, give four independent squareclasses by their branch valuations, and adjoining i recovers the entire field. In particular the degree over Q(T) of the split field is 32 and of the fixed subfield is 16. For each parameter patch let B be the integral closure there in the split field, equivalently the closure over Gaussian coefficients by transitivity. Then B^σ is the integral closure in the fixed field (intersection with the fixed field inside the common field). The next calculations actually present that ring upstairs-invariant by downstairs integral generators. No Reynolds averaging in characteristic 2 and no assumption that a regular scheme stays regular across wild quotient.

Put u=r_0, h=(r_1−u)/2, k=(r_2−u)/2, w=r_3−u−1. The true finite chart is the independent monic algebra over D[u] with
    T=−u²,   h²+uh=8,   k²+uk=19208,
    w²+2L=0,    L=(u+1)w+u²+u−171.
These identities follow by squaring, and give all r_i back. The algebra is finite free of rank 16 over D[T], and injects in the intended field: after localization to Q(T) the images generate the degree-16 field and the ranks agree; freeness gives injectivity globally. Its complete-intersection presentation gives S₂. In char zero the cover is smooth by the usual distinct simple branches. At dyadic primes w=0 and h(h+u)=k(k+u)=0. For u nonzero the first two quadratic extensions are étale. At all such points with u²+u+1 nonzero in the residue field, L is a unit; w²+2L=0 cuts out a regular hypersurface in the regular ambient D[u,w], because 2 is a regular parameter at the contraction (quotient by 2 regular) and the equation has nonzero linear part. At u=0 only one closed point P with u=h=k=w=0 occurs. At u²+u+1=0 there are four degree-two points over F₂, indexed by h,k=0 or u in F₄. These are all codimension two, by finite flatness over the polynomial ring. Hence R₁ and S₂ prove normality; this is the globally correct fixed algebra on the finite chart, rather than a naive integral twist of the unramified normalization.

Use x=1/u, s=T⁻¹=−x², y_j=r_j/u on the reciprocal part D(d), where d=∏_{j=1}³ (1−b_j s). This open contains s=0; its complement is inside the finite parameter patch. The true ring is monic over D[x,1/d(−x²)] with H=(y_1−1)/2,K=(y_2−1)/2,W=y_3−1−x and
    H²+H=8x²,   K²+K=19208x²,   W²+2J=0,
    J=(1+x)W+1+x−171x².
Indeed y_3²=−1+343x². Rank, injection in the correct field and S₂ follow as above: after inverting x the fractions generate r_i; the x=0 generic-characteristic points are étale over the x-line since the y_j there are units. On x≠0 the ordinary normalization in char zero is smooth. At dyadic x=0 we have W=0, J=1 in the residue field; the first two extensions are étale and w²+2·unit regularity applies. At dyadic x≠0 the change x=1/u, H=h/u,K=k/u,W=w/u is exact. Hence the presentations agree on the overlap including at 2. This supplies R₁ and therefore the actual unrestricted finite-chart / restricted reciprocal-chart normalization cover. Infinity is regular without blowup. The four degree-two finite nodes may appear in this overlap, but are counted only once.

## Four conjugate-pair nodes and the unique deeper point

At u²+u+1=0 the substitution t=L is étale locally on the D[t,w] parameter chart since ∂L/∂u=w+2u+1≡1 there and the equation for u is monic. Together with the h,k étale adjunctions this gives an actual étale chart over D[t,w]/(w²+2t). The extension of the reduced point ideal (2,t,w) at each of the four points equals its reduced maximal ideal, since the local étale residue fibre is a field. Blow up their reduced union; the two true charts of the standard node are D[Y] and D[t,Y]/(tY²+2) as in DYADIC-OPEN §3. Their pullbacks resolve all four degree-two points, not just chosen geometric labels. P is disjoint. There L is a unit and 2=−w²/L; the maximal ideal is (w,u,h,k), and the three equations have only the one independent linear part, the class of 2 in a 5-dimensional regular ambient. Thus its embedding dimension is four while dimension is two.

We give precisely the replacements needed for the independently checked thirteen-step chart argument TERMINAL-RESOLUTION. At step n in {0,1,2,3} set u=wⁿS,h=wⁿA,k=wⁿB. Then
    Lₙ=(1+w)wⁿS+w+w^(2n)S²−171,
    w²+2Lₙ=0,    A²+SA=−w^(6−2n)/Lₙ³,
                   B²+SB=−2401 w^(6−2n)/Lₙ³.
These are equalities on the actual native ratio charts with Lₙ a unit. For the first three point blowups the w and S directions cover: the equations in the Rees algebra force the initial homogeneous A,B generators into any prime containing the initial w and S generators. The S chart w=Sz,A=Sa,B=Sb has the same displayed equations as in TERMINAL §I except for the new Lₙ and the minus sign in each of the last two right sides. Explicitly these sides are −S^(4−2n)z^(6−2n)/Lₙ³ and 2401 times that, and the left sides are a²+a and b²+b. In either chart the distinguished scale is a nonzerodivisor: in the polynomial ambient first impose the scale, then 2 times the substituted unit, then the independent monics. This is a regular sequence locally along the chart and can be permuted there to put the scale last. Inverting the scale restores the antecedent localized ring, proving equality with the actual ratio subalgebra. Modulo S the monics have derivative one and give a regular scale divisor; modulo w all S≠0 directions have invertible derivatives. On the w chart S=0 gives only the F₂ origin for n=1,2, and the F₂ point A=B=1 for n=3. All other points over the centers are regular.

At n=3 put G=−L₃⁻³, invert A and omit B temporarily to form C with equations w²+2L₃=0 and A²+SA=G. The scale test as above gives w a nonzerodivisor and C/(w)=F₂[A,A⁻¹] (S=A⁻¹−A). Thus at q=(w=0,A=1) C is regular with parameters w,p=A−1. In the three-dimensional regular ambient T'=C[e] at (w,p,e), put d'=B/A−1, e=d'−2400, t=1+G/A², v=e+t+4800. Direct division of the B equation by A² gives
    d'²+t d'−2400(t−1)=0,  equivalently  ev=−2400·2401=(75·2401/L₃⁵)w¹⁰.
Recover B=A(1+e+2400). Modulo the square of the ambient maximal ideal L₃≡1+w, G≡1+w and A⁻²≡1, since 2∈(w²). Therefore e,v,p are regular parameters. In the DVR T'/(e,v), w∈(p²), 2∈(p⁴), and now the **changed exact identity** is L₃³ A²=+1/4801, not −1/4801. Modulo p³ the right side is 1; L₃≡1+w and A²≡1+p². Consequently w≡p² mod p³ (minus is the same at this precision), order exactly two. Write w=αe+βv+γp² in T' with γ a unit.

The remaining ambient chart calculations depend only on ev=unit·w¹⁰ and this precise order. Applying TERMINAL §III, actual e and v charts after the first blowup have linear exceptional equation; the p chart has E₁V₁=H p⁸(αE₁+βV₁+γp)¹⁰ with H=75·2401/L₃⁵. At the sole crossing blow up again. The p chart has E₂V₂=H p¹⁶(αE₂+βV₂+γ)¹⁰, where the parenthesis is a unit at its sole crossing. Seven ordinary origin blowups reduce exponent 16 by twos to 2; the eighth resolves the smooth exceptional conic XY=δZ², δ≠0 even in char two. Each divided equation is nonzero mod scale, hence its quotient has no scale torsion, as shown in the bound chart lemma in TERMINAL §III. The two side charts have linear exceptional equations before the last smooth conic. This treats all directions over every center. Each intermediate exceptional fibre has exactly one nonregular point before the next step: at n=1,2 the origin has edim 4 (only 2 has a linear term); all later exceptional origins have equation in the square of the 3-dimensional ambient maximal ideal. Thus the claimed sequence of 3+2+8 ordinary blowups is intrinsic over P. With the first simultaneous four-node blowup this gives 14 dyadic operations for q⁰.

## Which other twists this covers

The full 16 deck signs of q⁰ preserve the initial reduced finite union over u²+u+1=0 (u changes only by sign) and P over T=0, and lift to each blowup by preservation of its reduced ideal. Every further center is the unique nonregular point of the exceptional fibre over its predecessor; hence is preserved by every already lifted sign. This circumvents the need for the high-order crossing coordinates to diagonalize the group action.

Let the residual coefficient ratios q_i/q⁰_i generate the scalar field F by their square roots. When their signed squarefree representatives are odd 1 mod4, F is unramified at 2 (maximal-order quadratic discriminants; composita of unramified local fields). Choose the actual open neighbourhood of 2 in Spec Z obtained by removing all odd primes of bad contact, coefficient numerator/denominator divisors, and discriminants of the relevant scalar fields, together with all odd modification primes in RAMIFIED-THREE. The normalization of that neighbourhood in F is finite étale Galois. The actual X_q and X_q⁰ on the neighbourhood become isomorphic with the same parameter after this scalar extension: on both charts normalize in the same identified extension field, using étale compatibility of normalization. The descent action is scalar Galois combined with the individual deck signs of q⁰. Globalize the displayed finite dyadic closed centers of X_q⁰ on this open (special-fibre closed points remain closed globally; localization at 2 changes none of their local stalks or exceptional fibres). Outside 2 the model is unchanged, with smooth total space on this neighbourhood since all odd contact and coefficient primes were excluded. Descend its invariant ideals successively across the finite **étale** cover, as in SIGN-DESCENT §3, and blow them up downstairs. The descended model is regular because its étale pullback is regular. This use of unramified ratios is deliberately separate from the ramified Gaussian invariant calculation for the base case.

On the intersection with D(2) the modifications just constructed are identities on X_q, and the prior arbitrary-twist model on D(2) is also X_q there by the finite exclusions. Glue there using these actual identifications. The resulting integral model dominates X_q, is proper over Z by locality on the base, and is flat by torsion-freeness over the localized DVRs. Its generic fibre is the original parameter-preserving twisted smooth curve. Example: q=(3,7,11,5) has signs of signed squarefree representatives (3,3,3,1) modulo 4, hence the constructed model extends through 2; q=(3,1,1,1) is **not covered** by this argument. The closed-point counts above are for q⁰, not assertions about the residue degrees after descent. This neither settles all dyadic ramification nor characterizes a minimal or semistable model; no height/rank or main theorem follows.
