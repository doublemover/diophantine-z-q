# Reciprocal chart and a proper regular model over Z[1/70]

**Claim and prior dependency.** This is a mathematical continuation of the independently reviewed finite-chart argument in ARITHMETIC-OPEN.md, SHA-256 26421951be1395855b8a0396ce993c23f320935dc02617a388cb9f7e9e04387c. All assertions here concern the original constant-one twist only. Write R=Z[1/70], M for the base change to R of OriginalIntegerClosureGluing318.model of that twist. M is the projective-line two-chart normalized model in the original common function field L; its structure map is proper by OriginalIntegerGlobalFiniteNormalization318.proper_separated. The two successive blowups at the same selected p and reduced residual Q give a regular total space N, proper and flat over R, with unchanged generic fibre. Neither a global Lean theorem nor regularity at 2,5,7 is claimed. Properness over R, not projectivity of an arbitrary proper scheme, suffices here.

## 1. Identify a regular open in the exact reciprocal normalization

Use s=T⁻¹ only as the reciprocal parameter acting on L. The normalized reciprocal chart is literally the integral closure of Z[s] in L for this action (OriginalIntegerReciprocalNormalization318.reciprocalBaseAlgebra and Closure). Its raw rank-sixteen order has generators zᵢ=s rᵢ on the Laurent overlap, satisfying zᵢ²=s(1−cᵢs), where c=(0,32,76832,−343). This is the existing reciprocal embedding, not the four finite-chart roots reused without scaling. In particular x=z₀ satisfies x²=s. Put d(s)=∏_{i=1}³(1−cᵢs), let D=R[s,1/d], and let H be the raw reciprocal order after this base change. The exchange to R and inversion of d are localizations; d(s) is nonzero since d(0)=1. H is a domain embedded in L with fraction field L (OriginalIntegerReciprocalOrderField318.fieldEmbedding_injective, actual_fractionField). Its generator x is not zero: s is the inverse of the nonzero generic parameter in L. The reciprocal parameter need NOT be a unit in H.

Define the explicit D-algebra
  C=R[x,1/d(x²),w₁,w₂,w₃] / (wᵢ²−(1−cᵢx²), i=1,2,3),
with s mapping to x². The three monic adjunctions give a free rank-eight module over R[x,1/d(x²)], which itself is free of rank two over D (adjoin x with x²=s). In particular multiplication by x is injective on C, by freeness over the polynomial localization. Map H→C by s↦x², z₀↦x and zᵢ↦x wᵢ. These formulas preserve the literal four relations. After inverting x the reverse map takes wᵢ to zᵢ/z₀ and x to z₀. It respects the three relations by z₀²=s; both composites fix all generators. The map C→C[1/x] is injective; the latter is the domain H[1/z₀] inside L by the just-computed isomorphism. Consequently C is an actual subring of this same L with fraction field L, with exactly the chosen reciprocal coefficient action. We have *not* inverted x on C. The source component PrimeThreeReciprocalRatios318 specializes the field identities to the existing actualMap; the localization isomorphism and remaining geometric identifications are given in this argument, not in elaborated Lean.

Since d(x²) is invertible every 1−cᵢx² is invertible, hence each wᵢ is a unit. Each monic quadratic adjunction is standard étale: the right side is already a unit in its base, 2 is a unit in R, so the derivative 2wᵢ is invertible. The composite C is étale over the regular ring R[x,1/d(x²)], hence regular and normal. Since C is integral over D, is integrally closed, and has fraction field L, it equals the integral closure of D *inside L*. Indeed every element of L integral over D is integral over C and hence belongs to C; the reverse inclusion is finiteness. Integral closure commutes with localization, so Spec C is precisely D(d) of the original reciprocal normalized chart over R.

This calculation includes all infinity points, since d(0)=1. It also distinguishes the reduced infinity locus from the fibre: modulo x the equations give three independent signs wᵢ=±1; 2 is invertible and the quotient splits into eight copies of R. The inverse image of the parameter divisor s=0 is instead cut out by x², not x, so it is not being identified with those eight reduced sections. No smoothness of the morphism to the projective parameter line at s=0 follows.

## 2. Glue via global centers, not along a fictitious unchanged overlap

The finite normalized chart and this *restricted* reciprocal chart cover M. Indeed the reciprocal complement of the full finite chart lies over s=0: the common normalized overlap is the localization at s by OriginalIntegerReciprocalNormalization318.overlap_localization and is glued to the localization at T of the finite chart. Remaining points are in D(d). On the intersection both charts embed into L using the same scalars as the original two-chart gluing (OriginalIntegerClosureGluing318.intersection_square). Nothing identifies different choices of residue automorphism at the closed point.

The selected p has characteristic three, T=2, so it belongs even to the full Laurent overlap (a reason not to glue an unchanged *full* reciprocal chart). But at p, s=1/2=2 mod 3, and c₁=32=2 mod 3, hence 1−c₁ s=0; in particular p is not in Spec C. It is closed in M: it lies in the closed characteristic-three fibre, a finite-type F₃-scheme, with residue field F₉. Blow it up globally with its reduced structure. The full preimage of the finite open is the actual first blowup of the finite chart, by flat base change for open immersions. The residual point Q there is closed globally for the same reason, being in the closed fibre over p and having finite residue field over κ(p). Use its reduced closed structure for the second global blowup, producing N. Restricting to the finite chart recovers both blowups with their exact centers (not merely isomorphic exceptional curves), while over Spec C both blowups are the identity. By the preceding finite-chart theorem and §1 all stalks of N are now covered and regular, including every point over the reciprocal boundary. No second modification is imposed on a singular-overlap point by gluing.

Finiteness of the normalization and properness of M over R are for the actual whole model, not inferred from properness of an affine chart. Each coherent closed center on the Noetherian model has a blowup proper over its base (locally on target, ordinary finite-generated Rees Proj); the composite N→Spec R is proper. The charts of M are integral subrings of L glued along the dense common localization, so M is integral. The blowups remain integral by their ordinary affine Rees chart description, and are isomorphisms off p and hence on the entire characteristic-zero fibre. The structure map dominates Spec R (already the generic point maps to the generic point). Each stalk of N is therefore torsion-free as an R-module, hence flat over the PID R. Thus this is a proper flat regular model of that unchanged generic fibre over Spec R, not a claimed resolution in excluded characteristics and not a Néron/minimal/semistable or KS-metric theorem.
