# Ramified 3 by a direct cluster blowup over the coefficient extension

**Result, independently checkable mathematics rather than a compiled global theorem.** For every rational q_i≠0, the original four-root normalization for r_i²=q_i(T−b_i), b=(0,32,76832,−343), has an explicit proper flat regular modification over **Z[1/gcd(2,Δ)]**, where Δ=|disc Q(√q₀,…,√q₃)|. In particular **Z[1/2] works for all twists**; if the scalar field is unramified at 2, the construction works over all Z. The new argument covers all masks I={i:v₃(q_i) odd}≠∅. It uses a different upstairs construction from the earlier selected-point blowup over the original 3-adic base: it neither assumes ramified base change commutes with that normalization nor identifies this sequence with the earlier two blowups. The unresolved explicit place is now **ramified 2** only.

## 1. The exact normalized upstairs chart

As in RAMIFIED-FIVE use D=Z_(3), a_i=q_i·3^(−v₃(q_i)), e_i=v₃(q_i) mod 2, ε_i=(−1)^{e_i}. D₁=D[α_i:α_i²=a_i] is a possibly disconnected finite étale torsor for G₀={±1}⁴. Work at any place above 3. D₂=D₁[ρ]/(ρ²−3) is ramified with local DVR uniformizer ρ. Use the untwisted roots over D₂. The semilinear H=〈σ〉 action is σ(ρ)=−ρ and σ(u_i)=ε_i u_i; G₀ compensates the unit-root signs on the u_i. Their invariant generic roots α_iρ^{e_i}u_i give exactly the rational twist after square rescaling. As previously, over a component of D₁ the degree is 32 before H-invariants, and the 16-degree twisted subfield is the fixed field through independence of all four branch valuations over constant extensions.

Write u=u₁,v=u₂,w=u₃ and b=u₀. Over the extended coefficient base the untwisted finite parameter chart is the raw monic order
  v²−u²=−25600ρ²,   w²−u²=125ρ²,    b²=u²+32.
Here T=u²+32, v²−u²=−76800, w²−u²=375 in the actual common field. This is already the **normalization in the extended generic field**. On each component of the coefficient base it embeds by freeness over D₂[u] and the exact integral generic field; the three monics are a regular sequence, giving S₂. Outside the triple collision at T=2 in characteristic 3 there is at most one vanishing root, so eliminate T at that root and adjoin the other three unit roots étale-locally over a regular polynomial ring. In characteristic zero contacts are distinct. The collision primes have ρ=u=v=w=0 and b²=2: finitely many closed points of height at least two by flat going-down over D₂[u]. Thus R₁ holds; Serre's criterion proves normality. This proof concerns the total ring over D₂, not normalization of a special fibre. Near infinity the prior reciprocal chart with x²=s and three inverted unit ratios is smooth, hence still the same integral closure over D₂ by its monic presentation and common field. These two parameter charts cover the normalized projective model.

## 2. Two actual blowups before the semilinear quotient

Blow up the reduced union with ideal (ρ,u,v,w), two geometric points labeled by b²=2. The residue quotient is étale in b, so this is the literal reduced structure. The blowup is covered by ρ and u directions: if both degree-one forms vanish at a homogeneous prime, the displayed quadratic Rees identities force v and w directions to vanish too. More explicitly, (v t)²−(u t)²=−25600(ρ t)², and similarly for w. In the ρ-chart put U=u/ρ,V=v/ρ,W=w/ρ. Its equations are
  V²−U²=−25600,   W²−U²=125,   b²=ρ²U²+32.
The monic ring is free over D₂[U], so has no ρ-torsion, and inverting ρ restores the original substitution. Its image is exactly the ratio subring. In the u-chart keep u, P=ρ/u, V'=v/u, W'=w/u; one has
  uP=ρ,   V'²=1−25600P²,   W'²=1+125P²,   b²=u²+32.
The base uP=ρ is a domain (primitive linear polynomial), so the monic adjunctions are u-torsion-free and u inversion restores the original. These are the genuine two ordinary charts, agreeing on the overlap P=1/U. Above the center with P=0 there are eight geometric points, b²=2 and V',W'=±1. The cover is étale there over uP=ρ, so total space is **already** regular though its special fibre crosses. Every other exceptional point lies in the ρ-chart.

Here
  (W−V)(W+V)=25725=8575ρ²,    3∤8575.
In characteristic 3, V²=W²=U²−1. If V or W is nonzero then both are nonzero and b is a unit, so the chart is smooth over D₂ in parameter U. At V=W=0, U²=1,b²=2 give four geometric points. Put X=W−V, Y=(W+V)/8575. This is an invertible linear change and XY=ρ² exactly; U,b are locally standard étale adjunctions because they are units. Thus the four points are honest total-space nodes with embedding dimension three. Blow up their reduced union. Their ideal is the étale pullback of (ρ,X,Y) locally because the closed residue extensions are étale. As in RAMIFIED-FIVE, the actual ρ-chart is a torus ξη=1 (ξ=X/ρ,η=Y/ρ); the X-chart has XP=ρ, Y=XP², with symmetric Y-chart. Nonzerodivisor and inverse-localization checks identify the actual blowup. All charts are regular. Both blowups are H×G₀-equivariant: first the whole fixed-base collision fibre's reduced support, then its reduced nonregular locus on the actual intermediate surface. We have a projective and regular upstairs W* with no unexamined nonclosed vertical points.

## 3. Every mask on the true special fibre

Apart from the central T=2 fibre the only special horizontal branch left is T=0; on its eight sheets a nonempty mask fixes a point exactly for I={0}, with the smooth coordinate b=u₀ anti-invariant. Infinity with its three ratio labels is fixed exactly for I={0,1,2,3}, and its smooth coordinate x=s u₀ is anti-invariant. Other nonbranch labels are exchanged. All fixed-point counts below are geometric; ρ always changes sign.

On the central ρ-chart the extra minus from dividing by the **ramified** scale matters:
  U↦−ε₁U,   V↦−ε₂V,   W↦−ε₃W,   b↦ε₀b.
At V,W both nonzero, b also nonzero, so preservation requires ε₀=+, ε₂=ε₃=−. If ε₁=+ this is I={2,3} and U=0 is necessary; V²=W²=−1 and b²=2 label eight isolated points. U is an anti-invariant smooth parameter. If ε₁=− this is I={1,2,3} and the entire smooth central curve here is fixed; U is invariant and ρ is anti-invariant. On the eight uP=ρ crossing points, stability of b,V',W' requires ε₀=+, ε₂=ε₃=ε₁. In the nontrivial case I={1,2,3}, u changes sign and P=ρ/u does not. Thus these fixed crossings have one invariant tangent parameter and do not produce singular quotient points.

At a conic from V=W=0, the original labels U,b require ε₀=+, ε₁=−. Otherwise σ exchanges conics and fixes no point on them. Three alternatives remain:
* I={1}: both V,W change sign, hence X,Y,ρ all change sign. The whole exceptional conic is pointwise fixed. The torus coordinate ξ is invariant and ρ anti-invariant; at its X-chart end XP=ρ, X changes sign and P is fixed. The Y end is identical.
* I={1,2,3}: V,W and so X,Y are invariant. On the conic torus ξ,η change sign, fixing nothing; the two ends are fixed separately. In the X-chart XP=ρ the signs are (+,−), with the symmetric Y-chart likewise. These are regular-quotient crossings, independently of the fixed central smooth curve.
* I={1,2} or {1,3}: write s=−ε₃. Then X↦dY, Y↦d⁻¹X with d=8575s. The conic ends exchange. In the torus ξ↦−d/ξ, fixing exactly the two distinct geometric roots of ξ²=−d at each of four conics. The coordinate v=(ξ+d/ξ)/2 is anti-invariant, vanishes at these labels and has derivative 1 there; the completion has parameters v,ρ both anti-invariant. Thus each mask contributes eight isolated points. No total-space chart assumes an exceptional coordinate is invertible at an end.

Precisely five nonzero masks have isolated points on this upstairs model: {0}, full, {2,3}, {1,2}, {1,3}, eight each. The remaining ten have none. This result depends on original root indexing; substituting the mask table from 5 would be false.

## 4. Descent of the final blowup, and what remains

Apply the projective finite-group quotient and completed-local-ring argument of RAMIFIED-FIVE §4 to W*/(H×G₀). The separate G₀ quotient is finite étale descent in the unit-root constants. After unramified label splitting and completion, a pair of exchanged local factors gives one regular factor. In an isolated smooth fixed chart D̂₂[[v]] with v,ρ both anti-invariant, its invariant algebra is D̂₁[[A,z]]/(z²−3A), A=v²,z=ρv. On a fixed smooth branch the invariant coordinate and anti-invariant ρ give a regular power series ring over D̂₁. At a fixed crossing XY=ρ with tangent signs (−,+) or (+,−), the invariant algebra is D̂₁[[A,Y]]/(A Y²−3) after possibly exchanging X,Y, hence regular by the linear coefficient −3. These cover all labels and fixed conic ends listed above. The eight isolated points when present are precisely the geometric nonregular points of the quotient. Blow up their intrinsic reduced finite union over F₃. It is compatible with residue splitting (perfect field); the polynomial node z²=3A resolves in the genuine 3 and A charts. As previously, flat base change, regularity of the excellent local-ring completion morphism, and faithful-flat descent justify regularity of the actual affected blowup stalks. No pointwise rationality assumption on this center.

Dominance of the regular upstairs model gives a morphism to the original twist normalization: every fixed generic element integral over the parameter ring belongs to each normal local ring above that affine parameter patch. Thus the morphisms glue, are invariant, and descend to a proper modification of the actual X_q with the precise twisted generic fibre. Integrality and dominance imply flatness over the localized PID. To globalize, invert 2,5,7, all primes ≠3 in the q_i numerators and denominators, and all other discriminant primes. This is an actual open neighbourhood of 3 on which the unit/D₂ algebras and the two closed centres described above are defined. The finite normalization over the parameter commutes with étale restrictions, is explicitly identified at 3, and is smooth away from 3 on this neighbourhood since other collisions are inverted. Away from 3 these constant covers are étale, the auxiliary blowups are identities and the twist quotient is the unchanged X_q. On the intersection with the preceding Z[1/gcd(6,Δ)] model (for ramified 3), both modifications are therefore X_q: all its other possible modification primes were inverted in this neighbourhood. Gluing yields the announced Z[1/gcd(2,Δ)] model. If 3 is unramified the previous construction already suffices. Nothing here supplies an explicit modification of a generally ramified dyadic twist, a minimal or semistable final model, a height/rank statement or a proof of the final 318/326 theorem.

As a practical check, the recipe gives an all-Z model whenever the odd unit part of each q_i has squarefree representative 1 mod 4 and its 2-valuation is even (equivalently, the signed squarefree representative d_i of each squareclass is odd and 1 mod 4). Indeed Q(√d_i) has discriminant d_i when d_i≡1 mod4, otherwise 4d_i; a compositum of extensions unramified at 2 is unramified there, and a ramified subfield forces ramification in the compositum. For instance q=(−3,1,1,1) has Δ=3 and is handled over all Z; q=(3,1,1,1) has Δ=12 and the present construction gives Z[1/2], not an explicit resolution over 2. These examples distinguish signed twist squareclasses with the same 3-mask.
