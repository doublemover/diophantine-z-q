# The zero pairing matrices and what the full tower does not distinguish

Fix the actual rational E_n and the same three bounds: DoubleQuotient finite with cardinality at most 8, and allPlacesImage with cardinality 8. We describe the global (summed) matrices, **not** the numbers at individual places. Local invariants at individual bad places can be nonzero and cancel. No arbitrary auxiliary twist or different pole is substituted.

**Matrices at all finite powers.** The repository's actual_sha_higher_levels proves that for each k, Sha(E_n)[2^k] is finite cyclic. For any x,y in the 2-primary Sha there are indices i,j killing them. Both lie in the same cyclic group of level max(i,j). The classical alternating elliptic Cassels–Tate pairing is zero on each such pair: writing them as multiples of a common generator gives an integer multiple of its self-pairing, which is zero. This requires alternation but not the external theorem identifying its radical, and does not mistake the whole group for a cyclic group. Pull back along both Kummer quotient maps. The [2^i],[2^j] Selmer pairing is zero for all i,j≥1. In particular the actual three-dimensional binary Sel₂ pairing matrix under the order-eight assumption is the zero 3-by-3 matrix in **every** basis; no explicit local entry or realization of the extra Sel₂ class is deduced. This concerns the cohomological Sel₂ identified with the given all-places image, not an arbitrary list of local symbols. Our separate [2],[2] calculation identifies its exact cochain recipe; for higher powers we invoke classical pairing functoriality, not higher-order cochain formulae or uniqueness of a higher Weil pairing.

**Every inverse transition is onto.** The specialization of [Morgan–Smith §6.1 and Propositions 3.5–3.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.08530) to [2^i] and [2^j] says the left radical is the image of [2^j]:Sel_(2^(i+j))→Sel_(2^i). All pairings just proved zero, so these transition maps are surjective. For j=1 these are inverse/reduction maps induced by [2] on torsion, **not** the project’s direct transition induced by inclusion of torsion. In Kummer coordinates reduction on the rational-point quotients comes from the same point, and on the Sha quotient it is multiplication by two. This gives coherent *Selmer* lifts, not necessarily coherent rational points. Full duality further says the primary Sha itself is either zero or Prüfer; this agrees with the two arithmetic possibilities, but the preceding matrix computation did not presuppose that classification.

**Stronger nondetection: actual inverse system up to isomorphism.** Put T=E_n(Q)_tors, a fixed finite group, and R_k=Z/2^k. In either of the two remaining branches, there are isomorphisms of the (unmarked) groups Sel_(2^k) with (T/2^kT) ⊕ R_k, simultaneously for all k≥1, such that the inverse transitions induced by [2] are the ordinary reductions on both factors. This includes the common embedded torsion Kummer subgroup; the rational Kummer image of the last summand is *not* preserved between branches. Here is a proof, not an inference from matching orders.

• If E_n(Q) has rank one and primary Sha is zero, pick a generator of the free summand in a Mordell–Weil splitting. At every level the exact Kummer sequence identifies Sel_(2^k) with E_n(Q)/2^k. These isomorphisms commute with reduction, and the extra generator is rational.

• If E_n(Q) is finite and the primary Sha is Prüfer, the Kummer sequence is 0→T/2^kT→Sel_(2^k)→P[2^k]→0 for P≃C_(2^∞). Fix generators p_k of its cyclic levels with 2p_(k+1)=p_k, using one Prüfer isomorphism. Lift p₁ to s₁. Given s_k, choose any lift t of p_(k+1). The reduction [2]t minus s_k is in the torsion Kummer image, by the diagram of the Kummer sequences and the identity 2p_(k+1)=p_k. Lift that correction through the **surjective** T/2^(k+1)T→T/2^kT and subtract it from t. This produces s_(k+1) with [2]s_(k+1)=s_k and the prescribed p_(k+1) as image. Each Sel_(2^k) is annihilated by 2^k since its cohomology coefficients E[2^k] are. The order of s_k is therefore exactly 2^k, as its quotient image has that order. The section a↦a s_k splits the Kummer sequence as abelian groups; the chosen sections commute with reductions by construction. The extra generator now maps to nonzero Sha and has no rational Kummer preimage.

In particular the inverse limit of the abstract Selmer tower is T[2^∞] ⊕ Z₂ in both branches, with limits understood as topological groups under their profinite topology. Neither its group structure, the growth counts, nor these zero pairing matrices select a rational rank-one point. Extra arithmetic, for example an exhibited global nontorsion point or independently proved finiteness of the primary Sha for the required twists, really is needed. The general pointwise 2-converse preprint is not used here. This does not prove impossibility of point certification from additional *marked localization data* or new arithmetic. The source component handles the locally cyclic matrix implication only; this is not a kernel-certified tower construction.
