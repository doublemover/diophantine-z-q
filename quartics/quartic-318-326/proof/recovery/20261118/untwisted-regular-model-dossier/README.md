# Untwisted quartic 318/326: a proper flat regular arithmetic model

The six dated stages in this packet construct, **at the level of independently reviewed mathematics**, a proper flat regular model over Z of the constant-one-twist curve with function field generated by r_i²=T−c_i for c=(0,32,76832,−343). Each global statement concerns the exact normalized two-chart model in that same field and modifications that are the identity on the generic fibre. The claim is about the regularity of the total space. It is not a claim of smooth or reduced special fibres, minimality, semistable reduction, or extension to arbitrary twists.

| Stage | Place or bridge | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Selected 3-adic chart → arithmetic affine open | Two blowups over 3; proper birational model regular on the untwisted finite normalized chart above D(70). |
| 2 | Infinity | The restricted reciprocal ratio chart includes every point at infinity; proper flat regular model over Z[1/70]. |
| 3 | 5 | One additional blowup at the reduced F25 point; regular over Z[1/14]. |
| 4 | 7 | Three further blowups at F49 points; regular over Z[1/2]. |
| 5 | 2, normalization and ordinary nodes | Explicit half-root normalizations, four finite and four infinity F2 nodes resolved; one genuine singular F2 point remains. |
| 6 | 2, residual point | Three preparatory blowups, two crossing-direction blowups, then eight split-product blowups resolve that last point. |

Counting all disjoint centers separately gives 27 points; treating the eight disjoint simple dyadic nodes as one union gives 20 blowup morphisms. This is the count for the displayed construction, not a minimality bound.
