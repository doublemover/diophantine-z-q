# Real-place detection of the particular rational coordinate defect

**Independent of the tame-local argument.** This is an application of a published global arithmetic theorem, not a from-scratch proof or a Lean implementation of that theorem. It targets one precise defect under its actual Sha membership hypothesis, not a vanishing assertion for arbitrary degree-three classes.

Fix n∈ℚ with n≠0 and the two continuous cocycles χ,ψ from the repository's original elliptic model. Assume the full class of the first torsion representative belongs to the repository's actual Sha. Put G=Gal(ℚ̄/ℚ) with Krull topology and C=defectCup(n,hn,χ,ψ):G³→ℤ/2. The two direct statements in ActualCasselsTateDefectCup318 are exactly the continuous degree-three cocycle requirements: `defectCup_locally_constant` and `defectCup_cocycle`, with the trivial action on ℤ/2 and the usual alternating signs. Positive cohomological degrees here use **continuous** inhomogeneous cochains with discrete coefficients, not abstract cohomology of the underlying group.

1. Take J.S. Milne, *Arithmetic Duality Theorems*, Chapter I, §4, Theorem 4.10(c), with the localization map defined just before the statement. [Author's theorem text, printed p. 70](https://www.jmilne.org/math/Books/adt1.pdf). Put K=ℚ and S=all places; unrestricted extensions give G_S=G and the S-integer ring is ℚ, where 2 is a unit. The module M=ℤ/2 is finite, continuous, has trivial action, and degree r=3 is in range. The restriction map on H³ is an isomorphism onto the product over real places. For ℚ there is exactly one real factor. Only injectivity is needed here. In positive degree 3 the Tate convention at the real place agrees with ordinary group cohomology.
2. The real restriction of **this same C** represents zero. Indeed the first coordinate of `actual_sha_everywhere_primitive` gives a locally constant b_∞ on Gal(ℝ̄/ℝ)² whose ordinary coboundary is C precomposed in each argument with `Field.absoluteGaloisGroup.map (algebraMap ℚ ℝ)`. This is the real localization used in the theorem: the field-map construction chooses an extension of the embedding to the algebraic closures and restricts automorphisms along it. One may use that same choice in defining localization; alternate choices conjugate it and do not alter zero of the resulting cohomology class. This step uses the **coordinate** primitive. A unit-valued local primitive alone would not imply this binary assertion.
3. Thus [C]=0 in continuous H³(G,ℤ/2). By the cochain definition, there exists a continuous b:G²→ℤ/2 with ∂b=C as functions, not merely equality after restriction or equality up to class. With finite discrete target this is the required locally constant b. No assertion is made that its restrictions equal the previously chosen local primitives; their differences are degree-two cocycles.
4. The coefficient map `binaryUnit` is the G-equivariant inclusion of the sign values. Apply it to b and use the literal theorem `unitDTwo_binary` from ActualCasselsTateUnitCoefficients318. The result B= binaryUnit∘b is locally constant and satisfies unitDTwo(B)=unitDefect(n,hn,χ,ψ) in (ℚ̄)^× on every triple, for the actual nontrivial action on all units. No theorem of global degree-three vanishing for arbitrary unit cochains is used.

**Exact limits.** The conclusion is a global continuous primitive for the named binary defect and its image in algebraic-closure units for a Sha input. The real factor can itself have nonzero H³(ℝ,ℤ/2); the argument does **not** say H³(ℚ,ℤ/2)=0. It supplies no geometric Weil-pairing identification, no sum-of-local-invariants theorem for the constructed pairing, no nondegeneracy, no rank bound and no accepted Lean boundary. The companion Lean source exposes real detection as an explicit parameter, not as an axiom, sorry, or implemented Milne theorem.
