# A single real-quadratic coordinate tests the two rational ranks together

**The original supports.** Take the split-refined simultaneous positive squarefree pair n,nθ, n≡23 mod24, θ≡1 mod24 squarefree and coprime to n. The proof applies to scalar as well as ray contacts. If θ=1 the two targets coincide: use the earlier ordinary Heegner trace test on n, not a trivial “second” genus character. For θ>1 put D=−nθ, K=Q(√D), F=Q(√θ). Both oriented level primes split in K. E=E₋₁ and E^(D)=E_(nθ) in the earlier notation. In its Hilbert class group C, χθ is a nontrivial genus character, with χθ(σ)=(θ/48)=+1 because θ≡1 mod24. Thus the previously identified Fricke reflection s fixes F, negates √D, and acts on the CM image P by P+sP=T=(3,0).

Split the packet into its two *ordinary, unweighted* genus cosets and write A_±=Σ_(a∈C, χθ(a)=±1)aP. Both lie in E(KF). For each, sA_±=−A_± because |kerχθ| is even (the original class groups have positive 4-rank) and the cosets are invariant under inversion. Under the quadratic twist map (X,Y)↦(DX,D√D Y), they give two conjugate points Z_+,Z_- on E_(nθ)(F): an ideal class of negative χθ extends the nontrivial automorphism τ of F while fixing K and swaps the two sums. Their sum is the rational image of the ordinary full trace; their difference is τ-anti and therefore gives a point on E_((nθ)θ)≃E_n over Q. It is the χθ-weighted trace in the same CM packet.

For positive squarefree m>1 under consideration E_m(Q)_tors=E_m[2]: a rational half of any nonzero root requires both root differences there to be rational squares. Only top root m has both positive, and m is nonsquare. Rational three-torsion would require a rational root of 3u⁴+8u³−18u²−9 (the 3-division polynomial at x/m), impossible mod5 with leading coefficient a unit. Mazur's theorem now suffices for full rational 2-torsion. Consequently for affine [2]Z_±,

    x([2]Z_+)≠x([2]Z_-)   ⇔ both rational projections are nontorsion.

Indeed equality of x means [2]Z_+=±[2]Z_-, and either rational projection is torsion iff its double is O. The test is false if [2]Z_+=O (its conjugate is then O). Thus irrationality of one real-quadratic coordinate gives literal positive rational ranks on the two original twists, with no qualification about ranks over F. It does not suffice that either A_± individually be nontorsion. The unchanged original Sel₂/marking controls then apply; for a numerical pair with Sel₂ of size eight, rank one and Sha[2∞]=0 follow immediately. Algebraic values may also be distinguished after good reduction modulo a rational prime splitting in F that avoids their denominators: distinct conjugate x-values differ at all but finitely many such primes. An exact common prime may detect all four positive tests by Dirichlet and avoiding the finitely many bad primes. No universal positive test is proved.

**A genuine fixed obstruction and a way to change it without changing either target.** For an unramified genus character χδ of K' of odd fundamental discriminant D', automorphic induction of χδ is the sum of quadratic characters δ and D'/δ. Hence the Rankin L-function factors as L(E^(δ),s)L(E^(D'/δ),s), including ramified Euler factors by Artin formalism for the biquadratic field. The unramified class-character height formula of [Gross–Zagier, Theorem I.6.3, proved in V.1; V.2.1 for the ordinary trace](https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/zagier/files/doi/10.1007/BF01388809/fulltext.pdf) gives nontorsion of the χδ projection iff the central derivative of this product is nonzero. Here the chosen g-parametrization has nonzero differential and is in the corresponding newform factor; a cusp subtraction contributes only torsion (and cancels for nontrivial χ). All CM discriminants here are negative fundamental, coprime to level 48 split at 2 and 3; class characters are unramified. The δ=1 version is the ordinary-trace theorem. The positive δ≡1 mod24 factors below all have sign +, their negative partners sign −. In particular the simple paired test needs L(E^(θ),1)≠0 as well as the two nonzero target derivatives; otherwise its θ component is torsion for every eligible n.

This limitation is realized by the known prepared θ=409 case. The fixed E^(409)=E₋₄₀₉ has the exact rational point (−43200/169,16750440/2197). It is nontorsion: the two good reductions at 5 and 7 both have 8 points, ruling out odd torsion, and no nonzero rational 2-torsion can be halved since even the top positive root 3·409 has nonsquare difference 4·409. By the analytic-rank-zero theorem of Gross–Zagier/Kolyvagin, L(E^(409),1)=0. The paired test with k=1 therefore always fails at θ=409, including the previous n=2159, even though the two ordinary traces in their *separate* fields are both nontorsion. This is not an obstruction to the actual ranks.

To move this fixed factor, take positive squarefree k>1, k≡1 mod24 and gcd(k,nθ)=1. Change **only** the CM field to K'=Q(√D') with D'=−knθ. In C'=Cl(K') use χk and χ_(kθ); they are distinct nontrivial genus characters. Their signed traces W_k,W_(kθ) on E transform by the quadratic characters of D'/k=D and D'/(kθ)=−n: both signed positive k and kθ have value +1 on the reflection, so the *other* quadratic extension of each genus character is its −1 eigenspace. After twisting by D, let P_0 be the rational point corresponding to W_k, and P_1 the F point anti-invariant under τ corresponding to W_(kθ). Set Z=P_0+P_1. Then τZ=P_0−P_1. The Galois transformation laws here hold exactly for the signed traces, not merely modulo torsion, by cancellation of the cusp constant. The two rational projections are doubled, which does not alter nontorsion. The same inequality of x([2]Z) and x([2]τZ) therefore tests nontorsion of both. The fixed L-values are now L(E^(k),1) and L(E^(kθ),1), independent of n. If both are certified nonzero, this one-coordinate test is equivalent to simultaneous nonvanishing of the two original derivatives, without needing to compute either separately. No simultaneous auxiliary nonvanishing theorem or uniform k is assumed.

For θ=409 and n=2159, k=73 is admissible, and both fixed factors are rigorously nonzero: 6.31<L(E^(73),1)<6.32 and 1.23<L(E^(29857),1)<1.27. Hence the shifted paired test at K' has two nontorsion components by the earlier two actual derivative/trace certifications. This last implication is theoretical; it is not an assertion of a numerical evaluation of the much larger shifted CM packet. For these even-sign factors the central Mellin formula is 2Σ a_m exp(−cm)/m, c=π/(2√3 k), with a_m the level-24 eta coefficients times (−k/m) at odd m and 0 for even m. This follows by splitting the weight-two Mellin integral at the fixed point of the functional equation, as for the preceding derivative formula. By |a_m|≤2m the remainder after T is bounded by 4 exp(−c(T+1))/(1−exp(−c)). even.py uses the exact pinned coefficient generator and Arb. It also certifies 10.09<L(E^(457),1)<10.10.

**Positive control with one CM computation.** Set β=17, θ=457, n=1751=17·103, d=nθ=800207. The prepared square b=(204/439715)² has 0<b<17, reduced square denominator, only 2,3,17 in its numerator, depths 4,2,2 there. The identity 17·12²+457·20569²=439715² gives 1−b/17=457(20569/439715)². This is a stand-alone control, not the actual 15-label/common-index construction. The support matrices satisfy the same zero-intermediate-prime template, and an independent exact Kummer screen gives eight Sel₂ classes including (17,1) on each. At D=−800207 there are 520 reduced classes, 260 per χ₄₅₇ coset. Direct complex-ball sums using the explicit φ give respectively

    839001 < x([2]Z_+) < 839002,       806454 < x([2]Z_-) < 806455.

The difference exceeds 32546, so both exact rational ranks equal one; their 2-primary Sha are trivial and the literal β marked Selmer class is globally rational on each by the Kummer sequence. The unramified Gross–Zagier factorization also gives analytic rank one (hence finite full Sha by Kolyvagin) for each, not a numerical BSD inference. The checker pins the previously reviewed modular map code; coset characters use a primitive represented integer coprime to 2D and the ordinary Jacobi symbol. This does not instantiate an original indexed pole or infer a uniform irrationality result. No Lean acceptance is claimed.
