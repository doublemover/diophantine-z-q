## A different mixed normal order

Let u=y_j, h=(y_k−u)/2, w=y₃, f=f₀+a₃d u², D=e_j−e_m, H=d(e_j−e_k)/4=2^h₀H₀, M=aD/2=2⁴M₀ and
    b=((1+u)y_m−u w)/2,    C₀=(ad−a₃d)/2.

    w²=2f,   h²+uh=H,
    b²+w u b=M(1+u)²+u²(A₀+ad u+C₀u²).
Indeed subtracting squares proves the b relation and proves b integral; recover y_m=(2b+uw)/(1+u) and all original roots. Over the u-line these three independent monics give the correct generic field of degree eight (distinct simple branch valuations over the original degree-16 t-field, t=e_j+d u²). They form a complete intersection with scale w a nonzerodivisor and hence no lost raw component. Characteristic zero recovers the original smooth cover.
