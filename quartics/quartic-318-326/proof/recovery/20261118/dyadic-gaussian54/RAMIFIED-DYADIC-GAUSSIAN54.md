# The 54 odd-last positive cases on the quarter chart, including a repeated cubic cusp

Use the alternative actual parameter modification and resolved exterior of ODDBRIDGE. Here q₃=+1, so its common finite t-chart comparison is over the true Gaussian coefficient DVR O, π=1+i, 2=ηπ², η=−i, with contacts c=(0,8,19208) and z_i²=q_i(t−c_i). Dividing even-coefficient roots by π and using i to remove minus signs gives effective coefficients either 1 (odd input) or η (even input). All these divisions are integral on the normalized chart since they give unit-slope-square radicands; the generic root substitutions are reversible and give the same normalization. Six all-even mixed originals and 48 mixed-parity originals are considered (not labels multiplied by the chart-étale degree). We resolve the entire finite chart, hence all these original curves over Z.

The standard chart verification used throughout is as follows. When scaling roots of separate unit-leading monics over a base surface chart at regular parameters, an ordinary ratio presentation embedded in the original field is verified by freeness over the genuine regular base blowup chart, nonzerodivisor scale and restoration of the old ring on scale inversion. A further integral scale adjoined by a monic yields precisely the normalized chart if it includes all ordinary ratios and is normal; the complete intersections here have isolated exceptional bad points (the calculations check all of the exceptional line) and are unchanged off scale, so R₁/S₂ suffices. For a smooth tangent-conic singularity, the ordinary charts of the regular ambient after dividing by scale squared are saturated since the conic equation restricts nonzero in the exceptional polynomial ring, and smoothness gives regularity at every direction. These conventions apply at each step below. All residue computations use the whole affine or projective line and not just its F₂-points.

## Six identical effective coefficients; initial charts for the other 48

When all three effective coefficients equal η, take u=z₀, h=(z₁−u)/2, k=(z₂−u)/2 (roots notation now scaled). The independent monics h²+uh=−2η, k²+uk=−2η K with K=2401 are the true finite normalization: free rank four over O[u] in the correct field, étale at generic and nonzero dyadic u, smooth char zero, unique isolated u=h=k=π=0. Blow up that reduced point on π,u directions. On the u chart π=uR,h=uH,k=uI the two derivatives in H,I are units on the fibre, RHS regular (π² multiples). On the π chart u=πS,h=πA,k=πB we have A²+SA=G, B²+SB=K G with G=−η²=1. Regular where S≠0; at S=0, A=B=1. Eliminating S in the A-unit neighbourhood, set e=B/A−K, Y=(2K−1)+G/A². Direct expansion gives e(e+Y)=−K(K−1), with unit-slope reverse substitution. Here Y=(2K−1)(1−Γ/A²) with Γ=−G/(2K−1). This Γ has an étale square root γ: 2K−1≡1 mod8, and i²=−1. Thus Y=unit·p(p+2γ) for p=A−γ. The RHS is a unit times π¹⁰. Apply the explicitly checked split-contact construction: on π side p=πⁿP, e=π^(2n)E for n=1,2, adjoin one extra integral scale at each normalized blowup, obtaining E(E+unit·P(P+π^(2−n)ηγ))=unit·π^(10−4n). At n=1 only the exceptional P=0 point can remain; at n=2 the two simple roots P=0,1 give exactly two nodes E(E+t)=unit·π² with t,π regular parameters, each resolved by one ordinary blowup. For the 48 mixed-parity originals, let j<k be the two equal effective coefficients d, m the other, d_m=λd with {d,d_m}={1,η}; λ=η or i. Set u=z_j, h=(z_k−u)/2, v=z_m−u,

On successive π charts at n=1 (or n=1,2,3 for pair12) put u=πⁿS,h=πⁿA,v=πⁿB:
    A²+SA=H/π^(2n),  B²+2SB+π J S²=M/π^(2n).

At R=0 two A labels and one B'=0; the tangent form is B'²+sR plus an R² term exactly for n=3, always a smooth conic, resolved in one ordinary point blowup. At the continuing n<3 pair12 origins both A,B vanish. All consumed ideals are genuine reduced maximals.

## Pair12: the two different residual constants at the third step

Write A(A+S)=Uπ⁶, U a unit, B²+2SB+π J S²=R₀. Because c_j−c_m=8, R₀=d_m η³. The fibre has B=1. If d_m=1 then d=η, λ=i, R₀=i. For S≠0,1 the nonzero π-linear term gives regularity; the only roots are S=1 or 0 as the coefficient of π from 1−i is a unit with residue 1. At S=1 the A cover is étale (two labels). Take β=π−1, satisfying (β+1)²=R₀+λ=2i=π². Shift x=S−1, y=B−β to get
    y²+2(β+1+x)y+π J x²+2(β+1−λ)x=0 .
This is a standard Gaussian isolated point with both J and δ=β+1−λ units. One ordinary point blowup on π,x directions has π-chart x=πs,y=πa with equation a²+π[η(β+1+πs)a+J s²+ηδ s]=0. There are two distinct roots of the bracket at a=0, each giving a node a²=πt with t a local parameter by the s derivative. The normalized-free x chart at π=xR,y=xv gives the additional R=0 tangent conic v²+J xR; one blowup resolves each of the three nodes. This argument also applies to later equations of this displayed form with arbitrary β+1 replaced by any integral coefficient and δ unit.

At S=0 for R₀=i the B-base is regular with parameters S,p=B−1: the defining equation has unit π-linear term from 1−i. In its quotient S=0, B²=i gives π of order exactly two in p (2p divisible π²p and i−1 unit times π). Hence with regular threefold parameters X=A,Y=A+S,p, we have π=αX+β'Y+γp², γ unit. If d_m=η then d=1, λ=η, R₀=1. All S≠0 directions are regular (only π-linear J S²). At S=0 shift v=B−1 and blow up π=S=v=A=0 with π,S directions: v²+2(1+S)v+π J S²+2S=0, A(A+S)=U π⁶.     Q(Q+P)=Uπ⁴,   V²+π[η(1+π P)V+J P²+η P]=0 .

All ratios are monics over the regular base blowups and normal isolated-point charts as specified above.

## Pairs involving 0: exact norm and the split Γ case

## Unsplit Γ: a triple-point normalization followed by three verified cusp cycles

Suppose d=1, λ=η. Then Γ=λ N/Λ differs from λ by a multiple of π¹⁰ (N and Λ both 1 mod32). In particular H₀'=(1−Γ)/π and J=(1−λ)/π are units and differ by a multiple of π⁹. Put p=A−1 and H'=H₀'+ηπ p. Then Y=E(p²+π H'). First blow up the reduced origin with π,p directions, setting on π side p=πP,v=πB. The equation B²+2(Y/π) B+π J (Y/π)²=Dπ¹² is regular everywhere on that side since Y/π is a unit there. On p side π=pR,v=pB, t'=Y/p=E(p+R H'):
    B²+2t'B+pR J t'²=D p¹² R¹⁴ .
Only R=0 needs attention (B=0), a singular closed point. Blow it up on p,R directions and normalize with B=p²Z, R=pT on the p side. For t=t'/p=E(1+T H') this yields
    Z²+η p³T² t Z+T J t²=D p²²T¹⁴,   π=p²T .

The ordinary preceding side had only isolated bad points. Coverage follows throughout from the leading quadratic forcing the last direction zero when the two base parameters vanish.

At T=1, t=0, Z=0 the base has regular parameters p,t (mod p, t=1+T). Shift Z=z+t. Then the coefficient of t² is N'=1+T J+ηp³T² and the coefficient of t z is ηp³T²(1+p). In the *actual quotient by t*, T=−1/H' and
    N'=(H'−J)/H'+ηp³/H'²=(H₀'−J)/H' .
Indeed H'−H₀'=ηπp=ηp³T. The right side is in (π⁹)⊂(p¹⁸) there; this cancellation is essential. Also N'≡1+T=t mod p. We can therefore write N'=B₀t+p¹⁸B₁ with B₀ a unit, B₁ regular. The exact local equation now has the shape
    z²+p^a M t z+(B₀ t+p^b B₁)t²=U p^c,    (a,b,c)=(3,18,22),
with M,B₀,U all units from the regular base. This is more than a tangent-cone congruence.

For a≥1 in the following three cycles, blow up first on p,t directions ordinarily: in the t direction p=tR,z=tV the divided equation has constant t B₀ plus t^bR^b B₁, linear term in t with unit at V=0, and regularity everywhere over the center (RHS U t^(c−2)R^c). The p side t=pS,z=pV is
    V²+p^a M S V+p B₀ S³+p^b B₁S²=U p^(c−2).
There S≠0 is regular, only the origin remains. Blow it up and normalize by S=pP,V=p²Z on the p side; the resulting equation is the original shape with (a,b,c) replaced by (a−1,b−2,c−6), in parameters p,P,Z.     Z'²+S^(a−1)R^a M Z'+R B₀+S^(b−2)R^b B₁=U S^(c−6)R^(c−2).
The R derivative at R=0,Z'=0 is a unit, and nonzero R overlaps. All exponents are nonnegative, scale saturation and normalized extra-scale tests apply. The cycle takes (3,18,22) to (2,16,16), (1,14,10), (0,12,4). Each consumed origin is actually singular by the lack of a linear term; nothing depends on counting rational directions only.

No extraction of a square root of Γ was made in this branch.
