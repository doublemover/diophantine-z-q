This works even on the scalar pole where b and r can differ. Assume first θ>1 for the paired application. We change the auxiliary CM field used to detect ranks, never n or its Selmer data.

**A two-edge operation with opposite primal and dual bits.** Some i,j differ in both b and r. Indeed if no two points (bᵢ,rᵢ) in the binary square differ in both coordinates, their set is contained in a row or column; both bit functions are nonconstant. Choose a fresh prime k≡1 mod24, nonsquare mod p_i,p_j and square at every other p|d. Nonzero residue choices, CRT and Dirichlet give arbitrarily large choices avoiding any finite set. As k≡1 mod4, reciprocity makes each new edge symmetric; prescribing k introduces no change in old off-diagonal symbols. The augmented Laplacian L' for d'=kd adds the two edges and toggles only the two old diagonals. For v=e_i+e_j, its new diagonal is zero (two edges). If (x,y) is in ker L' the final equation says x_i=x_j. The old equations are then Lx=(x_i+y)v. Pairing with r forces x_i+y=0 since r_i≠r_j, so x∈〈1,b〉 and x_i=x_j eliminates b. Thus (x,y) is constant. Negative D' is odd fundamental and the previous genus map derivation applies verbatim to arbitrary such squarefree products independently of the original Selmer finite controls. Therefore

**An unconditional rational genus contribution, with a divisibility bound.** In the new Hilbert class field H'/K', let A be the odd class subgroup, H₂=(H')^A its genus field, G=Gal(H₂/K')=(C₂)^t. With the fixed E=E₋₁ and oriented CM point P, set Q=Tr_(H'/H₂)P. The Fricke reflection s satisfies Q+sQ=T=(3,0) as in the odd-trace proof because K' is split at the level. Also E(H₂)[2∞]=E[2](Q), by the same unramified square-root test. In particular Q cannot differ by torsion from an s-anti-invariant point: the 2-primary component of the s-norm of any torsion point is zero. This argument is over K', not an assumption on ranks of the old fields.

For each χ∈G^*, put Wχ=Σ_(g∈G)χ(g) gQ, a signed full CM trace. Since inversion acts trivially on G, s commutes with it on H₂. Hence sWχ=−Wχ: the extra term is zero by the χ-sum for nontrivial χ and by the even order of G for trivial χ. The ideal-class action is via χ. Each Wχ consequently identifies with a rational point on the unique extension of χ to Gal(H₂/Q) that has sign −1 on s. Concretely the character-selection rule is as in the paired/reflection packet; for χ=1 the relevant quadratic twist is by D'. In particular χ_k yields the desired E_(nθ), and χ_(kθ) the desired E_n.

Character orthogonality gives the exact identity Σχ Wχ=2^t Q. Suppose every Wχ were divisible by 2^t modulo torsion **within its own rational-twist Mordell–Weil group**. For each choose a preimage Rχ in that group; viewed back in E(H₂), each satisfies sRχ=−Rχ. Summing shows 2^t(Q−ΣRχ) is torsion, hence Q differs by torsion from an s-anti-invariant point, impossible. Therefore at least one rational genus trace is nontorsion and has divisibility <2^t in its own Mordell–Weil lattice (equivalently its image in that lattice mod 2^t is nonzero). This removes the possibility that *only* quartic CM components contribute and records a precise divisibility target for the residual interference. It does not identify either χ_k or χ_(kθ) as the successful character: the other signed divisor twists must still be addressed to infer the requested ranks. If all other characters satisfy the stated divisibility, at least one of the two desired rational traces survives, not automatically both.
