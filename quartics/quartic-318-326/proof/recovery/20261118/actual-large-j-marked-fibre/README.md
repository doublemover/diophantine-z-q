# Large genuine j-fibres on the marked analytic quotient

There is B independent of N and H such that a nonempty j-fibre over |c|>B on the unmarked upper half-plane is one SL₂(Z)-orbit. The proof reduces to the standard fundamental domain, bounds the continuous modular j on its compact truncated core, and recovers the actual q-coordinate from reciprocal j on the high part. Equal q means integer translation. No global injectivity theorem for j is supplied or assumed.

For the true marked Γ_H quotient the inverted coset representatives therefore cover the whole analytic fibre of j, giving finitely many points and cardinality at most [SL₂(Z):Γ_H]≤N[(Z/N)×:H]∏(1+1/p). No freeness at elliptic points, surjectivity for arbitrary target values, or identification with the generic degree of the rational algebraic cover is asserted. This is static independently checked research source, not a Lean replay.
