# A positive rational rank on matching Jacobians at the unchanged ray pole, and the limit of this constant-coefficient strategy

Write b=17βr², D=1−βr²=θz² with r,z nonzero rational and positive squarefree β,θ. Assume 17|β, β nonsquare, θ a 17-adic unit locally square, and 0<b<17. This applies to a true prepared ray numerator β=17 times five distinct primes 1 mod408; for the positive construction require β a sum of two rational squares. Keep this entire b, not just its first class. On the family of matched quartics in the previous companion use
    m=βk², C=θ(1−m), J: y²=x(x−βC(25m−9))(x−16βCm).
The sought full 2-cover has ordered split Kummer class δ=(1,β,β). Here are two distinct results.

**At the fixed b:** infinitely many explicit rational slopes k yield pairwise geometrically nonisomorphic matching Jacobians of positive rational rank, with companion ε=(β,1,β) represented by an explicit nontorsion point. Indeed EVERY k satisfying [C]=β works (k≠0); the given hypotheses guarantee infinitely many. If θ>1 and gcd(β,θ)=1, rational solubility of H_k at any of these slopes requires rank at least TWO, not just the proven positive rank. No local or global claim for δ at these arbitrary new slopes is assumed. The result is specific to this marked Jacobian, not a lower bound for the original E_n.

**Over Q(r):** trying to make C *any* constant squareclass c by a rational formula k=R(r) cannot yield a generic rational point on this H. We determine the exact generic Jacobian rank: it is 1 for c=β in Q×/Q×² and 0 otherwise. Even for c=β its rational 2-Kummer image misses δ. This is a no-go for a restricted algebraic construction, not a no-go on any individual specialization or for nonconstant C.

## Fixed pole and a literal nontorsion point

Put u=s/β, v=t/β for s²+t²=β. There are infinitely many such rational (s,t), parametrizing a rational circle through any starting representation. Take v≠0, k=(r+u)/(1+βru), avoiding the finite degeneracies. Then
    1−βk²=(1−βr²)(1−βu²)/(1+βru)²
            =θz²·βv²/(1+βru)²,
so C=βq² for q=θzv/(1+βru). The fractional-linear function of u is nonconstant as 1−βr²>0. The positive squarefree β in each true ray is a sum of two squares by its prime factors, whereas if β has a prime 3 mod4 this constant class is impossible. Indeed θ is itself a norm from Q(√β) by D=θz², while 1−βk² is always a norm. Thus [C]=β forces β to be a norm, equivalently −1 is a norm (Norm(√β)=−β). For positive nonsquare β, x²−βy²=−1 is precisely a rational sum-of-two-squares representation of β after dividing by y². This proves the claimed dichotomy of *availability*, not that every scalar pole is impossible for other C.

Scale x,y by (βq)²,(βq)³ to obtain the Q-isomorphic actual Jacobian
    F_m: Y²=X(X−a)(X−d),     a=25m−9,  d=16m.
The point P=(25m,45m) has product (25m)·9·(9m)=2025m² and Kummer class (β,1,β). In this normalization a fixed β and slope suffice to compute the marked point. In Q₁₇, if v(k)≥0, then θ(1−βk²) is a local square unit up to an even valuation, contrary to [C]=β. Hence v(k)<0 and v(m)<0 is odd. F_m is the ramified twist by m of
    Y²=X(X−(25−9/m))(X−16),
which has good reduction at 17 (its roots reduce to 0,8,16). In the ramified quadratic splitting field the residue field is unchanged and rational torsion reduces to its negative on the good curve, hence has reduction of order at most 2. The formal logarithm is injective on the maximal ideal there: v₁₇(x)≥1/2>1/(17−1) on a nonzero formal parameter, so in the integral differential's logarithm the linear term dominates all higher ones. Reduction on torsion is injective. Therefore F_m(Q)_tors is exactly its four rational 2-torsion points, and P is nontorsion for EVERY such k. The ratio a/d=(25m−9)/(16m) is nonconstant and j has finite fibers in that ratio, proving the assertion of infinitely many geometric classes.

For θ>1 coprime to β, set y=1−m. Its class is βθ and y>0 (C=βq²). The Kummer triples for the three nonzero torsion points have first/second/third slots, respectively,
    T₀: (a d, −a, −d),   T₁: (a, a(a−d), a−d),   T₂: (d,d−a,d(d−a)).
Here d∼β, a−d=−9y∼−βθ; ∼ denotes equal squareclass. T₀ has third class −β, distinct from third classes of δ,ε,δε. T₁ has third class −βθ, again distinct from all three by positivity. T₂ has class (β,βθ,θ), whose second slot excludes both ε and δε because θ>1 and β,θ are independent, and whose first excludes δ. All three candidate classes are nonidentity. Consequently ε,δ are independent modulo the torsion image. Any rational lift of δ would give, together with P, two independent directions in the free rational Mordell–Weil group modulo 2, hence rational rank at least 2. The sufficient rank bound is not claimed, and at arbitrary k new bad places can even obstruct δ locally.

## The generic surface and its descent

Fix any c in Q×. Over Q(k) the Jacobian for squareclass C=c is the constant quadratic twist by c of the model with roots 0, a₀=β(25βk²−9), d₀=16β²k². Its geometric elliptic surface over the k-line is rational. At k=0 the double zero of d₀ gives I₄; at the simple zeros of a₀ and a₀−d₀ (k=±3/(5√β), ±1/√β) the fibers are I₂. In each case exactly two roots collide, c₄ is a unit and the multiplicative discriminant order is twice the root difference order. At infinity scale X by k², Y by k³: all three reduced roots 0,25β²,16β² are distinct. The minimal discriminant has degree 12, hence χ=1 and the nonconstant elliptic surface over P¹ with section is rational of Picard number 10. [Shioda, On the Mordell–Weil lattices, §10](https://rikkyo.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/10027/files/AA00610867_39-02_05.pdf), [Schütt–Shioda, Elliptic surfaces, §§6,8](https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.0298). The Shioda–Tate formula gives geometric rank precisely 10−2−3−4=1.

In the base c=1 model one explicit section is
    P₀=(25β²k²,45β²√β k²).
It is nontorsion. Over the algebraic closure of constants write T=√βk, X=βx,Y=β√β y. At T=2 the normalized roots are 0,91,64 and P₀ specializes to (100,180); the duplication formula gives x(2P₀)=3364/25. At the good prime 5 this is a nonzero point in the torsion-free formal group, proving infinite order by specialization. Constant conjugation sends P₀ to itself or to its negative with character χβ, and k is fixed. It spans the one-dimensional geometric rational MW vector space. On the c-twist the character is χ_(cβ), so the rational rank is zero unless cβ square; in the latter case the displayed section descends, proving exact rank one.

Even geometrically no rational function-field point of order 4 exists: the split-root halving criterion for T₀,T₁ requires the function a₀, up to a nonzero constant, to be square, impossible because it has simple zeros. For T₂ the function a₀−d₀ has simple zeros. Hence all 2-primary torsion is the four roots, sufficient to compute the rational torsion image modulo 2 regardless of odd torsion. For arbitrary constant c≠β in squareclass, δ is none of those four Kummer classes: T₀ and T₁ have a nonconstant squareclass in the first slot coming from 25m−9; T₂ has a nonconstant class from 1−m in the second. The identity does not give δ. The rank-zero generic rational Kummer image therefore misses δ. For c=β normalize as F_m. The same first/second slots show that none of δ,ε,δε is a torsion class. Since rational rank is exactly one, ε spans its rational Kummer image modulo torsion, and δ is again absent. The actual degree-two-fiber torsor thus has nonzero order-two Weil–Châtelet class for EVERY constant c. No assertion of arithmetic Sha over the function field is needed.

Finally consider the genuinely generic input r with complementary squareclass [1−βr²]. If a rational slope R(r) makes C constant in squareclass, we must have
    1−βR(r)²=c(1−βr²)w(r)²
for a rational w and that same constant class c. R is necessarily nonconstant; over Qbar the only points of odd pullback multiplicity among the two target branch points ±1/√β are the two source branch points ±1/√β (poles have even multiplicity for 1−βR²). Each of the two source points maps to a target branch point, with odd multiplicity. They are conjugate over Q and R is rational, so they map to *different* target branch points. In either target fiber every other multiplicity is even. Therefore deg R is odd. Under the finite function-field extension Q(k)→Q(r), k↦R(r), the nontrivial order-two torsor just computed cannot become trivial: corestriction after restriction multiplies its H¹ class by this odd degree. This proves the generic uniform-formula exclusion; it does not forbid a positive rational section after other base changes not satisfying the stipulated squareclass relation.

The controls give literal slopes at the two small and historical five-prime prepared examples, with honest global positive-rank ε-marking. They do not fabricate the global ε into a δ marking, nor require a numeric 15-label assignment to prove the fixed-input implication. The rank and function-field arguments are mathematical, not new Lean elaborations.

## Exact generic rank on the fixed-complement slope conic

For a FIXED ray input β,θ, keep k variable in the unrestricted matched torsor H. Its geometric Jacobian rank over Qbar(k) is zero. Here is an independent Picard-number computation. Over Qbar, take the two-variable change v=(t+k)/(1+βkt), k=(v−t)/(1−βvt), and e(t)=(1−βt²)(1−9βt²)/(1+3βt²)². The verified matching identity gives squareclass θe(t)e(v) on the total double cover. Geometrically it is birational to the Kummer quotient of E×E where E is the smooth double cover z²=e(t); the product coordinates descend (z₁,z₂)↦z₁z₂. With a branch as origin, the two deck involutions are negation. The cross ratio of ±1/√β, ±1/(3√β) is 1/4 up to the six usual permutations; j(E)=35152/9 is nonintegral, so E has no CM. Thus Picard(E×E) in the sense of Néron–Severi rank is 2+rank End(E)=3, and the resolved Kummer surface has geometric Picard number 3+16=19. For example blow up the 16 fixed points first, then take the involution quotient: pullback identifies rational numerical divisor classes on the quotient with the invariant classes upstairs by push-pull and symmetrization; all three classes and the 16 exceptional classes are fixed. The roots of q₁ give geometric sections of H over Qbar(k), hence this smooth elliptic surface itself computes its Jacobian there (no claim about rational descent of this untwisted Kummer model).

In the preceding constant surface the unrestricted coefficient adds the quadratic twist by 1−βk² up to a geometric constant. At its two simple zeros, the I₂ fibers become I₂*, with 7 components each, the root lattice D₆. The I₄ at 0 and the other two I₂ fibers are unchanged, and infinity sees even pole order and remains good over algebraically closed constants. Their root lattices already contribute 3+2+2·6=17. Adding fiber and section exhausts the 19 classes, so Shioda–Tate gives rank zero even geometrically. The arithmetic geometric distinction matters: no general result about unmarked simultaneous twists on a split Kummer surface supplies the missing original full markings on this arithmetic surface.

Now over the rational conic h²=(1−βk²)/(βθ) of the positive-rank construction, the extension of function fields over the k-line is quadratic (two simple branch points). The geometric rank of F_m over this extension is the sum of its rank one over the old field and the rank of its twist by (1−βk²)/(βθ); the latter is precisely the unrestricted Jacobian up to constant/scaling. That summand has geometric rank zero as just shown. Rational rank over the positive slope conic is thus exactly one as well, witnessed by P. Combined with the fixed-b torsion argument, a rational point of the matched H among these slopes requires a genuine specialization jump from generic rank one to at least two. We have proved no such jump for a general original ray row.
