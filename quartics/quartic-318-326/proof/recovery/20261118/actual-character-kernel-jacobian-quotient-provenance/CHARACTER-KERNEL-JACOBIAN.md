# The same newform quotient survives descent to the character-kernel Jacobian

**Target.** In V13, lines 8964–9023, assume the actual normalized primitive weight-2 form f at exact level N with nebentypus ε, the Q-defined surjection q:J₁(N)→A_f and the previously identified characteristic-zero rational Tate constituent for the chosen embedding (the bounded Jacobian-realization receipt). Prove the outstanding quotient/factor input for the later J_H consumer. Do not assume that an arbitrary rational quotient on a different curve has the correct Tate constituent.

**The Q model.** Put H=ker ε, containing −1 by weight two. On the rational point-of-order marking, a diamond d multiplies the marked point by d. Mascot, Research in Number Theory 8 (2022), article 48, §2.1, eq. (2.1.8), gives the rational function field of X_H by invariants in the full modular function field; its defining upper triangular group has unrestricted first diagonal entry and thus surjective determinant. The X₁ and X_H in this construction are over Q (the **full symplectic level N** curve generally is not). The compactified quotient map π:X₁→X_H and the diamonds descend over Q on this model. Its degree is n=|G|, G the effective image of H in the coarse-curve automorphism group, not automatically |H|. The base changes used to check the formulas below cause no change of field in the conclusion.

**Why q is H-invariant.** The old Q-defined q has pullback of its invariant differentials equal to the full span of coefficient-conjugates of f (Shimura Thm 1 as reviewed, V13). Under a diamond h, a conjugate σf has eigenvalue σ(ε(h)); the inverse-action convention changes this to its inverse, still 1 on H. See Baker, González-Jiménez, González & Poonen §3.2.1. Consequently q∘h and q have the same pullbacks of every differential of A_f. Their difference is a homomorphism of abelian varieties in characteristic zero with zero differential, hence zero (its connected image has zero dimension, by separability). Thus q∘h=q for all h∈G, as homomorphisms, not just on the newform's one eigenspace. Older notational variants using a multiplicative instead of a point marking do not change the kernel: the rational dual-isogeny transformation sends an embedding Z/N→E to the dual embedding μ_N→E/⟨P⟩ and inverts diamonds. On complex newforms the corresponding Fricke transformation preserves the whole conjugate span by the previous Li review; that is enough for H-invariance even under this transport. We do not change the given Q-isogeny class in order to deduce its Tate representation from character labels alone.

**Actual factor, without a rational idempotent.** Write pull=π*, push=π_* on Jacobians. On geometric degree-zero divisor classes, pull(push(D))=Σ_{h∈G}hD: for each geometric point its stabilizer size equals its tame ramification index, so the formula remains valid at cusps or fixed points. Summing the identity over divisors and passing to classes proves it over the algebraic closure and hence over Q. Define the ordinary Q-homomorphism b=q∘pull:J_H→A_f. It satisfies b∘push=[n]∘q, as all n translates have the same q-image. Since q and multiplication by n on a characteristic-zero abelian variety are surjective, so is b (check on geometric points). This proves a genuine quotient onto the *same* A_f, not merely a dimension inequality or a comparison of Fourier coefficients. Poincaré reducibility over Q gives A_f as Q-isogeny factor of J_H; no coefficient extension, no polarization-degree bound, no doubling of n for −1. Equivalently the generic Tate quotient onto V₅(A_f) splits by semisimplicity. This works also if 5|n because the representation used here is rational, not an integral direct-summand assertion.

**Check and downstream.** Mascot §2.2 (2.2.1) separately gives the Q-isogeny decomposition of this precise J_H in terms of all weight-2 newform orbits of divisor levels with the induced kernels; the exact-level orbit [f] contributes. This is corroboration, not use of his later mod-ℓ reduction procedure as a characteristic-zero Tate theorem. With the independently reviewed common rational Tate constituent over the bounded field L′ and absolute simplicity of A, Faltings Hom plus Poincaré then give an L′-isogeny A×Z→J_H and genus ≥2. L′ is not enlarged by a coefficient embedding, and we have **not** bounded that isogeny's degree here. Upstream modularity/twist/conductor, actual cusp/finite arithmetic data and metric/Jacobian height, image-height and isogeny comparison to A, and all-point NonCMInputs remain separate. The Lean companion proves the abstract norm/surjectivity implications on additive geometric point groups, not the construction of J_H or Shimura's theorem.
