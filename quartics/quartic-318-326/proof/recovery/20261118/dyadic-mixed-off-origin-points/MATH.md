# Initial singular centers for all 240 unequal types, and resolving those away from T=0

For the representatives q∈{±1,±2}⁴ with q₀,q₁,q₂ not all equal, the starting dyadic normalization has **exactly one F₂-point over T=0**. Its total-space embedding dimension is 4, 5, 6 or 8, occurring in 174, 6, 36 and 24 representatives respectively. In each of the twelve finite exceptional and twelve infinity exceptional representatives identified in RAMIFIED-DYADIC-MIXED240, the indicated extra parameter value has exactly two F₂-points, both embedding dimension 3. There are no other initial singular points. We then resolve *all* those extra points by an explicit pair of ordinary blowup stages; this leaves just the one T=0 point per mixed representative unresolved by these explicit stages. This does not assert that any of the high-embedding-dimension origins has been blown up here.

## Evaluation of finite fibres

The point data are evaluated on the exact initial normalization produced by the separately supplied basis engine, not the raw nonnormal ring. initial-points-240.json has one record for each representative. For each we compute the t=0 finite fibre; only for the 24 extra types do we also compute t=1 on the finite chart, or t=0 on the reciprocal chart. All other potential values are excluded by the direct off-origin normalization proof. In all there are 264 computed finite algebras of dimension 16 over F₂. We use bit i for the i-th **normalized basis vector** in these data (not the i-th raw radical). The arrays use the exact deterministic engine.py bound in the earlier computation.

Given a specialised multiplication table S, square Frobenius F is obtained from the diagonal. The image of F⁴ is S_red *embedded as a subalgebra*: every nilpotent in this dimension has sixteenth power zero; on the quotient product of finite fields the operation is bijective, and the image has exactly the quotient dimension and contains no nilpotents. Solve multiplication for the identity and the F−1 linear kernel for idempotents. Split that identity successively with this idempotent basis: in a product of residue fields the fixed subspace is exactly the product of copies of F₂, so its basis distinguishes every pair of field factors. The resulting primitive idempotents e index the closed points, of residue degree r=dim(e·im F⁴). The map b↦e F^m(b) with m a multiple of r and at least four represents actual evaluation at the chosen residue factor (kills nilpotents and is identity Frobenius power on that field). The script ensures it preserves all multiplication equations. All the resulting degrees are one here; the computation permits nontrivial degrees rather than silently restricting to rational points.

For the cotangent rank use 17 affine polynomial variables, the parameter and sixteen normalized basis generators Y_i. Locally their table relations g=Y_iY_j−Σc_k(t)Y_k suffice, since 1−the basis expression for the ring identity multiplied by any Y_i follows from the table, and some Y_i is nonzero at a field character. See the previous basis note for this presentation. The mixed row [δg,(∂_t g)²,(∂_{Y_k}g)²] uses δg=(g(X²)−g(X)²)/2 before reduction. The implementation calculates the arithmetic column in the residue field at t₀∈{0,1} by δ(g+h)=δg+δh−gh mod2. For the term −c_k Y_k it is ((−c_k(t₀)−c_k(t₀)²)/2)Y_k², and δ(Y_iY_j)=0. Summing in the chosen residue algebra including the cross terms, followed by field Gaussian rank, gives embedding dimension 18−rank. In fact all fibres here split over F₂, so one may instead directly evaluate g(t₀,0/1 lifts)/2 mod2. Normalized basis construction is checked by its own ring-theoretic derivation, and the geometric justification for this rank criterion is in RAMIFIED-DYADIC-ALGORITHM. The source and hashed outputs make the reported finite computation falsifiable; no inference from the nonreduced fibre alone is made.

Across 240 representatives there are 240 high-edim F₂-origins plus 24 extra finite and 24 extra infinity F₂-points, i.e. 288 initial singular closed points in total *counted across these distinct representatives*. For unramified scalar variations of a given representative these are geometric counts: the unique geometric origin still gives one F₂-point and its embedding dimension is unchanged. A two-point exceptional pair could instead become a conjugate pair of degree two; we do not assert identical rational splitting under every such twist.

## A uniform local equation for the extra Gaussian points

Use the actual parameter-étale normalized Gaussian factors of RAMIFIED-DYADIC-MIXED240. Write O=Z_(2)[i], π=1+i, η=2/π²=−i. Permitted further unramified coefficient extensions are distinguished from this ramified field inside the normalized factor. On the finite exceptional type (p₀,p₃)=(0,1), write q₀=d, q₃=2a for d,a=±1. Locally at T=1 in the notation of the bound paper
    V²=λ U²+μ,   λ=adη,   μ=343aη .
Let x=U−1. Choose β²=λ+μ by an *étale* extension of O if needed; β is divisible by π. For d=1, λ+μ=344aη=π⁶(43a η⁴)=π⁶·43a. For d=−1, λ+μ=342aη=π²(171aη²)=−171aπ². Both residual constants are rational odd units. Their square roots exist after adjoining the square root of the choice of their sign that is 1 modulo 4, a quadratic extension unramified at 2 (then use i to undo any sign). Thus this extraction is available via a finite unramified extension already over the original dyadic coefficient ring. Put y=V−β−x:
    y²+2(β+x)y+π C x²+2δx=0,   C=(1−λ)/π,   δ=β−λ .
C is a unit since λ≡1+π mod π², and δ a unit since β≡0. This coordinate change does not require inverting π.

On the exceptional reciprocal type (p₀,p₃)=(1,1), write q₀=2d, q₃=2a. At s=0 we have V²=λ U²+μ, λ=343a/η, μ=ad. Choose β with β²=ad, available in O via i, and put x=U,y=V−β−x. The identical displayed equation holds with C=(1−λ)/π, δ=β; again λ≡1+π mod π² and δ a unit. The remaining h_j are finite étale at these closed points; the product component description is itself a parameter-étale pullback. It suffices to prove regularity over all the indicated base origins there. This uniform equation explains why mere tangent-cone smoothness at the original singular point cannot be assumed (its quadratic initial form can have a repeated factor).

## Two actual ordinary blowups

Blow up the reduced point (π,x,y). Its π,x directions cover since a homogeneous prime containing their initial forms must contain the initial y form by the quadratic equation. On the π chart x=π S,y=π A:
    A²+π[η(β+π S)A+C S²+ηδ S]=0 .
Modulo π we have A=0. At dyadic points not solving C S²+ηδ S=0 in the residue field, the term multiplying π is a unit, so the total-space hypersurface is regular. The polynomial has exactly two distinct roots since C and ηδ are units. Near either root the coefficient in square brackets is an étale local S-coordinate: its derivative in S reduces to ηδ≠0. Thus these are standard nodes A²=πt up to sign, and a single ordinary blowup resolves them via its genuine π,t charts (same scale saturation as DYADIC-OPEN §3). The h_j étale pullbacks preserve the reduced node ideals. On the x chart π=xR, y=xZ:
    xR=π,    Z²+x η R²(β+x) Z+xR(C+ηδ R)=0 .
At R≠0 this overlaps the π chart. At x=R=0 there is one origin Z=0 in the regular ambient O[x,R,Z]/(xR−π), and its quadratic leading form is Z²+Cbar xR, a smooth projective conic in characteristic two. One ordinary blowup at this point resolves all directions (in the x and R charts solve linearly for the other ratio; on the Z chart both ratios are nonzero). The first π and x chart rings are monic over O[S] and O[x,R]/(xR−π) respectively; their scales are nonzerodivisors, and inversion restores the original equations, proving equality with the unsaturated-free true ratio charts. The final divided smooth-conic equations are likewise nonzero modulo scale, giving cancellation and saturation in the ambient regular charts.

Thus above each extra *geometric* singular point there are three singular geometric successors after its first blowup, which are all resolved by a second ordinary blowup of their reduced union. Since the original locus is the whole reduced singular union outside T=0, and the successive union is the whole reduced singular locus over that closed union, these ideals are intrinsic and their finite étale pullbacks are precisely the reduced geometric unions. Therefore the construction takes place on the actual X_q without needing any descent through a wild ramified constant extension. The local regularity tests are faithfully flat étale, actual closed schemes over 2 are proper centers, and the birational model stays proper and flat with the same generic fibre. At T=0 the scheme was not changed. The two stages do nothing for the 216 representatives without extra singular values. A full evaluated resolution *over that high-edim origin* remains separate; the universal all-twist algorithm supplies mathematical termination, not its explicitly computed exceptional chains here.
