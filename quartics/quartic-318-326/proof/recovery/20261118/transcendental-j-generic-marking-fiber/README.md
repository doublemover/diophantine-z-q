# Arithmetic generic fibre at a large transcendental analytic j
Choose an honest analytic period τ with arbitrarily large j(τ) transcendental over the integers. Its evaluation embeds the arithmetic open-j coordinate ring and its fraction field in the complex numbers. The complex fibre of the actual generic elliptic curve agrees with the earlier marked curve. A surjection from its literal exact-order points to geometric primes of a finite étale algebra, compatible with diamonds, bounds the invariant rank by N[U:H] times the Euler factor. This period is not generic over complex constants, and the point map and modular field identification are not constructed here. Static mathematics/source review, not a kernel result.
