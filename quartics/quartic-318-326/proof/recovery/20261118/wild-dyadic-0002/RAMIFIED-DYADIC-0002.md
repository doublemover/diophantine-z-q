# Dyadic odd valuation in the fourth root: an explicit normalized intermediate blowup

Let X_q be the normalization of P¹_Z in Q(T)(r_i : r_i²=q_i(T−b_i)), b=(0,32,76832,−343). For q⁰=(1,1,1,2) and any q obtained by multiplying its four entries by independently dyadically unramified rational squareclasses, there is an explicit proper regular model over Z with flat structure morphism and a modification to X_q. The previously reviewed arbitrary-twist open D(2) handles the odd primes. At 2 this proof uses one **normalized, non-reduced-center** blowup between ordinary point blowups. The unramified or Gaussian residual proof alone does not apply. Assertion is source mathematics, not Lean-checked.

## Normalized affine patches, including the wild fixed algebra

Over D=Z_(2) put u=r₀, h=(r₁−u)/2, k=(r₂−u)/2, w=r₃. Put f=u²+343. The true finite chart is
    T=u²,   h²+uh=−8,   k²+uk=−19208,   w²=2f.
It is finite free of rank 16 over D[T] by separate monic adjunction, embeds in the intended branch-independent degree-16 field by the generic rank check, and is a complete intersection, hence S₂. At dyadic primes w=0 and h,k independently equal 0 or u in the residue field. Where u is nonzero their equations are étale; where f is a unit the w-equation has linear part a unit times 2 in the regular D[u,w] local ring (2 a regular parameter); hence regular where both hold. Only u=0 (one degree-one point P), u=1 (four degree-one points) can fail, all codimension two by flat going-down. Characteristic zero is smooth by distinct simple branch contacts and the standard quadratic étale factors. Consequently R₁/S₂ proves this is the integral closure, not a special-fibre order guess.

On the reciprocal parameter s=T⁻¹, use x=1/u, s=x², restrict to D(∏_{j=1}³(1−b_j s)). Put H=h/u,K=k/u,W=w/u:
    H²+H=−8x², K²+K=−19208x², W²=2(1+343x²).
This finite free chart is the actual normalization: generic field and finiteness as above; it agrees exactly on x≠0 with the finite chart, while at dyadic x=0 the first two polynomials are étale and the last equation has independent linear part 2. At x=0 in characteristic zero W is a unit, so smooth there. Use S₂/R₁ again. Infinity is regular, with no guessed reciprocal twists. For the quadratic wild scalar field Q(√2) with independent untwisted roots a_i, the diagonal involution conjugates scalars, negates only a₃, and fixes a₀,a₁,a₂. Its fixed field is exactly L_q⁰ via w=√2 a₃ and r_i=a_i for i<3 (degrees 32 and 16 via branch valuations). On either patch the invariant subalgebra of the *true* integral closure in the split field equals the displayed algebra, by intersection with the fixed field and transitivity across the scalar integer ring Z[√2]. No ramified descent of the regular modification is invoked.

## The four u=1 points

Forget h,k étale-locally there, and put v=u−1. The base surface is w²=2(v²+2v+344). The reduced origin (2,v,w) pulls back to the reduced union locally at the four residue labels. Blow up this union. The leading homogeneous direction satisfies W₀²=0 if both 2 and v directions vanish (indeed the RHS is order at least 3). Thus 2,v charts cover. On the 2 chart v=2V,w=2Y:
     Y²=2(V²+V+86).
On its exceptional line only V=0,1 can be bad, because the coefficient is otherwise a unit. Near both use t=V²+V+86 as an étale coordinate (derivative 2V+1 a unit), giving the ordinary node Y²=2t. On the v chart put 2=vQ,w=vZ:
     vQ=2,    Z²=vQ(1+Q+43vQ³).
v-regularity follows by monic extension over D[v,Q]/(vQ−2) (domain; primitive linear polynomial); the 2 chart is monic over D[V]. After scale inversion both give exactly the original localization, so these are saturated ordinary ratio charts. On v=0, Q≠0 overlaps the 2 chart; the single Q=0 point has ambient regular parameters v,Q,Z (ambient D[v,Q,Z]/(vQ−2)) and quadratic leading form Z²−vQ. In all characteristics, including 2, the projective conic Z²=vQ is smooth: if v=Q=0 its equation forces Z=0, impossible. Blowup at this ordinary point resolves: each divided equation on a regular ambient blowup chart has the corresponding nonzero smooth conic chart modulo scale, implying no scale torsion (cancel after reducing mod scale) and regularity at all exceptional primes. The two étale 2-chart nodes also resolve in one ordinary blowup apiece by DYADIC-OPEN §3. Thus over each u=1 label there are precisely three first-stage singular F₂ points (the two double points in the 2 chart and this distinct v-direction). All twelve may be blown up in one reduced union. The four étale residue labels are preserved over these fibres by flat pullback; no restriction to rational directions in concluding regularity. Off the centers the operation is the identity.

## Three preparations at u=0

Here f is a unit and 2=w²/f. P has maximal ideal (u,h,k,w) and embedding dimension four (only the w-equation has a linear part, namely 2 in the regular ambient). Blow up the origin and follow the w chart three times as in TERMINAL-RESOLUTION §I. With u=wⁿS,h=wⁿA,k=wⁿB, n=0,1,2,3,
    fₙ=343+w^(2n) S²,  w²=2fₙ,
    A²+SA=−w^(6−2n)/fₙ³,    B²+SB=−2401 w^(6−2n)/fₙ³.
For n=0,1,2 the w,S directions cover by the equations in the Rees algebra. In the S direction w=Sz,A=Sa,B=Sb: the first equation is S²z²=2fₙ and the right sides of a²+a,b²+b are −S^(4−2n)z^(6−2n)/fₙ³ and 2401 times this. Their reductions define étale algebras over the regular exceptional z-line. For both charts the scale is a nonzerodivisor by imposing it first, then 2 times the unit, then the two independent monics (permute this regular sequence locally to place the scale last); after scale inversion the reverse substitution is exact. On the w chart S≠0 gives étale exceptional-divisor equations. The sole remaining point is w=S=A=B=0 at n=1,2 (edim four as before), and at n=3 the sole candidate has w=S=0,A=B=1. All are closed of residue F₂.

At n=3 abbreviate f=f₃, G=−1/f³. Omit B and invert A and f in the actual two-equation chart to obtain the base C near A=1,w=0. C/(w)=F₂[A,A⁻¹] (S=A⁻¹−A); w is a nonzerodivisor by the same chart test. Thus C there is regular local of dimension two with parameters w,p=A−1. In the surface put d'=B/A−1, e=d'−2400. Substitution in B²+SB=2401G gives the actual finite quadratic presentation over C
    e(e+t')=H w¹⁰,   t'=4801−1/(f³A²),   H=−75·2401/f⁵ .
Indeed d'²+(1+G/A²)d'−2400G/A²=0 and the split product is −2400·2401. Here t'∈(w,p)²: f−343=w⁶ S², (4801−343⁻³) has 2-valuation exactly 1, and 2=w²/f. In particular e,e+t',p are NOT regular parameters of the 3-dimensional ambient. The previous terminal argument cannot start here.

## The normalized blowup replacing the failed transverse crossing

At this point blow up the ideal (w,p) of the surface, supported on the single indicated point (mod w,p the e-equation is e²=0). This gives a coherent ideal globally: restrict on an affine neighbourhood so the support is just the closed point, and extend by the unit ideal on its complement. Locally the surface is finite free of rank two over C on basis 1,e; the two blowup rings are therefore the flat pullbacks of the actual charts of the regular-surface blowup of C at (w,p). Now normalize these two charts. Write b=w or p for the chart scale; by t'∈(w,p)² the function T_b=t'/b² belongs to its regular ambient base chart C_b. Adjoin E=e/b², monic with
     E(E+T_b)=H w¹⁰/b⁴.
This algebra is finite over the unnormalized chart (E integral, includes e=b²E), monic free over C_b, and embeds in the same degree-two generic extension: inverting b restores the original ring and b is a nonzerodivisor by freeness. On overlaps the E coordinates differ by the square of the invertible ratio. Away from b=0 the old model was regular in this neighbourhood (shrink at the point). The following calculation gives regularity on the exceptional divisor except at most one closed point. Thus the new chart is S₂ by its regular-base monic hypersurface and R₁ by the dimension-two finite-flat codimension argument, hence is precisely the normalization in the correct fraction field. The normalization morphism is finite on these charts and an isomorphism away from the closed center, so the modification is proper, not a detached coordinate substitution.

In the p chart w=pR, reduction modulo p gives T_p=1+R² mod p (coefficients evaluated in F₂). In detail set c=(4801−343⁻³)/2, a rational 2-adic unit; 4801·343³−1=193737667206≡2 mod4. Write D₀=(343⁻³−f⁻³)/w⁶ in C, regular via the difference-of-cubes factorization. The numerator A²t' is exactly
     2c + w⁶ D₀ + 4801(2p+p²).
Since 2=w²/f, at R=0 the derivative in E is the unit T_p, so the normalized finite algebra is étale and all these directions regular. All R≠0 points also lie on the w chart. In the w chart p=wP₁,
    T_w = (c/f + w⁴ D₀ + 4801(wP₁/f+P₁²))/(1+wP₁)².
Modulo w this is 1+P₁². At all P₁≠1 in the residue field the derivative in E is a unit; at P₁=1 there is just E=0. Neither test ignores non-rational exceptional points.

In the local ring of the regular ambient base chart at w=0, P₁=1, put z=P₁−1. Its regular parameters are w,z. Here T_w≡w modulo (w,z)²: c/f+4801 has zero residue, and differs from the even rational c/343+4801 by a multiple of w⁶, hence has order ≥2. The P₁² term has no linear z contribution, while 4801wP₁/f has nonzero linear w contribution; denominator is a unit with residue 1. In the regular 3-dimensional ambient with the additional parameter E, the three elements E,V=E+T_w,z therefore form a regular parameter system. Modulo w one has T_w=(P₁−1)² in F₂[P₁]. Consequently, in the DVR ambient/(E,V), w has order exactly 2 in z: the quotient by w has length two, since it is F₂[P₁] localized at P₁−1 modulo (P₁−1)². Hence w=αE+βV+γz² with γ a unit. The surface equation on this chart is exactly
     EV=H w⁶,
with H the previous unit. This proves that the remaining point really is singular (embedding dimension three), but restores a transverse pair after the normalized blowup.

Blow up twice at the successive z-direction crossing origins. In the first z chart E=zE₁,V=zV₁ the equation is E₁V₁=H z⁴(αE₁+βV₁+γz)⁶. After E₁=zE₂,V₁=zV₂ it is E₂V₂=H z⁸(αE₂+βV₂+γ)⁶, whose last factor is a unit at the only crossing. The two side directions have linear exceptional equations; each divided equation has nonzero reduction, so cancellation of the scale proves the native ordinary blowup chart matches, as in TERMINAL §III. From exponent 8, three further origin blowups give 6,4,2, and the fourth resolves the smooth conic XY=δZ² in residue characteristic 2. These blowups account for all exceptional points, not only the continuing chart. This finishes the dyadic resolution.

## Descent and global scope

For arbitrary q_i/q⁰_i dyadically unramified, the scalar compositum of their square roots is unramified at 2. Use its **maximal order** finite étale on an actual neighbourhood of 2, excluding all odd primes of bad contact, denominators, ramification and odd modification. After this base change the actual X_q identifies with X_q⁰ by branch-field scaling and étale normalization, with descent action given by constants and individual deck signs. The initial four u=1 centers and the next twelve singular points over them are stable unions; P over T=0 and each following unique singular point over its predecessor are invariant. For the nonreduced-center step one must check the ideal, not just the point. For deck signs ε_i one has w'=ε₃w, u'=ε₀u, h'=ε₁h+((ε₁−ε₀)/2)u. At n=3 this gives A'=ε₃(ε₁A+((ε₁−ε₀)/2)S); the half-gap is an integer. Since S=A⁻¹−A modulo w, S∈(w,A−1). Therefore A'−1∈(w,A−1): the constant sign gap 0 or −2 is already in (w²). Applying the inverse sign gives equality. The stable ideal descends across the **étale** ratio cover; its blowup commutes with flat base change, and taking its finite normalization commutes with this étale base change. All later reduced singular origins descend successively; at each stage their uniqueness follows from the verified charts (remaining origins with EV-type equation have embedding dimension three). Thus the resulting model has regular total space by étale descent. All described local closed centers extend on X_q⁰ over the chosen genuine base open, taking the unit ideal away from the support for the one nonreduced center. Outside 2 in this open the construction is the identity on the smooth normalization. Glue it to the earlier odd-prime model from RAMIFIED-THREE along their actual common unmodified X_q; properness is local on the base and flatness follows from integrality and torsion-freeness over the localizations of Z. The generic fibre is unchanged. Example q=(5,−3,13,10) is in this class since its four ratios with (1,1,1,2) are 5,−3,13,5. The fourth coordinate may have any odd valuation at 2 after changing square representatives, but its ratio to 2 must be dyadically unramified; no claim for arbitrary fourth-root odd valuation. Not minimality, semistability, KS/dualizing conclusions or the main theorem, and no local Lean run.
