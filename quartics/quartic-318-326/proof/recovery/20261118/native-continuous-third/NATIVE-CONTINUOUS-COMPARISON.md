# Reconstructing the degree-three continuous comparison without changing the representative

**New source/mathematics, not a recovered historic artifact.** The pinned native complex is `TopRep.homogeneousCochains X` in Mathlib's ContCohomology/Basic at 56a005855ea17e0b0958f73ec801ae140b0a1679. It is not the ordinary inhomogeneous complex of all set-theoretic cochains of the infinite group. Its own file lists the n-ary comparison for locally compact groups as a TODO. The following establishes that comparison mathematically in the degrees needed here, and gives a precise formalization specification.

Let G be the absolute Galois group of a characteristic-zero field with the Krull topology, and A its algebraic-closure units, written additively only through `Additive`, with the discrete topology. The action is the actual Galois action on units; it is jointly continuous: for a fixed u the stabilizer contains the open subgroup fixing the finite normal closure of u, and varying u in its singleton is permitted. Thus A defines the continuous ℤ-linear coefficient system X. Compact Hausdorff G is locally compact; none of this gives discrete topology on G.

**Native cochains and evaluation.** In degree n the native object is the invariant submodule of (n+1)-fold iterated C(G,−) applied to A. Equip continuous maps with the compact-open topology. Currying identifies jointly continuous maps G^(n+1)→A with these nested continuous maps: curry one variable at a time, with inverse continuous evaluation because each group-variable space is locally compact (and finite products are locally compact). The action on each nested function is diagonal: at a point (x₀,…,xₙ), action by s evaluates to s·f(s⁻¹x₀,…,s⁻¹xₙ). This follows successively from `ContRepresentation.coind₁_apply_apply`. Consequently native invariance is **equivariance** f(sx₀,…,sxₙ)=s·f(x₀,…,xₙ), not pointwise G-fixedness of its values.

**Two exact coordinate maps.** For native invariant f define E_n(f)(g₁,…,gₙ)=f(1,g₁,g₁g₂,…,g₁⋯gₙ). For a continuous inhomogeneous c define H_n(c)(x₀,…,xₙ)=x₀·c(x₀⁻¹x₁,x₁⁻¹x₂,…,xₙ₋₁⁻¹xₙ). Every map used here is continuous, hence each inhomogeneous cochain into A is locally constant. Left translation preserves the increments, proving H_n(c) is invariant. E_n H_n = id by successive cancellation. For H_n E_n, the partial products telescope to x₀⁻¹x_i: multiplying coordinates by x₀ and applying equivariance yields f(x₀,…,xₙ), not a conjugate or an inverse of f. These are additive group isomorphisms on cochains in degrees 2,3,4. A convergence topology on cohomology is not needed for the zero-class test.

**Differentials.** Native `TopRep.d X 0` is the constant map. Its successor is constant minus postcomposition with the preceding differential, so induction at a tuple gives δ_h f = Σ_i (−1)^i f(x₀,…,omit x_i,…,xₙ₊₁). The invariant differential is its restriction, by `homogeneousCochains.d_apply`. Evaluate at prefixes (1,g₁,g₁g₂,…). Omission of the leading 1 gives the actual g₁ action on the remaining increments, by equivariance; interior omissions multiply exactly the adjacent g_i g_(i+1); final omission drops the last argument. Thus E_{n+1}δ_h = δ_in E_n for n=2,3. In multiplicative coordinates these are exactly the repository's `unitDTwo` and `unitDThree`: action on the first term, alternating division/multiplication thereafter. The inverse H commutes by applying the inverse isomorphisms, not by dropping the leading action.

**Same-class zero criterion.** Let z be any particular value in `continuousUnitThreeCocycles`, not merely an abstract isomorphism class. H₃(Additive.ofMul∘z) is closed by the n=3 formula. Define its native class by applying `ContinuousCohomology.π X 3` to that very native cycle (via the standard cycles/kernel isomorphism). In `TopModuleCat`, the native cokernel used for homology is literally the module quotient by the algebraic range, with its quotient topology; **not the closure of the range**. Thus that class is zero exactly when H₃(z)=δ_h t for some actual native degree-two cochain t. Applying E₂,E₃ and the differential square gives z=unitDTwo(E₂t), with E₂t locally constant. Conversely an actual locally constant b with unitDTwo(b)=z gives primitive H₂(b). By the definition of the recovered quotient `ContinuousUnitHThree`, that is precisely `QuotientGroup.mk z = 1`. For z=`continuousDefect`, the right side is `actualThirdObstruction = 1`.
