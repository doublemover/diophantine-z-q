# The odd fourth sign and a uniform all-fourth-coefficients corollary

For d=±1 take q⁰=(d,d,d,−1), in the original parameter-preserving field r_i²=q_i(T−b_i), b=(0,32,76832,−343). We construct a proper flat regular model over Z of the actual twist normalization, also for independent dyadically unramified scalar multiples of these four coefficients. Combined with the independently reviewed untwisted, wild (−,−,−,+) and four (d,d,d,2a) cases, this gives an explicit all-Z model **for every fourth rational coefficient** as soon as the first three signed squarefree representatives are odd and all congruent modulo 4. No condition about their squares is imposed at odd primes, handled by the arbitrary-twist D(2) model. This does not treat unequal first-three odd residue types or even first-three squarefree representatives.

## True dyadic parameter charts

On the finite chart over D=Z_(2) use u=r₀, h=(r₁−u)/2, k=(r₂−u)/2, w=r₃−u−1. Then
    T=d u²,  h²+uh=−8d, k²+uk=−19208d,
    w²+2L=0,  L=(1+u)w+((1+d)/2)u²+u+172.
The L formula follows from r₃²=−d u²−343. The independent monics give an S₂ complete intersection and finite free rank 16 over D[T], embedding in the actual field by matching its degree at the generic point (independence by the four branch valuations). In char zero simple branch points are smooth. Mod 2, w vanishes; for nonzero u the h,k adjunctions are étale. If L is a unit the w-equation has independent ambient linear parameter 2. For d=1 the only possible nonregular finite points are therefore the single P above u=0, and four F₂ points at u=1. Near the latter the coordinate t=L is étale in u since its u-derivative is 1+w+2u. Thus these are actual étale copies locally of w²+2t=0, not an inference from their reduced fibres. For d=−1 only P remains on the finite chart. All candidates have codimension two by finite flatness, hence R₁; the chart is the true normalization.

On D(∏_{j=1}³(1−b_j s)) around s=0 set x=1/u, s=d x², H=h/u,K=k/u,W=w/u. The actual equations are
    H²+H=−8d x²,   K²+K=−19208d x²,
    W²+2J=0,    J=(1+x)W+(1+d)/2+x+172x².
These are rank-16 monics and agree literally on x≠0 with the finite chart; at infinity in characteristic zero they are smooth (unit ratios expressed before shifts). At x=0 over 2 when d=1 J is a unit; when d=−1 there are four degree-one points, with t=J an étale coordinate in x (derivative W+1+344x a unit) in the local neighbourhood. For étaleness use the étale Jacobian test for the one equation J−t in the relative variable x, without dividing by 172. R₁/S₂ again gives the true reciprocal normalization; those four points have the standard node with its actual reduced center. The reduced union of four simple nodes, finite u=1 for one sign or infinity for the other, resolves in one simultaneous blowup by the exact 2,t charts of DYADIC-OPEN §3. The finite labels h,k or H,K give étale pullback of the reduced base node locally, so no thickening is substituted for its center. In the reciprocal open an overlap point already seen on the finite chart is not counted twice.

The wild scalar invariant description does not require quotienting a regular scheme. Over the compositum of the square roots of the signs d and −1, in the untwisted branch field the diagonal scalar/sign group has fixed field exactly L_q⁰, by degree 16 and the branch valuations. On each parameter chart its actual normal integral ring has fixed subalgebra equal to its intersection with L_q⁰. This is precisely the displayed algebra, independently shown normal finite with the indicated fraction field.

## One unramified extraction separates two actual residual labels

For local calculations only adjoin α with α²=−343 via β=(1+α)/2, β²−β+86=0. This monic algebra is finite étale over D (2β−1 a unit at every prime over 2; the discriminant −343 a unit), a quadratic Galois torsor as maximal order locally at 2, with both residue roots since β²−β=0 over F₂. Its generic field is Q(√−7), NOT Q(i) or a wild extension. Calculations on the two dyadic localizations use α a unit with residue 1. Étale normalization preserves the already matched base chart. Write y=r₃−α, so
    y²+2αy=−d u² .
The reduced point P over u=0 on each coefficient sheet has ideal (2,u,h,k,y); here w=y−u+α−1 and α−1=2(β−1). Blow it up; the 2,u directions cover, since the degree-two equations force the initial h,k,y directions into any homogeneous prime containing initial 2,u. On the u chart put 2=uQ,h=ua,k=ub,y=uY:
    uQ=2,   a²+a=−d uQ³,   b²+b=−2401d uQ³,   Y²+αQY=−d .
All three adjunctions monic, scale u a nonzerodivisor over the regular base chart D'[u,Q]/(uQ−2) locally over a coefficient DVR D'. After inversion these are the actual original equations. Modulo u, Y is a unit, and αY is the derivative in Q of the Y-equation. Thus its divisor is smooth (solve for Q given Y≠0), with a,b étale, so every exceptional local ring of this chart is regular. This includes non-rational directions.

On the 2 chart set u=2S,h=2A,k=2B,y=2Y:
    A²+SA=−2d,   B²+SB=−2·2401d,   Y²+αY=−d S² .
2 is a nonzerodivisor by independent monics and the scale inversion comparison again proves saturation. The Y-equation is étale near the entire dyadic fibre. Where S≠0 the A,B equations are étale too. Above S=0 there are A=B=0 with Y=0 or its distinct other residue root α. Étale-locally at each forget Y and work at the origin in the first two equations; the local extension of its reduced origin is that reduced point because the local étale fibre is a field. The separated points above P are exactly the possible nonregular ones in this first exceptional fibre. The two Y labels are not assumed to descend individually.

## Exact split product at each label

In the first-root base put X=A, Y₀=A+S. This gives XY₀=−2d. The local base C at the origin is regular with parameters X,Y₀ (localized semistable DVR chart with the coefficient uniformizer linear in its equation). In the regular 3-dimensional ambient C[e] at its origin, use e=B−A and v=e+X+Y₀ instead of B. The second quadratic is exactly
    ev=2400 XY₀=H X⁶Y₀⁶,   H=−75d.
The first equality follows by substituting B=X+e,S=Y₀−X in B²+SB=2401XY₀. The second uses 2400=75·2⁵, 2=−d XY₀, d²=1. In particular e,v,X are a regular system of ambient parameters because Y₀=v−e−X. The surface origin has embedding dimension three. This is a different residual from the exponent-16 untwisted or the exponent-8 fourth-even calculation; no wild fixed-point criterion is used.

Blow up the origin with these three parameters. In the e,v side charts the divided equation has linear exceptional equation, since the term H X⁶Y₀⁶ has order twelve. In the X chart e=XE,v=XV we obtain
    EV=H X¹⁰(V−E−1)⁶.
Outside E=V=X=0 the exceptional divisor EV=0 is regular. At the indicated origin its last factor is a unit, hence this is the transverse type EV=X¹⁰·unit. Four subsequent ordinary origin blowups give successively exponent 8,6,4,2. The fifth at exponent 2 resolves the smooth conic EV=δZ² in characteristic 2; the two side charts of each prior step have linear divisor equation. For all chart identifications use the local ambient scale test of TERMINAL §III: each divided equation is nonzero modulo the scale in the polynomial exceptional plane, so cancellation gives scale-torsion-free quotients; localization recovers the old surface. Each nonterminal origin has edim 3 versus dimension 2, so its uniqueness as a singular point on its exceptional fibre is justified. Pull back these six point-blowups at each split label along the Y-étale chart. Taking the disjoint union of the relevant closed points at each stage resolves them without choosing either label. All intermediate points are closed over the original special fibre.

## Descent, projection to the actual twist, scope

The reduced node union is characterized by its parameter fibre and the unique residual P over T=0. Thus all 16 deck signs preserve their ideals, and the extra unramified coefficient Galois group preserves them too. After the P blowup the new union over P is intrinsically the reduced nonregular locus in that exceptional fibre. Each of its successors until the last is the unique nonregular point over its predecessor. Thus these finite unions and their reduced ideals are stable for the entire semilinear group even if a sign or coefficient Frobenius permutes the Y labels. Reduced structures commute with étale scalar changes. Alternatively the first two centers are already explicit downstairs and the later finite reduced singular loci can be descended stage by stage. Use a neighbourhood of 2 on which the compositum of Q(√−7) and the square-root fields of q_i/q⁰_i is finite étale; both are unramified at 2, since the signed squarefree representatives of the latter are odd ≡1 modulo 4. This Galois group acts on the actual base-changed normalization by deck signs and constants just as in SIGN-DESCENT §2. Descend the ideal inclusions, take ordinary blowups, and use faithful-flat étale descent of regularity. This strategy neither descends through the potentially ramified field of the original coefficients nor assumes a wild quotient regular.

Choose this as an actual open in Spec Z also excluding the finitely many odd contact, coefficient, discriminant and previous odd modification primes. Each dyadic center is a closed finite scheme in the special fibre and defines the stated modification on that open. Outside 2 in this neighbourhood the modifications are identities and X_q is smooth. Glue to the prior arbitrary-twist D(2) model on the common unchanged X_q as in RAMIFIED-THREE. Properness is local on the base, and the resulting integral surface is flat over Z. Its generic curve and parameter action are unchanged.

For the corollary select d=±1 to match the common first-three signed squarefree residues modulo 4. If q₃ has even 2-valuation, its unit squarefree representative is either 1 modulo 4 (previous all-plus fourth cases) or 3 modulo 4 (this note). If q₃ has odd 2-valuation, choose 2a according to its signed odd unit class modulo 4 and apply RAMIFIED-DYADIC-DD2. The ratios used in every case then satisfy the genuine dyadically unramified criterion. Example (3,7,11,3) uses (−1,−1,−1,−1); (1,5,13,−1) uses (1,1,1,−1). This is not a model for all twists, and no minimality, semistability, rank, height or global Lean theorem follows.
