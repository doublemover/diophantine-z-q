# Two explicit directions covering the actual first blowup, all 240 mixed origins

For each of the 240 unequal-first-three representatives q∈{±1,±2}⁴, this packet computes two explicitly encoded functions f,g in the genuine maximal ideal m of the unique F₂ origin over T=0. With J=(f,g), exact finite-ring arithmetic certifies

    m^(k+1) = J m^k

in that local ring. In 234 representatives the displayed k is 2; in six it is 1. Therefore D_+(f·z) and D_+(g·z) cover the **actual ordinary point blowup** above that origin. Its true affine rings are B[m/f] and B[m/g] in the fixed branch field; one must still normalize and study these rings to evaluate all successor points. This eliminates the careless alternative of replacing the center by the unrelated parameter ideal (2,T), and gives the Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity at every initial mixed origin: 4 for 180 and 8 for 60. The packet does not claim these two individual rings are regular or already normal.

## How to decode the two generators

Start with the relevant finite normalization record of initial-bases-512.jsonl.gz, obtaining the ordered free basis b_0,…,b_15 over D[T]. All coordinates below use exactly that deterministic basis, not the raw monomial ordering. Reduce the ring at (2,T) and read the unique F₂-point character: its ith coordinate is the ith bit of the integer origin, zero-based. The 18 candidate generators in order are
    g_0=2,  g_1=T,  g_(i+2)=b_i−bit_i(origin) for 0≤i<16 .
They generate m: their common quotient identifies the whole algebra with the residue F₂, by the multiplication-table presentation. The list cotangent records the indices of a linearly independent spanning subset of their classes in m/m². For each binary integer in masks, sum g_(cotangent[j]) over its set bits j, with plain integer coefficient +1. The resulting two actual elements are f and g. Thus a record produces literal Laurent/field polynomials, not just functions known modulo the maximal ideal. For q=(1,−1,1,1) the encoded pair is equivalent to (r₀,1+r₃), in agreement with the analytic first-blowup calculation for this family.

## Finite torsion arithmetic, and why it certifies equality in the local ring

Write B for that finite-chart normalization and I=(2,T)B. B is free of rank 16 over D[T]. B/I^N is a finite additive 2-group, with coordinates on T^j b_i (0≤j<N) modulo 2^(N−j). Here N=4 for every reported test. Its log₂ order is 16N(N+1)/2=160. The fiber B/I has a unique geometric residue point, rational over F₂, so B/I^N is local and the projections from B or B_m give the same finite group. Multiplication by 2,T and the b_i−bit_i is given by exact polynomial multiplication and the supplied structure coefficients of B; terms of degree ≥N in T and multiples of the indicated coordinate moduli vanish. The code uses an equivalent common-modulus embedding: multiply every T^j coordinate by 2^j and work inside (Z/2^N)^(16N). Thus a structure coefficient of degree a contributes an extra factor 2^a in the operation matrix. Initial vectors and all generating sets remain inside the correct embedded subgroup.

Compute the images G_j=(m^j+I^N)/I^N by multiplying a generating set of G_(j−1) by each of the 18 generators. Reduce the finite additive generating rows by exact 2-adic pivot elimination: in each pivot column pick the smallest nonzero 2-adic valuation v, scale the row by an odd unit to get pivot 2^v, eliminate its column from the others, and include 2^(N−v) times the pivot row in the later residual subgroup. That last operation is necessary because annihilating its pivot need not annihilate the rest of the row. This constructs generators and computes the logarithm of the group order by adding N−v for each pivot. ordinary F₂ rank of these integer rows would be incorrect.

For each proposed pair and k∈{1,2}, the final strict_cardinality_check is an *exact* comparison
    |(Jm^k + m^(k+2) + I^N)/I^N| = |(m^(k+1) + I^N)/I^N|.
The data store the two base-2 logarithms. The left group is visibly contained in the right, so equal cardinalities imply equality. Since I⊂m and N≥k+2, I^N⊂m^(k+2), this gives m^(k+1)=Jm^k+m^(k+2). Apply Nakayama to the finite module m^(k+1)/Jm^k over the **actual local ring**: m times it is itself, so it vanishes. The individual candidate functions in J are still exactly the lifted functions in the output; the computation did not merely find abstract tangent directions. All certificate powers are small enough that the quotient truncation is mathematically sufficient.

The preliminary search, not needed to trust its final integer-cardinality check, operates on m^j/m^(j+1), which is an F₂-vector space. Digits of a group element in the finite strong pivot generators are reduced mod 2 together with digits of the denominator subgroup and the torsion-carry relations of every pivot generator. The code checks the resulting dimension against the difference of the two exact group logarithms. Products by sums of selected linear directions are then linear maps on these graded pieces; this permits checking all binary pairs quickly. The successful pair is always rechecked with its *full* finite additive matrices as above. For k=2 the output also checked k=1 across the searched choices, but no claim of absolute minimality of a reduction number is required.

## Blowup and multiplicity consequences

In the Rees ring of m, degree k+1 belongs to the homogeneous ideal (fz,gz), where z denotes the Rees variable. Therefore any homogeneous prime containing both contains all positive-degree generators in its radical, impossible in Proj. This proves two-chart coverage on the local scheme (and on a suitable neighbourhood of the origin). These charts are the full ratio algebras B_m[m/f] and B_m[m/g], not the possibly smaller algebras B_m[g/f] and B_m[f/g]. The Rees ring of m is finite over that of J since degrees beyond k follow by multiplying by J; equality of their normalized blowups follows by taking integral closure in the common graded fraction field (or equivalently the finite birational map of their Proj and normalization in the common function field). This does NOT assert equality of their unnormalized blowups.

The same identity gives m^(k+1)⊂J, hence I^N⊂J and the finite computation of |B_m/J| is exact. This is a two-element m-primary ideal in a two-dimensional Cohen–Macaulay local ring (the initial normalization is finite free over D[T] and is normal). The generators form a system of parameters and a regular sequence. For such a sequence the associated graded ring for J is (B_m/J)[X,Y], so e(J)=length(B_m/J). The reduction identity gives e(m)=e(J) directly: J^n⊂m^n⊂J^(n−k) for n≥k and the two bounding length polynomials have identical quadratic leading term. This is the stated local Hilbert–Samuel multiplicity, i.e. the degree of the tangent cone counted scheme-theoretically, not the number of exceptional curves or successor singularities. The data's hilbert list contains only h_j=length(m^j/m^(j+1)) for j=0,…,3, not a claimed entire Hilbert series.

Exact counts from the 240 records: (k,e)=(2,4) in 174, (2,8) in 60, (1,4) in 6. Over unramified scalar variations the geometric reduction identities transport étale-locally, but the particular binary pair may change by a sign action and is not claimed to descend as the identical pair to every twist. The canonical point blowup itself does commute with that base change. No Lean acceptance or complete resolution of the 240 origins is implied.
