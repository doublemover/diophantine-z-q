# Prescribing the absolute 8-rank bit without changing the two Selmer matrices

Retain the frozen genus packet hypotheses. For the three actual *ray-prepared* contacts, β=17 times five distinct positive primes ≡1 mod408; those five are not 17. All p|β are 1 mod4, β≡1 mod8, and the two support kernels are as in the original producer. The following proof applies to any such β, or more generally any squarefree β with all its primes 1 mod4, β≡1 mod8, and 17|β for the particular switching prime below. No assertion of these extra hypotheses is made for the fourth, scalar β.

For d=βγ, the genus packet supplies a primitive solution βU²+γV²=W², gcd(W,2d)=1, V≠0, and ε(γ)=(W/β), with ε=−1 for 8-rank zero and +1 for 8-rank one. The kernel gives (γ/p)=+1 for every p|β. Write (γ/p)₄ for the sign γ^((p−1)/4) mod p. Reducing W²=γV² mod p gives (W/p)=(γ/p)₄(V/p); indeed raise the equality to (p−1)/4, with p∤VW. For an odd prime r|V one has r∤β by the preceding unit condition. If r|W then r|U by the equation, contrary to primitivity; hence U and W are units mod r and (β/r)=+1. Quadratic reciprocity gives (V/β)=1: odd factors contribute (β/r)=+1, the sign and 2 contribute +1 since β>0 and β≡1 mod8 and all its primes 1 mod4. Thus

    ε(γ) = ∏_(p|β) (γ/p)₄,
    ε(γθ)/ε(γ) = ∏_(p|β) (θ/p)₄.

Here the comparison is multiplicativity; the inputs to every quartic symbol are quadratic residues since the row conditions hold for both supports. The second sign equals (ℓ/β) for the actual prepared denominator in the earlier packet; this new formula does not require extracting ℓ.

One can select the absolute sign while keeping the paired offset fixed. Before freezing the final prime q₀, write n=Aq₀. Keep q′≡q₀ mod each other old prime except 17 (including all primes of θ). At 17 take either q′≡q₀ mod17, or q′≡2q₀ mod17. The factor 2 is a square but not a fourth power mod17 since 2⁴≡−1. Take q′≡23A⁻¹ mod24 (the usual split refinement, 7 mod12). The congruences are at coprime moduli and all prescribed residues are units, so CRT/Dirichlet give arbitrarily large allowed choices. Quadratic symbols at all old primes, and reverse symbols by reciprocity and fixed mod4, stay unchanged. At both n and nθ the actual first-order matrices and sharp finite controls therefore hold on the newly chosen products. Both products are 23 mod24. The choice at 17 switches both quartic bits; their ratio is fixed. In particular the restricted or alternatively the full field can be made to have e=2, and both can simultaneously have e=2 iff their relative sign is +1. This is a fresh choice before the rank witnesses: existing rational points are not transported. The three original contact indices may choose primes independently, avoiding all finite exclusions. The common source values are not instantiated; the scalar pole always retains the general genus test, not necessarily this tuning.
