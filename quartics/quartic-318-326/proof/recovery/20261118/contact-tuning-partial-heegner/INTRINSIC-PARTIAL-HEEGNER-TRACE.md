# An intrinsic nonvanishing component of the actual Heegner point

Take either split-refined original support d=n or nθ from the indexed producer (or any positive squarefree d≡23 mod24 satisfying the same genus calculation). Write D=−d, K=Q(√D), C=Cl(K), H its Hilbert class field, A=C_odd, L=H^A. Let E=E₋₁: Y²=X(X+1)(X−3). The quadratic twist by D is the original E_d. The theorem below uses an actual oriented Heegner point of level 48 for the maximal order, rather than a formal class-group module.

**The rational Fricke translation.** For the modular map φ of the split-Heegner packet use f=η₂η₄η₆η₁₂, g=η₄⁴η₁₂⁴/(η₂η₆η₈η₂₄), A₀=(f+g)/2, B=f(2z), x=B/A₀ and y=(q dx/dq)/A₀. Here η_k=η(kz). The previously verified identities are y²=x⁸+14x⁴+1, t=x−1/x, w=y/x², X=(w+t²+2)/2, Y=tX, φ(∞)=O and φ*(dX/2Y)=g dq/q. The level-48 newform has Fricke eigenvalue −1 (analytic sign +1, see [48.2.a.a](https://www.lmfdb.org/ModularForm/GL2/Q/holomorphic/48/2/a/a/)); thus φ∘w₄₈+φ has zero differential and is constant in characteristic zero. Its value is T=φ(0) at the cusp ∞. We compute the sign as well as the order. As z=iv tends to 0 along v>0, η(kiv)~(kv)^(-1/2) exp(−π/(12kv)). Hence f~(24v²)^(-1)exp(−π/(12v)), B~(96v²)^(-1)exp(−π/(24v)), g~(48v²)^(-1)exp(−π/(24v)). In particular x→1. All eta factors and A₀ are positive along this ray. The real y starts at +1 at ∞ and cannot vanish since x⁸+14x⁴+1>0. Hence y→4, w→4, t→0 and T=(3,0), not O.

**Odd-part trace over the true class field.** Let x₀∈X₀(48)(H) be an oriented Heegner point. Complex conjugation c and the Fricke involution both reverse its full level orientation (the dual isogeny replaces the chosen norm-48 ideal by its conjugate); C acts transitively on the packet of any fixed orientation. Thus some σ∈C has w₄₈x₀=σ c(x₀). This is the usual CM reciprocity on Heegner points, cf. [Gross–Zagier, I.1](https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/zagier/files/doi/10.1007/BF01388809/fulltext.pdf) and [He–Hu–Tian, §3.2](https://www.sciengine.com/doi/pdf/C674839BE2234E14B7ACCE693F245CF6). Only the reciprocity description is used, not a nonvanishing theorem for a different twisting family. In Gal(H/Q)=C⋊〈c〉, conjugation acts by inversion on C, so s=σc is an involution normalizing every subgroup of C. Put P=φ(x₀), Q=Σ_(a∈A) aP. Then Q∈E(L) and

    P+sP=T,                Q+sQ=|A| T=T.

We have E(H)[2∞]=E[2](Q). In fact for a half of any nonzero 2-torsion root, its two differences from the other roots must be squares in H (the elementary 2-Kummer criterion). In order of roots −1,0,3 these conditions require √−1, √−3 and √3 respectively. H/Q is unramified at 2 and 3 since H/K is unramified at finite places and both split in K. The indicated quadratic subfields ramify at 2 or 3. Thus H has no such half and no point of order 4. If Q were torsion its 2-primary part would be rational and killed by 2, contrary to Q+sQ=T after projecting to the 2-primary part. Consequently Q is nontorsion on every original split-refined support, without any numerical nonvanishing assumption.

**A character cuts the extension degree.** B₂=Gal(L/K)=C/A is abelian of exponent 2^e, with B₂≅(C₂)^(t−2)×C_(2^e) by the reviewed genus computation; t is the number of primes of d. In the complex representation generated by the B₂-orbit of Q modulo torsion, some χ-isotypic projection of Q is nonzero. Set U=kerχ (no assumption that χ is trivial). The trace Q_U=Σ_(u∈U)uQ lies in E(M) for M=L^U, and has nonzero χ-projection, namely |U| times that of Q, hence is nontorsion. Also Q_U+sQ_U=|U|T, and Z=Q_U−sQ_U=2Q_U−|U|T is nontorsion with sZ=−Z. Thus Z gives a nontorsion point on the exact twist E_d over F=M^〈s〉 (using restriction of the standard quadratic-twist isomorphism). Indeed s restricts nontrivially to K, M=FK, and M/F is quadratic with character D. Also

    M/K is cyclic unramified of degree |χ|,     [F:Q]=[M:K]=|χ| divides 2^e.

M/Q is generalized dihedral; F is its specified reflection field, not assumed Galois or totally real. In the 8-rank-zero case e=2, one gets a nonzero Heegner component on E_d over a field of degree 1, 2 or 4 within this constrained unramified class field. The field and character may differ between paired twists. The content is the nonzero class-field CM component, not the bare existence of a point over some uncontrolled quadratic field.

**Why rational nonvanishing still does not follow.** The full R=Tr_(L/K)Q may have zero image modulo torsion. As a formal control take G=C₂×〈s〉 acting on V=Z⊕C₂ by a(m,b)=(−m,b), s(m,b)=(−m,b+m mod2). These are commuting involutions. With Q=(1,0), T=(0,1) one has Q+sQ=T, all 2-primary torsion is fixed, but Q+aQ=0. The same model inflates along any nontrivial quadratic quotient of B₂. It is not claimed to be the actual CM orbit. On the indexed curves one still needs a nonzero trivial B₂ component (or another method) to prove the rational rank, original markings and final theorem. There is no transfer of ranks from numerical examples, and no Lean elaboration in this packet.
