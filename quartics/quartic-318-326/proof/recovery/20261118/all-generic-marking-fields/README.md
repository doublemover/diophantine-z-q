# Simultaneous algebraic realization of every generic primitive marking
A complex coordinate with finite orbit under all automorphisms fixing the base field is algebraic: a transcendental coordinate belongs to a transcendence basis, translations of the basis variables extend through the algebraic closure, and yield infinitely many distinct translates. Apply this to the genuine finite set of exact-order points on the complex arithmetic generic elliptic curve. Adjoining every marking coordinate and taking its normal closure inside the complex numbers gives one finite Galois subextension realizing every label. For each point and diamond subgroup its actual orbit fixed field satisfies the previously proved degree bound. This proves simultaneous existence without assuming the complex numbers are algebraic over the generic field. The comparison with a chosen coarse modular function field is still separate.
