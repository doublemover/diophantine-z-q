# Opposing odd majority pair: all 48 remaining mixed-parity even-fourth types

As in PARITY24 choose the direct or integral exchanged parameter chart so that two first-three working coefficients are odd and one even; working fourth is 2a₃. Now the two odd coefficients have opposite signs. Pick j<k their indices, signs d and −d, even minority index m with q_m=2a. With two contact tuples e this gives 2·3·2·2·2=48 distinct original types, disjoint from both matching-sign cases. We explicitly resolve these origins. The important change is that the first **normalized** point blowup needs a new product on both charts. The second normalized blowup admits a genuine étale comparison on only one chart; its nonoverlapping side corner requires a different normalization.

Put u=y_j, w=y₃, f₀=a₃(e_j−e₃), f=f₀+a₃ d u², w²=2f,
    H=d(e_k−e_j)/2=2^h H₀,   M=a(e_j−e_m)/2=2⁴M₀,
    A₀=(ad−f₀)/2, C₀=(ad−a₃d)/2, θ∈{0,1} with θ≡A₀ mod2 .
Here h=4 or 9. On the neighbourhood inverting f,1+u set v=(y_k−u)/w, b=((1+u)y_m−uw)/2. The true starting order is given by two monics over the regular u,w base:
    f v²+w u v+u²=H,
    b²+w u b=M(1+u)²+u²(A₀+ad u+C₀u²).
The second follows from the even-radical half-product and the first from the opposite odd root. The free rank and field comparison follow by recovering y_k,y_m and the branch degree (eight over the u-line). At a generic vertical point the b residue quadratic has RHS with nonzero derivative u², giving a field, so w is still a uniformizer in the regular height-one stalk after b. In that DVR shift v by u/(1+u); the equality has nonzero linear-in-w constant w u²/(1+u), since f·(u/(1+u))²+u²−H is divisible by 2. Hence the full vertical generic ring is regular, though v has a repeated residue. Char zero smoothness, finite flatness and R₁/S₂ give the normalization. Its sole origin has m=(w,u,v,b).

## The first actual point blowup and the necessary product

The w,u directions cover by the leading quadratics. On w side s=u/w, x=v/w−s, y=b/w−θs. The saturated ordinary chart is monic and the equations are
    f x²+(2f+w)s x+(f+w+1)s²=H/w²,
    y²+(2θ+w)s y+(θ²−A₀+θw−ad u−C₀u²)s²=M(1+u)²/w².
Here u=ws; write these as x²=w A, y²=w B, with A affine linear in x alone and B affine linear in y alone (coefficients are in the base ring). The divisibilities use the indicated high contact orders, w²=2f and u=ws. Adjoin l=xy/w; its products satisfy xy=w l, xl=A y, yl=B x, l²=AB. Therefore the module on 1,x,y,l is closed under products: the affine-linear A(x),B(y) expressions reduce any xy by wl. It is free of rank four by generic field independence and finite over the ordinary ratio ring; it includes every ordinary generator. Along w=0 all primes have x=y=0; then Ā=s² and B̄=s²(θ+s). Hence l²=s⁴(θ+s) on the reduced residue directions. At s≠0, A is a unit, w=x²/A and y=xl/A. The quotient by x there gives exactly the smooth curve l²=s⁴(θ+s), with derivative s⁴ in s. To see exactness, modulo x set w=y=0 after localizing at A; the presentation by the displayed table is exact because it spans the four independent module elements (with unit 1). Thus the dimension-two total local rings there are regular. At s=0 only x=y=l=0; A,B lie in the square of the maximal ideal there, as is seen by dividing the two equations by w (the high RHS are of order ≥5). Consequently x²,y²∈m³ and l²∈m⁴; all the table equations together with base w,s give no linear relation and the remaining origin is singular.

On the u side R=w/u, x=v/u−1,y=b/u−θ, adjoin l_u=xy/u from x²=u A_u,y²=u B_u. Indeed the analogously divided equations now have constants f+w+1 and θ²−A₀+θw−ad u−C₀u². On the exceptional line Ā_u=R, B̄_u=θR+1 when x=y=0. At R=0, B_u is a unit; eliminate u=y²/B_u and x=yl_u/B_u. The divisor by y is exactly l_u²=R(θR+1), smooth by the R derivative 1. Thus this corner is regular; the rest overlaps w side. Both finite presentations are Cohen–Macaulay since free over a regular two-dimensional base (base parameters regular). They are normal by Serre since their only possible nonregularity on the exceptional divisor is the single closed w-chart origin. Off the chosen scale the original normal ring returns. Consequently they are the true finite normalizations of the blowup and match on overlaps; the functions called l on the two sides need not be identical.

## The second normalized blowup, main w side

Blow up (w,s,x,y,l). Its w,s directions cover (x²,y²∈m³ and l²∈m⁴). On the w chart write
    P=u/w², X=x/w=(y_k−u)/w³−P, Z=y/w=b/w²−θP, l/w=XZ .

Then
    y'_m²=σ d (T−e_m)
where T is the working parameter. The other two roots y_j,y_k already have opposite unit signs, w is the same original even last root. Thus this is precisely a working all-unit mixed first-three triple with last 2a₃ and the **same contacts**. The replacement is generically reversible on this mixed chart (recover y_m=π y'_m/δ), not a global isomorphism assumed over a ramified constants extension.

Take the independently calculated **second w-chart** of PREFIX24 for that unit triple. If y_i' is its repeated-sign reference (smaller index), y_b' its other matching root and y_c' the opposite sign, its exact generators over the coefficient base are

To prove the comparison is **the finite integral closure of our ordinary blowup w chart**, not just the same generic curve, note that every old ratio belongs to this étale extended comparison chart. The comparison covers **all** points of the exceptional w chart: f,1+u are units everywhere over the original origin and the corresponding comparator denominators are units there (its own 1+y_i' if used has residue 1).
