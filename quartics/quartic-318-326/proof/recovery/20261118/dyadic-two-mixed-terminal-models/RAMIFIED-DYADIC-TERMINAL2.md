# Two genuinely mixed odd dyadic patterns: all successors explicitly resolved

For d=±1 consider q⁰=(−d,d,d,−1). The original normalized projective four-root model admits an explicitly chart-verified proper flat regular modification over **all Z**, also after independently multiplying its four scalar entries by dyadically unramified rational squareclasses. These are genuinely mixed first-three dyadic classes. Starting from the first ordinary blowup in RAMIFIED-DYADIC-FIRST12, we resolve its last two singular geometric points by further ordinary point blowups. We neither assert the same calculation for the other ten mixed odd types nor derive semistability or minimality.

## Exact successor, and the two charts of its next blowup

The equal roots are j=1,k=2, minority m=0 in the first-blowup paper. Each successor there is étale-locally (the separated Z label on a finite étale chart) the closed origin in
    A²+SA=−75d·2⁶,      V²+2 S V+2S²=−d·2³ .
Indeed H/4=d(32−76832)/16=−4800d, M/2=d(0−32)/4=−8d. The coefficient DVR D' is unramified at 2 with residue F₂ for the split auxiliary coefficient sheets. Total-space local dimension is two and its embedding dimension is four. The étale map sends the local reduced point ideal to the maximal ideal, because the étale fibre there is a field. Blow up (2,S,A,V) and pull back this picture across that map, taking both labels. The 2, S directions cover the exceptional fibre since their vanishing forces the initial A and V degree-one generators to vanish by the quadratics. On S direction put 2=SQ,A=Sa,V=SW. Then
    a²+a=−75d S⁴Q⁶,          W²=SQ(−dQ²−W−1).
They are exact monics over the regular ratio base D'[S,Q]/(SQ−2), so S a nonzerodivisor and scale inversion gives the native old localized equations. Above S=0 all Q≠0 also lie on the 2 chart; at Q=0 there are two a labels and W=0. The a extension is étale. The W equation has smooth projective tangent conic W²+SQ over the residue field in regular ambient parameters S,Q,W. One ordinary blowup of those reduced nodes is regular at every exceptional direction: the two S,Q side charts are linear modulo scale, on the W chart both ratios are nonzero. Smooth divided conic equations are nonzero on the ambient exceptional affine planes, and give scale-torsion-free hypersurface charts by reduction and cancellation. Thus this corner contributes no further successor.

On the 2 chart S=2s,A=2b,V=2w the true equations are
    b²+s b=−75d·2⁴,       w²=2f,  f=−d−s²−s w .
Here monic freeness over D'[s] gives scale saturation. On the dyadic fibre w=0 and b(b+s)=0. If s≠0,1 in the residue field, f is a unit and the b adjunction étale, so this is regular (w²−2f has the independent linear parameter 2). We check the entire remaining s=1 and s=0 loci separately.

## s=1: one more blow followed by an ordinary smooth-conic point

The b extension is étale at s=1, giving two F₂ labels. In the w-base put t=s−1 and δ=(1+d)/2. The equation is
    w²+2(1+t)w+2t²+4t+4δ=0.
At the origin (2,t,w) this is in the square of the ambient maximal ideal and yields a nonregular surface, with independent étale labels as above. Blow up the reduced union of these origins. The 2,t directions cover: the homogeneous equation forces the initial w generator into a prime containing initial 2,t. On the 2 chart t=2T,w=2W, the equation
    W²+(1+2T)W+2T²+2T+δ=0
is étale along the special fibre. On the t chart 2=tP,w=tW, the equation
    W²+P(1+t)W+tP+tP²+δP²=0
is monic over the regular D'[t,P]/(tP−2). These are true scale charts by nonzerodivisor and inversion. At t=P=0 there is one W=0 origin per label with tangent conic W²+PW+tP+δP². The t derivative forces P=0 at a singular conic point, then its equation gives W=0 and the P derivative forces t=0; this is impossible projectively. A single ordinary blowup resolves it, including non-rational directions by smoothness and the scale cancellation test. P≠0 already belongs to the 2 chart. This settles the s=1 locus independently of the sign of d.

## s=0: four ordinary preparations, and a genuinely smooth last direction

Here f is a unit and 2=w²/f. Thus (w,s,b) is the reduced point ideal and
    b²+s b=−75d w⁸/f⁴.
Blow up its origin, following the w direction four successive times. On the w chart after n=0,1,2,3,4 let s=wⁿS, b=wⁿB. Put
    fₙ=−d−w^(2n) S²−w^(n+1)S,
    w²=2fₙ,       B²+SB=−75d w^(8−2n)/fₙ⁴ .
At n=1,2,3 only the origin w=S=B=0 of this chart can remain singular. At n=0,1,2,3 the w,S directions cover the ordinary blowup: if both leading forms vanish, the monic relation forces the initial B form to vanish. The next w chart is displayed. On the S chart put w=SR and B=SC; the first equation is S²R²=2fₙ with substitution and the second is
    C²+C=−75d S^(6−2n)R^(8−2n)/fₙ⁴ .
For both charts the scale is a nonzerodivisor: in the polynomial ambient with fₙ inverted, impose the scale first, then the first equation reduces to two times a unit, then the independent monic. These form a regular sequence, locally permutable along the chart to place the scale last. Inversion gives the exact previous scale localization and the ratio generators give the actual Rees chart. On the S directions the exceptional monic over F₂[R] is étale (derivative 1), hence the total space is regular. On the continuing w chart modulo w the right side is 0 for n=1,2,3 and equals 1 for n=4. If S≠0 the B-derivative is nonzero. If S=0 at n=1,2,3, the origin really is singular: its regular two-dimensional first-equation base has parameters w,S (w a nonzerodivisor and its quotient the regular S-line), and the remaining monic equation is in the square of the ambient maximal ideal, giving a nonregular hypersurface. At n=4, the exceptional equation B²+SB=1 implies B≠0 when S=0, so the derivative with respect to **S** is a unit. The exceptional divisor is smooth at all its primes, and the old total-space ring is regular there. This last calculation depends on having a *single* remaining quadratic; it is not the previous four-root two-equation terminal case. No new singular point remains.

## Proper global modification and scope

After the initial mixed-odd origin blowup from the prior packet only its two singular successors required study. Above each the first new blowup creates: the resolved-by-one-blow side nodes; the s=1 points resolved in two more steps; and the s=0 point resolved in four more steps. These are disjoint closed branches. They may be carried out simultaneously round by round, blowing up the full reduced nonregular locus at each round, with a shorter branch unchanged once regular. Thus the initial blowup plus at most five further simultaneous blowup rounds suffice over this original dyadic origin. At each intermediate point the chart proofs cover the entire exceptional fibre; ordinary blowup charts are domains via the indicated saturation and known generic field, complete intersections with at worst finitely many singular points, so already normal. There is no additional modification away from the origin and its successors.

Every reduced center is intrinsic as a whole finite singular union, hence stable under scalar and deck signs even when they interchange étale residue labels. Across a maximal-order finite étale coefficient extension adjoining only square roots of the unramified ratios (and the split unramified auxiliary α from the prior paper), true normalized models and the ideals compare compatibly. Descend their ordinary blowups by flat base change; regularity descends étale locally. Spread the finite closed dyadic centers over a genuine base neighbourhood of 2 which omits all other possible odd coefficient, contact and modification primes. On its overlap with the reviewed arbitrary-twist D(2) model both are the original unchanged X_q; glue to obtain the asserted proper integral regular model, flat over Z. In concrete terms these two new patterns have odd signed squarefree representatives, q₁,q₂ equal modulo 4, q₀ of opposite sign type, and q₃≡3 mod4. The result is geometric/source-level, not Lean-accepted, with no rank, minimality or final 318/326 consequence.
