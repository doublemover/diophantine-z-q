# Independent fixed-window lower-bound and upper/lower comparison audit

Scope: manuscript a0950ac7, distinct cleared slopes, fixed Gaussian ray cutoffs; source 70e66e8b. I find the lower-bound argument sound. This was not obtained by applying a stated polynomial Chebotarev theorem. It uses unconditional [Frei–Tinková 1.4, (1.15)](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.17441v3) and the [published Tao–Ziegler Thms 9, 11 and 13, Cor. 3](https://doi.org/10.1017/fmp.2017.3), with the following additional checks.

* At each **fixed** prime-cutoff field, conductor D and sieve level v, the number of centering terms, congruence/wrap cells and step sizes q is finite. The cube gradients have a shared nonzero coordinate and are pairwise distinct at every p not dividing Wq. Polytopes after wrapping/positive support have fixed-scale affine intercepts O_v(n), allowed by FT's relative size. Narrow steps J are proportional to n at fixed v; dividing its o_v(n^(2a+1)) error by nJ^(2a) therefore still gives o_v(1). On D residue classes, Dη=(D/φ(D))μ (at the minimal conductor first; ordinary factors at extra primes in D are absorbed), and φ(W)/W cancels *all* constant local factors. Remaining collisions p|q,p>v cost at most Σ1/p≪log(q)/(v log v)≪1/log v. The sum of normalized volumes of decomposed cells is at most one, not their count multiplying the local error. Higher prime powers give a negligible error at frozen v since each gradient is nonzero. For a single active form or none the same bound or direct lattice count applies.
* Theorem 13 (quadratic differences, after reordering the centered factor first) asks for an average of boxes with nonzero linear step polynomials bounded by A^C, the number of steps and auxiliary variables bounded prior to κ selection. Enlarge A'=A^B0, κ'=κ/B0 first; f is genuinely bounded by the ordinary prime majorant at BOTH truncations. On M1≈τ√(n/W)/A the published Thm11 upper scale is actually (A′ n)^(1/2), stronger than A′√n but still satisfied. Its first part, via Cor.3, gives the SAME f′ simultaneously close in the relevant average of boxes and all step-q cubes. Its second part, restricted to the ordinary weight, is NOT applied. Theorem 9's δ is independent of A′ and its box-to-cube test at J=M1²/(A′)^(2a) is proportional to n at frozen sieve level. Triangle with the bounded masked periodic μ thus controls f−μ in the required box. Both arbitrary domination and the centering cancellation are essential. The general one-polynomial case also follows directly by summing the one-form PNT in a fixed residue class for each w, but the actual distinct-slope application has k>1.
* N and T need not be uniform in the field, modulus D, or fixed τ. For arbitrary prescribed errors choose v→∞ sufficiently slowly that the finitely many Gowers/FT tests and counts at each v hold for every N beyond their threshold. Dense and concatenation theorems allow such slower choices; equivalent diagonal construction works on an arbitrary sequence violating the claimed liminf. Dependence of the bound for the bounded model on fixed κ′ does not change the argument. At residue reassembly the total multiplier on a uniform error is **not** (W/φ(W))^k: the density of tuples giving unit residues cancels this, leaving ∏_{p|W} b_p uniformly bounded. I checked CRT substitution at five mixed prime-power W,D pairs independently and recomputed 27 ordinary factors/12 cube-gradient cases (see adjacent script/replay).
* If all masked vertices lie in [5n/8,7n/8] and shifts have size ≤n/32, the attained integer x and all inputs are genuine, never cyclic wraps in the integer count. Conversely all x in [11n/16,13n/16] permit every y in the retained range. This yields at least 1/8 of the normalized CRT product before harmless vanishing errors, so the stated 1/10 is independent of K,L, and of fixed small τ. Taking L so the previously audited uniform C/(L log L) is below the ray lower constant ≫(log L)^(-4k), THEN freezing the field and τ and letting N grow, leaves strictly positive weight. No simultaneous uniform-in-growing-field asymptotic is used or implied.
* Consequently the full finite-position polynomial prime + forbidden adjacent-norm pattern exists at paper level, including all cross positions.
