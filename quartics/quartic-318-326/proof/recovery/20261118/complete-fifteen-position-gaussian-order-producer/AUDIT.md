# The upper-tail half of the simultaneous Gaussian prime construction

Scope: the log-weighted **upper bound** for forbidden adjacent-norm divisors from the pinned manuscript a0950ac7, Proposition v5-obstruction-tail (not a pattern lower bound). Fix distinct cleared coefficients, k positions, and τ>0 small enough that u+c_jw² stays comparable to N for N/2≤u≤N, w≤τ√N. Write B=√N. For any fixed τ and L large enough (exceptional primes depend only on the fixed coefficients), the bad tuple count with weight ∏log p_j is

    ≪ τ N^{3/2}/(L log L) + O_τ(N^{3/2−1/800}).

The implicit main-term constant is independent of L and τ. This counts potential Gaussian generators for every sign pair; it suffices in particular for the cross-position, cross-sign problem.

1. **Medium distinct-position upper sieve.** Put κ equal to the two coefficients' difference. Prime norms give a primitive solution of the nonsingular isotropic homogeneous equation a²+b²−c²−d²=κw². Browning–Loughran Thm 4.1 really allows five variables (author PDF, p.17), arbitrary subsets of nonzero nonsingular residue vectors away from 2κ, uniform error O_ε,ψ(B^{5/2+ε}m^{5/2+ε}). Use smooth compact majorant with min paired radial norms comparable to 1, w length O(τ). Integrating first in one radial norm squared gives bounded Jacobian and O(τ) singular integral; derivative constants can depend on τ in the error. Primitive Möbius inversion is legitimate, including B/h<1 by bounded compact support. For all good p the primitive local factor is 1−p^{-4}: the nonzero quadric has p⁴−1 points, all smooth; vectors divisible by p give geometric ratio p^{-3} before primitive inversion. The two divisibility equations at ℓ constrain at most 4ℓ² quadruples with w (one in the inert case) out of ℓ⁴−1 primitive residues. These bounds follow independently by the split linear-factor description of two lines at each position with w² giving the single remaining equation; inert case forces both Gaussian differences zero. For the d-sieve impose some prime form zero at each p|d. Conditioning on w≠0 makes the k removed norm levels disjoint; counting the norm fibres gives g(p)=(k+2χ₄(p))/p+O_k(p⁻²), including the two distinguished levels. For w=0 precisely the common zero norm is removed. Chinese remaindering separates d and ℓ. Hence X_ℓ≪τB³/ℓ² and remainder ≪_τ (Bℓd)^{5/2+ε}. Mertens mod 4 gives ∑ g(p)=k loglog y+O(1). In the squarefree Euler product over p≤y, relative weights h(d)=∏g/(1−g), the expected log d≪log y, so the part with d<z retains at least half for y a small power of z. Omit any one good ℓ uniformly. Selberg upper weights give denominator ≫(log z)^k and error from at most z² pairs with lcm<z² bounded by B^{5/2+ε} ℓ^{5/2+ε} z^{7+O(ε)}. With z=B^.01 and ℓ≤B^.01 the sum over ℓ is O_τ(B^{2.7}); the main term saves (log B)^{-k} and ∑_{ℓ>L}ℓ^-2≪1/(L log L).

2. **Filling the short one-position box argument.** A common odd divisor of N(z−1),N(z+1) forces Re z≡0, (Im z)^2≡−1 mod ℓ, ≤2ℓ residue vectors including free w. For sieving the distinct-position prime forms relative to this one position and κ_j shifted to include 0, the number removed mod p is (p−1)(ν₀+(k−1)(p−χ))+ν₀, ν₀=p+(p−1)χ. Thus g(p)=(k+χ)/p+O_k(p⁻²) again has dimension k. For modulus m=ℓ[d,e]≤B^.03 and fixed smooth weight with support in the box, summing residue-vector box errors costs O_τ(m³(B/m+1)²)=O_τ(B²m) by unit-cube Riemann approximation. The sum over ℓ,d,e, d,e<z, including divisor-sized weights, is O_τ(B^{2.1}), far inside 2.7. The volume main coefficient is O(τ). No dependence on ℓ is hidden in the error constants.

3. **Large divisors on distinct positions.** Verified both coordinate systems x0=a+c, x1=a−c, x=b−d, z=b+d, x0x1=R=κw²−xz after harmless direction changes. For R=0 there are O_ε(B^{1+ε}) triples, O(B) further coordinates. At ℓ|x0,x1 with R≠0 count R=hℓ², and bound representations using integer factorizations of xz and x0x1, treating xz=0 separately for at most two w at fixed R; cost O_ε(B^{3+ε}/(L log L)). Otherwise fix t∈{x1,x0}, ℓ∤2t, t|R. After clearing denominators of G1 and G1−H1 the common factors to sieve are P=κw²+2t and (t²+x²)(t²+z²) for t=x1, or P'=κ(t+2)w²−2xz and K=(R+t²+2t)²+t²(x+z)² for t=x0. Direct expansion verifies. The latter P' is rank-three homogeneous irreducible and K has nonzero constant for t≠−2,0; at t=−2, neither x nor z divides K. No common prime content (second contains the coefficient 1 of x²z²); after lattice substitutions, primes dividing the determinant are among 2t and can be divided out as common content.

Let q be the product of good primes of t outside 2κ. Fixed q allows O_ε,κ(B^ε) possible t by the elementary Rankin bound on the exponents. The ternary rank at each p|q is 3. Browning–Heath-Brown Lemma 4.1 gives ≤9^{ω(q)}q lattices of determinant q² covering q|R (also nonprimitive triples); ≤O(9^{ω(q)}q³T^{-3}) have largest successive minimum>T. λ3≤q≲B and the determinant bound gives O(B³/q²) integer vectors per lattice, including for q=1. Total cost of large-minimum lattices ≪ B^{5+ε}T^{-3}. On the others a genuine integral lattice basis of sizes comparable to successive minima has inverse coordinates ≪B/λ_i (adjugate and λ₁λ₂λ₃≍det). Box volume ≪B³/q²; min side ≫ B/T; heights polynomial in B. Apply their Lemma 2.1 to the two coprime polynomial pullbacks, multiply by number of lattices, sum q. Cost ≪ B^{3+ε}/(Llog L)+B^{2+ε}T. Putting T=B^{3/4} yields error ≪ B^{11/4+ε}, plus first large-prime term. At a single position use the ordinary three-variable box with coprime polynomials Re z, (Im z)²+1. Taking L₀=B^.01 and exponent ε=.001 allows all logarithmic weights with room to spare and needs no τ in the power-saving error.

**Remaining logical condition:** a quantitative lower bound on the SAME τ√N window for cutoff-sieved Gaussian-ray prime patterns whose constant eventually beats C/(L log L). Qualitative TZ recurrence in positive prime density, or a growing list of actual eligible isolated grids, is not such an estimate. No whole simultaneous-prime theorem, order-CRT, or original PointPair follows from this upper bound alone. No Lean execution.
