# One blowup at the characteristic-five point; extension over Z[1/14]

**Result proposed, not Lean-compiled.** For the constant-one twist, the proper normalized two-chart model modified at the two previously identified centers above 3 and at ONE additional reduced F25 point above 5 has regular total space and is proper and flat over R=Z[1/14]. Only the characteristic-five step is new here. No assertion about 2 or 7, other twists, a minimal or semistable model, special-fibre smoothness, or any arithmetic height consequence.

## 1. Match the actual normalized order before blowing up

Let D=Z_(5). In the exact finite raw four-root order over D put u=r₁, v=r₂, w=r₃, b=r₀. Its tensor presentation consists of four monic root equations with independent variables over D[T]. Eliminating T gives the SAME ring
 O = D[u,v,w,b]/(v²−u²+76800, w²−u²−375, b²−u²−32).
Indeed T=u²+32. The original ring embeds into its original common function field L with that field as its fraction field; localizing coefficients preserves this. At a prime with at most one vanishing root eliminate T using that root (any one if none vanish). The other three root adjunctions, localized at their respective nonzero roots, are standard étale over the regular polynomial ring in the chosen root; 2 is invertible. Hence the local ring is regular. In characteristic zero contacts never coincide. In characteristic 5 the residues are 0,2,2,2. A collision necessarily has T=2, u=v=w=0 and b²=2. The sole underlying raw prime is
  m=(5,u,v,w),  O/m=F₅[b]/(b²−2)=F₂₅.
Here 2 is not a square in F₅, so m is maximal. The quotient of the whole parameter fibre need not be reduced; it has only the displayed prime after killing the nilpotent roots.

O is a Noetherian Cohen–Macaulay domain: its defining monic equations in fresh variables are successively nonzerodivisors (also after localization at primes containing them) in a regular ambient polynomial ring. Thus O satisfies S₂. The only possible nonregular prime m has height ≥2 by flat going-down for the finite free original map D[T]→O and the strict chain (0) ⊊ (5) ⊊ (5,T−2) ending below m. So O satisfies R₁. By Serre's criterion O is normal. The coefficient localization of the actual normalized finite chart is exactly O in L: localization commutes with integral closure and O is now integrally closed in that field. This is a coefficient-localized equality, not an unproved claim that the characteristic-five normalization of a fibre is its fibrewise normalization.

The scheme-theoretic fibre over 5 is unchanged when passing from R to D. Thus m corresponds to a reduced closed point P₅ of the global normalized finite chart and of the normalized two-chart model over R (a finite residue field over a closed base point). Its restricted defining ideal is exactly m. This point is disjoint from the centers above 3. Blow up P₅ with its reduced structure. By flat base change for blowups its pullback over D on the finite chart is Bl_m Spec O. The task now is to verify total-space regularity of this precise blowup, not of a related strict transform.

## 2. Two charts suffice and both are saturated

Write π=5, so v²−u²=−3072π² and w²−u²=15π². Denote the four degree-one elements of the Rees algebra by P=πt, U=ut, V=vt, W=wt. Its Proj is covered by D₊(P) and D₊(U): the homogeneous identities V²−U²=−3072P² and W²−U²=15P² imply that a homogeneous prime containing P,U must contain V,W too, hence the whole positive-degree ideal. This is not an assumption that a 5-direction is nonzero on the full exceptional curve.

* The π-chart is O[u/π,v/π,w/π]. With U=u/π,V=v/π,W=w/π it has the presentation
  D[U,V,W,b]/(V²−U²+3072, W²−U²−15, b²−25U²−32).
  The proposed ring is free of rank eight over D[U] by the three monic quadratics, hence π-torsion-free. After inverting π the substitution has the explicit inverse u=πU, v=πV, w=πW (and b fixed), giving O[1/π]. It therefore injects into O[1/π], and its image is precisely the stated ratio subalgebra since it contains O and all three named ratios. No saturation or hidden embedded component was dropped.
* The u-chart has P=π/u, V=v/u, W=w/u and base D[u,P]/(uP−5). Adjoin b,V,W with
  b²=u²+32, V²=1−3072P², W²=1+15P².
  The base injects after inverting u: uP−5 is irreducible in the UFD D[u,P], by primitivity as a linear polynomial over D[u] (u and 5 are relatively prime there) and Gauss's lemma. Thus its ideal is prime and u is nonzero in the quotient. All three adjunctions are monic, making u a nonzerodivisor on the result. Inverting u gives O[1/u] by the explicit inverse substitutions P=5/u,V=v/u,W=w/u. Consequently the displayed ring is exactly O[5/u,v/u,w/u], not the total transform obtained by cancelling u in a non-injective ring.

Both chart descriptions agree on the actual native Rees overlap: on D₊(P)∩D₊(U), the π-chart U is invertible and the u-chart P=1/U, V_u=V_π/U, W_u=W_π/U; u=5U and b is unchanged. Inverting P (respectively U) on just this overlap is legitimate, while neither π nor u is generally a unit in the whole chart.

## 3. Regularity at all exceptional directions

It suffices to consider points in the special fibre of these charts: outside m the blowup is an isomorphism and O was regular there. On the π-chart with 5=0 one has W²=U², b²=2.
* For U≠0 in the residue field, W and b are nonzero. Write U²=V²+3072 and adjoin U, W, b in this order to the regular base D[V], using their displayed monic equations and localizing at the respective roots. All three derivatives are units there. This covers V=0 as well; we do not assume V is invertible in this case.
* For U=0, W=0, V²=−3072=3 mod 5, and b²=2. Both V and b are nonzero. Use the base D[U,W]/(W²−U²−15) and adjoin V and b by the two monic unit-derivative equations. The base stalk at the contraction (5,U,W) is regular: in the regular local ring D[U,W]_(5,U,W), 5,U,W are a regular system of parameters, and W²−U²−15 has nonzero linear term −3·5 modulo the square of the maximal ideal. The quotient by that parameter is regular of dimension two. The étale adjunctions transport this regularity to each actual point, including all possibilities for the retained quadratic residues.
* On the u-chart we only need points outside D₊(P). Its ratio coordinate P is zero in the residue field, not globally zero. At a point above m we also have u=0. The three quadratic residues satisfy V²=W²=1 and b²=2, all nonzero. Adjoining those roots is standard étale around that point. The base stalk of D[u,P]/(uP−5) at u=P=0 is regular because uP−5 is −5 modulo (5,u,P)² in the ambient regular local ring. This handles the exceptional directions at infinity on the π-chart rather than identifying a ramified nodal fibre with a singular total space.

## 4. Actual global object and its remaining boundary

Start with the actual integral-closure two-chart projective-line model over R (proper by the already identified finite normalization and proper base). Take the two previously identified closed centers supported over 3, then the closed reduced P₅ at the unaffected characteristic-five fibre. Existence of the first two centers over R requires no extension of a nonproper open subscheme: the original F₉ points live in characteristic-three fibres of the model and first blowup, which are unchanged by R→Z[1/70] and are closed globally. The construction of the earlier points in the common closed fibres and the reduced structures are exactly as in the prior affine/projective contracts. The third center is supported away from them. All three blowups are proper and birational; over D(5) in Spec R they restrict to the earlier reviewed regular model over Z[1/70]. For any remaining point mapping to 5, localization of coefficients from R to D leaves its total-space stalk unchanged. The finite normalized chart and the near-infinity chart D(d(s)), d=∏ᵢ₌₁³(1−cᵢs), still cover: at s=0, d=1. The reciprocal calculation in PROJECTIVE-OPEN uses only 2 invertible, the same generic field, and d a unit on that chart; monic freeness, ratio reversal, and standard étaleness apply verbatim over R and D. At P₅ we have T=2, s=3 in F₅, c₁=2, hence d=0. So the near-infinity chart is unaffected by the new center; the old characteristic-three centers also miss it as proved previously. Regularity above 5 is now proved on the entire finite and reciprocal cover. These cases exhaust Spec R. The model is integral and dominates Spec R, so its stalks are torsion-free, hence flat over the PID R. The generic fibre is unchanged by the three vertical centers.
