# Three blowups above 7; a regular model over every odd prime

**Claim.** Over R=Z[1/2], take the actual normalized two-chart model of the constant-one twist. Retain its two established blowups above 3 and the one above 5. Three more blowups at reduced F49 points above 7 produce a proper, flat, regular total space, without changing the generic fibre. This is a mathematical source argument, not a compiled global Lean theorem, and does not extend to prime 2 or other twists. The number of blows stated is the length of this construction, not a minimality result.

## Input and first center

Work first over D=Z_(7), π=7. Reindex u=r₀, v=r₂, w=r₃ and b=r₁; the retained root is NOT the one retained at 5. Now T=u² and the raw order is
 O=D[u,v,w,b]/(v²−u²+32π⁴, w²−u²−π³, b²−u²+32).
Here 76832=32·7⁴ and 343=7³. This order is a domain in the same fraction field L, finite free over D[T]. As at five, it is Cohen–Macaulay by its original monic complete-intersection presentation. In characteristic zero there is no collision; in characteristic seven the contacts are 0,4,0,0. Away from a collision, eliminate T at any vanishing root and adjoin the others on the unit-derivative opens. This gives regularity away from the sole collision at T=0. There all three u,v,w vanish in every residue field; b²=−32=3 mod 7, nonsquare over F7. The unique raw prime is m=(π,u,v,w) with residue F49; it has height ≥2 by going-down for the finite free map from D[T], lifting (0)⊊(7)⊊(7,T). Thus R₁ and S₂ hold and O is normal by Serre. The authentic normalized coefficient-localized finite chart is O since it is the normalization in L. This does not normalize the special fibre instead. P₇ denotes the corresponding reduced closed point on the global model, unchanged by the earlier blowups supported over 3,5.

**Chart certificate used below.** For a domain B and ideal I=(s₀,…,sₙ), the standard Rees chart with generator t=sᵢ is the subalgebra B[I/t] inside B[1/t]. To identify a presented chart it suffices to check (i) all generators map to these exact ratios and generate the image; (ii) the presented ring after inverting t is B[1/t] by inverse substitutions; and (iii) the presented ring is t-torsion-free. For the later charts we prove (iii) as follows. In the polynomial ring over the DVR, impose first t=0, then the equation reducing to π, then the displayed nonzero dehomogenized quadratic in a polynomial ring over F7, then the monic equation in b. This is a regular sequence: successive elements are nonzerodivisors. Localize at any prime containing all these equations and t. All elements lie in that local maximal ideal, so permutability of regular sequences in Noetherian local rings puts t last. Thus t is a nonzerodivisor on the presented quotient at all such primes; away from t this is automatic. This proves global t-torsion-freeness. If the quotient by t is regular at a point over t=0, the total local ring there is regular: dimension rises by one and a minimal set of generators for the quotient maximal ideal plus t generates the maximal ideal. This argument establishes *total-space* regularity without identifying special fibres with regular schemes.

## I. Blow up m

The π and u charts cover: in the Rees algebra with homogeneous directions P,U,V,W, the identities V²−U²=−32π²P² and W²−U²=πP² show P,U in a homogeneous prime force V,W into it. In the π chart now write U=u/π, V=v/π, W=w/π. Its presentation is
  V²−U²=−32π²,   W²−U²=π,   b²=π²U²−32.
Monicity gives freeness over D[U] and no π-torsion, and inverting π reverses the substitutions. In the u chart, P=π/u, V'=v/u, W'=w/u give
  uP=π,  V'²=1−32π²P²,  W'²=1+πP²,  b²=u²−32.
The base D[u,P]/(uP−π) is a domain by primitivity of the linear polynomial in P; monic adjunctions give u-torsion-freeness, and inverting u reverses the substitutions. Thus both rings are the precise ordinary blowup charts. On the overlap P=1/U with the two other directions divided by U.

On the u chart outside the π chart, above m, u=P=0 in the residue field; V', W', b are units. Their quadratic adjunctions are étale locally, and the base local ring is regular since uP−π has nonzero linear π term. On the π chart above π=0 we have V²=W²=U² and b²=3. If U is nonzero, three derivatives 2V,2W,2b are units, so this is étale locally over regular D[U]. Otherwise U=V=W=0 and there is exactly one underlying closed F49 point Q. On this whole signed-root-retaining chart its reduced ideal is (U,V,W) since π=W²−U². Q does not lie on the other first chart: its π-chart ratio U=u/π is zero. The two chart and overlap descriptions show that no other directions are missing.

## II. Blow up the reduced point Q

Globally Q is closed since the fibre over P₇ is closed and of finite type over F49 and the residue field is finite. Take the reduced structure. Its ideal on the first π chart is (U,V,W); off Q the blowup is the identity. Within that chart the second U and W charts cover. Indeed the homogeneous generators are U'=Ut,V'=Vt,W'=Wt and πt=W·W'−U·U'. Thus U',W' in a prime force πt into it and V'²−U'²=−32(πt)² forces V' in it. No assertion that all directions have U nonzero.

* In the U chart set h=W/U, j=V/U, and keep U. Then
  π=U²(h²−1),    j²−1=−32U²(h²−1)²,    b²=π²U²−32.
  Modulo U the successive equations are π, j²−1, b²+32. Apply the regular-sequence chart certificate above; after U is inverted one recovers V=Uj and W=Uh, and the displayed ring is the full Rees ratio chart. The exceptional quotient is F49[h,j]/(j²−1), two affine lines since 2 is invertible. It is regular, so the total space is regular at every point in this chart over Q.
* In the W chart set a=U/W, c=V/W, retain W. Then
  π=W²(1−a²),    c²−a²=−32W²(1−a²)²,    b²=π²W²a²−32.
  The equations modulo W are π, c²−a², b²+32. The middle factor is nonzero in a polynomial domain, even though it factors. The same regular-sequence certificate and inverse substitutions U=Wa,V=Wc prove this is the exact chart without W-torsion. On the overlap one has a=1/h, c=j/h. Outside the U chart, at a point above Q, a=W=0, hence c=0 and b²=3. There is exactly one point Q' with residue F49; its reduced ideal is (W,a,c), since π is already in its square. Q' is disjoint from the second U chart and is globally closed by the same finite-residue and closed-fibre argument.

## III. Blow up Q' and check every chart of the tangent conic

Use all three actual standard charts for (W,a,c); their projective directions are (w₀,a₀,c₀). On the chart distinguished by variable z in (W,a,c) write W=z w₀, a=z a₀, c=z c₀, setting the corresponding one of the three direction coordinates equal to 1. After substitution the displayed ring over D, including the retained b, has equations
  π = z²w₀²(1−z²a₀²),
  c₀²−a₀²+32w₀²(1−z²a₀²)² = 0,
  b² = π² z⁴w₀²a₀² −32.
For EACH of the three choices, its proposed chart generators are the two ratios and z plus b; inverting just z recovers the preceding W chart with z inverted. For torsion-freeness, reducing modulo z gives the required regular sequence with π, the nonzero dehomogenization of c₀²−a₀²+32w₀², then b²+32 (monic). Thus these are precisely the three Rees charts. Where two distinguished directions are nonzero, reselecting the scale divides the old direction vector by its new distinguished coordinate and multiplies z by that coordinate; the normalized triples are related by these coordinate changes of actual fractions. This is the ordinary blowup gluing; it introduces no inverses of exceptional coordinates away from their overlaps.

The quotient by z is the corresponding affine chart of c₀²−a₀²+32w₀²=0 over F49. Its projective conic is smooth: the three derivatives 2c₀,−2a₀,64w₀ cannot vanish simultaneously at a nonzero direction in characteristic seven. In a chart where one direction is set to 1, Euler's homogeneous identity shows that vanishing of the other two derivatives on the conic would force the remaining one to vanish as well. This proves regularity on each entire dehomogenized chart, not just at F49-rational points. With z a nonzerodivisor, the total space is regular above Q'. All other points were already regular and are unchanged by blowing up Q'.

## Global join and limits

The characteristic-seven points of the ring calculations lie on the actual finite normalized chart. They give closed points of the appropriate global successive models by the identical special fibre after coefficient localization, finite residue field, and the already closed base centers; take reduced structures at every step. Flat base change of each blowup recovers the precise local scheme just computed, so regularity at any original total-space point over 7 follows: a coefficient localization inverting only integers not divisible by 7 does not change its local ring. No regularity is inferred merely from regularity of a special-fibre ring. The finite and restricted reciprocal charts of PROJECTIVE-OPEN.md still cover. The regularity proof on reciprocal D(d(s)) uses only invertibility of 2 after restriction, monic freeness and equality of fraction fields. The new P₇ lies over T=0 on the parameter line and so belongs to no reciprocal chart at finite s=1/T; neither subsequent point affects the reciprocal open. The three older vertical centers, and their reduced structures, restrict unchanged from the earlier construction by identification of their closed fibres (not by treating a local scheme as an open). Outside characteristic seven the three new blowups are identities and the reviewed proper flat regular model over Z[1/14] applies. The two cases exhaust Spec Z[1/2]. Properness of the normalized model and the successive proper blowups, integrality of the Rees charts in the same generic field, dominance over the PID Z[1/2], hence torsion-free flat local rings, give the claimed proper flat regular model with original generic fibre.
