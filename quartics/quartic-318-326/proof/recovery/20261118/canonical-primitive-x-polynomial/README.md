# Canonical polynomial of the genuine primitive x-coordinates
For each level N>1, choose the previously constructed single finite Galois extension of the arithmetic generic j-field containing all exact-order points. Take the product of X−a over the DISTINCT x-coordinates a of the actual markings. Invariance under every Galois automorphism gives coefficientwise descent. The resulting monic polynomial has no repeated root, degree equal to the number of distinct nodes, and its complex roots are exactly the true primitive x-values. This is a construction over Q(j), not over C(j). The companion invariant-field bound now requires only irreducibility of this constructed polynomial, not a presupposed equation, annihilation or numerical rank. The classical identification and irreducibility are separately supported by van Hoeij–Smith; they are not asserted to have been proved in Lean. N=1 uses the j-line itself, not the x-coordinate of the identity at infinity. Mathematical/static source review only.
