# One primitive equation at every transcendental analytic j
One polynomial over the fixed arithmetic generic j-field controls the true complex x-values at all actual periods with transcendental j. The coefficients of that same polynomial must be evaluated under the embedding belonging to each individual period. The finite simultaneous geometric marking field embeds anew over this target action; finite cardinality plus injectivity gives all geometric points and no arbitrary correspondence of individual labels is promised. The result does not infer analytic continuation from one numerical zero or prove irreducibility. Independent source review, not kernel acceptance.
