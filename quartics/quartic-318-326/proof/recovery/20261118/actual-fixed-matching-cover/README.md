## Source-level obstruction to inversion (not an obstruction to the new curve)

Let r=integerR(a) and A=114444r. Work on any a with a≡1 or 3 mod5 and 11 exactly dividing r. This set is a union of 80 classes modulo 605 by the integer formula in verify.py; for 1,3 the residues of r mod121 are 22,99. In all cases r≡1 mod5, A≡4 mod5. On the actual chart of the integral common index h≠0 use m=n−1≠0, H=Nm(U_h), c=Nm(U_m), N=Nm(1+U_h²/r), Q=c/(H r¹⁵), W=N²+rQ², b=A(W/(1+A))². The existing source Prepared explicitly requires v₁₁(W)=0, since 11|A. At 11, K=Q(√2) is inert/unramified. The two terms of W have opposite valuation parity. Hence v₁₁(N)=0. As v₁₁(r)=1, v_w(1+U_h²/r)=min(0,2v_w(U_h)−1), so v_w(U_h)≥1. The original hP thus reduces to the identity: x(hP)=1/U_h². By the projectively checked order 5 of P at 11 this forces 5|h.

At 5 the field is also inert/unramified and P has order 5 in good reduction. If 5∤m, mP has integral unit abscissa (the only possible nonidentity x=0 is of order 2, not in this odd cyclic subgroup), so v₅(c)=0. As h is formal, v₅(H)>0, N≡1 mod5, and v₅(Q)<0. Thus v₅(W)<0. If 5|m, both indices are formal. Unequal v₅(h),v₅(m) give nonzero v₅(Q)=2(v₅(m)−v₅(h)) by the formal log, and hence v₅(W)≤0. At equal valuations let t_j=−x(jP)/y(jP) be the formal local parameter. Then (U_j/t_j)²=1−δU_j⁴, using the original cubic y²=x³−δx. Thus U_j/t_j reduces to ±1, of norm 1 over F₂₅/F₅. The formal logarithm has leading coefficient 1 and matches the multiples of 5P; t_m/t_h≡m/h mod5 when both ratios are units. Consequently c/H≡(m/h)² mod5, Q is a square unit since r≡1, and W=N²+rQ²≡2 mod5. So v₅(W)≤0 for *every* permitted scalar label.

Since 5|1+A and A is a unit, b has negative even valuation. Its complement has the local class of −A/17 (factor out (W/(1+A))²), and B has that of 17A. Both are nonsquare units in classes at 5 (−A≡1, 17A≡3). The previous necessary/sufficient 5-adic test rules out Z²=θ(9−βt²)(1−9βt²). ## Every completion, at every prepared numerator and complement

The simple roots of q₁ are α=±1/(3√β) and q₂ has their φ-preimages. All four are distinct in characteristic zero: a common root would have φ(α)=α or −α. The former requires k(1−βα²)=0, impossible. The latter requires k(1+βα²)=−2α i.e. 25m₀=9, also impossible. These statements remain valid modulo p≥5 with p∤βk and det φ a unit (at p=5 the second equality is impossible). Treat roots projectively. Thus Hₖ is smooth of genus one. All four branch points are nonrational and lie in Q(√β).

* At 2,3 and all finite p|β, θ is a local square by preparation. At 2 the factors 1−m₀ and 1−9m₀ are 1 mod8; at 3 they are 1 mod3, and at every odd p|β they are 1 mod p. Hence t=0 gives a local rational point. The real place need not use t=0: β>0 so the branch points are real.
* At p|θ outside these places the prepared square denominator implies v_p(D)>0 and B=289(1−D) is a square locally, so β is square. In fact **any** place where β is square has a rational smooth branch point.
* At remaining p≥5, β,θ,k are units. If either m₀=1 or 25m₀=9 mod p, then β is square locally (the latter cannot occur at 5), so a branch point works. Otherwise the two separable quadratics remain disjoint and the binary quartic with unit prefactor has good reduction; Hasse's bound gives an Fp-point and smooth lifting gives a Qp-point. This accounts for all primes, not merely originally specified support. Smooth local neighborhoods let one avoid the finite set of branches, poles and boundary ordinates. The k=12 construction therefore repairs, rather than ignoring, the actual 5-adic obstruction above.

## Exact fixed full 2-Selmer class and global test

Let J=Jac(Hₖ). For D∞ the rational divisor class of a degree-two fiber of t, the map Hₖ→J given by P↦[2P−D∞] is a 2-covering (over Qbar choose one branch as origin). Its torsor of preimages of the identity comprises the four possible ramification points. Over K=Q(√β) these are all rational. Nontrivial conjugation exchanges the pair R± over ±1/(3√β), and exchanges the other pair too. The point T=[R₋−R₊] is nonzero rational 2-torsion; all geometric 2-torsion of J is rational, since the double transposition fixes all three 2+2 partitions. Consequently the explicit full class of this cover is the cocycle g↦0 on G_K and g↦T on its other coset. The preceding local proof puts this particular class in Sel₂(J/Q). Its image in Sha(J)[2] is zero precisely when Hₖ(Q) is nonempty. In particular global vanishing is sufficient for the exact literal PointPair at the same original b. Nonvanishing would obstruct this one correspondence, not other parameters or the general surface. J and its class are fixed for each b, as distinct from choosing and resolving a new E for every attempted second point. No finiteness assumption on Sha or conditional parity theorem is used.
