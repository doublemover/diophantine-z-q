# Compare the actual rational Heegner traces, not just their vanishing

Fix one original odd target m>1, positive squarefree, m≡11 mod12, with at least two prime factors; write b=−m, E=E₋₁. An admissible even partner a is a positive squarefree integer ≡1 mod12 prime to m, with ab≡1 mod24. The discriminant ab is negative fundamental and the level 48 splits there. Let W(a;b) denote the **unnormalized sum over all ideal classes**, with coefficient the genus character χ_a, of the actual earlier parametrized CM point of discriminant ab, viewed on E^(b)=E_m over Q. For a=1 this is the ordinary trace. The earlier reflection argument proves rationality under the twist, exactly: when a=1, the class number is even since m has at least two prime factors, killing the cusp translation. The choice of initial orientation/class may introduce a sign or a torsion translation and is harmless below. The two cases are (m≡23 mod24, a≡1 mod24) and (m≡11 mod24, a≡13 mod24).

Let C(a) be the signed coefficient of Yoshida's Φ_(3,−3), the ternary difference of the preceding packet for a>1. **C(1)=1, not 2:** the two theta series have coefficient difference 2 at 1, and the unary correction G₂ contributes 1. Indeed in Yoshida's notation θ_(ψ,c)=Σ_(j∈Z)ψ(j)j q^(cj²), and the only contribution to G₂ at q¹ is (1/2) θ_(χ_−3,1), whose coefficient is 1. Pick any admissible a₀ with C(a₀)≠0 (one may take 1 in the first case, any allowed prime ≡13 mod24 in the second).

Then for some sign, in the rational Mordell–Weil group modulo its torsion,

    C(a₀) W(a;b) = ± C(a) W(a₀;b).

This is an equality of rational group elements up to torsion, not merely a relation of the formal divisor classes or an assertion about the ratio when the target is already presumed nonzero.

**Proof with the field normalizations explicit.** For fixed φ, the [Gross–Zagier character height formula I.6.3–6.5](https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/zagier/files/doi/10.1007/BF01388809/fulltext.pdf) gives

    ĥ_abs(W(a;b)) = c_φ √|ab| L(E^(a),1) L′(E^(b),1),

with the same positive c_φ for all these discriminants. The original formula uses the unnormalized character sum and height over the full Hilbert class field: its factor 1/h cancels against ĥ_H=2h ĥ_abs by I.6.4. The root-of-unity parameter is 1 since |ab|>4. Passing from the fixed rational newform-isotypic elliptic factor of J₀(48) to E changes the canonical height by a fixed positive factor (fixed isogeny/polarization). The twist identifications are isomorphisms over Qbar preserving the standard symmetric height. The Rankin product is the genus factorization; the second factor has negative sign. There is no residual ratio of class numbers, covolumes or compositum degrees.

Now [Yoshida II, Theorem 3.1 and the Φ_(3,−3) specialization in §3](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/kyushujm/56/1/56_1_147/_pdf/-char/ja) gives

    C(a₀)² √a L(E^(a),1) = C(a)² √a₀ L(E^(a₀),1).

Both squarefree positive arguments are prime to 6 and have equal squareclasses at 2 and 3 by their common mod24 residue. The target here is the paper's positive family y²=x(x+a)(x−3a). No BSD converse is used. Combining formulas gives equal heights of C(a₀)W(a;b) and C(a)W(a₀;b). If the target derivative vanishes, both traces are torsion. Otherwise Gross–Zagier–Kolyvagin gives rank exactly one for E^(b)(Q), so equality of heights in that rational lattice implies equality up to sign. This also covers C(a)=0 since then W(a;b) is torsion.

**Implications for selecting the two original projections.** For any original m≡23 mod24 and positive admissible a, the true rational trace W(a;−m) is |C(a)| times the unshifted ordinary trace, modulo torsion and sign. In particular for θ=457, n=1751 in the preceding prepared example the nonzero θ-signed trace in the full CM packet is *16 times* the restricted ordinary trace modulo torsion/sign. At θ=409, n=2159, in the auxiliary genus-only K'=Q(√−73·883031), the two desired rational traces are *8 times* the ordinary trace at 883031 and *16 times* that at 2159. The earlier auxiliary genus bound guarantees one rational trace is NOT divisible by 8 modulo torsion in its own lattice. Both of these target traces ARE divisible by 8 and are known nontorsion. Thus that guaranteed low-divisibility component necessarily belongs to a different rational divisor twist, not merely possibly so.

More generally C(p)≡0 mod8 for every prime p≡1 mod24. The following elementary proof supplements the above formulas. All nonzero-coordinate solutions in either ternary count have orbit size 8 under independent signs. Among the zero-coordinate planes for Q₁=x²+3y²+144z² and Q₂=3x²+9y²+16z², no coordinate axis represents such a prime, so planes can be counted individually. Q₁ with x=0 is impossible mod3, z=0 contributes 4 (p=x²+3y²), and y=0 corresponds to p=x²+(12z)². Q₂ with z=0 is impossible mod3, x=0 corresponds to p=(3y)²+(4z)², and with y=0 to p=3x²+(4z)². The representation p=a²+3b² is unique in positive integers for p≡1 mod3; this follows from the class number one binary form of discriminant −12 (or the norm in Z[(1+√−3)/2], whose units permit an integral representative unique up to signs). Here p≡1 mod8 implies b is divisible by 4: if b odd then a is even and p≡3 or 7, impossible; if 2||b then a odd and p≡5. This last fact applies to the coefficient-3 coordinate b, but the square coordinate a is odd, so Q₂ with y=0 gives NO solutions: if the square coordinate 4z were even, p≡3 or 7 mod8. For p=A²+B² there is one unordered positive pair, one coordinate odd and the other divisible by 4 because p≡1 mod8. Exactly one coordinate is divisible by 3 since p≡1 mod3. If the even coordinate is divisible by 3, Q₁(y=0) contributes 4 and Q₂(x=0) contributes 0; if the odd coordinate is divisible by 3, the contributions are 0 and 4 respectively. Thus the net plane contribution is either 8 or 0 modulo 8, proving the assertion. In fact this same residue is consistent with C(73)=−8, C(409)=0, C(457)=−16.

Consequently *every* nontrivial prime seed k≡1 mod24 in the m≡23 construction costs at least three powers of 2 in the target rational lattice; it is not generally an integral primitive detector. In contrast, for m≡11 mod24 choose a₀ any admissible prime 13 mod24. Every such prime k has C(k)≡4 mod8 by Yoshida II, Thm 3.8. All nonzero W(k;−m) have **exactly the same 2-adic divisibility** as W(a₀;−m) in that rational lattice. This eliminates the introduced 2-adic loss, not the possibility the target trace is torsion. For the common θ409,k109,n323 example the relative multipliers in absolute value with a₀=13 are 1 for W(109;−132107) and 4 for W(109·409;−323), since C(13)=4, C(109)=−4 and C(44581)=16. No original indexed target rank is proved. The congruence claim here is about *prime* 1-mod24 seeds, not arbitrary composite kθ.
