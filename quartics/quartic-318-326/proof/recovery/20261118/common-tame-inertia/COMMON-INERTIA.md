# A single normal local coefficient field for a finite family of tame primitives

**Status:** mathematical argument for complete discretely valued fields with finite residue. The two Lean source files in this packet prove conditional algebraic pieces only, not the existence or inertia-isomorphism theorem below. No local Lean run.

Let F be a complete discretely valued field with finite residue field of characteristic p. Fix a finite Galois extension K/F, let D = Gal(K/F), and let I be its inertia group. Both rings of integers use the unique prolongation of the valuation. Suppose I is cyclic of order n prime to p. For finitely many labels j use the following precise output of the selected tame-root construction:

* A unit u_j in the integers R of K, fixed by I.
* A selected coefficient field M_j obtained by taking the fraction field of a domain T_j localized at the chosen prime of R[X]/(X^n-u_j). Its I-action extends the old one on K and fixes the image t_j of X.
* A cochain d_j : I → M_j^× and a cochain c_j : I × I → K^× satisfying
  (g·d_j(h)) d_j(gh)^(-1) d_j(g) = c_j(g,h) in M_j^×, through the given K-embedding.

The prior cyclic primitive construction provides just these bounded inputs for each cocycle under its stated hypotheses. The presentation implies **M_j=K(t_j)**: the image of the root algebra is generated by R and t_j, localization only inverts elements with nonzero image, and fractions add no new field generators. In particular this uses neither irreducibility of X^n-u_j nor injectivity of the unlocalized root algebra. The original action fixes t_j because the chosen root-algebra action fixes X and the localization and fraction actions extend it.

**Claim.** There is a single finite Galois N/F containing K and K-embeddings i_j:M_j→N for all j such that restriction gives an isomorphism I(N/F)→I. Each original primitive transports through i_j to the genuine common inertia, for the cocycle inflated using that same restriction.

**Construction.** In a fixed separable closure of F containing K let N be the field generated over K by all roots of X^n-τ(u_j), as τ varies over D and j over the given finite labels. The family is finite, separable since every radicand is nonzero and n is nonzero in K, and stable under every F-embedding of K. Thus every F-embedding of N into the closure preserves N (it permutes the root family and preserves K), so N/F is finite Galois. This reason does not require a product polynomial without repeated factors. Every selected M_j embeds over K: the minimal polynomial of its actual t_j divides X^n-u_j and splits in N. Fix such an embedding and retain this same embedding for all values of d_j. All the root images are units of O_N, since their n-th powers are units and valuations multiply by n.

**Identify inertia.** Let J=I(N/F), and let π:J→I be restriction. It maps onto I by [Stacks, Lemma 0BSX](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BSX), applied to the normal domain O_F, its integral closures in the two Galois fields N,K, and the prime of O_N above the prime of O_K. The complete local hypothesis gives the unique primes used here. For γ∈J and any conjugate radicand τ(u_j), its restriction πγ fixes τ(u_j), since I is normal in D and u_j is I-fixed. For every root x above that radicand, γx and x therefore have equal n-th powers, nonzero residue, and identical reductions. The divided-difference identity in the first Lean module gives γx=x: its cofactor reduces to n times the (n−1)-st power of that nonzero residue. Thus **every** γ in J fixes every added root. In particular ker π fixes K and every generator of N/K and is trivial. This proves the isomorphism; no splitting of D or lifting of D to the individual M_j was used. This argument also proves the relative inertia of N/K at the chosen prime is trivial.

**Transport and exact domain.** For γ∈J, the maps γ∘i_j and i_j∘(πγ) on M_j agree on K by restriction, and on t_j because the original chosen action and the new inertia both fix its image. Hence the maps agree on K(t_j). Set d'_j(γ)=i_j(d_j(πγ)). Its first coboundary at (γ,δ) is i_j(c_j(πγ,πδ)) by this equivariance, not by assuming the quotient acts on all of N. For an empty label family use N=K. For n=1 the same argument holds. The construction handles finitely many labels simultaneously and makes no compatibility assertion between different choices of the individual d_j.

**What is not proved.** No full decomposition-group equivariance of the selected primitives, no identification of mixed cochains for arbitrary lifts of Frobenius, no compatible family for a varying infinite tower, no wild case, and no global H³ or rank conclusion. The finite Galois field, actual inertia restriction, and application to the particular old action are argued in mathematics here, not claimed as Lean-verified declarations.
