# A single quadratic-field norm target equivalent to the aligned global marking at a fixed prepared pole

Here b is one of the four actual prepared poles from the reviewed complete prime-order producer, or any rational number satisfying the same preparation. B=17b=beta u0², D=1−b/17=theta z0², V=B²D, with positive squarefree beta,theta; 17|beta. Assume theta>1; theta=1 is separately easy (any nonzero-ordinate first point transports to the square-related partner). The curve is E_m:y²=x(x−m)(x+3m), m≠0. Order Kummer coordinates as (x,x−m,x+3m); the standard derivative convention is used at a root. The assertion below is exact for **equal full markings**. The source needs only a common first coordinate: globally soluble source pairs with genuinely unequal other marks are NOT claimed to be exhausted. The strong class searched in the previous actual Selmer packets has c=1.

Let K=Q(sqrt(theta)), conjugation sigma. Fix c∈Q× modulo squares. A single sufficient rational target is X=a+z sqrt(theta) with z≠0 and
  N(X)=beta u²,  N(X−1)=c v²,  N(X+3)=beta c w²,   uvw≠0.     (N)
For an exact equivalence with both ordinates nonzero, retain the open test that the two explicitly recovered traces below avoid rational 2-torsion. Then (N) is equivalent to existence of two rational points of nonzero ordinate on E_n and E_(n theta), for SOME common rational n≠0, both with full marking (beta,c,beta c). If a target n is prescribed, also require that the reconstructed class be [n] or [n theta], interchanging the two curves in the second case. All assertions concern rational arithmetic, not reduction of a finite Selmer word.

**Forward and explicit recovery.** Write F(X)=X(X−1)(X+3)=A+H sqrt(theta). Its norm is a nonzero square s² by (N). Choose either sign for s for which A+s≠0: at least one works. Then (F(X)+s)²=2(A+s)F(X). Thus n=2(A+s) and gamma=(F(X)+s)/n satisfy F(X)=n gamma², without factoring n or theta. If H≠0 the product of the two candidate n is 4 theta H², so the choices exchange twist classes. If H=0 the second class is still furnished by multiplying n by theta and dividing gamma by sqrt(theta). All other choices of rational n for the same X differ from these by squares since ker(Q×/Q×²→K×/K×²)={1,theta}.

The point P=(nX,n²gamma) lies on E_n(K) with nonrational x (z≠0). Let S=P+sigma P and U=P−sigma P. Neither is O, as the two abscissae differ. The chords through P and sigma P and through P and −sigma P have rational and purely sqrt(theta) coefficients respectively: conjugation preserves the first and carries the second to its negative line in the y coordinate. Their third intersections have the abscissae of S and U. For any e in {0,n,−3n}, substitute x=e into the cubic minus the square of the respective line to get
  (x(S)−e) N(x(P)−e)=ell_plus(e)²,
  (x(U)−e) N(x(P)−e)=ell_minus(e)².
These are rational even before squareclass projection. The anti-invariant point U gives the rational T=(theta x(U), theta sqrt(theta) y(U)) on E_(n theta). Its e'=theta e differences have the same squareclasses as those of S, **not an extra theta**, since ell_minus(e)∈sqrt(theta)Q. Also N(x(P)−e)=n² N(X−e/n). Thus the common full marking is exactly (beta,c,beta c) away from the roots.

If one merely wants a literal point pair (zero ordinates allowed), the 2-torsion test may be dropped. Both recovered abscissae are still nonzero and of class beta. Indeed a zero intercept at 0 would force the other two intersections with y=kx to have product −3n² (divide the cubic by x), contradicting N(x(P))=n² beta u²>0. Thus the two x=0 classes never occur. Other possible rational points of order two have actual nonzero x, so the first abscissa mark continues to hold there; this literal-pair corollary does not require interpreting the vanishing factors by the chord expression. Under the nonzero-ordinate test there are no exceptional factors at all. One may skip that test when neither [n] nor [−3n] nor [n theta] nor [−3n theta] equals [beta]; those are the only possible root abscissae besides zero.

**Converse with the correct Galois obstruction.** Suppose S,T rational of nonzero ordinates on E_n,E_(n theta), sharing the FULL marking (beta,c,beta c). Denote the canonical twist isomorphism over K mapping T to (x(T)/theta,y(T)/(theta sqrt(theta))) by psi. Use the same labels 0,1,−3 for corresponding 2-torsion roots. Choose halves H1,H2 in their respective geometric groups. The Galois cocycles sigma' H_i−H_i coincide under identification of the two rational 2-torsion groups. Indeed the split Kummer map gives the exact squareclasses of the three differences, relative to the corresponding three rational roots (or equivalently the three evaluations of the Weil pairing), and is functorial with these labels; there is no theta multiplier between the two Kummer target coordinate conventions over Q. Equality of cocycle classes is exact equality of the cocycles because E[2] has trivial Galois action, so every coboundary vanishes. Consequently P=H1+psi H2 is fixed by Gal(Qbar/K). For any element restricting to sigma in Gal(K/Q), the twist transport changes sign and the two common 2-torsion corrections cancel (2T_2=0); hence sigma P=H1−psi H2. Its two traces are exactly S and psi T, not just these up to translation by rational 2-torsion. If x(P) were rational, sigma P=±P and one trace would be O; if P were a root or O, both traces could not have nonzero ordinates. Thus x(P)/n gives X∈K\Q and F(X)≠0. The rational and imaginary chord identities above, with nonzero trace factors, recover all three norm equations and the required open test. Replacement of n by a square preserves Kummer coordinates; replacement by n theta just interchanges the same two twists.

**Concrete rational search form.** For the selected full marking c=1, eliminate a from (N). With u,v,w,z nonzero this gives exactly
  beta(w²−4u²)+3v²=12,
  4 theta z²=(beta u²+1−v²)²−4 beta u²,
  2a=beta u²+1−v².
The first equation is a split rational quadric, factored as beta(w−2u)(w+2u)=3(2−v)(2+v); e.g. v=2,w=2u is one ruling. On the chart t=(w−2u)/(2−v)≠0, once v is chosen, 4u=3(2+v)/(beta t)−t(2−v) and 2w=3(2+v)/(beta t)+t(2−v). Deleted lines remain available directly from the original equations. There is still one genuine residual square condition; a nonempty quadric, local feasibility, or positive rank on an unrelated twist does NOT solve it.

On an easier sufficient slice take X=lambda(1+z0 sqrt(theta)), with R=1−theta z0²=b/17=beta(u0/17)². This guarantees N(X) in beta's class for lambda≠0. The single quartic
