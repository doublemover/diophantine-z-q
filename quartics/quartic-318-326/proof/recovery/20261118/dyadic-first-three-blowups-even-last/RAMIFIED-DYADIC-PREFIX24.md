# Three actual origin iterations for 24 mixed dyadic types with even last coefficient

Take the 12 representatives q=(d0,d1,d2,2a) and the 12 representatives q=(2d0,2d1,2d2,2a), d_i,a∈{±1}, d0,d1,d2 mixed. In the second case first make the genuine dyadic parameter automorphism t=375T/(T+343), y_i=r_i/r_3, y_3=1/r_3. The exact generic coordinate and field inverse and integral closure identification on the projective parameter line are those of RAMIFIED-DYADIC-2222; the proof of that identification does not assume common signs. The first three transformed coefficients are odd units times squares, with signed unit classes given by a common sign multiple of the original d_i since 343+b_i all coincide mod 4. The fourth still has odd valuation. After *unramified* scalar étale comparison the resulting data thus have the form in the first case but with changed branch contacts. The transform interchanges the old last branch and infinity; mod 2 it preserves the old origin. Write t for either working parameter, e=(0,32,76832,−343) or (0,32,1120/3,375). The raw working roots satisfy y_i²=d_i(t−e_i) (0≤i≤2), y₃²=2a(t−e₃). All statements below are local about the one original geometric origin on the true starting normalization. Other singular points have been fully resolved separately in MATH (initial mixed points), and are not included in our counts of successors.

Let j<k denote the majority pair, m the minority, and d the majority sign after unramified scalar comparison. Put
    u=y_j, h=(y_k−u)/2, w=y₃, f=f₀+ad u², f₀=a(e_j−e₃),
    H=d(e_j−e_k)/4=2^h₀ H₀, M=d(e_m−e_j)/2=2⁴M₀ .
Here h₀=3 for pair 01 or 02 and 8 for 12, with H₀,M₀ odd dyadic units in both contact configurations. On the open f≠0 (unit) around the origin set l=(y_m−u)/w. The *actual normalization* has equations
    w²=2f,    h²+uh=H,    f l²+w u l+u²=M .
Indeed these identities follow by subtracting the raw squares and 2=w²/f. The algebra is finite free of rank eight over the u-line localized at f (three successive unit-leading monics); its full generic field has that degree by the simple branch valuations, or by starting in the full degree-16 field over the t-line and using t=e_j+d u². Hence it embeds in the field, recovers all raw roots and is integral over the localized order. It is a complete intersection, and in characteristic zero reduces to the original smooth covering since 2,w are units. At the generic dyadic u, w is a uniformizer of the (u,w) base, h is étale, and f reduces to (1+u)²; shift l=u/(1+u)+v. Its defining equation, at v=w=0, has constant part modulo w² equal to w u²/(1+u): indeed f·[u/(1+u)]²+u²−M has an even numerator. Thus the equation has a nonzero linear w term and gives a regular local ring over each generic vertical h-label despite the repeated square in the residue equation. Every height-one prime over 2 is such a generic prime by finite flatness over the parameter line. Complete intersection S₂ and R₁ prove the claim. The only point over u=0 has maximal ideal (w,u,h,l); w²=2f accounts for 2. The base surface in u,w is regular along w=0 (w a nonzerodivisor, quotient the open F₂[u]); all later base blowup charts below are regular there.

## Three native main directions and their side conics

For n=1,2,3 blow up the remaining main reduced closed origin, with old ratios S_old=u/w^(n−1), A_old=h/w^(n−1), B_old=l/w^(n−1) and put V_old=B_old−S_old; initially these are u,h,l,l−u. At the old origin for these three steps A_old=V_old=0 (for n=3 this refers to the n=2 model). The maximal ideal is (w,S_old,A_old,V_old). Its w,S_old directions cover the exceptional fibre: the two quadratic leading generators for A_old,V_old force both to vanish at any homogeneous prime with the first two directions zero. For H,M divided by w^(2n−2) use 2=w²/f and the valuations above: at the pre-centers for n≤3 these powers are at least two, with any exact-degree-two term containing w. At n=1 the quadratic V equation can have S_old² terms but these vanish on the proposed missing directions. Outside the exceptional fibre the blowup is unchanged.

On the w chart set S=S_old/w,A=A_old/w,B=B_old/w,V=B−S. Thus u=wⁿS etc. The exact equations are
    w²=2f,  f=f₀+ad w^(2n)S²,
    A²+SA=H₀ w^(2h₀−2n)/f^h₀,
    f V²+(2f+w)S V+(f+w+1)S²=M₀ w^(8−2n)/f⁴ .
On w=0 one has V=0 and the A extension is étale for S≠0. There the term (f+w+1)S² has nonzero first-order coefficient S² in w: f₀+1 is divisible by 2=w²/f and all other terms except w are divisible by w². Hence the total space is regular there at all geometric points. At S=0 the sole geometric residue is V=0 and A=0 except at n=3,h₀=3, where A=1. For n<3 or for n=3,h₀=8 both equations have zero linear parts, hence the main origin has embedding dimension four. For n=3,h₀=3, putting p=A−1 gives S as the only nonzero linear part of the first equation (odd rational constants equal 1 modulo w², f−f₀ divisible by w²); the V equation still has zero linear part. Hence the unique main successor has embedding dimension three in that case. It is **not yet resolved** at either embedding dimension.

On the S_old side set s=S_old, w=sR, A_old=s A', V_old=s V'. On this base u=(sR)^(n−1)s. The genuine two monics are
    A'²+A'=H₀ s^(2h₀−2n)R^(2h₀−2n+2)/f^h₀,
    f V'²+(2f+w)V'+f+w+1=M₀ s^(8−2n)R^(10−2n)/f⁴ .
Only R=0 needs a separate check, where A' takes two étale labels and V'=0. In the regular ambient with parameters s,R,V' the tangent quadratic of the last relation is
    V'²+sR+δ_n s²,    δ_1=1, δ_2=δ_3=0.
Indeed 2f=w² and f₀+1 is a multiple of 2, here of order ≥4; u² contributes s² only for n=1. The right hand side has order ≥6 and the V'-linear coefficient lies in (s,R)². This conic is projectively smooth: its s,R derivatives force R=s=0 and then the form forces V'=0. Each of these two geometric corner points is an ordinary double point resolved by one ordinary reduced-point blowup; the s,R charts cover the conic and the divided equations are smooth on the divisor, including any extension of residue fields. This is a distinct closed set from the main origin: the two projective directions w≠0,S_old/w=0 and S_old≠0,w/S_old=0 are different.

For saturation at each main blowup the base charts are precisely those of the regular (w,S_old) base blown up at its regular parameters. The described rings adjoin separate unit-leading monics to those base charts (on the side use V_old/s rather than B_old/s; they differ by 1). They are free there, the chosen scale is a nonzerodivisor, and inverting it gives the old exact algebra localized at that scale. Thus they are the literal ordinary ratio charts inside the common field. They are complete intersections, with any exceptional singularity isolated over the closed base point in dimension two, and unchanged off the old point; hence normal by R₁/S₂. At each side conic the ordinary ambient blowup division by the scale squared is saturated because the residual conic equation is nonzero on each scale chart (nonzerodivisor on the ambient exceptional polynomial ring); its smoothness gives total-space regularity. These side blowups can be performed before the next main step or on disjoint centers with it. In particular the iteration is not merely a base blowup subsequently misidentified with the reduced-origin blowup.

After the three main reduced-point blowups and resolution of their six geometric side nodes there is exactly **one** nonregular geometric point left over this origin per representative, of edim 3 for pairs involving root 0 (16 types), or 4 otherwise (eight types). The two side points per step are geometric statements; rational splitting need not persist for arbitrary twists. Centers descend: the two side points form the equal-embedding-dimension-three side locus identified by the two chart directions, and the unique main point is separately preserved. More explicitly scalar deck signs preserve the line of w and the line of S_old (both scaled only by signs, at each stage) in the Rees directions. Étale base change preserves each reduced union and the ordinary blowups; alternatively one may blow up all current nonregular points simultaneously at each stage, finishing only the final side union using its indicated stable directions. For arbitrary independently unramified coefficient factors, the same conclusions follow by finite étale comparison, not a quotient through a wild ramified extension. All modifications are proper birational, flat over the arithmetic base by integrality with unchanged generic field. This is an evaluated partial resolution of the mixed origins, not a terminal explicit resolution, no semistability/minimality or Lean claim.
