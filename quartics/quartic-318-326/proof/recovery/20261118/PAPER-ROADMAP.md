# What remains after the prime/order and height arguments

Snapshot: [`cb9e6faf`](https://github.com/doublemover/diophantine-z-q/commit/cb9e6fafbec6178822af4434b1257efd92a5944f). **The final equivalence is still open.** The two directions now have different status.

**A zero has integer input: a classical argument is assembled.** The [five-contact theorem](five-contact-49-over-8-classical-theorem/FIVE-CONTACT-49-OVER-8.md) supplies the exact universal height bound, with exponent 49/8, for coprime integer coordinates away from the five contacts. Its [review](five-contact-49-over-8-classical-theorem/REPORT.json) checks the assembly using specified earlier geometric constructions and cited classical results, not a new derivation of every input. Together with the [existing conditional soundness endpoints](../../progress/LiteralSoundness318326.lean), this gives the classical soundness argument for both published quartics. Lean still assumes [`FiveContact49Height`](../../progress/ContactHeight318.lean); it has not verified the complete geometric argument.

**An integer has a rational witness: the prime and common-index step is no longer the main gap on paper.** The [fifteen-position construction and CRT join](complete-fifteen-position-gaussian-order-producer/ORIGINAL-318-JOIN.md) produces simultaneous primes, compatible orders and common indices under its stated preparation hypotheses. The [independent review](complete-fifteen-position-gaussian-order-producer/ORIGINAL-318-GAUSSIAN-ORDER-JOIN-SECOND-REVIEW.json) concerns an existence proof, not a printed numerical grid or a Lean theorem. Those congruences do not supply the missing rational ordinates.

**Next mathematical target:** for each integer input, produce one admissible common choice for which the scalar and three detector pole conditions have the required **rational** points with their specified markings. At the [explicit indexed example](indexed-free-first-twist-rank-one/README.md), the first marked point and exact first rank do not give the partner's global lift. The [generic index-two obstruction](generic-pointpair-index-two/README.md) is about the function field, not a disproof for every specialization. A successful construction must preserve the shared labels and index, meet all remaining source guards and fill the original 288-equation witness. The [earlier guide](FRONTIER.md) separates the verified geometric components from these arithmetic requirements.
