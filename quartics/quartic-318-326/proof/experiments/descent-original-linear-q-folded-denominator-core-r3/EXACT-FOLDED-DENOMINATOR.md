    pole = (q · (∏ the five contact primes n_i)) · (τ/(A^5 q^3 Δ^3))^2.

At the new order-only q, the verified local fold gives v_q τ=0, v_q Δ=−1, hence the explicit square factor has valuation 0 and the pole has valuation +1. The new module assumes only nonnegative valuation of that same square factor at the five actual contact primes (the large deterministic branch aims to prove equality 0). The only integral support is these six factors. At every other prime the full pole valuation is even; at these support primes nonnegative valuation removes all denominator valuation.
