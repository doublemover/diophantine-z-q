# The old additive short-exact row needs no directional row

Let `J : IsPullback i a p (Spec.map x)` be the actual old reduced
fibre of the original smooth family `p:P→Spec T` at the selected
`k`-algebra section `x:T→k`. Here `T` is over the original residue
field `k`; it is the actual chart after making that relative field
base change, not a mixed-characteristic lift of `k`. Keep the actual
structural map `a:A→Spec k`. The terminal's output is the short
exactness of the actual additive sheaf row with terms

```text
i* p* Ω(Spec T/k) → i* Ω(P/k) → Ω(A/k).
```

The arrows are exactly `i*(dp)` and `di`, not independently
replaced by sheaf isomorphisms. Their composite is zero by `J.w` and
the one-owner old marked-zero proof. No directional complex supplies
that equality.

For each actual affine open of the old total scheme, its inverse
image on the actual closed fibre is affine. The old fibre square
produces the actual ordered ring pushout `B = C ⊗[T] k`, with the
literal section maps from the two schemes. The affine scalar lemma
uses the same accepted structure-sheaf formula and is generic in
the base ring: it is now declared in the old square owner rather
than imported through the directional fibre-kernel owner. The two
existing old affine differential arrows use literal `Spec` and
`Spec.algebraMap` instead of importing two abbreviations from the
directional sheaf-arrow owner. They compare both real arrows to
the actual tensor extensions on the same normalized affine, with
no free fibre or vertical independent frame.

Smoothness of the literal affine map supplies the split
transitivity row before reduction: the stock Jacobi–Zariski exact
sequence and projective formally-smooth relative Kähler module
supply a right inverse. Its scalar extension remains split even
when the affine closed immersion itself is not flat. The additivity
of tilde transfers this splitting to the true affine restriction.
The preimages of the affine basis of P cover the original A, so the
checked actual additive-stalk criterion gives global short
exactness. This proof uses no directional extension, neither
directional exactness nor the existence of a dual-number tangent.

The preceding endpoint returned its answer on the original row
expressed in directional compositional coordinates. The new
terminal instead returns the direct old row. To use the **existing
directional boundary** theorem as-is, its first remaining
directional datum is the actual right-compatible transport of the
