# Construct the primitive on the literal upper half-plane

disks centered at (n+1)i with radius n+1 exhaust the genuine upper
half-plane and each contains i; the proof is the exact squared norm
and the Archimedean bound. Mathlib's primitive-on-a-disk theorem
makes each local primitive; derivative uniqueness on the actual
nested disks glues the primitive normalized at i, with its actual
derivative. No global primitive is supplied.
