1. For a map f:X→Y and an open immersion j:U→Y, genuine lifts of every native Spec κ(x) map imply range f is contained in range j. Evaluate the exact morphism square at the native closed point, then use the open-immersion lift. Empty sources are included.
2. For a rational point of the original arbitrary-line parameter, install its precise coefficient action. The old geometric zero-specialization equivalence and the accepted nonzero field producer give a lift with its original projection equation. No separate nonzero premise is introduced.
3. At each point y of the actual nowhere-zero open N, take K=κ(y) and j:Spec K→N. Obtain the rational parameter map from Spec.preimage of j followed by the original inclusion. The old all-scheme empty-family criterion proves the needed zero-scheme emptiness.
4. Assemble the whole N→source map by native open lifting; cancellation of the original source open immersion proves uniqueness and equality with every earlier residue lift. Cancellation of open immersions proves this is itself an open immersion.
5. The old whole-test empty-family criterion and the same mono cancellation give both inverse maps for the actual subfunctor inside the kernel source, with literal precomposition naturality.
6. Restrict the existing smooth normalization along this open immersion. The original coefficient square and residue equations remain exact. Nothing here identifies the image open or proves saturation under normalization fibers.

The field hypotheses occur only in the field producer. The global residue construction does not claim a nonzero element over a general ring is a unit. The native residue theorem is used for points of N itself, not only rational points over the original coefficient field.
