Unlike the prior inequality, the actual canonical hyperbolic quotient measure
restricted to an injective chart image is exactly the pushforward of the
upper half-plane's own hyperbolic measure restricted to that chart. Choose the
already-green adapted fundamental domain containing the given chart. Its excess
over the chart among representatives of these orbits lies in the countable
union of intersections with nonidentity translates. Each such overlap is
null by the actual fundamental-domain property, so the restrictions are equal.
No independent chart volume is assumed and no chosen arithmetic point is used.
