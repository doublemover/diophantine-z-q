# Lift the actual positive [1/n] point through the named embedding

Take one designated algebraic-closure embedding extending the exact
chosen complex Gaussian embedding. The strict original uniformization
supplies the actual [1/n] point, and strict Algebraicity33 shows its
two coordinates are integral over the base. Splitting their minimal
polynomials inside that embedding's range puts the same point over
the designated algebraic closure. The old coordinate-adjoin contract
is explicit: the output point lies over precisely that adjoin, not a
new number-field model. No finite torsion module or Galois premise.

One unchecked receiver and fifty-four exact accepted developer sources;
the four common exact owner bytes are unchanged. No claim about actual
primitive-point construction, the original legacy aliases, or arithmetic.
