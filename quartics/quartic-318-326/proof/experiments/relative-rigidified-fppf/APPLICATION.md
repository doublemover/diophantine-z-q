# Original rigidified fppf representatives and neutrality

Four new sources extend the previously frozen fppf and actual-section
packets. Fifteen byte-identical producer sources close the imports against
the pinned 1,837-source baseline. Copies are not additional results.

1. **Original maps.** `kernelMap` is the original line-class pullback restricted
   to the actual section-restriction kernels. It is not a map defined by
   conjugation with the desired quotient/kernel equivalence.
2. **Pointed squares.** The hypotheses are the two actual scheme identities
   `a ≫ p = p' ≫ b` and `e' ≫ a = b ≫ e`. Original pullback composition proves
   restriction naturality. No Cartesian, flat, reduced or field-only
   restriction is added to these statements.
3. **Normalization naturality.** The original formula
   `c * (p*(e*c))⁻¹` commutes with that pullback. The exact quotient/kernel
   equivalence is then proved natural for the original maps. This statement
   is about line classes; it does not choose natural trivializations of the
   original line objects.
4. **Actual rigidified local representatives.** `rigidifiedImage` passes an
   original rigidified line through the original relative quotient and actual
   sheafification unit. `local_rigidified_iff` identifies the canonical image
   sieve with arrows admitting those exact objects, and
   `local_rigidified_cover` proves that same sieve is fppf covering.
5. **Naive neutrality.** Triviality in the original relative quotient is
   equivalent to existence of an original base line and an actual original
   module-sheaf isomorphism from its pullback. Both directions use the actual
   base-line range and the faithful original class/isomorphism relation.
6. **Full neutral sieve.** `neutralSieve_iff` identifies every arrow of the
   actual naive equalizer sieve with the actual base-line isomorphism after
   the full original fiber-product map. No coverage index or nonreduced
   fiber is discarded.
7. **Both directions after sheafification.** Local injectivity of the actual
   unit proves that a neutral sheaf class gives an fppf covering neutral
   sieve. Conversely, the actual associated sheaf's separatedness and the
   exact unit naturality show that such a covering sieve forces neutrality.
   `neutralSieve_cover_iff` is therefore an equivalence, not only a sufficient
   local condition.
8. **Actual source consumer.** `ContactFppfRigidifiedLocal318.sourceSheaf` is
   the associated fppf sheaf of the actual contact family after the same
   explicit finite etale field cover. Its local representatives use the
   same original point section and every further scheme test. This does not
   silently identify it with restriction of the rational-base sheaf; that
   site-base-change comparison is a separate construction.
9. **Boundary.** No effective descent theorem for chosen line objects or
   compatible rigidified morphisms is proved here. Trivialization choices are
   not asserted to be natural or unique. The section kernel is not degree
   zero. Degree, representability, the Jacobian, polarization, norm/cotangent
   comparison and isogeny remain open beyond the stated results.
