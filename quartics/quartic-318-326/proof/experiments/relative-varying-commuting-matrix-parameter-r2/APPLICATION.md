Constructed: polynomial is MvPolynomial((sigma×iota×iota)⊕iota,R); ideal is generated by ALL commutator entries.

Justification and boundary: The quotient keeps nilpotents and arbitrary test coefficients, and no radical is taken.

3. **index_coverage.** Required: Commutation is required for every ordered pair of generators and every ordered pair of matrix indices.

Constructed: The relation index is sigma×sigma×iota×iota and ideal uses its full range.

Justification and boundary: The forward proof and inverse ideal-kernel proof are pointwise on this entire set, with no omitted diagonal/off-diagonal entries.

4. **transition_maps.** Required: Coefficient change must match the same represented point.

Constructed: mapData applies the test algebra homomorphism to every entry and vector coordinate.

Justification and boundary: forward_natural proves agreement with composition of actual coordinate algebra maps. This is coefficient naturality, not a chart-gluing transition theorem.

5. **hypothesis_checks.** Required: Quotient.lift requires all ideal elements killed, not only an informal relation list.

Constructed: evaluate_relation kills every generator, and Ideal.span_le supplies inclusion of the full ideal in the actual kernel.

Justification and boundary: The lift is the pinned Ideal.Quotient.liftₐ; the inverse proof uses quotient and polynomial algebra-hom extensionality.

Constructed: VaryingQuotientMatrixCoordinates318 uses the preceding CyclicQuotientMatrices318 matrix/vector definitions on the specified original source elements.

7. **normalizations.** Required: No frame quotient, determinant inversion or unproved rank condition may be silently imposed.

Constructed: This ring parametrizes framed commuting matrices with a marked vector, with no cyclicity restriction.

Justification and boundary: Source equations, cyclic determinant opens and changes of frame remain explicit future steps.

8. **downstream_boundary.** Required: Original varying quotient representability needs source relations, cyclic completeness and actual scheme gluing.

Constructed: Only the commuting-matrix/vector algebra parameter and action-to-coordinate map are constructed here.

Justification and boundary: Do not claim a Hilbert/Picard/Jacobian, source-quotient classifier, determinant cover or universal finite-flat family over this whole unconstrained parameter.

9. **evidence_acceptance.** Required: Exact source bindings and independent source/hosted kernel checks are separate.

Justification and boundary: Static validation passes only structure; hosted elaboration/axiom audit and independent acceptance remain pending.
