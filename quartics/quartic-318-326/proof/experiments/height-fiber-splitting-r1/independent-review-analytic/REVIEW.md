   product polynomial splits into (X-r_i)(X+r_i) by its literal root equation.

   IsSplittingField predicate. Normality follows for this same field. Separability
   comes from characteristic zero, not an unsupported assertion that the product
   polynomial has distinct roots. This also covers branch and dependent-squareclass
   specializations.
3. The sign group is literally Fin 4 -> Bool with xor, cardinality sixteen.
   Its precomposition permutations satisfy the actual group multiplication law.
   Field automorphisms act by postcomposition. Their commutation on the same
   set of actual algebra homomorphisms is definitional. Faithfulness follows by
   evaluating at the surjective quotient map, not by postulating a representation.

   surjective. The exact FiberCentralizer318.faithful_torsor_card_dvd hypotheses

5. The scheme consumer is the original contact model, with structure map exactly
   modelMap followed by the original projective-line structure map. The point
