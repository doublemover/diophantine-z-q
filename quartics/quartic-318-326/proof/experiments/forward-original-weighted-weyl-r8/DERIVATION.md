# Mixed rather than separable weight control

## Exact two-axis Abel identity
rectangularWeighted(f,k,g,N,M) is the literal double sum g(i,j)f(i)k(j), with arbitrary g. Abel in the second axis gives a terminal row and every second-axis difference. Finite distributivity and exact sum commutation identify each with an original first-axis weightedPrefix. The finite mixed variation is variation(g(-,M),N)+sum_j<M variation(g(-,j)-g(-,j+1),N). Apply the original one-axis Abel bound to ALL those rows/differences and the second-axis prefix bound to ALL their terminal/prefix factors. Norm/triangle inequalities give product-of-prefix-bounds times this exact mixed variation. No rank-one/separability assertion on g occurs.

## All boundary and interior terms remain
The mixed-variation budget includes g(N,M), N first differences on the top boundary, M differences on the right boundary and every N*M interior mixed difference. Bounds A,D0,D1,D01 give A+N D0+M D1+NM D01. If N,M<=RB, first steps are bounded by D0/B,D1/B and genuine mixed steps by D01/B², the result is A+R D0+R D1+R² D01, for B>0 and nonnegative increment constants. This is a sufficient finite criterion, not a claim that mixed differences follow from first differences alone. Production of the actual smooth-weight mixed constants remains separate.
