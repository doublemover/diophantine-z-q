# Quaternionic surface period and Hodge carrier

This isolated four-module packet constructs the complex period carrier used for a real-split quaternion representation. It deliberately distinguishes the dimension-two surface carrier from the genus-five contact curve and its dimension-five Jacobian. It does not use the elliptic open-j family or the genus-zero level-three j atlas.

For Im(τ)>0 the literal map M↦M(τ,1) is a real-linear equivalence M₂(R)≃C², with its inverse calculated in coordinates. It constructs a complex structure squaring to−1 and commuting with left matrix multiplication. The complexified period map has an explicit rank-two kernel, a splitting, and a frame z↦rows(z_i,−z_iτ). Differentiating this actual inclusion and projecting gives−z, hence a bijective second fundamental map. Finally, for a specified quaternion representation and original subring/order O, the additive quotient uses precisely the image of O under the original period map and the order action actually descends.

This is an analytic-linear prerequisite, not an abelian-scheme or PEL-moduli construction. No full lattice/discreteness, compactness, polarization, algebraization, maximal order, discriminant210 identification, contact-moduli isomorphism, or metrized KS multiplier210 is supplied by these statements. The order is not replaced by standard integer matrices. A companion quaternion construction will provide the concrete representation; its remaining arithmetic/order conditions remain visible.

The quotient takes an arbitrary specified ring representation ρ and actual subring O, retaining their identity; it does not silently substitute M₂(Z), a Jacobian, or an elliptic j-family.
2. Indices: Both matrix rows and columns range over all Fin2 indices. Every order element and every original period is covered by the equivariance and quotient-action theorems. No component or root choice is suppressed.
3. Domain: Real period invertibility and transported complex structure require Im(τ)>0. Complex kernel and second-fundamental-map statements hold for every complex τ and do not claim a polarized torus at non-upper-half-plane parameters. Ring/order quotient statements need only the displayed representation and subring.
4. Coefficients: The inverse divides by Im(τ), justified by strict positivity. Kernel rows are exactly (z_i,−z_iτ), their derivatives are (0,−z_i), and projected derivatives are−z. No scalar rescaling, square root, or210 multiplier is inserted into the Hodge or variation claims.

6. Transition maps: Left matrix multiplication intertwines the real period map, commutes with the constructed complex structure and preserves the entire complex kernel. The original order's action descends through the actual additive subgroup quotient, with its map on representatives proved literally.
