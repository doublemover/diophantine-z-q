# Application contract: Construct bounded varying-rank Hilbert families

1. **source_requirements.** Required: Represent all ranks zero through N without connectedness assumptions.

Constructed: Actual scheme coproduct of fixed-rank parameters and actual coproduct of their whole original universal families.

Justification and boundary: Indexed coproduct topology proves literal component Cartesian squares.

2. **constructed_objects.** Required: Retain the unchanged original source and complete universal closed structure.

Constructed: Actual original source map, coefficient projection, full p-first ambient map and component source Cartesian squares.

Justification and boundary: A whole ambient component cover proves global closed immersion, not just support equality.

3. **index_coverage.** Required: Derive standard universal finiteness and bounded rank.

Constructed: Finite, flat and locally finite presentation from the component cover; rank computed on each component and bounded everywhere.

Justification and boundary: A rank equality characterizes membership in the actual parameter component.

4. **transition_maps.** Required: Use intrinsic bounded whole-family input.

Constructed: Full original ideal sheaf with whole finite-flat/FP projection and rank at most N at every base point.

Justification and boundary: No chosen decomposition, chart support, global basis, Cartier property or classifying map is assumed.

5. **hypothesis_checks.** Required: Derive the complete rank decomposition.

Constructed: Local constancy of the actual rank yields clopen loci, an entire cover and empty categorical intersections between different ranks.

Justification and boundary: This handles arbitrary disconnected and nonreduced coefficient schemes, including empty rank pieces.

6. **consumer_identity.** Required: Construct actual bounded classifying maps.

Constructed: Each derived rank piece has standard fixed-rank full ideal data; the all-scheme fixed-rank equivalence supplies local maps and whole-cover gluing supplies the global map.

Justification and boundary: Compatibility uses actual monomorphism cancellation or empty intersections, not chosen pointwise agreement.

7. **normalizations.** Required: Extract actual standard bounded families.

Constructed: Every bounded parameter map gives the whole original universal pullback and its canonical full kernel ideal with all properties and bound derived.

Justification and boundary: Full ideal coefficient comap and its genuine Cartesian square preserve the standard bound.

8. **downstream_boundary.** Required: Preserve the inverse and Picard boundaries.

Constructed: Both actual constructions and classifying-map evaluations are implemented.

Justification and boundary: Global bounded inverse identities/naturality, relative Cartier criterion and represented Picard/Jacobian remain separate.

9. **evidence_acceptance.** Required: Bind every source and actual application.

Constructed: Ten connected sources over exact908 with indexed coproduct and flat-rank primary APIs.

Justification and boundary: Static and independent source/API review are separate from hosted kernel acceptance; no local Lean or CI cap changes.
