   ring of integers and specified prime over (p). For two nonzero integer
   radicands of odd valuation, valuation additivity makes their product even.
   The actual product x*y has this product as its square. Its inertia invariance,
   together with fixing y, gives invariance of x after cancellation of the
   nonzero original y. Its nonvanishing follows from the literal nonzero radicand.
2. All four scaled quotient-field roots are covered. If every radicand valuation

   actual odd root is selected among the four, and every other root is handled
   by the even case or the product case. No squareclass independence, generic
   degree assumption or larger normal field is introduced.

   hypothesis. Fixing all scaled roots therefore fixes the original four roots.
   Original tensor generator extensionality and quotient-map surjectivity prove
   the automorphism is identity. The identity is of the full original field,
   not merely an action on a finite list of labels.

   Equality of evaluations implies t-inverse times s fixes that root; subgroup

   previous kernel argument forces identity. Its orientation yields s=t.
