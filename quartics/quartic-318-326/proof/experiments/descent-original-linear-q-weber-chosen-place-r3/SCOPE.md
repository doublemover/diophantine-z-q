# Actual selected Gaussian place and its integral over-place

A three-module Mathlib-only initial candidate. The original signed
literal pair a,b with a²+b²=p gives i₀=−a/b in F_p, with no independently
selected square root. The kernel of GaussianInt→F_p evaluated at i₀ is
the exact principal ideal (a+bi) and gives a literal height-one
valuation on FractionRing GaussianInt. Its residue equivalence sends
the same integral i to i₀ and its complex embedding sends i to Complex.I.

For any integral extension height-one prime above that exact kernel,
the prime residue embedding is induced by actual reduction of the
original Gaussian integers. It has contracted residue cardinal p,
the literal root condition for the prime-to-p specialization theorem,
and nonzero residue of M exactly when requested with p∤M. The third
module uses pinned integral lying-over to exhibit an actual over-prime.
No elliptic curve, arbitrary field-action, uniformization, or prime
distribution premise is pushed into these slots. Hosted compile
pending; finite controls are exact elementary arithmetic only.
