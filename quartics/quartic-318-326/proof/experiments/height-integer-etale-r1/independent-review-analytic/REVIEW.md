## Application comparison

1. The carrier remains the original constructed contact number field and its
   actual ring of integers. N is exactly the original fifteen-prime badProduct
   times the literal odd-contact radical. Positivity proves the required nonzero
   localization elements. No smaller constant-prime set or full contact radical
   is silently substituted.
2. The etale-locus proof covers every prime of that ring outside N. At a nonzero
   prime it takes the norm of the actual contraction to Z; the pinned integrality
   theorem proves this is a rational prime and supplies the actual prime-over
   instance. The exact integer ideal membership criterion converts divisibility
   of N to membership of N in P. Both bad-prime and contact-prime exclusions
   therefore follow for this same prime. The earlier unramified-at result then
   applies to the original P, with every hypothesis supplied.
3. The zero prime is treated separately. The primary generic unramified theorem
   requires a characteristic-zero integral-domain extension, torsion freeness and
   integrality, all present for the actual integer ring. Thus the generic point
   is not omitted when claiming the whole basic open. Finite presentation over Z
   is derived from the original finite module and the noetherian integer base.
   Flatness and unramifiedness supply IsEtaleAt by the actual pinned criterion.
4. The exact basic open D(N) is included in the actual etale locus. The primary
   localization equivalence requires finite presentation and then concludes
   Etale Z (Localization.Away N). The displayed localization of Z is also etale;
   the actual algebra tower permits restriction of scalars to Z[1/N]. This
   constructs global etaleness rather than assuming an etale model from generic
   field separability.
5. baseMap is precisely Localization.awayMap of the original Z inclusion. The
   scalar tower is the equality of the actual integer ring homomorphisms. The
   localized finite-module theorem uses powers of N on the base and their exact
   algebra-map image on the target, whose equality with powers of the target N
   is explicitly supplied. The target is a domain since that N is nonzero.
   The injectivity criterion for awayMap is discharged using the original
   integer inclusion, not an unproved assertion about spectra.
6. Faithful flatness uses the primary domain/flat/integral/faithful-action theorem.
   Etale gives flatness; finiteness gives integrality; the target-domain and
   injectivity facts supply its remaining hypotheses. The theorem derives prime
   spectrum surjectivity from integral lying-over. Consequently the actual Spec
   of baseMap is finite, etale and surjective. Ring/scheme comparison directions
   are correct. The singleton fppf statement consumes flatness, surjectivity and
   local finite presentation of this same Spec map, supplied by its proved etale
   and surjective properties.
7. integerToField is the actual localization lift into the unchanged L, with
   its restriction to every element of the original integer ring proved. The
   localization/fraction-ring theorem consumes the original integer ring's
   domain, its fraction field L and the displayed scalar tower. It identifies

   baseToRational map and localization extensionality give the exact rational

   silently assert uniqueness of arbitrary field embeddings.
8. All nine predecessor sources are byte-identical to the already accepted

   it concerns additional fine markings and a universal surface. The present
   cover is an integral model of the smaller field extension only. No prior

   changed or reclassified by this review.
9. The bounded contract accurately excludes a scheme generic-fiber pullback
