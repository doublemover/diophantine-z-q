# Component progression by finite quotient

This one-source continuation is pending hosted Lean checking and non-author review. No local Lean compilation was run. It depends only on the pinned Mathlib, not on uncompiled elliptic modules.

## Argument

Let G be a topological abelian group with a dense integer orbit generated by a. For positive k, let H be the closure of the integer orbit of k a. H is a closed subgroup. The quotient G/H is T1, and the image of a has additive order dividing k. Its integer orbit is therefore finite and closed. It is also dense, by continuity and surjectivity of the quotient map, so it is all of G/H. A finite T1 space is discrete, hence H is open as well as closed.

Every clopen neighborhood of zero contains the connected component of zero. Thus G^0 is contained in H for every positive k. If k a itself belongs to G^0, then its integer orbit and closure are contained in that closed subgroup, giving H = G^0. This does not require proving that multiplication by k is surjective on G^0 or constructing a real additive cover.

A fixed return m a in G^0 supplies the step condition for every m dividing k. In a compact G, the standard positive-recurrence theorem then gives arbitrarily late hits in every ambient open set meeting G^0, after any translate b in G^0.
