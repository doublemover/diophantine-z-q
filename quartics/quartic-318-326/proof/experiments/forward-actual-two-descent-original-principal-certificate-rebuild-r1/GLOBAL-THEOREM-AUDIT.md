# Scope boundary

At Ta, n≠0 is retained for the actual DVR. At O the pole calculation works for every n and arbitrary a. All off-support points have actual unit representatives unconditionally; the normalized valuation-zero corollary explicitly requires that particular stalk to be a DVR. We do not assert every scheme stalk is a DVR.

General divisor-functor and associated-sheaf APIs, all translations, same polarization theta lifts and geometric Weil-pairing identification remain separate. No external statement is an axiom and no structural result is a kernel result.
