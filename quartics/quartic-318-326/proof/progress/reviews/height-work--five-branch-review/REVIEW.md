# Independent five-branch finite group-action review

Accepted within the stated scope. The exact frozen source and audit were copied into this separate directory, freshly rebuilt with kernel checking, and recursively audited: twelve declarations, standard axioms only. Producer-source hashes were checked again after replay.

The mathematical review checks the actual stabilizer action, not only a numerical orbit formula. The faithful tame formal action implies tangent injectivity and at most two stabilizer elements. Nontriviality makes the order exactly two. The unique nonidentity fixer proves injectivity of the fixed-point incidence projection, and branch nontriviality proves surjectivity. Consequently the sum of fixed-locus cardinalities equals the ramification-locus cardinality.

The four distinct eight-point fixed-locus injections are disjoint by that uniqueness theorem and imply at least32 ramification points. The displayed degree16/genus5 Riemann-Hurwitz identity then forces quotient genus0 and total40. Each orbit has8 points by the actual orbit-stabilizer identity; the quotient has exactly5 orbits.

Retained geometric inputs are the actual curve/complete-local action realization, the actual Riemann-Hurwitz identity, and the four published quotient fixed-locus realizations. No claim of a Lean construction of those objects, or of GeometricRealization, is made.
