## 3. Uniform j-expansion majorant

Put r=|q|≤1/100. The usual normalized identities are

E4(q)=1+240 sum_{n≥1} sigma_3(n) q^n,
Δ(q)=q product_{n≥1}(1−q^n)^24,

so |E4|<4 at r≤1/100. For the product, the convergent logarithmic majorant is

≤ 24 sum r^n/(1−r^n)

≤ 24(1/100)/(1−1/100)² < log 2.
