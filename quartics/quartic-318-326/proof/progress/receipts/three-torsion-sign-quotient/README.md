# Actual sign quotient of primitive three torsion

The full scalar unit group modulo three is {1,−1}. For the actual rank-eight torsion equation algebra, this packet identifies its fixed algebra under that full diamond action with the actual rank-four x-coordinate algebra.

The proof works over a nontrivial commutative ring with invertible 3 and unit short-Weierstrass discriminant. Every element of the quadratic y-extension is expressed as a+by over the x algebra. Negation fixes a and sends by to −by. Because 2 and y are actual units, a fixed element has b=0. The resulting algebra equivalence is the canonical coefficient inclusion, not an abstract equal-rank isomorphism.

This is the algebraic sign quotient needed when comparing a chosen elliptic family with coarse modular sources. The eight points on a chosen elliptic curve should not be confused with the four generic points over j of the effective coarse Γ1(3) quotient: central −I identifies opposite markings. This packet proves the exact arithmetic invariant-algebra statement; it does not itself construct an analytic family comparison, descent, additive/symplectic level-three marking, PEL source, or global multiplication kernel.
