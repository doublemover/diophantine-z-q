# Exact regular-trace application

1. **Required coefficient hypotheses.** IntegralFieldTrace318 requires a normal domain A with its fraction field K, a finite A-algebra B, the specified maps into L and scalar towers A→B→L and A→K→L, and an explicitly finite-dimensional K-algebra field L. Algebra.isIntegral_trace is used only with that finite-dimensional hypothesis present. No freeness of B as an A-module is assumed or invented.
2. **Constructed regular value.** The actual image of x:B in L is integral over A by Module.Finite A B. Its K-linear field trace is integral over A, and normality puts that value in the original A. The injective A→K map proves additivity, scalar compatibility, the degree-times-scalar formula and uniqueness. This is the regular extension of the specified field trace; the helper does not assert an arbitrary B→L map is a fraction-field map or identify it with a different module trace.
3. **Actual Scheme maps.** AffineDominantTrace318 uses a finite dominant morphism f between integral Schemes, a nonempty normal affine target open U, and the actual section hom f.app U. The generic field algebra is exactly AffineDominantNorm318.fieldAlgebra f, from DominantFunctionField318.map f. map_germ supplies the exact tower relation. finite_app and the actual target germ fraction-ring theorem discharge the coefficient hypotheses.
4. **Interface and linearity.** The Scheme-level transfer is an AddMonoidHom, avoiding any unbound implicit source-section scalar action. Its separate linearity theorem explicitly states trace(f.app U a*x)=a*trace(x). Its embedding theorem is equality after the actual target germ with the actual source generic field trace, and pullback is the exact generic-degree multiple. No RingHom or multiplicativity is claimed for trace.
5. **Actual fifth specialization.** FifthChartTrace318 uses the unchanged FifthCompletedGlobal318.map and both original FifthChartNorm318.chartOpen values. Affineness, nonemptiness and normality are taken from the same exact original chart maps. generic_finite follows from the actual generic pullback finrank eight, not from a separately chosen field action. The generic comparison is with the immutable FifthGenericTrace318.trace.
6. **Domain coverage.** Each target chart is the whole original affine chart and each source domain is its entire inverse-image open. Branch points and elliptic zero are retained. These are individual chart transfers; no automatic claim of restriction compatibility or all-open gluing is made. The proof neither discards nilpotent test inputs nor uses tests to replace actual regular sections; the current source/target family itself is the fixed integral rational model.
7. **Exact downstream identities.** trace_pullback gives 8*s on actual target sections, and linearity preserves the literal f.app action. zero_of_generic_trace uses the actual injective target germ to lift a zero of the actual generic trace to equality of the whole regular section. It does not deduce arbitrary Scheme-map identities from field points.
8. **Evidence and authorship.** Strict first-time checks and required-endpoint recursive audit cover three modules. The scalar-tower setup is adapted from geometric's frozen norm4 packet, while the additive/integral-trace proofs are new. The author wrote generic trace3, earlier fifth short/branch/infinity/parameter and completed-curve work; those are retained predecessors, not self-independently accepted. The separately authored norm4 received a bounded consuming review with that collaboration disclosed. A local read-only symlink overlay exposed three already-checked official Mathlib modules absent from the base cache; no predecessor was rebuilt or edited.
9. **Boundary.** This packet provides genuine regular-function transfer prerequisites. It does not yet construct a trace on sheaves of rings/modules, differentials or line bundles, a Picard/Jacobian norm, polarization, Weil pairing or PEL model, or accept the full manuscript. Further compatibility and geometry require their own exact proofs and bindings.
