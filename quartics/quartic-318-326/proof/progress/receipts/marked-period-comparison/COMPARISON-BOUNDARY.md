# What is now bound, and what still must be constructed

The two recovered lines of work have different endpoints. They must not be conflated.

## Analytic endpoint

For N >= 5, the actual Gamma1(N) quotient of the upper half-plane has a compact connected complex analytic all-cusp filling with genuine rotated width-q charts. Genuine j is holomorphic on the original quotient and meromorphic in every chosen chart of the filled curve. At a cusp of actual width w it is G(q^w)/q^w, with G(0)=1, hence has order -w. The reciprocal has order w. The finite cusp complement is exactly its pole locus.

The selected marking is the actual point represented by 1/N. Actual integral modular transport fixes that marking exactly for Gamma1(N). Equality in the genuine quotient is equivalent to existence of such a marking-preserving transport. For H a subgroup of units modulo N, preservation of its marking orbit is exactly c=0 and a in H, equivalently c=0 and d in H; the two recorded diamond units are inverse.

## Arithmetic endpoint already available in predecessors

`PrimitivePointedModel318` starts with an algebra B over the literal open-j base, a faithfully flat algebra S, an actual equivariant splitting of S tensor B as the function algebra on primitive residue pairs, and a field-valued point. It constructs the invariant quotient, the domain component through that same point, and its proper normal arithmetic model. The point and its coefficient maps are preserved.

This is a substantial constructed model, but the input algebra and splitting are not yet the relative primitive N-torsion scheme. The separate geometric-model continuation constructs low-order examples; it does not supply general N at this checkpoint.

## Remaining comparison obligations

1. Construct the relative primitive N-torsion source of the literal elliptic scheme, together with its actual equivariant geometric splitting and point evaluation. This feeds the existing arithmetic pipeline without a substitute algebra.
2. Prove the algebraic moduli classification on the open j-line. The actual complex point transport now proved is one direction of the comparison; it does not yet prove classification of all complex elliptic curves or of all their algebraic isomorphisms.
3. Construct the actual H quotient on the analytic side, using the proved inverse-unit convention rather than identifying a with d by mistake. When minus one belongs to H, the central ambiguity disappears in the H-orbit; that assertion does not make the Gamma1 quotient itself a fine universal algebraic family.
4. Identify the same algebraic open cover with the analytic quotient, then extend across all cusps and identify the specific compactified model and its actual j-map. An arbitrary proper normalization or an abstract isomorphic compact surface is insufficient.
5. Preserve the point-level rational model. The point-of-order-N and mu_N-embedding conventions must not be identified by the identity on the upper half-plane. A Fricke/dual-isogeny transport changes the analytic parameter and diamond convention; it must be constructed when used.
6. Separately identify the required PEL/Shimura source, Hodge and Kodaira-Spencer objects, regular or semistable model, and global Arakelov realization. The auxiliary elliptic modular comparison does not itself discharge those obligations.

No statement in the new packets supplies any of these unproved comparisons as a hidden premise or asserts a final refined geometric realization or Diophantine characterization.
