## Precise boundary

The formal series extension, field extension, finite freeness, separability, conductor, different ideal and canonical exponents are proved. This does not construct an actual completed local curve ring or its coefficient field, identify a geometric group action with this formal chart, or prove a global arithmetic-surface extension theorem. General coefficient domains with 2 a unit and mixed-characteristic shifted fixed points are separate subsequent work. The normalized-coordinate isomorphism is explicit, but no unproved geometric identification is inserted into the different theorem.
