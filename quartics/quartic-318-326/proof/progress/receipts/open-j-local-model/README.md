# Actual open-j normal model: codimension-one completions and even contact

Two modules, eight public theorems and 11 exhaustively audited declarations, strictly rebuilt with `debug.skipKernelTC=false`. All recursive axioms are standard (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`).

## Genuine source objects

`OpenJLocalModel318` takes an actual domain B finite etale over the literal `OpenJModel318.Base N`, the compatible integral polynomial algebra, and N nonzero. Its model is exactly `integralClosure (Polynomial ℤ) B`, not a new abstract model record. The normal-model producer derives finiteness, normality and Noetherianity internally. At an actual prime-spectrum point of height one, the checked height-one theorem gives the actual Spec stalk DVR.

The packet proves that the actual completion of that stalk is a DVR, faithfully flat over it, and preserves every canonical valuation. Formal coefficients follow the actual map from the integral j-line into its integral closure, then the actual structure-sheaf germ, then the actual completion.

## Contact hypothesis reduced to the actual value

`EvenValuationNormalization318` derives a uniformizer/unit expression from the finite even value of the canonical DVR valuation. It then proves that the ORIGINAL quadratic contact algebra `RootRing f` has a constructed finite etale integral closure in its genuine generic fiber, an injective actual order map, and an actual generic-fiber algebra isomorphism. The unit and uniformizer are not hypotheses. This includes contact order zero; the quadratic algebra may be split.

The actual-model specialization `even_contact_normalization` combines this with the derived stalk DVR. `even_contact_formal_map` constructs the formal-series map with parameter equal to the original f when its even value is positive. Positive order is required only for formal evaluation; no claim is made about arbitrary formal evaluation at a unit.

## Retained geometric boundary

An actual connected finite etale open cover B and an actual height-one point are still inputs. The source curve/cover identification, its branch parameter f and even valuation, and invertibility of two at the point must be realized by the geometric source. No global compactification, resolution, adjunction, intersection, or Arakelov identity is claimed. In particular this does not assert the global geometric realization or unconditional final theorem.
